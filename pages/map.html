Concept Embedding Map

1259 AIMech concepts (curated records and AIO ontology classes) embedded with BAAI/bge-large-en-v1.5 over names, definitions, synonyms, classification, computational roles, and relationships, projected to 2D with PaCMAP. Points are colored by origin and kind/branch; click a point to open its concept page.

This map plots 1259 of the 1929 concepts this site lists. The other 670 are curated concepts with no coordinates in data/embeddings/corpus_map.json, the embedding file the map is drawn from. They were created from 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-19, after the newest plotted concept (2026-10-16), so the embeddings predate them. They are listed in Browse and appear here once the embeddings are regenerated (just embed, then just embed-map).
