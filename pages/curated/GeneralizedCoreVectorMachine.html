Generalized core vector machine

Algorithms AIMech:GeneralizedCoreVectorMachine DRAFT
Aliases: Generalized CVM, Generalized core vector machines
A kernel optimization algorithm that converts a quadratic program with a positive-semidefinite Gram matrix and simplex constraints into a center-constrained minimum enclosing ball, then approximates its solution by repeatedly enlarging and reoptimizing a core subset.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the Tsang-Kwok-Zurada generalized CVM solver, including its linear-term and nonconstant-diagonal reduction. The CVR formulation is a specific application; original CVM and BVM remain separate algorithms. Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #center-constrained-simplex-reduction
For a finite positive-semidefinite Gram matrix K and objective max b^T*alpha-alpha^T*K*alpha over alpha>=0 with sum(alpha)=1, choose eta so Delta=b-diag(K)+eta*1 is nonnegative. Augment each feature by sqrt(Delta_i) and constrain the added center coordinate to zero. The minimum-enclosing-ball dual becomes max alpha^T*(diag(K)+Delta)-alpha^T*K*alpha, which is the original objective plus eta. This removes the constant-diagonal restriction and permits a linear objective term; it does not solve arbitrary quadratic programs with different feasible constraints or indefinite kernels.

Mechanism #constrained-core-growth-and-distance
Maintain a core subset and its exact center-constrained minimum enclosing ball. Check whether every transformed point lies within the current radius multiplied by 1+epsilon; otherwise add a farthest outside point and recompute the constrained core ball. The center is c=sum_i alpha_i*phi_i in the unaugmented feature coordinates, and squared distance to augmented point j is alpha^T*K*alpha-2*(K*alpha)_j+K_jj+Delta_j. Kernel evaluation avoids explicit feature coordinates. The procedure differs from BVM fixed-radius center movement and extends the original CVM reduction.

Limitation #shift-dependent-approximation
Under exact core optimization and exhaustive outside-point checks, the final core radius R satisfies R<=R_star<=(1+epsilon)*R. Therefore the multiplicative objective certificate concerns the objective after addition of eta. Changing eta preserves the exact maximizing alpha but can alter when an approximate run stops. As an exact curator illustration, use base points0 and2, b=diag(K), eta=10000 and epsilon=1/1000. The one-point core at0 has radius squared10000 and the other squared distance10004, below the expanded squared radius10020.01; it can pass enclosure although its unshifted objective is0 and the exact optimum is1 at center1. With eta0 the same core fails. This is a stopping-test example, not a claim about the historical implementation or predictive error.

Limitation #core-size-and-implementation-limits
For positive approximation tolerance in the stated small-tolerance regime, the exact farthest-point/core-optimization analysis gives O(1/epsilon) core size and updates, with linear dependence on dataset size at fixed epsilon under the paper’s kernel-cost and inner-QP model. Dense-core auxiliary space is O(1/epsilon²), excluding input storage. Selecting eta and finding or certifying violations still require data access. The experiments instead use numerical SMO, caches, all data in memory and59-point probabilistic searches. Sampled termination is not an exhaustive enclosure certificate, and the paper’s bounds do not imply size-independent total memory, dimension-independent kernel cost or universal practical speed.

Scope #formulation-and-empirical-scope
The source derives generalized reductions for its paired squared-slack regression formulation, binary L2 SVM with observation-specific penalties, and a pair-difference ranking formulation with squared slack and a learned margin. These are specific reformulations, not every SVR or ranking-SVM variant. The reported experiments concern regression and imbalanced face classification; ranking experiments are explicitly future work. Shared optimization machinery does not imply universal accuracy, generalization, fairness or performance superiority.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The source assumes valid positive-semidefinite kernels and particular simplex-compatible formulations. Approximation guarantees depend on the additive objective shift, exact inner optimization and exhaustive violation checking.
  • All23 pages of the two primary PDFs were visually read. Source-specific claims are retained with their versions; historical software access failed, and no optimizer, training or benchmark was executed.
  • Exact rational calculations test negative learned width, primal-dual scaling and shift-sensitive termination. They are curator derivations from the displayed mathematics, not reproduced implementation behavior.
