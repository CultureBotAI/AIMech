Hammerstein–Wiener model

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #static-linear-static
Write the deterministic cascade as y=h(G(psi(u))), using h for the forward output function. The input function acts on each input sample, the linear block carries the dynamics, and h acts on its output. Equivalently, a finite-state linear block has x_next=A*x+B*psi(u) and y=h(C*x+D*psi(u)). With psi(v)=v², causal filter G=(1,2), h(v)=v+v³, inputs (1,2,-1) and zero prehistory, outputs are (2,222,738).

Scope #two-block-special-cases
An identity input function psi gives a Wiener model, and an identity output function h gives a Hammerstein model. If G is the identity, the model reduces to the static composition h(psi(u)); if both static functions are identities, it reduces to G. These inclusions do not make the ordered three-block family identical to either two-block family or to an identification algorithm.
  • supports Source: Data-Driven Prediction and Control of Hammerstein-Wiener Systems with Implicit Gaussian Processes · Locator: Introduction, Section 2 Equation 1 and Section 3.2 Remark 4, PDF/printed pp. 1,3,6; curator algebraic specializations in research/discovery/three-block-cascade-boundaries.py
Limitation #internal-gain-and-printed-formula
For nonzero a,b in the zero-state deterministic formulation, psi_new=a*psi, G_new=(b/a)*G and h_new(v)=h(v/b) preserve the response. With a=2,b=3 the finite example still gives (2,222,738). By contrast, the source’s printed Remark 3 transformation (a*psi,G/(a*b),b*h) gives (10/9,30,90) for the same example and does not generally preserve a nonlinear output function. The counterexample and corrected coordinate rescaling are curator derivations, not an author-issued correction.
  • supports Source: Data-Driven Prediction and Control of Hammerstein-Wiener Systems with Implicit Gaussian Processes · Locator: Section 2 Equation 1, PDF/printed p. 3; Section 3.2 Remark 3, p. 6; exact source-formula and coordinate-rescaling checks in research/discovery/three-block-cascade-boundaries.py
Scope #noise-location-and-predictor-scope
The inspected GP predictor places noise between G and the output function, giving h(z+e), and assumes a stable G and a monotone invertible output map. These are conditions of that particular noisy predictor, not requirements for evaluating every forward deterministic cascade. Noise before h is generally not independent additive noise after h: for h(z)=z+z³ and independent standard Gaussian e, E[h(z+e)]-h(z)=3z. The predictor’s virtual derivative observations promote monotonicity but do not guarantee it globally.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • This maintained record selects real-valued scalar discrete-time deterministic cascades. Noise models, multivariable, parallel, feedback, time-varying and neural variants require explicit additional scope. Finite examples use causal FIR blocks and zero prehistory; general linear blocks may have infinite impulse-response memory or specified nonzero initial states.
  • A memoryless function is a single-valued map on its stated domain. Forward composition does not in itself require inverse functions, monotonicity, Gaussian inputs, polynomial parameterization or a GP prior. Any use of inverse-coordinate predictors must state the valid domain and stronger assumptions.
  • Representation, minimality, internal identifiability, stable simulation and estimation accuracy are separate questions. Internal gain normalizations alone do not settle all ambiguities; overlapping special cases do not establish family equivalence.
  • The 2026 source is a preprint, and this record does not adopt its predictor as a general performance or control guarantee. The printed gain formula is contradicted by an explicit curator example. Multivariable Jacobian/determinant claims, GP uncertainty calibration and closed-loop guarantees remain separate audits.
  • All pages of the cited version were visually read. No model was trained, no empirical result was replicated and no paid provider was run. This record remains DRAFT; author self-review is not independent scientific approval.
