Upward-downward nested antithetic mixture

Algorithms AIMech:UpwardDownwardNestedAntitheticMixture DRAFT
A finite conditional Markov update that chooses with equal probability between complete upward and downward nested antithetic modification kernels for the same target distribution.

UDNAM is a constant equal choice between complete upward and downward NAM kernels, with consistent independently selected valid tie policies. A mixture is not a single supplied-order NAM modification, sequential U-then-D composition or state-dependent component selection. The finite (4,3,2)/9 two-positive-diagonal witness excludes every single NAM ordering. UNAM tie invariance and DNAM tie sensitivity are distinguished. Shared local reversible kernels or special cases with other samplers do not establish identity.

6 Claims
Mechanism #complete-upward-and-downward-components
Let pi be a strictly positive normalized probability mass function on a finite state space. Construct UNAM from a nondecreasing probability order and DNAM from a nonincreasing order. Each is a complete NAM construction starting with K(i,j)=pi(j). At a stage whose remaining block has K(i,j)=r*pi(j), let q be its focal-state mass and s the nonempty tail mass. Set delta=r*min(q/s,s/q). Increase focal-to-tail entries by delta*pi(j) and tail-to-focal entries by delta*q; decrease the focal diagonal by delta*s and tail-to-tail entries by delta*pi(j)*q/s. Previously determined entries are unchanged. Continue on the tail if q<s; if q>=s its block becomes zero and the construction stops. A one-state target already has the identity kernel. The component is not an incomplete first-stage modification.

Mechanism #constant-half-mixture-selection
Write U and D for the two completed component matrices. UDNAM uses M(i,j)=(U(i,j)+D(i,j))/2. It can sample a fresh fair component choice for each local update and then sample that component row, or sample directly from the averaged row. The half weights do not depend on the current state label. Selecting a component once for a whole chain, applying U followed by D, or using current-state-dependent component weights does not define this specified transition matrix. Both component ordering rules define all rows for the same target.

Mechanism #reversible-mixture-and-conditional-use
Both U and D are nonnegative stochastic matrices satisfying detailed balance with pi. Their arithmetic average therefore has row sums one and satisfies pi(i)*M(i,j)=pi(j)*M(j,i), so it preserves pi. Used for a valid full conditional with other coordinates fixed, the update preserves the joint target. The probability ordering may change when that conditioning environment changes. Fixed mixtures and fixed sequential compositions of valid conditional updates preserve the joint target too; the local reversibility statement does not imply that a full sequential sweep is reversible.

Scope #downward-tie-policy
UNAM is unchanged by permutations within equal-probability groups, whereas DNAM can depend on its tie order. UDNAM inherits this dependence and requires a stated consistent downward tie policy. For pi=(1,3,3,5)/12, the original exact calculations use upward orders (1,2,3,4) and (1,3,2,4), with corresponding downward orders (4,3,2,1) and (4,2,3,1), in one-based labels. The UNAM kernel is unchanged, but the UDNAM diagonals are respectively (0,1/42,0,5/44) and (0,0,1/42,5/44). Both mixtures remain reversible. Unlike the paired-tower mixture, UDNAM does not need its two component orders to be exact reversals for reversibility, because each component is already reversible.

Scope #distinct-mixture-and-self-mass
The equal mixture is not generally one NAM focal ordering. At pi=(4,3,2)/9, its diagonal is (5/56,1/30,0), with two positive entries, whereas a complete single-order NAM construction has at most one. Original exact calculations confirm the difference for all six focal permutations on this target, retaining duplicate resulting matrices in that enumeration. UDNAM also need not minimize self transitions: this target has no mass exceeding one half, yet its mixture has positive stationary self mass. Agreement with UNAM, DNAM or other samplers on special targets does not identify the full constructions.

Limitation #invariance-is-not-convergence
For a uniform binary target, both components and their mixture flip the state deterministically. For two independent uniform binary coordinates, a fixed full scan maps (a,b) to (1-a,1-b), so it has two closed classes even though the joint uniform target is invariant. Thus a reversible local mixture and reduced self transitions do not establish aperiodicity, joint exploration or convergence from every initial state. The source discusses efficiency comparisons under additional assumptions; this record makes no general variance, spectral-efficiency, burn-in, finite-run accuracy or runtime claim.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The maintained scope is a finite strictly positive normalized target and consistent complete component kernels. Zero-current conventions, approximate normalization and countably infinite upward ordering are outside this scope.
  • The source calls this equal mixture UDNAM. Fixed/upward/downward NAM policies, ZDNAM completion, UDST and arbitrary mixtures of invariant kernels are not exact aliases for it.
  • The original FHY1992 full text was not obtained. Historical attribution of the UNAM component does not identify every earlier estimator or truncated modification with the full UDNAM mixture.
