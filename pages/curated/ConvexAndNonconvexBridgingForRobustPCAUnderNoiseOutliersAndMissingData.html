Convex and Nonconvex Optimization Bridging for Robust PCA under Noise, Outliers, and Missing Data

Mathematical Methods AIMech:ConvexAndNonconvexBridgingForRobustPCAUnderNoiseOutliersAndMissingData DRAFT
Aliases: Convex-Nonconvex Bridging for Noisy Robust PCA, Chen-Fan-Ma-Yan Primal-Dual Bridging for Principal Component Pursuit, Factored Nonconvex Certificate for Nuclear-Norm + L1 Robust PCA
A unified convex–nonconvex optimization analysis and estimation framework (introduced by Yuxin Chen, Jianqing Fan, Cong Ma, and Yuling Yan in 2021) for noisy Robust Principal Component Analysis with missing data, where partial observations on $\Omega \subseteq [n]\times[n]$ obey $Y_{i,j} = M_{i,j}^\star + S_{i,j}^\star + E_{i,j}$ with rank-$r$ positive semidefinite signal $\boldsymbol{M}^\star = \boldsymbol{U}^\star\boldsymbol{U}^{\star\top}$, sparse arbitrary-magnitude outliers $\boldsymbol{S}^\star$, and dense sub-Gaussian noise $\boldsymbol{E}$. Rather than constructing a fragile least-squares dual certificate directly for the convex Principal Component Pursuit (PCP) program $\min_{\boldsymbol{M}\succeq\boldsymbol{0},\boldsymbol{S}} \frac{1}{2}\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{M}+\boldsymbol{S}-\boldsymbol{Y})\|_F^2 + \lambda\,\mathrm{Tr}(\boldsymbol{M}) + \tau\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{S})\|_1$ (Equation 2.3), the method analyzes an auxiliary nonconvex Burer–Monteiro factored estimator $\min_{\boldsymbol{U}\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times r},\boldsymbol{S}} \frac{1}{2}\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{U}\boldsymbol{U}^\top+\boldsymbol{S}-\boldsymbol{Y})\|_F^2 + \frac{\lambda}{2}\|\boldsymbol{U}\|_F^2 + \tau\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{S})\|_1$ (Equation 3.1) via alternating gradient descent on $\boldsymbol{U}$ and exact soft-thresholding $\boldsymbol{S}^{t+1} = \mathcal{S}_\tau(\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Y} - \boldsymbol{U}^t\boldsymbol{U}^{t\top}))$ (Algorithm 1). Using leave-one-out trajectory analysis to prove that the nonconvex stationary point $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}},\widehat{\boldsymbol{S}})$ satisfies $\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}\widehat{\boldsymbol{H}} - \boldsymbol{U}^\star\|_{2,\infty} \lesssim \frac{\sigma\sqrt{n\log n}}{p\sqrt{\sigma_{\min}}} \sqrt{\frac{\mu r}{n}}$ and strict residual spectral bound $\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Y} - \widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}^\top - \widehat{\boldsymbol{S}})\| < \lambda$ (Lemma 4.1), Proposition 4.1 proves that $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} = \widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}^\top, \widehat{\boldsymbol{S}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} = \widehat{\boldsymbol{S}})$ automatically satisfies the KKT optimality conditions of the convex program (Equation 2.3). Consequently, Theorem 2.1 establishes that the convex PCP solution is automatically exact rank-$r$, achieves optimal entrywise $\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_\infty$ and $\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_F$ error bounds without extra $\sqrt{n}$ suboptimal factors, and recovers the outlier support $\mathrm{supp}(\boldsymbol{S}^\star)$.

Distinct from `AIMech:DebiasedNoisyMatrixCompletionInferenceAndUncertaintyQuantification` (Chen, Fan, Ma, & Yan 2019, which studies outlier-free matrix completion with $\boldsymbol{S}^\star = \boldsymbol{0}$) and from `AIMech:ImplicitRegularizationInNonconvexStatisticalEstimationByLeaveOneOutTrajectoryAnalysis` (Ma, Wang, Chi, & Chen 2020, which analyzes unregularized nonconvex algorithms in noiseless/Gaussian models without bridging to the global optimality and rank-$r$ exactness of convex nuclear-norm plus $\ell_1$-penalized Robust PCA).

Mechanism #claim-nonconvex-stationary-point-as-primal-dual-certificate-for-convex-pcp
Proposition 4.1 and Lemma 4.1 prove that whenever a stationary point $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}},\widehat{\boldsymbol{S}})$ of the nonconvex factored problem $\min_{\boldsymbol{U}\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times r},\boldsymbol{S}} \frac{1}{2}\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{U}\boldsymbol{U}^\top + \boldsymbol{S} - \boldsymbol{Y})\|_F^2 + \frac{\lambda}{2}\|\boldsymbol{U}\|_F^2 + \tau\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{S})\|_1$ (Equation 3.1) satisfies the spectral norm condition $\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Y} - \widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}^\top - \widehat{\boldsymbol{S}})\| < \lambda$, the pair $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} = \widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}}^\top, \widehat{\boldsymbol{S}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} = \widehat{\boldsymbol{S}})$ is the unique global minimizer of the convex Principal Component Pursuit program (Equation 2.3) and has exact rank $r$ (Sections 3.1 and 4.1, Equation 3.1, Proposition 4.1, Lemma 4.1).

Mechanism #claim-entrywise-linf-and-two-to-inf-accuracy-via-leave-one-out-soft-thresholding
Using $n$ leave-one-out auxiliary trajectories $(\boldsymbol{U}^{t,(m)}, \boldsymbol{S}^{t,(m)})$ that replace row/column $m$'s random observations, noise, and outlier locations with their population counterparts (Section 4.3, Equation 4.12) together with the non-expansiveness of the soft-thresholding operator $|\mathcal{S}_\tau(a) - \mathcal{S}_\tau(b)| \le |a-b|$, Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 3.1 prove that with $\lambda \asymp \sigma\sqrt{np\log n}$ and $\tau \asymp \sigma\sqrt{\log n} + \frac{\sigma\sqrt{n\log n}}{p}\frac{\mu r}{n}$, both the convex and nonconvex estimators achieve $\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_\infty \lesssim \frac{\sigma\sqrt{n\log n}}{p}\frac{\mu r}{n}$ and $\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_F \lesssim \frac{\sigma\sqrt{nr\log n}}{p}$ under outlier fraction $\alpha \lesssim 1/(\kappa^3\mu r)$ (Sections 2.2, 3.2, and 4.2–4.3, Theorems 2.1 and 3.1).

Application #claim-exact-outlier-support-recovery-and-sign-consistency
Because $\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_\infty + \|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{E})\|_\infty < \tau$ with high probability and $\widehat{\boldsymbol{S}}_{\mathrm{cvx}} = \mathcal{S}_\tau(\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Y} - \widehat{\boldsymbol{M}}_{\mathrm{cvx}}))$, Corollary 2.1 proves that no clean entry $(i,j)\notin\mathrm{supp}(\boldsymbol{S}^\star)$ is falsely flagged ($\mathrm{supp}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{S}}_{\mathrm{cvx}}) \subseteq \mathrm{supp}(\boldsymbol{S}^\star)$) and every observed outlier with $|S_{i,j}^\star| > 2\tau$ is recovered with its exact sign $\mathrm{sign}(\widehat{S}_{\mathrm{cvx},i,j}) = \mathrm{sign}(S_{i,j}^\star)$ (Section 2.2, Corollary 2.1).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The primal-dual bridging argument requires the outlier sparsity fraction alpha to satisfy alpha <= c / (kappa^3 mu r); when outliers are concentrated in a single row or column beyond this fraction, the low-rank incoherence of the iterates can be violated.
  • Exact recovery of the outlier support in Corollary 2.1 requires minimum outlier magnitude |S*_{i,j}| > 2 tau; smaller outliers below the soft-thresholding level tau are absorbed into the dense noise floor.
