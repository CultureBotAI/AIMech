Searching and Sampling Uniform Confidence Intervals for Invalid Instruments

Algorithms AIMech:SearchingAndSamplingUniformConfidenceIntervalsForInvalidInstruments DRAFT
Aliases: Searching and Sampling Confidence Intervals for Invalid Instruments, RobustIV Searching and Sampling, TSHT Searching and Sampling Uniform Confidence Intervals, Searching Confidence Interval for Invalid Instruments, Sampling Confidence Interval for Invalid Instruments
A post-selection-robust causal-inference methodology (introduced by Zijian Guo in 2023) for constructing uniformly valid confidence intervals for a treatment effect $\beta^*$ in linear instrumental-variable models with possibly invalid instruments when locally invalid instruments satisfy $0 < |\pi_j^*/\gamma_j^*| < s_j(n) \asymp \sqrt{\log n / n}$ (Definition 1, Equation 12) and cannot be consistently separated from valid instruments. Given an initial candidate set $\widehat{\mathcal{V}}$ (such as the symmetric-voting plurality set $\widehat{\mathcal{V}}^{\rm TSHT} = \{l \in \widehat{\mathcal{S}} : \max_{k \in \widehat{\mathcal{S}}, j \in \widehat{\mathcal{W}}} \widehat{\Pi}_{j,k}\widehat{\Pi}_{k,l} = 1\}$ satisfying $\mathcal{V} \subset \widehat{\mathcal{V}} \subset \mathcal{V} \cup \mathcal{I}(0, s(n))$ and $|\mathcal{V}| > |\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|/2$ under finite-sample plurality, Equations 30–34), the *Searching Confidence Interval* (Algorithms 1 and 3, Equations 15–20) evaluates for each grid point $\beta \in [L, U]$ the residual $\widehat{\pi}_j(\beta) = \widehat{\Gamma}_j - \beta\widehat{\gamma}_j$ and noise threshold $\widehat{\rho}_j(\beta, \alpha) = \Phi^{-1}(1 - \alpha/(2|\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|))\sqrt{(\widehat{V}_{\Gamma,jj} + \beta^2\widehat{V}_{\gamma,jj} - 2\beta\widehat{C}_{jj})/n}$, counts the implied valid instruments $M(\beta) = \sum_{j \in \widehat{\mathcal{V}}} \mathbf{1}(|\widehat{\pi}_j(\beta)| \le \widehat{\rho}_j(\beta, \alpha))$, and retains $\mathrm{CI}^{\rm search} = [\min_{\beta \in \mathcal{B}: M(\beta) > |\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|/2} \beta,\; \max_{\beta \in \mathcal{B}: M(\beta) > |\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|/2} \beta]$. The *Sampling Confidence Interval* (Algorithms 2 and 3, Equations 21–26) draws $M$ conditionally centered Gaussian resamples $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Gamma}}^{[m]}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}^{[m]})_{m=1}^M$ matching the asymptotic covariance $\widehat{\mathbf{Cov}}$, uses a shrinkage threshold $\lambda \asymp (\log n / M)^{1/(2|\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|)}$ (Proposition 1) so the resampled searches $[\beta_{\min}^{[m]}(\lambda), \beta_{\max}^{[m]}(\lambda)]$ using $\lambda\widehat{\rho}_j(\beta, \alpha)$ contain a realization with $\max_{j \in \mathcal{V}}|e_j^{[m]}| \le \lambda\widehat{\rho}_j$, and takes the union $\mathrm{CI}^{\rm sample} = [\min_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \beta_{\min}^{[m]}(\lambda),\; \max_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \beta_{\max}^{[m]}(\lambda)]$, achieving uniform asymptotic coverage $\ge 1 - \alpha$ and parametric $O_p(n^{-1/2})$ length without any separation condition on locally invalid instruments (Theorems 1–3).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Resolved against all 442 pinned AIO concepts and 672 maintained AIMech concepts. Distinct from `AIMech:TwoStageHardThresholdingWithVotingAndPluralityRuleForInvalidInstruments` (Guo, Kang, Cai, & Small 2018, which assumes well-separated invalidity levels so $\widehat{\mathcal{V}} = \mathcal{V}$ consistently and suffers post-selection undercoverage when locally invalid instruments satisfy $0 < |\pi_j^*/\gamma_j^*| < s_j(n)$), from `AIMech:SomeInvalidSomeValidInstrumentalVariablesEstimatorAndMajorityRuleIdentifiability` (Kang, Zhang, Cai, & Small 2016, which provides $\ell_1$ penalized point estimation under the 50% majority rule without uniform confidence intervals), and from `AIMech:SelfTuningPivotalHighDimensionalInstrumentalVariablesAndSensitivityConfidenceSets` (Gautier & Tsybakov 2018, which requires a user-specified bound $\Upsilon$ on invalid-instrument violations rather than searching and sampling over majority/plurality voting counts).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-locally-invalid-iv-post-selection-bias-and-searching-coverage
When locally invalid instruments satisfy $0 < |\pi_j^*/\gamma_j^*| < s_j(n) = 2\sqrt{\log n}\max_{k \in \mathcal{V}}|\mathrm{T}_{j,k}^0|$ (Definition 1, Equation 12), two-stage hard thresholding cannot separate them from valid instruments, biasing post-selection point estimators by $\sum_{j \in \widehat{\mathcal{V}} \setminus \mathcal{V}} \gamma_j^*\pi_j^* / \sum_{j \in \widehat{\mathcal{V}}}(\gamma_j^*)^2$; by contrast, whenever the initial set $\widehat{\mathcal{V}}$ satisfies the sandwich event $\mathcal{V} \subset \widehat{\mathcal{V}} \subset \mathcal{V} \cup \mathcal{I}(0, s(n))$ with $|\mathcal{V}| > |\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|/2$ (Equation 30, Lemma 1), every $\beta$ with $M(\beta) > |\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|/2$ must satisfy $|\widehat{\pi}_j(\beta)| \le \widehat{\rho}_j(\beta, \alpha)$ for at least one truly valid instrument $j \in \mathcal{V}$ by the pigeonhole principle, guaranteeing uniform asymptotic coverage $\liminf_{n \to \infty} \mathbb{P}(\beta^* \in \mathrm{CI}^{\rm search}) \ge 1 - \alpha$ and $O_p(n^{-1/2})$ length (Theorem 1 and Theorem 3).

Mechanism #claim-sampling-shrinkage-concentration-and-uniform-coverage
Given $M$ conditional Gaussian draws $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Gamma}}^{[m]}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}^{[m]}) \sim N((\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Gamma}}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}), \widehat{\mathbf{Cov}})$ (Equation 21), Proposition 1 proves that $\mathbb{P}(\min_{1 \le m \le M}\max_{j \in \mathcal{V}}|U_j^{[m]}(\beta^*)| \le \mathrm{err}_n(M, \alpha_0) \mid \mathcal{D}) \ge 1 - \alpha_0$ where $\mathrm{err}_n(M, \alpha_0) = [\{-2\log(1 - \alpha_0^{1/M})\}^{1/|\mathcal{V}|} / c(\mathcal{D})]\sqrt{\max_{j \in \mathcal{V}}\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}(\widehat{\Gamma}_j - \beta^*\widehat{\gamma}_j)} \asymp (\{\log(1/\alpha_0)/M\}^{1/|\mathcal{V}|} / \sqrt{n})$, allowing the Sampling Confidence Interval $\mathrm{CI}^{\rm sample}$ with shrinkage factor $\lambda \asymp (\log n / M)^{1/(2|\widehat{\mathcal{V}}|)}$ to shrink the noise threshold to $\lambda\widehat{\rho}_j(\beta, \alpha)$ while maintaining uniform asymptotic coverage $\liminf_{n \to \infty}\mathbb{P}(\beta^* \in \mathrm{CI}^{\rm sample}) \ge 1 - \alpha$ and strictly tighter finite-sample intervals than pure searching (Proposition 1, Theorem 2, and Theorem 3).

Scope #claim-data-dependent-lambda-selection-and-heteroscedastic-high-dimensional-extension
Remark 2 (Section 4.2, p. 16) provides a data-dependent rule that selects the smallest shrinkage factor $\lambda$ on a grid such that at least a pre-specified proportion $\mathrm{prop} = 10\%$ of the $M = 1000$ resampled intervals $[\beta_{\min}^{[m]}(\lambda), \beta_{\max}^{[m]}(\lambda)]$ are non-empty, and Section 5.2 (pp. 19–20) extends both Searching and Sampling CIs to heteroscedastic errors and high-dimensional covariates/IVs ($p \gg n$) by plugging in debiased Lasso reduced-form estimators $(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Gamma}}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})$ and heteroscedasticity-robust covariance matrices $(\widehat{\mathbf{V}}^{\Gamma}, \widehat{\mathbf{V}}^{\gamma}, \widehat{\mathbf{C}})$.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • When the initial set V_hat is constructed via plurality voting (Algorithm 3), Lemma 1 requires the finite-sample plurality condition |V| > max_{c != 0} |{j in S_str(n) : |pi_j* / gamma_j* - c| <= s_j(n)}| (Assumption 2', p. 18); if a cluster of locally invalid instruments within separation s_j(n) of zero or of a nonzero constant c outnumbers the truly valid instruments, |V| > |V_hat^TSHT|/2 fails and neither Searching nor Sampling guarantees coverage.
  • In Proposition 1, the resampling error bound err_n(M, alpha_0) scales as (log(1/alpha_0)/M)^{1/|V|} / sqrt(n), whose exponent 1/|V| suffers a curse of dimensionality when the number of valid instruments |V| is large (requiring larger M or yielding a shrinkage factor lambda closer to 1).
  • Primary-source typographical audit of Guo (2023, arXiv:2104.06911v5) uncovered five minor notation and typographical issues that are corrected in this record: (1) Section 2 p. 4 line 3 writes 'instead of a casual model' ('casual' instead of 'causal') and line 5 spells 'endogeneous'; (2) Section 3 Fig. 2 (p. 8), Section 7 Fig. 5 (p. 23), and Supplement Fig. E.1 (p. 67) label the horizontal axes 'Violation Stength' ('Stength' instead of 'Strength'); (3) Section 5 p. 17 line 18 writes 'we compare the number of votes to |V_hat|/2,.' with a stray comma-period ',.'; (4) Section 7 p. 21 line 16 writes 'phi* = (0.6, ..., 1.5)^T in (2) and psi* = (1.1, ..., 2)^T in (1)', swapping the equation numbers (phi* is the outcome covariate vector in Eq. 1 and psi* is the treatment covariate vector in Eq. 2); and (5) Supplement Sections A.4, A.6, E.1, E.2 (pp. 32-34, 64, 67-68) spell 'Homoscadastic'/'homoscadastic', Section C.1 p. 39 spells 'agumented covariance matrix', and Lemma 4 p. 53 spells 'satsifies'.
