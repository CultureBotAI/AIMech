Sparsegen

Mathematical Methods AIMech:Sparsegen DRAFT
A parameterized probability mapping that minimizes squared distance from a transformed score vector g(z), minus lambda times the squared output norm, over the probability simplex, for lambda less than one.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The general probability-mapping family of the 2018 primary paper, not a trained model. Sparsegen-lin and sparsehourglass are specialized members, not aliases for the whole family. Identity search and mapping context are retained in the discovery ledger.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #scaled-projection
The mapping equals sparsemax applied to g(z)/(1-lambda). Choosing g(z)=z and lambda=0 recovers sparsemax; the general family is not identical to that special case.
  • supports Source: On Controllable Sparse Alternatives to Softmax · Locator: Section 3, page 3, equation (2); Section 3.1, page 4, Example 3
Limitation #parameter-scope
Output sparsity depends on the transformation, parameter and input; a zero-valued output coordinate is not guaranteed for every input.
  • supports Source: On Controllable Sparse Alternatives to Softmax · Locator: Sections 3 and 3.2, pages 3-4, Figure 2 sparse and non-sparse regions

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The support-limit prose on page 3 reverses the direction described in Section 3.2 and equation (2); no limiting-behavior claim is asserted here.
  • Properties of arbitrary g or input-dependent parameter choices require their own assumptions; this record does not assert universal invariances or differentiability.
  • No benchmark reproduction or independent scientific review was performed.
