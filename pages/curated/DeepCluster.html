DeepCluster

Algorithms AIMech:DeepCluster DRAFT
Aliases: Deep clustering for unsupervised learning of visual features
A visual representation-learning procedure that alternates clustering current neural features into pseudo-labels and training the feature extractor and a classifier to predict those labels.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record covers the original k-means-based DeepCluster procedure of Caron et al. It is not generic deep clustering, standalone k-means, DeepCluster-v2, or a particular pretrained model. Cluster assignments provide training targets; the k-means centroid matrix is not used as the discriminative classifier. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Extract features of the unlabeled training images with the current encoder while holding its parameters fixed. In the original reported configuration, PCA-reduce and whiten these features, then L2-normalize them before k-means. Cluster them by alternating nearest-centroid assignment and centroid updates toward the squared-distance objective; use the resulting hard cluster indices as pseudo-labels. The paper handles empty clusters by perturbing a selected nonempty centroid and reassigning its members between the two centers. Then hold pseudo-labels fixed and optimize the encoder and a classification head with cross-entropy on augmented images. Sample approximately uniformly across nonempty pseudo-label groups so large clusters do not dominate training. Repeat full-dataset feature extraction, clustering and discriminative training. The pinned author implementation reinitializes the final linear classifier for each reclustering round, uses 20 k-means iterations, and uses sampled pseudo-labels rather than differentiating through k-means.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • K-means is nonconvex and the finite iteration implementation does not certify a global optimum. The alternating clustering and classification stages are not a proof of descent for one common objective or of general convergence.
  • Empty-cluster repair and pseudo-label sampling are practical safeguards, not a universal guarantee against collapsed representations. The inspected wrapper delegates clustering to Faiss; its library internals were not audited.
  • Uniform training exposure to pseudo-label groups does not force equal cluster sizes. Cluster IDs can be permuted or change between rounds and are not verified semantic class labels.
  • PCA dimension 256, Sobel filtering, architecture, augmentations and epoch schedule describe the original experimental configuration. Whitening and L2 normalization require suitable numerical handling; no performance equivalence for arbitrary variants is asserted.
  • Training discards ImageNet class labels, but the paper selects hyperparameters using labeled Pascal VOC validation. Full-dataset feature extraction has a cost; no training or benchmark reproduction was performed.
