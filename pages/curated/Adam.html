Adam

Algorithms AIMech:Adam DRAFT
Aliases: Adaptive moment estimation
Stochastic gradient optimization using bias-corrected exponential estimates of first and second gradient moments to scale parameter updates.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Adam specifies adaptive moment-based updates; the local SGD concept does not require these estimates. It is an optimizer, not a training task or a trained model.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #mechanism
The update divides the bias-corrected first moment by the square root of the bias-corrected second raw moment plus a numerical stabilizer.
  • supports Source: Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization · Locator: Section 2 and Algorithm 1, PDF/printed p. 2
Limitation #convergence-counterexample
In the paper's bounded, projected diagonal optimization setting, the analysis constructs stochastic convex problems on which Adam with fixed moment coefficients beta1 and beta2 in [0, 1), satisfying beta1 < sqrt(beta2), does not converge to the optimum.
  • supports Source: On the Convergence of Adam and Beyond · Locator: Section 2, PDF/printed pp. 2–3, including the debiasing footnote; Theorem 3 and scope discussion, PDF/printed p. 5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • No universal convergence or benchmark superiority claim is curated.
  • AdamW, AdaMax and other variants require their own identity assessment.
  • The reported counterexample is an existence result for the analyzed Adam setting, not a statement that Adam fails on every training problem or that all later variants share the result.
  • The analysis introduces projection and a decreasing step-size setting, omits debiasing for exposition, and states that its arguments extend to the debiased version; it does not analyze every implementation configuration.
