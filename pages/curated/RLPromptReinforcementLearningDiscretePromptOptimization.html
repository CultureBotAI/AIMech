RLPrompt Reinforcement Learning Discrete Prompt Optimization

Algorithms AIMech:RLPromptReinforcementLearningDiscretePromptOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: RLPrompt, Reinforcement Learning Discrete Text Prompt Optimization, Policy-Network Discrete Prompt Optimization with Stabilized Rewards
A gradient-free discrete text prompt optimization algorithm that formulates prompt construction z = [z_1, ..., z_T] in V^T as a sequential reinforcement learning problem max_theta R(y_LM(z_hat, x)) with z_hat ~ prod_{t=1}^T pi_theta(z_t | z_{<t}, x), parameterizes the policy pi_theta by inserting a single trainable MLP module into a frozen compact policy language model (such as distilGPT-2), and trains theta via on-policy Soft Q-Learning using input-specific z-score reward normalization z-score(z, x) = (R_x(z) - mean_{z'} R_x(z')) / stdev_{z'} R_x(z') and piecewise margin rewards R(x, c) = lambda_1^{1-Correct} lambda_2^{Correct} Gap_z(c).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from AutoPrompt (Shin et al., EMNLP 2020, AIMech:AutoPromptGradientGuidedDiscretePromptSearch), which requires white-box gradients from the target LM, from Black-Box Tuning (Sun et al., ICML 2022, AIMech:BBTBlackBoxTuningForLanguageModelAsAService), which optimizes continuous soft prompt embeddings via CMA-ES in a random linear subspace and requires server-side continuous embedding injection, and from heuristic discrete prompt enumeration/editing methods such as GrIPS (Prasad et al., 2022).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r726-claim-policy-mlp-and-sql
Rather than directly editing discrete vocabulary tokens or requiring gradients from the target task LM, RLPrompt inserts a 1-hidden-layer MLP (2048 hidden units, 3.1M parameters or 3.8% of backbone size) into a frozen 82M-parameter distilGPT-2 policy LM, updates only the MLP parameters via on-policy Soft Q-Learning (SQL), and discards the policy network after training to deploy the deterministic greedy discrete prompt.
  • supports Source: RLPrompt: Optimizing Discrete Text Prompts with Reinforcement Learning · Locator: Section 2.2-2.3 & Appendix A.1, Equation 2, Figure 1, pp. 3-4, 15
Mechanism #r726-claim-zscore-and-piecewise-reward
To stabilize RL training against input-dependent reward scales and adversarial single-class collapse, RLPrompt normalizes rewards across sampled prompt batches Z(x) per input via z-score(z, x) = (R_x(z) - mean R_x) / stdev R_x and uses a piecewise hinge-like classification reward R(x, c) = lambda_1^{1-Correct} lambda_2^{Correct} (P_z(c) - max_{c' != c} P_z(c')) that multiplies positive margins (Correct = 1) by a larger bonus weight lambda_2 > lambda_1.
  • supports Source: RLPrompt: Optimizing Discrete Text Prompts with Reinforcement Learning · Locator: Section 2.4, Section 3.1, & Section 3.2, Equations 3-4, Figures 3, 5, and 6, pp. 4-8, 15-17
Application #r726-claim-classification-style-and-cross-lm-transfer
With only 5 discrete tokens, RLPrompt achieves 75.8% average 16-shot accuracy on RoBERTa-large across 7 classification benchmarks (outperforming Fine-Tuning at 69.5%, Soft Prompt Tuning at 69.2%, Black-Box Tuning at 74.7%, GrIPS at 69.4%, and AutoPrompt at 56.7%), achieves state-of-the-art joint score J(C,S,F) = 61.4 on unsupervised Yelp sentiment style transfer with GPT-2-xl, and discovers gibberish discrete prompts that transfer across different LM families and scales (e.g., from RoBERTa to GPT-2 and from smaller to larger models).
  • supports Source: RLPrompt: Optimizing Discrete Text Prompts with Reinforcement Learning · Locator: Section 3.1-3.3, Tables 2-4, Figures 2 and 4, pp. 5-9
Limitation #r726-claim-gibberish-fluency-tradeoff-limitation
Prompts discovered by RLPrompt are typically high-perplexity ungrammatical gibberish (e.g., perplexity 254K on GPT-2), and constraining the policy's action space via top-20 LM probability filtering to enforce human-readable fluency reduces prompt perplexity to 82.1 but degrades Yelp style transfer joint performance J(C,S,F) from 61.4 to 46.7; moreover, RLPrompt lags full-model fine-tuning on many-class tasks with larger total training sets such as 10-class Yahoo and 14-class DBPedia.
  • supports Source: RLPrompt: Optimizing Discrete Text Prompts with Reinforcement Learning · Locator: Section 3.3 & Appendix A.2, Tables 5 and 8, pp. 8, 15-16

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Unconstrained RLPrompt discovers ungrammatical gibberish strings with very high language-model perplexity (254K), whereas enforcing fluency constraints via top-k action filtering substantially degrades downstream task performance (dropping J(C,S,F) from 61.4 to 46.7 on Yelp style transfer).
  • Requires 6,000 to 12,000 RL training steps (1.5 to 24 hours on an RTX 3090 GPU depending on task LM size) and underperforms full-model fine-tuning or continuous prompt tuning on many-class classification tasks with larger sample counts (such as Yahoo and DBPedia).
