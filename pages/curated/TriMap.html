A nonlinear dimensionality-reduction algorithm that selects weighted triplet comparisons from high-dimensional data and optimizes embedding coordinates to favor the corresponding ordering of pairwise similarities.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: TriMap: Large-scale Dimensionality Reduction Using Triplets · Locator: Section 3, PDF pp. 4–5; version-update notice on p. 1; global-score definition, Section 2, pp. 3–4
Identity Notes & Concept Scope TriMap denotes the sampled triplet embedding algorithm, not a three-valued segmentation trimap, generic ordinal embedding, its PCA-based global score, or PaCMAP’s pair losses. Version updates to sampling and weights are retained as method variations, not silently mixed with the older experiments. Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #method-scope
Sample neighbor-versus-farther triplets and additional randomly selected triplets ordered by input proximity. Weight triplets using input-distance differences and the version-specific smoothing rule. Minimize the sum of weighted s(i,k)/(s(i,j)+s(i,k)) losses with s(i,j)=1/(1+||y_i−y_j||²), commonly starting from scaled PCA coordinates and using momentum-based full-batch optimization. The inspected revision describes shifted tempered-log weights and updated triplet counts separately from older experimental settings.
  • supports Source: TriMap: Large-scale Dimensionality Reduction Using Triplets · Locator: Section 3, PDF pp. 4–5; version-update notice on p. 1; global-score definition, Section 2, pp. 3–4
Limitation #initialization-and-version-limit
Wang et al. challenge attributing global layout solely to the triplet loss: their analysis emphasizes initialization and the limited range of attractive and repulsive forces. Their inspected TriMap definition uses 55 triplets per point and the older exponential/log weight rule, so this qualification is version-scoped rather than a claim about all later code.
  • supports Source: Understanding How Dimension Reduction Tools Work: An Empirical Approach to Deciphering t-SNE, UMAP, TriMap, and PaCMAP for Data Visualization · Locator: Sections 3.3, 5.1 and 6.1–6.2, PDF pp. 10–11, 28–30 and 33–36; Figure 11, p. 29

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Triplet sampling, weighting, initialization and input metric influence the layout; minimizing the sampled objective does not guarantee satisfaction of every possible triplet or recovery of a unique manifold.
  • The v2 PDF says its displayed experiments use older code. It updates counts from 10×5+5 to 12×4+3 and replaces the earlier weight transformation; these are not results for a single identical software version.
  • Wang et al.’s initialization critique studies the older 55-triplet construction and should not be treated as a direct benchmark of every later revision.
  • The separately proposed global score is based on linear reconstruction relative to PCA; it is not a universal geometric-fidelity certificate, and its displayed normalization requires nonzero PCA residual error.
