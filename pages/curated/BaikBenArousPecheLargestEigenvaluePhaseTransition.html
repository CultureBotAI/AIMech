Aliases: BBP Phase Transition for Spiked Sample Covariance Matrices, Baik-Ben Arous-Peche Spiked Wishart Largest Eigenvalue Limit Laws, Generalized Tracy-Widom F_k and Finite-GUE G_k Spiked Covariance Transition, Non-Null Complex Wishart Largest Eigenvalue Fredholm Determinant Asymptotics
Baik-Ben Arous-Peche Largest Eigenvalue Phase Transition for Spiked Complex Sample Covariance Matrices is a random-matrix spectral fluctuation methodology introduced by Baik, Ben Arous, and Peche (2005) that characterizes the exact limiting distributions and scaling exponents of the largest eigenvalue lambda_1 of an N x N complex Gaussian sample covariance matrix S = (1/M) X X* formed from M independent samples with population covariance eigenvalues ell_1, ..., ell_r and ell_{r+1} = ... = ell_N = 1 as M, N -> infinity with M/N = gamma^2 >= 1. Using the Harish-Chandra-Itzykson-Zuber unitary integral and a double-contour Fredholm determinant representation P(lambda_1 <= xi) = det(1 - K_{M,N}|_{(xi,inf)}), steepest-descent analysis establishes a sharp phase transition at the critical spike value ell_c = 1 + gamma^{-1}: (a) when ell_1 = ... = ell_k = 1 + gamma^{-1} (0 <= k <= r) and ell_{k+1}, ..., ell_r < 1 + gamma^{-1}, lambda_1 fluctuates at order M^{-2/3} around the Marcenko-Pastur bulk edge (1 + gamma^{-1})^2 according to the k-th generalized Airy-kernel Tracy-Widom distribution F_k(x) (with F_0 = F_GUE and F_1 = F_GOE^2), and under critical scaling ell_j = 1 + gamma^{-1} - [(1 + gamma)^{2/3} w_j] / (gamma M^{1/3}) according to the multi-parameter interpolating distribution F_k(x; w_1, ..., w_k); and (b) when ell_1 = ... = ell_k > 1 + gamma^{-1} (1 <= k <= r) and ell_{k+1}, ..., ell_r < ell_1, lambda_1 separates to mu = ell_1 + ell_1 gamma^{-2} / (ell_1 - 1) and fluctuates at order M^{-1/2} with scale nu = sqrt(ell_1^2 - ell_1^2 gamma^{-2} / (ell_1 - 1)^2) according to the largest-eigenvalue distribution G_k(x) of a k x k Gaussian Unitary Ensemble (standard Gaussian G_1(x) = Phi(x) when k = 1).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Complex Gaussian unitary invariance requirement for the Harish-Chandra-Itzykson-Zuber derivation: The exact determinantal density (eq. 61) and double-contour Fredholm determinant (Proposition 2.1, eqs. 62-63) rely on the Harish-Chandra-Itzykson-Zuber integral over the unitary group U(N), which has no analogous closed-form elementary determinant over the real orthogonal group O(N), leaving the corresponding real sample covariance fluctuation laws as a conjecture in Section 1.3 (p. 11 & Section 2.1, p. 13).
  • Finite-rank perturbation of an isotropic bulk covariance: Theorems 1.1 and 1.2 assume that all but a fixed finite number r of population eigenvalues equal 1 (ell_{r+1} = ... = ell_N = 1, eq. 35) and that M/N = gamma^2 lies in a compact subset of [1, infinity).
  • Primary-source typographical audit of arXiv:math/0403022v2 (DOI:10.1214/009117905000000233): (1) On p. 3 (Section 1.1, line 5), 'S = (1/N) X X*' writes factor 1/N instead of 1/M (cf. p. 2 line 2 S = (1/M) X X', eq. 7, and eq. 59); (2) on p. 7 (eq. 29), 'G_1(x) = (1/sqrt(2 pi)) int_{-inf}^x e^{-xi_1^2/2} d xi_1 = erf(x)' writes 'erf(x)' for the standard normal cumulative distribution function Phi(x) = (1/2)[1 + erf(x/sqrt(2))]; (3) on p. 10 (eq. 47, Proof of Corollary 1.1), the upper bound inside P(.) prints '(1 + gamma^{-1})^2 + x M^{1/3} gamma / (1 + gamma)^{4/3}' instead of '(1 + gamma^{-1})^2 + x (1 + gamma)^{4/3} / (gamma M^{2/3})' from eq. (37), and eqs. (50) (p. 10) and (305) (p. 46) write 'P((lambda_1 - ell_1)(1/ell_1) sqrt(M) x)' omitting '<=' before 'x'; and (4) on p. 11 (Definition 1.3, eq. 54), the finite determinant writes '1 - <...>' in place of 'delta_{mn} - <...>' (cf. eq. 17), on p. 18 (eq. 93) the second argument of K_{M,N} writes 'mu + nu(x + u) / M^alpha' with 'u' instead of 'v', and on p. 34 (eq. 208) 'pi_j^{-1} = 1 + gamma^{-1} - w_j / M^{1/3}' omits the prefactor '(1 + gamma)^{2/3} / gamma' from Theorem 1.2 (eq. 55).
