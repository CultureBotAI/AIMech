MAXCFU

Evaluation Methods AIMech:MaxCFU DRAFT
Aliases: Optimization-based counterfactual-fairness sensitivity
A multivariate sensitivity algorithm that uses gradient ascent through fitted additive-noise models to search for large counterfactual prediction discrepancies under bounded correlations of structural error terms.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope MAXCFU is the named optimization-based tool for multivariate confounding, distinct from the bivariate grid procedure, its CFU discrepancy and the counterfactual-fairness criterion. The record describes the published search procedure, not a certified optimizer. Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #method-scope
Fit the working model and its residual-based predictor once. Parameterize candidate error correlations through a transformed matrix product, fit the alternative model for each candidate, propagate counterfactual features, and measure squared prediction changes using the original predictor and working-model residual transform. Algorithm 1 uses minibatches and automatic differentiation to ascend this discrepancy with respect to the correlation parameters, then evaluates it on the full data.
  • supports Source: The Sensitivity of Counterfactual Fairness to Unmeasured Confounding · Locator: Sections 4.1–4.3, equation (7) and Algorithm 1 MAXCFU, PDF pp. 6–8; Section 3.4 equation (6) for the discrepancy
Limitation #optimization-limits
The supplement identifies nested local likelihood fitting and a nonconvex sensitivity objective as computational obstacles. Its matrix counterexample shows that setting all allowed correlations to the same bound can produce an invalid covariance structure; it does not supply a general global-optimization guarantee.
  • supports Source: The Sensitivity of Counterfactual Fairness to Unmeasured Confounding — Supplementary material · Locator: Supplementary material, Appendices A–B, PDF p. 1

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The published off-diagonal tanh transformation gives a symmetric unit-diagonal matrix with bounded entries but explicitly does not guarantee positive semidefiniteness. A resulting matrix is therefore not automatically a valid correlation matrix.
  • Nested fitting and the outer search are nonconvex; local optimization, initialization and differentiation through fitting matter. No certified global worst-case value is asserted.
  • Sensitivity is conditional on the assumed structural model, predictor, intervention, confounding budget and sample. It does not identify the true hidden confounding or establish overall fairness.
  • The sparse clique parameterization is discussed but not exercised in the paper’s experiments, and its expressivity and numerical constraints are not certified here.
