Weighted A-star search

Aliases: Weighted A*, WA*
A heuristic best-first search algorithm that orders open nodes by g+w h using a fixed finite weight w greater than one, where g is path cost so far and h estimates remaining cost.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: When Does Weighted A* Fail? · Locator: Introduction, PDF p. 1: f-prime(n)=g(n)+w h(n), w in (1,infinity)
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The record concerns fixed-weight Weighted A-star, not a depth-dependent weight schedule, an anytime schedule of repeated searches, or weighted IDA-star. Pohl’s original expression (1−omega)g+omega h uses a different parameterization; for omega less than one, dividing by the positive factor (1−omega) gives w=omega/(1−omega), so w>1 corresponds to omega>1/2. This algebraic equivalence concerns priority ordering, not duplicate-policy equivalence. No normalized identity match was found across the 442 AIO and 124 prior native records; ignored-inclusive text hits in the main checkout were prior discovery leads, source citations and search-order contrasts, plus incidental dependency substrings, not existing concept records.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #search-procedure
Maintain a best-first frontier ranked by the cost already incurred plus a fixed amplified heuristic estimate, g+w h. The weight changes the tradeoff in node ordering while graph duplicate handling, tie-breaking and stopping rules must still be specified.
  • supports Source: When Does Weighted A* Fail? · Locator: Introduction, PDF p. 1: f-prime(n)=g(n)+w h(n), w in (1,infinity)
Scope #original-scope
The original paper combines cost-to-date and heuristic terms with complementary weights. Its basic graph model counts edges; it separately describes HPA+ updates that revise an open node or reopen an expanded node after a better route. These distinctions prevent treating every weighted priority implementation as the same graph algorithm.
  • supports Source: Heuristic Search Viewed as Path Finding in a Graph · Locator: Heuristic Path Algorithm, printed p. 196 / PDF p. 4; HPA+ update, printed p. 199 / PDF p. 7; Final Observations, printed p. 202 / PDF p. 10
Limitation #duplicate-bound-caveat
The comparison paper distinguishes the advertised weighted-search quality bound from its own duplicate-discarding experimental implementation, noting that ignoring duplicate improvements can relax the bound.
  • supports Source: A Comparison of Greedy Search Algorithms · Locator: Weighted A-star and Ignoring Duplicates, PDF p. 2

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Increasing the weight does not guarantee faster search or fewer expansions. The cited study’s empirical conditions are not asserted as necessary-and-sufficient conditions on all search spaces.
  • A w-suboptimality guarantee requires an admissible heuristic and appropriate graph-search and termination conditions. It must not be inferred from the priority formula alone or an implementation that indiscriminately drops improved duplicates.
  • The weight is finite and fixed in this record; w=1 gives the A-star priority, while a heuristic-only priority is curated separately.
