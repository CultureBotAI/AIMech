Nonnegative Matrix Factorization

Mathematical Methods AIMech:NonnegativeMatrixFactorization DRAFT
Aliases: NMF
A matrix-decomposition method that represents or approximates a nonnegative data matrix as the product of two entrywise nonnegative factor matrices, so each data column is expressed by additive nonnegative combinations of basis columns.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The mathematical factorization is distinct from a particular fitting algorithm, trained dictionary or application. The separately represented Lee-Seung multiplicative updates optimize two specified objectives. Nonnegative factors need not be sparse, orthogonal or uniquely determined; this is not the exact sparse-coding identity in AIO or ordinary PCA. Non-negative and British factorisation spellings name the same method. Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #factorization-scope
For V in the nonnegative n-by-m matrices, seek W of size n-by-r and H of size r-by-m with nonnegative entries and V approximately W H. Columns of W supply basis elements and columns of H their nonnegative coefficients. Choosing r below the data dimensions yields a compressed representation with product rank at most r, but small r is a modeling choice rather than a consequence of nonnegativity. Factors can be dense.
  • supports Source: Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization · Locator: Sections 1-2, Equation 1, PDF pp. 1-2
Scope #objectives-and-zeros
The inspected source fits either squared Frobenius error ||V-WH||_F^2 or generalized KL divergence sum_ij[V_ij log(V_ij/(WH)_ij)-V_ij+(WH)_ij]. The latter is a divergence between nonnegative arrays, not necessarily normalized probability distributions and not a symmetric distance. By continuity a zero V entry contributes the corresponding reconstruction, while a positive V entry with zero reconstruction gives infinite divergence. An observed zero contributes to these objectives; it is not automatically a missing value.
  • supports Source: Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization · Locator: Section 3, Equations 2-3, PDF p. 2; curator boundary conventions of the displayed divergence
Limitation #identifiability
Nonnegativity supports additive representations but does not guarantee recovery of unique physical parts or causes. For any positive diagonal D, W D and D^(-1) H give the same reconstruction, and a common factor permutation also preserves it. The nonconvex joint fitting problem and chosen objective further affect the result. These algebraic ambiguities remain even when a factorization has small reconstruction error.
  • supports Source: Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization · Locator: Sections 1-3, PDF pp. 1-2; curator invariances of Equation 1

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • No universal sparsity, uniqueness, causal interpretation or global optimization guarantee follows from the nonnegative constraint. Additional constraints or probabilistic models define narrower methods.
  • The two inspected objectives are representative source formulations, not an exhaustive definition of every NMF loss or algorithm. Missing-data masks, regularization, sparse constraints and probabilistic count models require their own specification.
  • The inspected paper is an algorithm and proof contribution; no new benchmark ranking, author implementation, empirical fitting or parts-recognition result was reproduced.
