Deep SHAP

Algorithms AIMech:DeepSHAP DRAFT
A compositional approximation of neural-network Shapley attributions using component-level SHAP values and backward multipliers.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Deep SHAP adapts DeepLIFT-style propagation with component SHAP values; it is a distinct approximation method, not the general SHAP definition or a synonym for all DeepLIFT rules.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #explanation-method
Compute attributions for supported simple network components, convert them to multipliers relative to reference expectations, and compose those multipliers backward to approximate whole-network feature attributions.
  • supports Source: A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions · Locator: Section 4.2, Deep SHAP, equations 13–16 and Figure 2B, PDF/printed p. 7

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Component-wise exact values do not imply exact Shapley values for an arbitrary composite nonlinear model.
  • Reference/background and feature-dependence assumptions affect the approximation; no causal-importance guarantee is curated.
