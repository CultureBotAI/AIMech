Neural autoregressive distribution estimator

Architectures AIMech:NeuralAutoregressiveDistributionEstimator DRAFT
Aliases: NADE
A fully visible autoregressive density model whose ordered variable conditionals are computed by neural networks that share input-to-hidden parameters and recursively reuse prefix computations.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record covers the original binary NADE construction of Larochelle and Murray. Its neural hidden activations are deterministic functions of the visible prefix, not stochastic latent variables to be summed out. Mean-field RBM calculations motivate the parameterization; NADE is a separately normalized directed model and does not require a pretrained RBM. Later order-agnostic, real-valued, document and conditional variants need their own scope assessments. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Fix an ordering of D binary observations. For each position i compute h_i=sigmoid(c+W[:,<i] x_<i) and r_i=P(x_i=1|x_<i)=sigmoid(b_i+V[i,:] h_i). The original motivation ties V to the transpose of W, while the paper explicitly also learns separate output weights V; sharing W and c across positions is retained. The joint probability is the product over i of r_i raised to x_i times (1-r_i) raised to (1-x_i). Initialize the hidden preactivation accumulator a_1=c and update a_(i+1)=a_i+W[:,i] x_i after evaluating position i. This reuses prefix work and supports O(DH) evaluation for the original one-hidden-layer construction with H hidden units. Train by differentiating the exact average negative log joint probability. Generate by sampling each visible bit from its prefix conditional in order; no stochastic hidden-state inference or RBM partition function is needed for these operations.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Exact joint evaluation and ancestral generation are properties of the specified NADE distribution, not evidence that it equals an RBM distribution or the data-generating distribution. Arbitrary missing-variable conditionals are not all directly available as forward prefix conditionals.
  • The variable ordering, hidden capacity and optimization affect the fitted model. Original single-hidden-layer complexity does not automatically apply to deeper or order-agnostic variants.
  • The inspected publisher PDF contains inconsistent executable-looking notation: Algorithm 1 initializes a multiplicatively updated probability to zero and places a plus between the two Bernoulli factors; the displayed accumulator-difference equation on printed p.31 uses i+1 where the prefix difference requires i. The curated formulas follow the chain-rule definition, Equations (9)–(11), and the accumulator update in Algorithm 1. These are disclosed mathematical interpretations, not an audit of author software.
  • The nonlinear hidden computation distinguishes NADE from a fully visible sigmoid belief network with only a logistic-linear conditional. Sigmoid hidden activations are the original choice, not a necessity for every valid neural conditional family.
  • No universal density-estimation advantage, fitted-model accuracy or training convergence guarantee is asserted.
