LaProp (Decoupled Momentum and Adaptivity Optimizer)

Algorithms AIMech:LaPropSeparatingMomentumAndAdaptivityInAdam DRAFT
Aliases: LaProp Optimizer, LaProp Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam, LaPropW and AmsProp and Centered LaProp
An adaptive first-order stochastic optimization algorithm that decouples parameter-space momentum from root-mean-square gradient adaptivity by normalizing the current gradient by its bias-corrected second-moment estimate prior to accumulating momentum. Given momentum parameter mu in [0, 1), adaptivity parameter nu in [0, 1), and bias correction factors c_m = 1 - mu^t and c_n = 1 - nu^t, LaProp updates n_t = nu n_{t-1} + (1 - nu) g_t^2, m_t = mu m_{t-1} + (1 - mu) g_t / (sqrt(n_t / c_n) + eps), and theta_{t+1} = theta_t - lambda_t m_t / c_m, bersama with decoupled-weight-decay (LaPropW), monotonic-second-moment (AmsProp), and variance-centered (Centered LaProp) extensions.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Adam (Kingma and Ba 2015) and ADOPT (AIMech:AdoptModifiedAdamOptimalConvergenceAnyBeta2): in the generalized adaptive update theta_{t+1} = theta_t - lambda_t G_t nabla l(theta_t) + mu_t K_t (theta_t - theta_{t-1}) (Eq. 2), Adam induces K_t = H_t^{-1} H_{t-1} (reweighting past parameter momentum by the current preconditioner and diverging when nu -> 0 with mu > 0), whereas LaProp sets K_t = I by normalizing g_t with contemporaneous bias-corrected sqrt(n_t / c_n) + eps before momentum accumulation (unlike ADOPT, which uses the lagged un-bias-corrected v_{t-1}). Originally circulated as 'LaProp: a Better Way to Combine Momentum with Adaptive Gradient' (v1) and revised as 'LaProp: Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam' (v3).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #parameter-space-momentum-decoupling-and-signed-gradient-interpolation
In the generalized adaptive framework theta_{t+1} = theta_t - lambda_t G_t nabla l(theta_t) + mu_t K_t (theta_t - theta_{t-1}) (Eq. 2, p. 3), Adam has K_t = H_t^{-1} H_{t-1} (Eq. 6), which causes accumulated parameter-space momentum to vanish when a single exploding gradient enlarges H_t and yields infinite update variance Var[lim_{nu->0} m_t / sqrt(n_t)] = infty when nu -> 0 and mu > 0 (Eq. 8, p. 4); LaProp sets K_t = I (Eq. 7) and smoothly interpolates to Signed Stochastic Gradient with Momentum theta_{t+1} = theta_t - (1 - mu) lambda_t sgn(nabla l(theta_t)) + mu (theta_t - theta_{t-1}) as nu -> 0 (Section 3, Table 1, p. 4-5).
  • supports Source: LaProp: Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam · Locator: Section 2.2-3.2, Eqs. 2-8 and Table 1, p. 3-5
Mechanism #unconditional-update-bound-proposition-1-versus-adam-proposition-2
Proposition 1 (Section 4, p. 6; Appendix A.1, Eqs. 10-12, p. 14) proves that the bias-corrected LaProp update magnitude is unconditionally bounded above by |m_t / c_m(t)| <= 1 / sqrt(1 - nu) for all mu, nu in [0, 1) independently of mu, whereas Proposition 2 (p. 6; Appendix A.2, Eqs. 13-15, p. 14) shows that Adam's update bound |(m_t / c_m(t)) / sqrt(n_t / c_n(t))| <= 1 / (sqrt(1 - nu)(1 - gamma)) with gamma = mu / sqrt(nu) holds only in the restricted regime mu < sqrt(nu).
  • supports Source: LaProp: Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam · Locator: Section 4, Propositions 1-2, p. 6; Appendix A.1-A.2, Eqs. 10-15, p. 14
Scope #convex-regret-bound-without-mu-sqrt-nu-coupling-theorem-1
Under convex objectives with bounded gradients ||nabla l_t(theta)||_infty <= G_infty, bounded iterate diameter ||theta_{t1} - theta_{t2}||_infty <= D, decaying stepsizes lambda_t = lambda / sqrt(t), and s_{t+1} / lambda_{t+1} >= s_t / lambda_t where s_t = sqrt(n_t / c_n), Theorem 1 (Section 4, Eq. 9, p. 6; Appendix A.3, Eqs. 16-26, p. 15-17) proves O(sqrt(T)) regret for LaProp across all mu, nu in [0, 1), replacing Adam/AMSGrad's coupled factor 1 / ((1 - mu)(1 - mu/sqrt(nu))) with 1 / ((1 - mu)(1 - nu)).
  • supports Source: LaProp: Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam · Locator: Section 4, Theorem 1 and Eq. 9, p. 6-7; Appendix A.3, Eqs. 16-26, p. 15-17
Application #noisy-rosenbrock-extreme-depth-mlp-transformer-and-atari-benchmarks
LaProp converges across the full adaptivity range nu in [0, 1) on high-noise Rosenbrock optimization and VGG-19 neural style transfer where Adam and AMSGrad diverge at small nu, trains 500-layer and 1000-layer ReLU networks on MNIST, accelerates IWSLT14 De-En and Wikipedia RoBERTa transformer training, and achieves higher Rainbow DQN rewards across 20 Atari-2600 games.
  • supports Source: LaProp: Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam · Locator: Section 5.1-5.5, Figures 2-6, p. 7-10; Appendix C, Figures 7-15, p. 20-27
Limitation #non-decreasing-preconditioner-ratio-assumption-and-centered-instability
Theorem 1's regret guarantee still relies on the monotonicity assumption s_{t+1,i} / lambda_{t+1} >= s_{t,i} / lambda_t (unless enforced via the AmsProp maximum operation in Algorithm 3, which empirically degrades early-stage speed), and the Centered LaProp extension (Algorithm 4, Appendix B.5, p. 19) is unstable during initial steps t < t_init and diverges under a constant non-zero gradient.
  • supports Source: LaProp: Separating Momentum and Adaptivity in Adam · Locator: Section 4, p. 7; Appendix A.3, p. 17; Appendix B.4-B.5, Algorithms 3-4, p. 18-19

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Theoretical convex regret guarantees in Theorem 1 require the non-decreasing preconditioner-to-stepsize ratio assumption s_{t+1,i}/lambda_{t+1} >= s_{t,i}/lambda_t unless paired with AmsProp's running maximum.
  • The Centered LaProp variant (Algorithm 4) can diverge when gradients are constant and requires an uncentered warmup phase t < t_init to avoid early-iteration instability.
