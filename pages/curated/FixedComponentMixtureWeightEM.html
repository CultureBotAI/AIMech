Fixed-Component Mixture-Weight EM

Algorithms AIMech:FixedComponentMixtureWeightEM DRAFT
Aliases: Mixture-Weight Expectation Maximization
An expectation-maximization specialization that keeps every component distribution fixed and updates only their simplex mixing weights by averaging current component responsibilities.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The name is a descriptive AIMech label for the fixed-component coefficient iteration in Csiszar-Tusnady Theorem5 and its mixture-decomposition interpretation. Fixed component laws do not mean merely a fixed number of components. This retains a distinct convex weight-fitting problem beneath generic EM; joint component-parameter fitting in Gaussian mixtures or MPPCA is outside it, and a mixture density network predicts input-dependent parameters instead. Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #responsibility-average
For fixed component probabilities mu_i(y) and a target probability p(y), let q_c(y)=sum_i c_i mu_i(y). With positive initial weights summing to one and q_c positive on target support, set r_i(y)=c_i mu_i(y)/q_c(y) and c_i_new=sum_y p(y) r_i(y). The update normalizes because the responsibilities sum to one on target support. For empirical frequencies, it is the average responsibility over observations. No component location, covariance or shape parameter is updated. The source gives the corresponding integral formula for general fixed finite measures.
  • supports Source: Information Geometry and Alternating Minimization Procedures · Locator: Section4 Theorem5 Eq4.12-4.16, pp223-224 / PDF19-20; Section5A, pp229-230 / PDF25-26
Scope #convex-coefficient-result
Under Theorem5: a probability target, finitely many fixed component measures, strictly positive simplex initialization and a finite best divergence, the coefficient vector converges to a vector attaining the minimum divergence. For fixed probability components in a finite observation space this is the convex mixture-weight likelihood problem. The proof does not require identifiable component representation, so the limit can depend on initialization; identical components preserve their initial relative weights. This guarantee does not extend to jointly moving component parameters.

Limitation #certificate-and-support
The theorem bounds the current divergence gap by max_i log(c_i_(n+1)/c_i_n) where the ratios are defined. A coefficient driven to zero because its component has no target-support mass can be removed with an explicit support convention. Arbitrary zero initialization instead may permanently exclude useful components. With mu_1=(0.8,0.2), mu_2=(0.2,0.8) and target(0.6,0.4), an interior start tends to weights(2/3,1/3), while start(1,0) stays on a suboptimal face. Continuous-observation likelihood requires fixed density versions and the appropriate lifted measure; empirical point masses cannot naively be assigned finite KL to a continuous density.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The number and distributions of candidate components are supplied. Fitting their weights does not choose a universally correct number of clusters or establish semantic component labels.
  • Convexity does not imply strict convexity or unique coefficients. General continuous-law and finite-measure versions retain the source integrability and support conditions.
  • Finite curator calculations check selected support, normalization and convergence boundaries. They are not author software, historical experiments or a proof of every source theorem.
