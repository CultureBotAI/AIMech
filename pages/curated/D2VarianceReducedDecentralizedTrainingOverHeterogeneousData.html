D2 (Decentralized Training over Decentralized Heterogeneous Data)

Algorithms AIMech:D2VarianceReducedDecentralizedTrainingOverHeterogeneousData DRAFT
Aliases: D2, D^2 Decentralized Training over Decentralized Data, Heterogeneity-Invariant Two-Step Decentralized Parallel SGD
A heterogeneity-invariant decentralized stochastic optimization algorithm (Tang et al. 2018, Section 4, Algorithm 1, Equation 3) over a symmetric doubly stochastic mixing matrix W that eliminates the inter-worker data-heterogeneity variance zeta^2 of standard Decentralized Parallel SGD (D-PSGD) by replacing the single-step gossip update X_{t+1} = (X_t - gamma G(X_t; xi_t)) W with the two-step gradient-difference recurrence X_{t+1} = (2 X_t - X_{t-1} - gamma G(X_t; xi_t) + gamma G(X_{t-1}; xi_{t-1})) W initialized via X_1 = (X_0 - gamma G(X_0; xi_0)) W.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from centralized federated control-variate methods such as SCAFFOLD (AIMech:ScaffoldStochasticControlledAveragingForFederatedLearning) and FedDyn (AIMech:FedDynDynamicRegularizationForFederatedLearning), which require a central server to aggregate control variates or model states, and from compressed decentralized methods DCD-PSGD/ECD-PSGD (AIMech:DcdPsgdAndEcdPsgdCompressedDecentralizedParallelStochasticGradientDescent) and Choco-SGD (AIMech:ChocoSgdAndChocoGossipDecentralizedCompressedOptimization), whose convergence bounds depend on the inter-worker gradient dissimilarity zeta^2.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r427-exact-inter-worker-heterogeneity-cancellation-and-nonconvex-rate
Whereas D-PSGD suffers a stationary drift proportional to outer gradient variance zeta^2 = (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n ||nabla f_i(x) - nabla f(x)||^2, D2's gradient difference G(X_t; xi_t) - G(X_{t-1}; xi_{t-1}) cancels the heterogeneous mean nabla f_i(x^*) at a stationary point X^* = [x^*, ..., x^*] (leaving only intra-worker sampling variance 2 n sigma^2) while preserving the global mean update X_bar_{t+1} = X_bar_t - gamma G_bar_t (Eq. 4), achieving the smooth non-convex convergence rate O(sigma / sqrt(n T)) completely independent of ongoing inter-worker variance zeta^2 (Section 5, Theorem 2, Corollary 3).
  • supports Source: D2: Decentralized Training over Decentralized Data · Locator: Section 4-5 Theoretical Analysis (Equation 4, Theorem 2, Corollary 3, Table 1) & Supplemental Material (Lemmas 4-8, Proof of Theorem 2)
Mechanism #r427-mixing-matrix-eigenvalue-stability-threshold-minus-one-third
Decomposing W = P Lambda P^T shows that each non-consensus eigenmode y_t^{(i)} (i >= 2) evolves according to the second-order characteristic polynomial r^2 - 2 lambda_i r + lambda_i = 0 with roots u_i, v_i = lambda_i +/- sqrt(lambda_i^2 - lambda_i) (Supplemental Lemma 6-7), which decay geometrically if and only if -1/3 < lambda_n(W) <= lambda_2(W) < 1; when lambda_n(W) <= -1/3, |v_n| >= 1 and the non-consensus mode diverges (Supplemental Material, lines 882-883).
  • supports Source: D2: Decentralized Training over Decentralized Data · Locator: Section 5 Assumption 1 & Supplemental Material (Lemmas 6-7, Equations 11-15)
Limitation #r427-indefinite-mixing-matrix-shift-and-two-step-memory-limitation
Requires the smallest eigenvalue of the mixing matrix W to satisfy lambda_n(W) > -1/3 (requiring a lazy self-weight shift (W + alpha I)/(1 + alpha) on bipartite graphs where lambda_n(W) = -1) and requires storing both the previous iterate X_{t-1} and previous stochastic gradient G(X_{t-1}; xi_{t-1}).
  • supports Source: D2: Decentralized Training over Decentralized Data · Locator: Section 4-5 Algorithm 1, Assumption 1 & Supplemental Material Lemma 7
Application #r427-non-iid-lenet-and-resnet-cifar10-decentralized-training-application
Applied to decentralized ring-topology training of LeNet and ResNet-20 on pathologically label-partitioned CIFAR-10 and MNIST where each worker holds only 1 or 2 classes, converging cleanly where D-PSGD diverges or stalls.
  • supports Source: D2: Decentralized Training over Decentralized Data · Locator: Section 6 Experiments (Figures 1-4), pp. 4853-4855

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Unstable if the gossip mixing matrix W has an eigenvalue lambda_n(W) <= -1/3.
  • Doubles the intra-worker stochastic gradient variance term from sigma^2 to 2 sigma^2 due to differencing G(X_t; xi_t) - G(X_{t-1}; xi_{t-1}).
