Nguyen–Wainwright–Jordan variational bound

Mathematical Methods AIMech:NguyenWainwrightJordanBound DRAFT
Aliases: NWJ bound, MINE-f objective, f-GAN KL bound, NWJ mutual information bound
A variational lower bound on Kullback–Leibler divergence that subtracts a product-distribution exponential critic moment from a joint-distribution critic expectation when specialized to mutual information.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope NWJ denotes the variational objective, also called the f-GAN KL bound or MINE-f objective in the inspected follow-up. The neural MINE-f estimator is an optimization configuration using this bound, not a separate formula here. The original paper also develops density-ratio estimation algorithms; this record does not equate the mathematical bound with those kernel estimators or with MINE’s Donsker–Varadhan objective. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For P absolutely continuous with respect to Q and a real critic T with well-defined expectations, set L_NWJ=E_P[T]−E_Q[exp(T−1)]<=KL(P||Q). The original representation is sup_(g>0){E_P[log g]−E_Q[g]+1}; substituting g=exp(T−1) gives the displayed objective. With P=p(x,y) and Q=p(x)p(y), it lower-bounds I(X;Y). Under positive density ratios and sufficient critic capacity, T*=1+log(dP/dQ) attains equality; boundary cases may require limiting critics. For a fixed critic with integrable moments, correctly sampled empirical means estimate this expected bound without a log-of-sample-mean bias. Optimizing a neural critic changes the statistical estimation and optimization problem and does not inherit convexity merely from the original function-space formulation.
  • supports Source: Estimating Divergence Functionals and the Likelihood Ratio by Convex Risk Minimization · Locator: Section II-A/B, journal pp. 5848–5849 (PDF pp. 2–3), Lemma 1 and Equations (5)–(9): variational characterization and positive density-ratio form
  • supports Source: On Variational Bounds of Mutual Information · Locator: Section 2.2, PDF p. 3, Equation (7), optimal critic and MINE-f/f-GAN naming; footnote 1 for TUBA reparameterization

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The exponential product term can have large or infinite variance. Unbiased estimation of a fixed critic’s objective is not unbiased estimation of true mutual information unless the variational gap vanishes.
  • A realized finite-sample value need not stay below the true divergence. Critic fitting and evaluation on the same data introduce selection effects; finite optimization and restricted function classes add approximation error.
  • The optimal critic includes a fixed normalization offset. Arbitrarily shifting a nonoptimal critic changes this objective, unlike the shift invariance of the population Donsker–Varadhan expression.
  • The original constrained and penalized density-ratio programs have their own assumptions. Their consistency or convexity results are not claimed for every neural parameterization. No estimator benchmark was reproduced.
