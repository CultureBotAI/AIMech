A GenSAT variant that chooses the least recently flipped variable among all flips that do not decrease the satisfied-clause count, or among all variables when no nondecreasing flip exists.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope IHSAT combines ISAT’s indifferent candidate-set rule with HSAT’s history-based picking rule in Gent and Walsh’s 1993 paper. It is not merely another name for greedy HSAT: a neutral old candidate can be selected while a positive-gain candidate exists, and when every move worsens the score, age selection can choose a move worse than the least-decreasing alternative. Memory is scoped to the current attempt and does not impose a tabu prohibition. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Begin a GSAT/GenSAT attempt with a random complete assignment and fresh attempt-local flip history. If the formula is not satisfied, compute each variable’s gain in satisfied-clause count if flipped. If any gain is nonnegative, make every variable with gain at least zero a candidate, without favoring a larger improvement over a neutral flip. If all gains are negative, make every variable a candidate. From this pool choose the one whose last flip in the current attempt was longest ago; treat never-flipped variables as oldest and break their ties with a fixed arbitrary ordering, as in HSAT. Flip it, update its timestamp and repeat within the selected attempt budget. New attempts retain the GSAT initialization behavior and reset the current-attempt history.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The fallback pool when all gains are negative contains all variables; it is not restricted to least-worsening moves. Replacing the nonnegative pool with only maximum-gain candidates yields a different rule.
  • The oldest-variable selector is deterministic once the current attempt and fixed ordering are specified, but initialization remains randomized. A rule excluding recent moves for a tenure would introduce tabu behavior not required by IHSAT.
  • The 1993 evidence is empirical. No completeness, polynomial runtime or universal superiority is established by the inspected method description, and no numerical benchmark claim is curated.
  • The GenSAT loop checks satisfaction before each flip; exact final-flip return handling and budget boundaries must be declared in an implementation. Finite failure is not an UNSAT certificate. No author solver was executed.
