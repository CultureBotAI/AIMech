Procrustean Matrix Decomposition and Two-to-Infinity Norm Singular Subspace Perturbation Bounds

Mathematical Methods AIMech:ProcrusteanMatrixDecompositionAndTwoToInfinityNormSingularSubspacePerturbationBounds DRAFT
Aliases: Two-to-Infinity Norm Singular Subspace Perturbation Bound, Cape-Tang-Priebe Procrustean Matrix Decomposition, Two-to-Infinity Norm and Singular Subspace Geometry, Orthogonal Procrustes Row-Wise Singular Subspace Perturbation
A deterministic and probabilistic row-wise singular subspace perturbation framework (introduced by Joshua Cape, Minh Tang, and Carey E. Priebe in 2019) based on the matrix two-to-infinity induced operator norm $\|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty} = \sup_{\|\mathbf{x}\|_2=1}\|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}\|_\infty = \max_{i \in [p_1]}\|\mathbf{A}_{i\cdot}\|_2$ (Equation 2.1, Proposition 2.1) and an exact four-term *Procrustean matrix decomposition* for perturbed singular subspaces $\widehat{\mathbf{X}} = \mathbf{X} + \mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{p_1 \times p_2}$. Because $\|\cdot\|_{2\to\infty}$ is not submultiplicative, the framework establishes the sharp asymmetric product inequalities $\|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}\|_{2\to\infty} \le \|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty}\|\mathbf{B}\|_2$ and $\|\mathbf{C}\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty} \le \|\mathbf{C}\|_\infty\|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty}$ (Proposition 6.5) together with the orthogonal Procrustes alignment $\mathbf{W}_U = \arg\min_{\mathbf{W} \in \mathcal{O}_r}\|\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}\|_F = \widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_1\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_2^T$ from the SVD $\mathbf{U}^T\widehat{\mathbf{U}} = \widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_1(\cos\boldsymbol{\Theta})\widetilde{\mathbf{U}}_2^T$ satisfying $\frac{1}{2}\|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}\|_2^2 \le \|\mathbf{U}^T\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{W}_U\|_2 = 1 - \cos\theta_1 \le \|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}\|_2^2$ (Lemma 6.7). Theorem 3.1 decomposes $\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}_U$ into four structured perturbation terms without requiring distinct signal singular values: $\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}_U = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^T)\mathbf{E}\mathbf{V}\mathbf{W}_V\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1} + (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^T)\mathbf{E}(\widehat{\mathbf{V}} - \mathbf{V}\mathbf{W}_V)\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1} - \mathbf{U}(\mathbf{U}^T\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{W}_U) + (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^T)\mathbf{X}(\widehat{\mathbf{V}} - \mathbf{V}\mathbf{W}_V)\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1}$. This yields deterministic and high-probability uniform row-wise subspace bounds $\inf_{\mathbf{W} \in \mathcal{O}_r}\|\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}\|_{2\to\infty} = O(\|\mathbf{U}\|_{2\to\infty} + r/\sqrt{p})$ improving on the classical Davis–Kahan–Wedin bound by $1/\sqrt{p}$ for incoherent subspaces even when the top $r$ singular values are repeated, with applications to planted clustering ($\mathbf{Z} + \mathbf{E}$), high-dimensional spiked PCA ($p \gg n$), symmetry-projected matrix denoising, and multi-layer network modularity / community recovery (Sections 1.4 and 4, Theorems 1.1, 3.1, and 4.1–4.4).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Resolved against all 442 pinned AIO concepts and 676 maintained AIMech concepts. Distinct from `AIMech:LInfinityEigenvectorPerturbationBoundAndRobustFactorCovarianceEstimation` (Fan, Wang, & Zhong 2018, which bounds individual eigenvector entrywise $\ell_\infty$ perturbations $\|\widetilde{\mathbf{v}}_k - \mathbf{v}_k\|_\infty$ under a strict individual eigengap $\gamma_0 = \min_{i \ne j}|\lambda_i - \lambda_j| > 0$ between each pair of top eigenvalues via Schur-complement Neumann series, failing when top eigenvalues are repeated, whereas Cape, Tang, & Priebe 2019 bound the orthogonal-Procrustes-aligned subspace difference $\|\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}_U\|_{2\to\infty}$ using only the subspace gap $\sigma_r(\mathbf{X}) - \sigma_{r+1}(\mathbf{X}) > 0$ via the exact 4-term Procrustean decomposition Theorem 3.1), and from `AIMech:EntrywiseFirstOrderEigenvectorApproximationAndLeaveOneOutPerturbationAnalysisForLowExpectedRankRandomMatrices` (Abbe, Fan, Wang, & Zhong 2020, which relies on row-and-column leave-one-out decoupling for symmetric random matrices with independent entries rather than deterministic Procrustean decompositions and asymmetric two-to-infinity operator norm inequalities).

Mechanism #claim-two-to-infinity-norm-geometry-and-asymmetric-product-bounds
For $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{p_1 \times p_2}$, Proposition 2.1 proves that the induced two-to-infinity operator norm $\|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty} = \sup_{\|\mathbf{x}\|_2=1}\|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}\|_\infty$ equals the maximum Euclidean row norm $\max_{i \in [p_1]}\|\mathbf{A}_{i\cdot}\|_2$ and satisfies $\|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{W}\|_{2\to\infty} = \|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty}$ for all $\mathbf{W} \in \mathcal{O}_{p_2}$ and $\|\mathbf{U}\|_{2\to\infty} = \sqrt{r/p_1}\,\mu(\mathbf{U})^{1/2}$ for $\mathbf{U} \in \mathcal{O}_{p_1 \times r}$; although $\|\cdot\|_{2\to\infty}$ is not submultiplicative, Proposition 6.5 establishes the sharp asymmetric product inequalities $\|\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}\|_{2\to\infty} \le \|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty}\|\mathbf{B}\|_2$ and $\|\mathbf{C}\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty} \le \|\mathbf{C}\|_\infty\|\mathbf{A}\|_{2\to\infty}$ (Sections 2.1–2.2 and 6.1, Proposition 2.1 and Proposition 6.5).

Mechanism #claim-procrustean-matrix-decomposition-and-deterministic-rowwise-subspace-bounds
For $\widehat{\mathbf{X}} = \mathbf{X} + \mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{p_1 \times p_2}$ with rank-$r$ leading singular subspaces $\mathbf{U}, \widehat{\mathbf{U}} \in \mathcal{O}_{p_1 \times r}$ and $\mathbf{V}, \widehat{\mathbf{V}} \in \mathcal{O}_{p_2 \times r}$ aligned by the orthogonal Procrustes polar factors $\mathbf{W}_U, \mathbf{W}_V \in \mathcal{O}_r$, Lemma 6.7 proves $\frac{1}{2}\|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}(\widehat{\mathbf{U}}, \mathbf{U})\|_2^2 \le \|\mathbf{U}^T\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{W}_U\|_2 \le \|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}(\widehat{\mathbf{U}}, \mathbf{U})\|_2^2$ and Theorem 3.1 establishes the exact four-term Procrustean decomposition $\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}_U = (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^T)\mathbf{E}\mathbf{V}\mathbf{W}_V\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1} + (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^T)\mathbf{E}(\widehat{\mathbf{V}} - \mathbf{V}\mathbf{W}_V)\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1} - \mathbf{U}(\mathbf{U}^T\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{W}_U) + (\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{U}^T)\mathbf{X}(\widehat{\mathbf{V}} - \mathbf{V}\mathbf{W}_V)\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}^{-1}$, yielding deterministic row-wise bounds under the subspace singular-value gap $\sigma_r(\mathbf{X}) > \sigma_{r+1}(\mathbf{X})$ without requiring distinct top-$r$ singular values (Sections 2.3, 3, and 6.2–6.3, Theorem 3.1, Corollaries 3.2–3.4, Lemma 6.7).

Application #claim-planted-clustering-spiked-pca-and-multilayer-network-recovery
Applying Theorem 3.1 to statistical signal-plus-noise models proves (1) in the $r$-block planted clustering model $\widehat{\mathbf{Z}} = \mathbf{Z} + \mathbf{E} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times p}$ with balanced cluster size $K = n/r$, Minimum Distance Assignment on $\widehat{\mathbf{U}}_Z$ achieves zero clustering errors with high probability when $\sigma_r(\mathbf{Z}) \gg \sqrt{r}(\sqrt{n} + \sqrt{p})$ (Theorem 1.1); (2) under random sub-Gaussian perturbations of a rank-$r$ symmetric matrix with $\lambda_r \gg \sqrt{p\log p}$, $\inf_{\mathbf{W} \in \mathcal{O}_r}\|\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}\|_{2\to\infty} = O(\|\mathbf{U}\|_{2\to\infty} + r/\sqrt{p})$ (Theorems 4.1–4.2); (3) in high-dimensional spiked covariance PCA ($p \gg n$, $p/(n\log n) \to \infty$), $\inf_{\mathbf{W} \in \mathcal{O}_r}\|\widehat{\mathbf{U}} - \mathbf{U}\mathbf{W}\|_{2\to\infty} = O(\|\mathbf{U}\|_{2\to\infty}/\vartheta^{(1)} + \sqrt{r\log p}/(\vartheta^{(r)}\sqrt{n\log n}))$ (Theorem 4.3); and (4) in $m$-layer stochastic blockmodel networks, averaging demeaned adjacency matrices achieves row-wise subspace error scaling as $O((m\rho_p)^{-1/2}(p\log p)^{-1/2}\|\mathbf{U}\|_{2\to\infty})$ (Theorem 4.4).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • In Theorem 3.1, the second term (I - U U^T) E (V_hat - V W_V) Sigma_hat^{-1} is bounded via ||(I - U U^T) E||_{2->inf} ||sin Theta(V_hat, V)||_2 / sigma_r(X_hat) or ||E||_inf ||V_hat - V W_V||_{2->inf} / sigma_r(X_hat); when the perturbation E is random and independent across entries, deterministic operator-norm bounds on this second-order product require the moderate signal regime lambda_r >> sqrt(p log p) (Theorems 4.1-4.2) rather than the minimal spectral threshold lambda_r >> sqrt(p) achieved by probabilistic leave-one-out decoupling.
  • The two-to-infinity operator norm ||.||_{2->inf} is not submultiplicative (for example, A = [[1, 1], [0, 0]] and B = [[1, 0], [1, 0]] satisfy ||A||_{2->inf} = ||B||_{2->inf} = sqrt(2) but ||A B||_{2->inf} = 2 = sqrt(2) ||A||_{2->inf} ||B||_{2->inf}), so chaining matrix products in 2-to-infinity norm requires either right-multiplication by spectral norms ||B||_2 or left-multiplication by l_infinity operator norms ||C||_inf (Proposition 6.5).
  • Primary-source typographical audit of Cape, Tang, & Priebe (2019, arXiv:1705.10735v3) uncovered four minor notation and typographical issues that are corrected in this record: (1) Section 1.4 Theorem 1.1 display (p. 5 line 2) writes 'U_Z W_{U_Z}' before the Procrustes notation W_U is introduced in Section 2.3 (whereas the theorem text on p. 4 line 23 writes 'an r x r orthogonal matrix W'); (2) Section 2.3 p. 8 line 5 writes 'corresponds to the the classical orthogonal Procrustes problem' (duplicate 'the'); (3) Section 6.1 Proof of Proposition 6.5 (p. 21 line 8) writes 'max_{i in [p_1]} |<CAx, e_i>|' where C in R^{p_4 x p_1} has p_4 rows so the row index set of CAx in R^{p_4} is i in [p_4] rather than [p_1]; and (4) Section 6.8 Proof of Theorem 4.2 (p. 29 line 17) writes '||U||_{2->inf} ||E||_2 ||lambda_hat_r|^{-1}' with a double vertical bar '||' before 'lambda_hat_r|^{-1}'.
