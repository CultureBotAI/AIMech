Submodular pick LIME

Algorithms AIMech:SPLIME DRAFT
Aliases: SP-LIME
A method selecting a budgeted set of local LIME explanations by greedily increasing weighted coverage of explanatory features.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope SP-LIME selects representative local explanations; it is separate from fitting each LIME surrogate and does not produce a globally faithful surrogate model.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #explanation-method
Build an instance-by-feature explanation matrix and global feature weights, then select instances by marginal gain in the total weight of features covered by at least one selected explanation.
  • supports Source: Why Should I Trust You?: Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier · Locator: Section 4, Algorithm 2 and equations 3–4, PDF/printed pp. 5–6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Coverage depends on the candidate instances, local explanations, interpretable features and importance weights.
  • The greedy coverage objective is not a guarantee of overall model trustworthiness; cross-image feature comparability requires additional choices.
