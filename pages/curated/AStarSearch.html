A-star search

Algorithms AIMech:AStarSearch DRAFT
Aliases: A* search
A heuristic graph-search method that selects an open node using the sum of its best known path cost from the start and an estimated remaining cost to a goal.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the graph-search algorithm conventionally written A*, not a graph language model or a learned policy. Its heuristic estimate is distinct from the exact remaining path cost.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #search-procedure
The procedure expands a minimum-score open node, updates successor path estimates, reopens a closed successor when a better estimate is found, and terminates when a selected node is a goal.
  • supports Source: A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths · Locator: Section II.A–C, printed pp. 101–102 / PDF pp. 2–3; Search Algorithm A* and equation 2

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The original analysis assumes graph arc costs bounded below by a positive constant; its optimality arguments depend on heuristic and graph assumptions.
  • This record curates the search mechanism, not a claim of fewest possible expansions or universal optimality. The 1972 correction and later optimal-efficiency analyses remain follow-up evidence.
