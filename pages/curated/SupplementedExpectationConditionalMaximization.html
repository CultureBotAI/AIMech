Supplemented Expectation Conditional Maximization

Algorithms AIMech:SupplementedExpectationConditionalMaximization DRAFT
Aliases: Supplemented ECM, SECM
A numerical supplement to expectation conditional maximization that recovers observed-data asymptotic parameter covariance from expected complete-data information and the local derivatives of both the ECM update and its frozen-expectation conditional-maximization sweep.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope SECM extends supplemented EM to an ECM fit. Its additional CM derivative accounts for incomplete joint maximization within each ECM iteration. It is a covariance-estimation procedure, distinct from ECM fitting, AECM, stochastic EM and an ordinary EM supplement applied without this correction. The supplemented-CM helper is described within this procedure rather than introduced as an ambiguous SCM record. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #separate-ecm-and-cm-derivatives
At the converged interior fit, estimate D_ECM by perturbing one input coordinate and applying one E-step followed by the full ordered CM sweep. Estimate D_CM using the same perturbed inputs but only the CM sweep with the converged conditional expected sufficient statistics held fixed, or compute it analytically from complete information and constraint derivatives. Recomputing an E-step inside this second calculation would estimate the wrong map. Derivatives use input-coordinate rows and output-coordinate columns.
  • supports Source: Maximum Likelihood Estimation via the ECM Algorithm: Computing the Asymptotic Variance · Locator: Sections 2.4 and 3, Eq 2.4.2-2.4.4 and Algorithms 2-3, pp.59-65 / PDF 5-11
Mechanism #corrected-covariance-product
Let Vcom=Ic^(-1), with Ic the expected complete-data information at the fit. The identity I-D_ECM=(I-D_EM)(I-D_CM) gives Vobs=Vcom(I-D_CM)(I-D_ECM)^(-1). The missing-data increment is Vcom(D_ECM-D_CM)(I-D_ECM)^(-1). Preserve matrix order. D_CM=0 reduces to supplemented EM; substituting D_ECM directly into the EM-only formula generally does not recover the observed covariance.
  • supports Source: Maximum Likelihood Estimation via the ECM Algorithm: Computing the Asymptotic Variance · Locator: Section 2.4 Eq 2.4.1 and Section 2.5 Eq 2.5.1-2.5.3, pp.59-60 / PDF 5-6
Scope #multicycle-fit-versus-probe
A multicycle-ECM fit may supply the converged parameter and nearby perturbation values, but the stated SECM calculation still differentiates the ordinary single-E ECM map and its frozen-Q CM sweep. Extra E-steps used while locating the fit cannot simply be retained inside the derivative probes without changing the matrix identity.
  • supports Source: Maximum Likelihood Estimation via the ECM Algorithm: Computing the Asymptotic Variance · Locator: Section 5.2, p.72 / PDF 18
Limitation #diagnostic-not-certification
The theoretically symmetric missing-data covariance increment is computed from separately estimated matrices, making appreciable asymmetry a useful indication of numerical or implementation errors. Tight fit tolerances, stable parameterization and meaningful perturbations matter. Symmetry is not sufficient proof of correct fitting, covariance or uncertainty calibration; do not force symmetry merely to conceal a failed diagnostic.
  • supports Source: Maximum Likelihood Estimation via the ECM Algorithm: Computing the Asymptotic Variance · Locator: Sections 3.4 and 5.1, pp.64-65,71-72 / PDF 10-11,17-18; curator common-scaling counterexample

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The regular interior and invertibility assumptions exclude treating a boundary or singular fit with the same inverse-information formula. A large missing-information fraction amplifies errors in map differentiation.
  • The straightforward sufficient-statistic routines in Section 3 assume an exponential-family complete model. The method needs expected complete information as well as ECM code; its cost and numerical precision depend on parameter dimension and conditioning.
  • The p.67 example prose refers to displayed covariance blocks where the general Eq 2.4.4 specifies information blocks. The record follows the general information-matrix formula, not that inconsistent prose.
  • A fitting sequence that reaches a coordinate exactly can make ratio probes undefined. The no-missing-information subcase and reduced parameterizations require the source’s additional handling.
  • All 21 primary-paper pages were read. Finite Gaussian examples verify both map derivatives, noncommuting matrix order and failure of symmetry as sufficient certification. The censored-normal and contingency-table studies, original software and interval coverage were not reproduced.
