Byzantine-Robust Stochastic Aggregation for Heterogeneous Datasets

Algorithms AIMech:RsaByzantineRobustStochasticAggregationForHeterogeneousDatasets DRAFT
Aliases: RSA, l1-RSA, lp-RSA, Byzantine-Robust Stochastic Aggregation
A family of Byzantine-robust distributed stochastic learning methods for heterogeneous (non-i.i.d.) worker datasets that reformulates distributed consensus optimization by introducing local worker variables x_i and a master variable x_0 coupled through an l_1-norm (or general l_p-norm, p >= 1) consensus penalty sum_{i in R} lambda ||x_i - x_0||_p, updating each regular worker via local stochastic gradient plus penalty subgradient and updating the master via the sum of bounded dual-norm subgradients from all workers.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from gradient-averaging or robust gradient-aggregation rules that assume i.i.d. worker gradients (Krum, Bulyan, Trimmed-Mean, Coordinate-Wise Median, Geometric Median / RFA), and distinct from proximal heterogeneous federated optimization without Byzantine robustness (FedProx). Specifically denotes the l_1-norm and l_p-norm Regularized Stochastic Aggregation (RSA) methods of Li, Xu, Chen, Giannakis, & Ling (AAAI 2019, Eqs. 5-11, Proposition 1, Theorems 1-4).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #r522-lp-penalized-master-worker-subgradient-recursion
In l_1-norm RSA (Eqs. 5-8) and general l_p-norm RSA (Eqs. 9-11, p >= 1), regular worker i in R updates x_i^{k+1} = x_i^k - alpha^{k+1}(nabla F(x_i^k, xi_i^k) + lambda * sign(x_i^k - x_0^k)) (or dual-norm subgradient in partial_{x_i}||x_i^k - x_0^k||_p), while the master updates x_0^{k+1} = x_0^k - alpha^{k+1}(nabla f_0(x_0^k) + lambda * (sum_{i in R} sign(x_0^k - x_i^k) + sum_{j in B} sign(x_0^k - z_j^k))), bounding every Byzantine worker's influence in dual l_b-norm (1/b + 1/p = 1) by at most lambda regardless of the magnitude of z_j^k.
  • supports Source: RSA: Byzantine-Robust Stochastic Aggregation Methods for Distributed Learning from Heterogeneous Datasets · Locator: Section 'Byzantine-Robust Stochastic Aggregation (RSA)', Eqs. 5-11 and Proposition 1, pp. 3-4
Scope #r522-exact-consensus-and-heterogeneous-convergence
Theorem 1 proves that in the absence of Byzantine workers (B = empty), the l_p-penalized objective (Eq. 9) achieves exact consensus x_i^* = x_0^* = tilde_x^* whenever lambda >= lambda_0 := max_{i in R} ||nabla E[F(tilde_x^*, xi_i)]||_b, and Theorems 2-3 establish O(1/(k+1)) sublinear convergence to a bounded O(lambda^2 q^2) neighborhood of tilde_x^* under arbitrary heterogeneous worker distributions D_i.
  • supports Source: RSA: Byzantine-Robust Stochastic Aggregation Methods for Distributed Learning from Heterogeneous Datasets · Locator: Section 'Theoretical Guarantees', Proposition 1 and Theorems 1-3, pp. 4-5; Supplementary Material pp. 10-16
Limitation #r522-nonzero-asymptotic-error-neighborhood-limitation
When q > 0 Byzantine workers are present, RSA converges to a non-zero asymptotic error neighborhood Delta_1 = O(lambda^2 q^2) / mu^2 around the true consensus optimum tilde_x^* (Theorems 2-3), creating a trade-off between choosing lambda >= lambda_0 large enough for consensus across heterogeneous workers and keeping lambda small to minimize Byzantine bias.
  • supports Source: RSA: Byzantine-Robust Stochastic Aggregation Methods for Distributed Learning from Heterogeneous Datasets · Locator: Section 'Theoretical Guarantees', Theorems 1-3 and discussion of Delta_1, pp. 4-5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Converges to a non-zero error neighborhood proportional to lambda^2 * q^2 when q > 0 Byzantine workers are present.
  • Requires selecting the penalty hyperparameter lambda large enough (lambda >= lambda_0) to enforce consensus across heterogeneous workers without excessively amplifying Byzantine bias.
