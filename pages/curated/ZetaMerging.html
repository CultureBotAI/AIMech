Zeta merging

Algorithms AIMech:ZetaMerging DRAFT
An agglomerative graph-clustering algorithm that selects the pair with the largest sum of conditional increases in average log-diagonal resolvent popularity.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Zeta merging is the named Algorithm 1 component of Zhao and Tang’s Zell method. Its input is an already constructed weighted graph and initial partition; it does not prescribe the l-links initialization. It differs from path integral clustering, which averages all endpoint-to-endpoint resolvent entries rather than logarithms of diagonal entries. Mechanism #method
Given a fixed nonnegative weighted adjacency P, a nonempty initial partition and target count m_c smaller than its initial count, choose damping 0<z<1/(2||P||_1), the sufficient range stated in Section 3.1 for nonzero P. For a cluster C use the principal submatrix P_C without local renormalization and R_C=(I−zP_C)^−1. Its popularity is chi_C=(1/|C|) sum_(i in C) log (R_C)_ii. Equivalently it is the average leave-one-vertex-out reduction of epsilon_C=−log det(I−zP_C). For U=A union B, chi_(A|U) averages log (R_U)_ii over i in A with divisor |A|. Merge the current pair maximizing (chi_(A|U)−chi_A)+(chi_(B|U)−chi_B); recompute affected affinities and repeat to m_c. The conditional increment is not chi_U−chi_A−chi_B.

Limitation #popularity-bound-qualification
Curator calculation: for P=[[0,1],[1,0]], z=1/4 and C both vertices, det(I−zP)=15/16 and both diagonal entries of (I−zP)^−1 are 16/15. Thus epsilon_C=chi_C=log(16/15)>epsilon_C/2. The z choice satisfies Section 3.1’s sufficient norm bound. The printed upper bound after Theorem 2 is not adopted. The reproducible finite calculation is saved as research/discovery/zeta-popularity-bound.py; it is not a benchmark of the clustering procedure.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Initialization, graph weights, damping and requested count determine the result; greedy merges cannot undo an initial grouping or certify a global partition optimum.
  • The paper prints z<rho(W) in Theorem 1, whereas the cycle power series requires |z|rho(W)<1. This record uses the stricter positive norm bound actually stated for Algorithm 1, rather than importing the printed general condition.
  • The printed bound 0<chi_C<=epsilon_C/|C| is not used. A two-vertex mutual-edge graph with z=1/4 gives chi_C=epsilon_C=log(16/15)>epsilon_C/2 under the paper’s own definitions; this is a curator counterexample, not an author erratum.
  • Zell’s full l-links and bandwidth conventions require separate clarification and are not specified by this component. No generic sparse-inverse runtime guarantee, automatic correct cluster count, or benchmark reproduction is asserted.
