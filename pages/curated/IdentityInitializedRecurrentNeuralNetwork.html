Identity-initialized rectified recurrent neural network

Architectures AIMech:IdentityInitializedRecurrentNeuralNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: IRNN
A recurrent neural sequence architecture using rectified linear hidden units, zero initial recurrent biases, and a trainable recurrent matrix initialized to the identity, with a scaled-identity initialization variant for shorter memory.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

6 Claims
Mechanism #initialization-and-transition
A hidden transition can be written h_t=ReLU(W_hh*h_(t-1)+W_xh*x_t+b). In the unscaled IRNN initialization W_hh=I and b=0, so the transition initially rectifies the previous state plus the input contribution. The recurrent parameters are subsequently trained by backpropagation through time. Identity is an initial value, not a constraint that holds at every training step. The source also studies a scaled-identity variant.
  • supports Source: A Simple Way to Initialize Recurrent Networks of Rectified Linear Units · Locator: Section 2, p. 2; experimental initialization and training in Section 4, p. 3
Limitation #active-path-gradient-boundaries
Under the unscaled initial parameters with zero input contribution, a nonnegative hidden state is preserved. Strictly positive preactivations have unit rectifier derivative, so an active direct recurrent path preserves its incoming derivative when no additional error contribution is added. If an input makes a preactivation negative, that component has zero local derivative; a zero preactivation requires a chosen derivative convention. Thus the source motivation of constant error propagation is conditional, not a guarantee for every state, input or trained parameter setting.
  • supports Source: A Simple Way to Initialize Recurrent Networks of Rectified Linear Units · Locator: Section 2, p. 2; curator differentiation of the stated rectified transition
Mechanism #scaled-identity-variant
The paper permits a scaled identity and uses 0.01*I for the TIMIT speech experiments, calling this configuration iRNN. With fixed initial W_hh=c*I, zero input and bias, and 0<c<1, a nonnegative state decays by c per step; an always-active direct derivative path decays by c^k across k steps. Inputs, activation gates and subsequent parameter updates change this simple calculation. The case-sensitive iRNN designation is treated here as a scoped initialization variant, not a second synonym that erases its distinction from the unscaled setup.
  • supports Source: A Simple Way to Initialize Recurrent Networks of Rectified Linear Units · Locator: Section 2, p. 2; Section 4.4, pp. 6–7; curator algebra
Scope #trainable-versus-fixed-structure
Because W_hh is trained, initially zero off-diagonal entries can become nonzero. The architecture does not require permanent diagonal recurrence, independent hidden units, fixed identity weights or orthogonality throughout optimization. The paper contrasts its trainable initialization with an earlier approach that retains a matrix fixed to identity or approximate identity for a subset of slower context units. An initialization convention and a persistent recurrent structural constraint are different identities.
  • supports Source: A Simple Way to Initialize Recurrent Networks of Rectified Linear Units · Locator: Section 2, p. 2; curator parameter-update consequence
Limitation #state-magnitude-and-forgetting
Identity initialization does not bound state magnitudes: a positive repeated input contribution can accumulate over time while rectifier units remain active. In the reported speech experiments full identity caused slow convergence, worse results and sometimes divergence, and the authors used a smaller identity scale to favor forgetting. This does not establish a universal scale, a bound on optimizer steps or immunity to vanishing and exploding gradients. Gradient clipping and other training settings remain separate choices.
  • supports Source: A Simple Way to Initialize Recurrent Networks of Rectified Linear Units · Locator: Section 4 setup, p. 3; Section 4.4, p. 6; curator input-accumulation example
Scope #evaluation-context
The paper evaluates adding tasks, sequential and permuted-pixel MNIST, billion-word language modeling and TIMIT frame prediction. Layer counts, parameter budgets, directionality and identity scale vary across comparisons; the speech results concern the scaled variant. The authors explicitly allow that different hyperparameter tuning could change comparisons with LSTM. The record makes no numerical benchmark, unconditional superiority or equivalence claim and does not prescribe one universal nonrecurrent-weight initialization or optimizer recipe.
  • supports Source: A Simple Way to Initialize Recurrent Networks of Rectified Linear Units · Locator: Section 4 and Figures 1–3, Tables 1–4, pp. 3–7

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Identity and zero recurrent bias are initialization conditions. Recurrent weights and biases may change during training; no permanent diagonal or orthogonal constraint is asserted.
  • Memory and derivative preservation require the stated initial parameters and activation/input conditions. Inactive units and a zero-activation derivative convention limit unconditional claims.
  • The scaled-identity iRNN configuration is retained as a documented variant. Its forgetting calculation assumes a fixed positive scalar, active paths and no input contribution, rather than arbitrary trained dynamics.
  • Unit initial recurrent gain does not cap state magnitudes or update sizes. Positive input can accumulate, and the paper reports unfavorable full-identity speech behavior.
  • Complete nine-page arXiv v2 paper, figures, tables and references were visually inspected; no author implementation, training, model evaluation or independent replication was performed.
  • Published comparisons depend on task, tuning, capacity and configuration. No current best performance, numerical benchmark or universal LSTM replacement is asserted.
