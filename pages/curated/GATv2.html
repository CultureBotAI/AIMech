Graph attention network v2

Architectures AIMech:GATv2 DRAFT
Aliases: GATv2
A graph neural architecture whose neighborhood attention scorer applies a learned scalar projection after a nonlinear transformation of the query-neighbor pair, allowing attention rankings to depend on the query node.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope GATv2 changes the scoring-function operation order of the original GAT. It is a distinct architecture, not a software version label or a synonym for every graph attention network.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #architecture-mechanism
Concatenate query and neighbor features, apply a learned affine transformation and LeakyReLU, and then project to an attention score; normalize scores over the neighborhood with softmax and use them to aggregate transformed neighbor features.
  • supports Source: How Attentive are Graph Attention Networks? · Locator: Sections 2.2 and 3.3, equations 3–4 and 7 with footnote 5, PDF/printed pp. 3 and 5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Dynamic attention is an expressiveness property of the parameterized scorer, not a guarantee that any trained instance learns useful node-specific rankings.
  • This record supports the architecture and operation order, not universal benchmark superiority or a causal interpretation of attention coefficients.
