Aliases: ASelite
An Ant System variant that retains the standard all-ant pheromone deposits and adds extra reinforcement to edges of the best-so-far tour after each construction cycle.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope The defining change is active extra reinforcement of the best-so-far tour, beyond merely remembering or returning it. The original Ant System paper describes the elitist strategy and the 1997 rank-based study names it ASelite with an explicit equation. It differs from rank selection, which can omit lower-ranked current contributions, and from single-best-only updates. Zero elite weight recovers the base update as a limiting configuration. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Use the ant-cycle construction rule and calculate all current tour lengths. Retain the shortest tour found so far with length L_best. For each edge, update tau_new=rho*tau+sum_k Q/L_k over every current ant whose tour used that edge, plus e*Q/L_best if the archived best tour uses the edge. Edges not used by a current ant receive no corresponding current deposit, and edges outside the archived tour receive no extra elite deposit. Rho is retention. The parameter e is interpreted in the original paper as the number of additional elitist ants; it weights an extra contribution without requiring those fictitious ants to construct new tours. A current best tour can receive both its ordinary all-ant contribution and the archive term. Repeat construction and reinforcement with declared parameters and stopping.
  • supports Source: The Ant System: Optimization by a colony of cooperating agents · Locator: SectionsII–III, PDF pp.5–8, ant-cycle construction/update; SectionV.C, PDFp.16, elitist strategy and footnote6
  • supports Source: A New Rank Based Version of the Ant System - A Computational Study · Locator: Section3.1, PDF pp.9–10 / printed pp.6–7, Equation3 and ASelite identity

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The method assumes valid positive-cost tours and well-defined inverse-distance construction weights. Extra elite reinforcement biases future sampling; it does not force future ants to take the archived tour or guarantee that its edges are globally optimal.
  • The original study explicitly reports premature concentration when elite reinforcement is too strong in its tested setting. A favorable elite count on one TSP instance is not a universal parameter recommendation or a convergence theorem.
  • This mechanism is not the same as copying a best chromosome unchanged into a genetic population, nor merely storing a best-so-far output. It retains every ant’s ordinary deposit; rank-based selection can truncate current contributions and single-best-only reinforcement omits the all-ant term.
  • Initialization, nonnegative elite weight, heuristic exponents, tour ties, constraints, randomization, stopping and floating-point behavior require a declared implementation. No benchmark outcome or universal superiority is curated.
