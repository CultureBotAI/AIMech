DC-BERT (Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding)

Architectures AIMech:DCBERTDecouplingQuestionAndDocumentForEfficientContextualEncoding DRAFT
Aliases: DC-BERT, Decoupled Contextual BERT, DC-BERT Decoupled Question-Document Contextual Encoding Framework
A decoupled contextual encoding framework for open-domain question answering document retrieval and reranking (Zhang, Nie, Geng, Ramamurthy, Song, & Jiang, SIGIR 2020) that replaces joint lower-layer question-document concatenation with dual BERT encoders—an online BERT that encodes the incoming question once into T in R^{N x d} and an offline BERT that pre-encodes and caches all candidate documents into T' in R^{M x d}—before adding trainable global position embeddings E_{P_i} and global type embeddings (E_Q, E_D), applying K upper Transformer interaction layers, and predicting relevance via a two-layer tanh MLP classifier over the concatenated question and document [CLS] representations p(Q_i, D_j) = sigma(MLP([o_[CLS]; o'_[CLS]])).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from MORES (AIMech:MORESModularizedTransformerBasedRankingFramework, which uses asymmetric query-to-document cross-attention Interaction Blocks leaving D unchanged) and from TwinBERT (AIMech:TwinBERTDistillingKnowledgeToTwinStructuredBERTModels, which pools each tower into a single vector before a cosine or max-residual crossing layer). DC-BERT retains token-level encodings T and T' from the dual BERT encoders, re-indexes their concatenation with trainable global position embeddings E_{P_i} and type embeddings E_Q, E_D initialized from pre-trained BERT, passes the sequence through K configurable upper Transformer layers (initialized from the last K layers of BERT), and concatenates both [CLS] outputs [o_[CLS]; o'_[CLS]] for binary reranking.

Mechanism #dc-bert-dual-bert-global-embedding-transformer-interaction
DC-BERT decouples lower-layer BERT contextualization into an online question BERT producing T in R^{N x d} and an offline cached document BERT producing T' in R^{M x d}—reducing lower-layer complexity across N_q questions and N_d candidate documents from O(N_q N_d (L_q + L_d)^2) to O(N_q L_q^2) online—and models cross-segment interactions by adding trainable global position embeddings E_{P_i} in R^d and global type embeddings E_Q, E_D in R^d before feeding the combined sequence into K upper Transformer layers and scoring relevance via p(Q_i, D_j) = sigma(MLP([o_[CLS]; o'_[CLS]])) (Equations 1-2).
  • supports Source: DC-BERT: Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding · Locator: Section 2 (Methodology), pp. 2-4, Figure 1, Equations (1)-(2)
  • supports Source: DC-BERT: Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding · Locator: inject_global_embeddings and dc_bert_reranker_prob verification
Mechanism #dc-bert-symmetric-pbt-and-ptb-evaluation-metrics
To measure a neural reranker's semantic retrieval capability relative to an initial TF-IDF retriever, DC-BERT introduces two symmetric metrics: PBT@N (the percentage of questions for which at least one top-N reranked document containing the answer span is not in the TF-IDF top-N documents, higher is better) and PTB@N (the percentage of questions for which at least one TF-IDF top-N document containing the answer span is not in the reranker's top-N documents, lower is better).
  • supports Source: DC-BERT: Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding · Locator: Section 3 (Evaluation metrics & Table 2), pp. 4-5
  • supports Source: DC-BERT: Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding · Locator: compute_pbt_and_ptb_at_n verification
Application #dc-bert-squad-open-and-nq-open-10x-speedup
With K = 1 interaction Transformer layer, DC-BERT achieves a 10.3x retrieval speedup over BERT-base on both SQuAD Open and Natural Questions Open while retaining 98% of downstream QA Exact Match accuracy (39.2 vs. 40.1 EM on SQuAD Open; 27.4 vs. 28.0 EM on Natural Questions Open) and substantially outperforming 2-layer DistilBERT (34.6 and 25.1 EM, Table 1).
  • supports Source: DC-BERT: Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding · Locator: Section 3 (Experiments — Table 1 & Table 3), pp. 4-6
Limitation #dc-bert-token-level-cache-storage-and-full-upper-attention
Because DC-BERT feeds all M document token vectors into K upper Transformer self-attention layers at query time, its online interaction still scales as O(K (L_q + L_d)^2) per candidate document and requires caching full token-level document matrices T' in R^{M x d} rather than a single pooled document vector.
  • supports Source: DC-BERT: Decoupling Question and Document for Efficient Contextual Encoding · Locator: Section 2 (Dual-BERT & Transformer components) & Section 3 (Figure 2b), pp. 2-6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires caching full token-level document matrices T' in R^{M x d} offline and running O(K * (L_q + L_d)^2) upper-layer Transformer self-attention per candidate document online.
