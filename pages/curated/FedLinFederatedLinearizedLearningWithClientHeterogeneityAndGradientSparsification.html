FedLin (Federated Linearized Learning with Client Heterogeneity and Gradient Sparsification)

Algorithms AIMech:FedLinFederatedLinearizedLearningWithClientHeterogeneityAndGradientSparsification DRAFT
Aliases: FedLin, Federated Linearized Learning, FedLin with Server and Client Gradient Sparsification
A federated optimization algorithm (Mitra et al. 2021, Section 3, Algorithm 1, Equation 8; Section 4, Algorithms 2-3) that resolves the speed-accuracy conflict under simultaneous objective and systems heterogeneity (variable local steps tau_i >= 1) by updating each client i via the fresh-gradient linearized correction x_{i,l+1}^{(t)} = x_{i,l}^{(t)} - eta_i (nabla f_i(x_{i,l}^{(t)}) - nabla f_i(bar_x_t) + g_t) with client-specific step-size eta_i = bar_eta / tau_i and current global gradient g_t = (1/m) sum_{i=1}^m nabla f_i(bar_x_t) (or error-compensated TOP-k sparsified gradients in Algorithms 2-3).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from SCAFFOLD (AIMech:ScaffoldStochasticControlledAveragingForFederatedLearning, Karimireddy et al. 2020), which uses stale control variates c_i from round t-1 that violate the exact single-step fixed-point property at x^* and lacks client- inversely scaled step-sizes eta_i = bar_eta / tau_i for arbitrary systems heterogeneity, and from FedProx (AIMech:FedProxProximalHeterogeneousFederatedOptimization) and FedNova (AIMech:FedNovaNormalizedAveragingForHeterogeneousFederatedOptimization), which Propositions 1-2 prove converge to distorted surrogate objectives under constant step-sizes.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r433-surrogate-distortion-of-fedprox-fednova-and-fedlin-linear-convergence
On heterogeneous quadratics f_i(x) = (1/2)(x - c_i)^T A_i (x - c_i), Propositions 1 and 2 prove that FedProx and FedNova converge to distorted surrogate minimizers weighted by Q_i = sum_{l=0}^{tau_i-1} [I - eta(A_i + beta I)]^l (Eqs. 5, 7, 9, 10), whereas FedLin (Algorithm 1) with eta_i = bar_eta / tau_i satisfies the exact fixed-point property x_{i,l}^{(t)} = x^* whenever bar_x_t = x^* and achieves global linear convergence f(bar_x_{T+1}) - f(x^*) <= (1 - 1 / (6 kappa))^T (f(bar_x_1) - f(x^*)) under arbitrary objective and systems heterogeneity (Theorem 1), matching a 2-client lower bound proving that local steps H cannot eliminate the kappa condition-number dependence (Theorem 6, Appendix E).
  • supports Source: Linear Convergence in Federated Learning: Tackling Client Heterogeneity and Sparse Gradients · Locator: Section 2 Propositions 1-2 (Equations 5-7, 9-10), Section 3 Theorem 1 & Theorem 6, and Appendix E
Mechanism #r433-asymmetric-server-versus-client-top-k-sparsification-theorems
Under contractive TOP-k gradient sparsification with parameter delta = d / k, FedLin reveals a fundamental asymmetry between server-side and client-side compression (Section 4): server-side sparsification converges linearly to the exact optimum x^* even without error feedback at rate 1 - mu / (2 (delta_s + sqrt(delta_s)) delta_s L) (Theorem 7) and improves to 1 - 1 / (96 kappa delta_s) with server error feedback (Theorem 8) because ||nabla f(bar_x_t)|| -> 0 at x^*, whereas client-side sparsification with error feedback converges linearly only to an O(delta_c D^2) neighborhood proportional to heterogeneity D^2 when nabla f_i(x^*) != 0 (Theorem 9, Corollary 1).
  • supports Source: Linear Convergence in Federated Learning: Tackling Client Heterogeneity and Sparse Gradients · Locator: Section 4 Gradient Sparsification in FedLin (Algorithms 2-3, Theorems 7-9, Corollary 1) & Appendices F-I
Limitation #r433-two-communication-phases-or-stale-gradient-tradeoff-limitation
Computing the fresh global gradient g_t = (1/m) sum_{i=1}^m nabla f_i(bar_x_t) requires an extra gradient-aggregation round per epoch, and stochastic noise in local mini-batch gradients reintroduces an O(bar_eta sigma^2) variance floor (Theorem 4).
  • supports Source: Linear Convergence in Federated Learning: Tackling Client Heterogeneity and Sparse Gradients · Locator: Section 3.1 Theorem 4 & Section 4 Discussion
Application #r433-heterogeneous-logistic-regression-and-cifar10-federated-classification-application
Applied to heterogeneous federated logistic regression and neural network classification on MNIST and CIFAR-10 under non-IID client partitions and random local iteration counts tau_i.
  • supports Source: Linear Convergence in Federated Learning: Tackling Client Heterogeneity and Sparse Gradients · Locator: Section 5 Experimental Results (Figures 1-3) & Appendix J

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Client-side TOP-k sparsification cannot eliminate the O(delta_c D^2) heterogeneity neighborhood without decaying step-sizes or uncompressed periodic syncs.
  • Local steps tau_i do not reduce the condition-number dependence kappa in the worst-case heterogeneous setting (Theorem 6).
