SoRA (Sparse Low-Rank Adaptation)

Algorithms AIMech:SoRASparseLowRankAdaptation DRAFT
Aliases: Sparse Low-Rank Adaptation, SoRA Proximal-Gradient Adaptive-Rank LoRA, Soft-Thresholded Gated Sparse Low-Rank Adaptation
An adaptive-rank parameter-efficient fine-tuning algorithm that initializes low-rank adaptation matrices W_d in R^{r_max x q} and W_u in R^{p x r_max} with a large upper-bound rank r_max, inserts a trainable gating vector g in R^{r_max} via z = W_u (g o (W_d x)), optimizes g using the proximal gradient soft-thresholding update g_{t+1} = T_{eta_t lambda}(g_t - eta_t nabla_g L_0(Delta_t)) under l1 sparsity regularization, and eliminates rows of W_d and columns of W_u indexed by zero entries I^(k) = {i | g_i^(k) = 0} after training to recover a concise LoRA module of rank r_max - |I^(k)|.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from AdaLoRA (AIMech:AdaLoRAAdaptiveBudgetAllocationForLoRA, which imposes explicit SVD orthogonality penalties R(U, V) = ||U^T U - I_p||_F^2 + ||V^T V - I_q||_F^2 and prunes triplets using heuristic moving-average weight-gradient sensitivity scores), from DyLoRA (AIMech:DyLoRADynamicSearchFreeLowRankAdaptation, which trains prefix sub-ranks via nested-dropout-style random rank sampling without sparse gating), and from RoSA (AIMech:RoSA, which trains a low-rank matrix plus a separate full-dimensional sparse residual matrix W^S). Formulated by Ding, Lv, Wang, Chen, Zhou, Liu, and Sun (EMNLP 2023, arXiv:2311.11696).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #sora-proximal-gradient-soft-thresholding-gate-update
SoRA inserts an element-wise gate g in R^{r_max} between down-projection and up-projection matrices so that z = W_u (g o (W_d x)), and updates g at step t via the proximal gradient mapping g_{t+1} = T_{eta_t lambda}(g_t - eta_t nabla_g L_0(Delta_t)) for the l1-regularized objective L(Delta) = L_0(Delta) + lambda sum_{k=1}^K ||g^(k)||_1, where T_xi(x) = sign(x) max(|x| - xi, 0) with threshold xi = eta_t lambda.
  • supports Source: Sparse Low-rank Adaptation of Pre-trained Language Models · Locator: Section 3.1-3.2, Eq. 6-11 and Eq. 13-14, p. 4
Mechanism #sora-zero-rank-post-pruning-and-svd-regularizer-elimination
Upon training completion, SoRA identifies the zero-gate index set I^(k) = {i in [1 : r_max] | g_i^(k) = 0}, drops the I^(k)-th rows of W_d^(k) and columns of W_u^(k), and absorbs the non-zero gate entries g_tilde^(k) into W_tilde_u^(k) diag(g_tilde^(k)) to execute inference as a standard LoRA module of rank r_max - |I^(k)| while avoiding AdaLoRA's orthogonality regularizer and reducing per-epoch training time by approximately 30%.
  • supports Source: Sparse Low-rank Adaptation of Pre-trained Language Models · Locator: Section 3.1 Post-pruning Eq. 12, Section 3.2 & Section 4.6 Table 4, p. 4-5, 8
Application #sora-glue-adaptation-and-sparsifying-scheduler-application
SoRA is applied to parameter-efficient fine-tuning of DeBERTaV3-base and RoBERTa-large across all eight GLUE benchmark tasks, outperforming LoRA and AdaLoRA with 31.5% and 28.3% fewer retained parameters respectively, and uses a progressive threshold-enlarging sparsifying scheduler (Algorithm 1) to characterize task-specific memorization and generalization compression inflection points.
  • supports Source: Sparse Low-rank Adaptation of Pre-trained Language Models · Locator: Section 3.3 Algorithm 1 & Section 4.2-4.4 Tables 1-2 and Figures 2-3, p. 5-8
Limitation #sora-unverified-cross-modal-instruction-tuning-limitation
Unlike fixed-budget pruning methods, SoRA controls retained parameter count indirectly through the sparsity threshold xi = eta_t lambda rather than an exact cardinality constraint, and its sparsity behavior in cross-modal and open-ended instruction-tuning regimes is not evaluated in the primary study.
  • supports Source: Sparse Low-rank Adaptation of Pre-trained Language Models · Locator: Section 4.5, p. 8 & Limitations section, p. 9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Controls final retained parameter count indirectly through the soft-thresholding hyperparameter xi = eta_t lambda rather than a hard parameter budget.
