FedIIR (Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Federated Learning via Implicit Invariant Relationships)

Algorithms AIMech:FedIirImplicitInvariantRelationshipsForOutOfDistributionFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedIIR, Federated Implicit Invariant Relationships, Out-of-Distribution Federated Learning via Inter-Client Classifier Gradient Alignment
An out-of-distribution (OOD) federated learning algorithm (Guo et al. 2023, Sections 3.1-4.2, Definition 1, Theorems 2-5, Algorithm 1) that learns domain-invariant input-target relationships E[Y^c | Phi(X^c) = z] across heterogeneous clients without sharing raw data, features, or environment identifiers by quantifying inter-client prediction disagreement I(Phi, C) = sup_z sup_{(c, c')} |w_c^*(z) - w_{c'}^*(z)| (Definition 1, Theorem 2), bounding its first-order parameter-space Taylor expansion by the classifier gradient discrepancy ||nabla_omega R_c(theta) - nabla_omega R_{c'}(theta)|| (Section 3.2), and minimizing the regularizer min_f E_{c ~ Q_{par}}[R_c(f) + (gamma / 2) ||nabla_omega R_c(f) - nabla_omega R(f)||^2] where theta = (phi, omega) separates the feature extractor phi from the shallow classifier omega (|omega| << |theta|) and the server tracks the global classifier gradient via an exponential moving average g_tilde_omega^t = upsilon g_tilde_omega^{t-1} + (1 - upsilon) g_bar_omega (Algorithm 1 & Appendix C).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Invariant Risk Minimization (AIMech:InvariantRiskMinimization, Arjovsky et al. 2019), which penalizes per-environment squared gradient norms ||nabla_{w|w=1.0} R^e(w o Phi)||^2 rather than inter-client classifier gradient variance ||nabla_omega R_c(theta) - nabla_omega R(theta)||^2 with server-side EMA tracking; and distinct from FedSR (AIMech:FedSrSimpleAndEffectiveDomainGeneralizationForFederatedLearning, Nguyen et al. 2022), which enforces conditional mutual information and L2-norm representation regularizers rather than classifier-gradient alignment.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r466-prediction-disagreement-taylor-bound-and-classifier-gradient-alignment-mechanism
Bounds pairwise client prediction disagreement after one classifier gradient step by sup_z ||nabla_omega w(z; omega)|| * ||nabla_omega R_c(theta) - nabla_omega R_{c'}(theta)|| via a first-order Taylor expansion (Section 3.2, p. 4) and minimizes E_c[R_c(f) + (gamma / 2) ||nabla_omega R_c(f) - g_tilde_omega^t||^2] using a server-maintained exponential moving average g_tilde_omega^t of shallow classifier gradients (Algorithm 1, p. 5).
  • supports Source: Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Federated Learning via Implicit Invariant Relationships · Locator: Section 3.2 Implicitly Learn Invariant Relationships & Section 3.3 Algorithm 1, pp. 4-5, and Appendix C, p. 14
Scope #r466-affine-hull-generalization-bound-and-convergence-scope
Proves that for any non-participating OOD client P_lambda = sum_{c in C_{par}} lambda_c P_c in the affine hull Lambda_nu = {lambda | lambda_c >= -nu, sum_c lambda_c = 1}, the worst-case risk is bounded by R(f) + M_tilde I(Phi, C_{par}) + M_tilde sup_{(c, c')} rho(P_c(X), P_{c'}(X)) with M_tilde = (1 + |C_{par}| nu) M (Theorem 3, p. 6), and establishes linear convergence under the mu-PL condition (Theorem 4) and O(1 / sqrt(T C K)) non-convex stationarity (Theorem 5).
  • supports Source: Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Federated Learning via Implicit Invariant Relationships · Locator: Sections 4.1-4.2 Generalization & Convergence Analysis (Theorems 3-5), pp. 5-7, and Appendices D-E, pp. 15-24
Limitation #r466-hessian-vector-product-and-step-size-scaling-limitation
Requires differentiating through the classifier gradient norm ||nabla_omega R_c(theta) - g_tilde_omega^t||^2 (introducing classifier Hessian-vector products H_c(omega)(g_c - g_bar^t) during local backpropagation) and restricts the theoretical local learning rate to eta_l <= 1 / (4 K L sqrt(1 + gamma^2)) under Moral-smoothness (Theorems 4-5, Appendix E.1).
  • supports Source: Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Federated Learning via Implicit Invariant Relationships · Locator: Section 4.2 Theorems 4-5, pp. 6-7, and Appendix E.1 Preliminaries, p. 17
Application #r466-federated-domain-generalization-benchmarks-application
Applied to federated domain generalization for unseen non-participating clients on RotatedMNIST, ColoredMNIST, PACS, OfficeHome, and DomainNet under partial client participation.
  • supports Source: Out-of-Distribution Generalization of Federated Learning via Implicit Invariant Relationships · Locator: Section 5 Experiments (Tables 1-3, Figures 2-4), pp. 7-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires second-order backpropagation through the shallow classifier gradient ||nabla_omega R_c(f) - g_tilde_omega^t||^2 during local training.
  • Theoretical step-size bound scales inversely with sqrt(1 + gamma^2) under Moral-smoothness assumptions.
