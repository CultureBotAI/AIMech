Deep Q-network learning

Algorithms AIMech:DeepQNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: DQN, Deep Q-network
A deep Q-learning method that trains a neural action-value function using replayed transitions and a separate target network.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the 2015 DQN learning procedure, not any arbitrary neural Q-function, one Atari checkpoint, or later Double DQN/Rainbow variants.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #learning-method
The 2015 procedure samples replay minibatches, regresses action values toward reward-plus-discounted target-network maxima, and periodically copies the learned network into the target network while collecting actions with an epsilon-greedy policy. Terminal transitions use reward-only targets.
  • supports Source: Human-level control through deep reinforcement learning · Locator: Methods, Model architecture and Training algorithm for deep Q-networks; Algorithm 1, PDF pp. 6–7 (unnumbered Methods pages)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The cited formulation selects among discrete actions; continuous-action maximization is not supplied by this algorithm.
  • The Atari results are not a general human-level intelligence or universal-control claim, and no benchmark result is curated here.
