Hungry Hungry Hippos layer

Layers & Modules AIMech:H3Layer DRAFT
Aliases: H3
A sequence layer that combines projected queries, keys and values with shift and diagonal state-space models, using multiplicative interactions to associate and retrieve sequence information.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope H3 is the layer defined in Fu and colleagues' Algorithm 1. Networks interleave H3 with MLP layers; the paper's hybrid H3-attention model additionally uses attention layers. Those complete networks and the FlashConv execution algorithm are not identical to this component.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
Project the input to Q, K and V; pass K through a shift state-space model. Within each head, feed the outer product of shifted keys and values through a diagonal state-space model, multiply by Q, then concatenate heads and apply an output projection.
  • supports Source: Hungry Hungry Hippos: Towards Language Modeling with State Space Models · Locator: Section 3.2, Algorithm 1 and the surrounding H3 Layer paragraph, PDF/printed p. 5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The scalar-head elementwise formula is a special case; general heads use outer products and matrix-vector contractions.
  • The complete H3 language model and hybrid attention architecture require additional blocks beyond this layer. No universal language-model performance or recall guarantee is asserted.
