MatDot and PolyDot Codes for Straggler-Resilient Coded Matrix Multiplication

Algorithms AIMech:MatDotAndPolyDotCodesForStragglerResilientCodedMatrixMultiplication DRAFT
Aliases: MatDot Codes, PolyDot Codes, Systematic MatDot Codes, MatDot and PolyDot Coded Matrix Multiplication
A family of algebraic coded matrix-multiplication schemes proposed by Dutta, Fahim, Haddadpour, Jeong, Cadambe, and Grover (2017/2020) that reduces the straggler recovery threshold for distributed multiplication AB of N x N matrices under a 1/m per-worker storage constraint from m^2 (in Polynomial Codes) down to 2m - 1 in MatDot Codes—by partitioning A vertically into m column-blocks [A_0, ..., A_{m-1}] and B horizontally into m row-blocks [B_0^T, ..., B_{m-1}^T]^T, encoding p_A(x) = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} A_i x^i and p_B(x) = sum_{j=0}^{m-1} B_j x^{m-1-j}, and recovering AB = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} A_i B_i as the coefficient of x^{m-1} in p_A(x) p_B(x)—and unifies MatDot and Polynomial Codes via PolyDot(m, s, t) Codes (with st = m) to trade off worker-to-fusion communication cost O(N^2 / t^2) against recovery threshold k = t^2(2s - 1).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Polynomial Codes (AIMech:PolynomialCodesForStragglerResilientHighDimensionalCodedMatrixMultiplication, which split A horizontally and B vertically into outer-product blocks requiring recovery threshold m^2 with O(N^2 / m^2) per-worker output communication). Dutta et al. (Allerton 2017 / IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 2020 / arXiv:1801.10292, Constructions 1-5 & Theorems 1-6, pp. 4-17) introduce four coupled constructions: (1) MatDot Codes (Construction 1, Theorem 1), which encode column-blocks of A as p_A(x) = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} A_i x^i and row-blocks of B as p_B(x) = sum_{j=0}^{m-1} B_j x^{m-1-j} so that AB = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} A_i B_i is the coefficient of x^{m-1} in the degree-(2m-2) product polynomial p_C(x) = p_A(x) p_B(x), achieving recovery threshold k = 2m - 1; (2) Systematic MatDot Codes (Construction 2, Lemma 1, Theorem 2), which encode p_A(x) = sum_{i=1}^m A_{i-1} L_i(x) and p_B(x) = sum_{i=1}^m B_{i-1} L_i(x) using Lagrange basis polynomials L_i(x) so the first m workers output uncoded A_{r-1} B_{r-1} while any 2m - 1 workers suffice for interpolation; (3) PolyDot(m, s, t) Codes (Construction 3, Lemma 2, Theorem 3), which partition A into a t x s block grid and B into an s x t block grid (st = m) to achieve recovery threshold t^2(2s - 1) (improved to t^2 s + s - 1 under generalized substitution x = y^t, z = y^{st}, Table I); and (4) n-Matrix Multiplication Codes (Constructions 4-5, Theorems 4-6).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #matdot-column-row-block-polynomial-encoding-and-systematic-lagrange-construction
MatDot splits A vertically into m column-blocks A_i in F^{N x (N/m)} and B horizontally into m row-blocks B_j in F^{(N/m) x N}, encodes p_A(x) = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} A_i x^i and p_B(x) = sum_{j=0}^{m-1} B_j x^{m-1-j}, and recovers AB = sum_{i=0}^{m-1} A_i B_i as the coefficient of x^{m-1} in the degree-(2m - 2) polynomial p_C(x) = p_A(x) p_B(x) from any 2m - 1 workers (Construction 1, Theorem 1); Systematic MatDot (Construction 2, Lemma 1, Theorem 2) uses Lagrange basis polynomials L_i(x) so the first m workers directly output A_{r-1} B_{r-1} while retaining the 2m - 1 worst-case recovery threshold.
  • supports Source: On the Optimal Recovery Threshold of Coded Matrix Multiplication · Locator: Section III, Construction 1, Equations (2)-(4) & Theorem 1, pp. 4-5; Section IV, Construction 2, Equations (6)-(8), Lemma 1 & Theorem 2, pp. 7-8
Scope #polydot-communication-recovery-threshold-interpolation-and-n-matrix-theorems
For any factorization st = m dividing N, PolyDot(m, s, t) splits A into a t x s grid and B into an s x t grid and uses the bijection f(alpha, beta, gamma) = alpha + t beta + t(2s - 1) gamma (Lemma 2, Equation 13) to achieve recovery threshold k = t^2(2s - 1) with per-worker communication O(N^2 / t^2) (Theorem 3), interpolating between MatDot (s = m, t = 1, k = 2m - 1) and Polynomial Codes (s = 1, t = m, k = m^2); Theorems 4-6 extend this construction to one-round coded multiplication of n >= 3 matrices with recovery threshold 2m^{n/2} - 1 for even n and (m + 1)m^{floor(n/2)} - 1 for odd n.
  • supports Source: On the Optimal Recovery Threshold of Coded Matrix Multiplication · Locator: Section V, Construction 3, Lemma 2, Theorem 3 & Table I, pp. 9-11; Section VI, Constructions 4-5 & Theorems 4-6, pp. 12-17
Limitation #matdot-worker-to-fusion-communication-expansion-and-tensor-product-decoding-limitation
Reducing the recovery threshold from m^2 to 2m - 1 in MatDot increases the per-worker output dimension from (N/m) x (N/m) to a full N x N matrix (increasing total worker-to-fusion communication from O(N^2) to O(m N^2)), and when coded computing is applied to operations whose output size matches their O(N^4) computational complexity (such as Kronecker tensor products A otimes B), fusion-node decoding overhead Theta(N^4 log^2 m) becomes a bottleneck (Section VII-A).
  • supports Source: On the Optimal Recovery Threshold of Coded Matrix Multiplication · Locator: Section III-B & III-C, p. 6; Section VII-A, pp. 18-19
Application #matdot-polydot-fault-tolerant-distributed-linear-algebra-and-deep-network-application
Applied to straggler- and fault-tolerant distributed matrix-matrix multiplication, chained multi-matrix products D^(1) D^(2) ... D^(n), and coded deep neural network training on unreliable compute clusters.
  • supports Source: On the Optimal Recovery Threshold of Coded Matrix Multiplication · Locator: Section I, pp. 1-2; Section V, Figure 6, p. 10; Section VI, pp. 12-17

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • MatDot requires each responding worker to transmit a full N x N matrix product to the fusion node, resulting in O(m N^2) total worker-to-fusion communication compared to O(N^2) for Polynomial Codes.
  • Chained one-round n-matrix multiplication codes require a recovery threshold that grows exponentially with the chain length n as Theta(m^{ceil(n/2)}).
