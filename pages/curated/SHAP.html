SHapley additive explanations

Mathematical Methods AIMech:SHAP DRAFT
Aliases: SHAP
An additive feature-attribution framework assigning Shapley values to a prediction through a specified function for evaluating feature subsets.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope SHAP denotes the attribution framework, not the software package or any one estimator such as Kernel SHAP. LIME instead fits a locally weighted surrogate objective that need not recover these Shapley values.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
In the paper's conditional-expectation formulation, feature attributions average marginal changes in expected model output across feature orderings and combine with a baseline prediction.
  • supports Source: A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions · Locator: Sections 3–4, equations 8–12 and Figure 1, PDF/printed pp. 4–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Feature-subset evaluation and dependence assumptions affect the interpretation; the paper distinguishes conditional expectations from independence approximations.
  • Attribution values do not by themselves establish causal effects or that a model is safe to deploy.
