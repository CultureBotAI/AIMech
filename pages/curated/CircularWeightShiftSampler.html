Circular weight-shift sampler

Algorithms AIMech:CircularWeightShiftSampler DRAFT

This record's curation method is recorded as ai_assisted (curator Codex, model GPT-6). Its saved reviews are listed under Curation History.

A finite-state Markov transition construction that partitions a circle into intervals with target-weight lengths and derives each transition from the overlap of a positively shifted current-state interval with destination intervals.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

6 Claims
Mechanism #ordered-circular-overlap-kernel
Take finitely many strictly positive weights w_i in one specified state order, let W=sum_i w_i, F_i=sum_{j<=i} w_j and F_0=0, and choose 0<s<W. The target probabilities are w_i/W. Shift the source interval [F_{i-1},F_i) forward by s modulo W. Let v_ij be the total length of its intersection with destination interval [F_{j-1},F_j), and set P(i,j)=v_ij/w_i. Equivalently, with Delta=F_i-F_{j-1}+s, the flow is max(0,min(Delta,w_i+w_j-Delta,w_i,w_j)) plus max(0,min(Delta-W,w_i+w_j+W-Delta,w_i,w_j)). Sampling a fresh uniform point within the source interval, shifting it forward and reading its destination interval gives the same transition law. The half-open interval convention specifies boundaries for this mathematical construction.
  • supports Source: Reducing rejection exponentially improves Markov chain Monte Carlo sampling · Locator: Section III, PDF p. 3, Equations 10–12 and Figure 1; Appendix A, p. 8, interval-overlap derivation
Mechanism #balanced-flows-and-conditional-use
Translation around the circle preserves length. The destination intervals partition the circle, so sum_j v_ij=w_i; the shifted source intervals also partition it, so sum_i v_ij=w_j. The transition rows sum to one and w/W is invariant. The ordering and shift specify the same kernel for every current state of this target. For a full-conditional update they may depend on the fixed conditioning environment, but an arbitrary current-label-dependent choice is not this balanced construction. Updating a valid full conditional with this kernel preserves the joint target. Fixed mixtures and fixed sequential compositions of target-preserving updates preserve it as well; a composition need not be reversible.

  • supports Source: Modifying Gibbs Sampling to Avoid Self Transitions · Locator: Section 2, PDF pp. 2–3, Equations 1–4 and update compositions
Scope #maximum-and-half-shift-policies
The shift parameter is part of the construction. With at least two positive-weight states, s=max_i w_i gives the Suwa–Todo maximum-weight policy in Suwa’s source-shift orientation. The choice s=W/2 is the half-shift policy and has symmetric flows, hence detailed balance. Both policies attain stationary self-transition probability max(0,2*max_i(w_i)/W-1). If max_i(w_i)<=W/2, every shift in [max_i(w_i),W-max_i(w_i)] has zero diagonal. When one weight exceeds W/2, some self probability is unavoidable. Arbitrary interior shifts need not minimize self transitions. ST and HST name specific policies in Neal’s shifted-tower presentation, not exact aliases for the entire parameterized construction.

Scope #orientation-order-and-mixture-boundaries
This record shifts the source interval by +s. Neal’s Equation 98 and Algorithm 7 instead move a source point by -s, equivalently shifting the destination tower forward. For the same order and shift their flow matrices are transposes, generally giving different kernels; at half shift they agree. For weights (2/5,3/10,1/10,1/5) and s=2/5, the positive-shift first row is (0,3/4,1/4,0), whereas Neal’s is (0,1/4,1/4,1/2). Suwa’s formula agrees with Suwa’s stated source geometry, so Neal’s footnote 18 allegation of a reversed-order formula error is not adopted. UST, DST and OHST specify ordering policies within the tower framework, including tie choices. Neal’s UDST explicitly mixes paired reverse UST and DST kernels equally; that mixture is outside this single-order, single-shift identity and is a separate mixture construction. No general equivalence of that mixture to one circle shift is asserted.

Limitation #reversibility-and-exploration-limits
Half shift is sufficient for reversibility, but non-half shifts are not necessarily nonreversible. For weights (3/5,2/5) and s=1/10, the matrix has rows (5/6,1/6) and (1/4,3/4), with equal opposing stationary flows. This qualifies the source’s blanket non-half nonreversibility statement. Invariance and minimum self probability also do not establish irreducibility or aperiodicity. Half shift on four equally weighted states gives two disjoint deterministic two-cycles; maximum shift on three equally weighted states gives a deterministic three-cycle. No universal variance, burn-in, finite-run accuracy or runtime advantage is claimed.

Limitation #printed-sampler-boundaries
The overlap formula defines the maintained transition. Neal’s Algorithm 6 dominant-current shortcut is valid for its minimum-self ST/HST choices but not for arbitrary shifts: with weights (3/5,2/5), current state 1 and s=1/10, it returns (1/3,2/3) instead of the overlap row (5/6,1/6). Algorithm 7 also does not satisfy its printed nonzero-output guarantee at every closed-uniform endpoint. With weights (1/5,3/10,1/2), current state 1, half shift and r=0, its printed traversal returns state 2 although the row is (0,0,1). This endpoint has probability zero under an ideal continuous uniform law and does not invalidate that law. These are explicit mathematical counterexamples, not an author-issued correction or a claim to have executed author software.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The maintained scope uses exact finite strictly positive weights, one consistent state order and an interior shift. A one-state distribution stays under every interior shift; its maximum-weight shift equals W and lies outside that interior parameter domain. Zero-weight current rows require a separate completion and are not specified here.
  • The descriptive record label covers Suwa’s parameterized overlap construction. It does not make the narrower ST/HST policies, ordered specializations or the UDST mixture broad exact aliases. Agreement with another sampler on a particular target does not establish identity of the full constructions.
  • The half-open interval convention and exact flow formula are mathematical specifications, not a verified finite-precision implementation. Countably infinite extensions, adaptive order selection and author code are outside this maintained scope.
  • Pinned arXiv and author-report versions are used with their distinct source conventions and caveats retained. No publisher-PDF equivalence, full Neal 84-page empirical review, benchmark reproduction or source-family exhaustion is claimed.
