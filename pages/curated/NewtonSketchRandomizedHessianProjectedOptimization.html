Aliases: Newton Sketch (Pilanci & Wainwright 2017), Sketched Newton Method with Tangent-Cone Gaussian Width Guarantees, Partial Newton Sketch with Exact Self-Concordant Barrier Hessian, Randomized Orthonormal System Hessian-Square-Root Newton Sketch, Newton Sketch Interior-Point Central-Path Barrier Method
Newton Sketch Randomized Hessian-Projected Second-Order Optimization and Sketched Interior-Point Barrier Method is a randomized second-order convex optimization and interior-point methodology introduced by Pilanci and Wainwright (2017, SIAM Journal on Optimization 27(1):205-245, arXiv:1505.02250v1, DOI:10.1137/15M1021106) for minimizing twice-differentiable convex objectives min_{x in C} f(x) over a closed convex constraint set C subseteq R^d. Given an n x d Hessian matrix square root nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} satisfying (nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2})^T nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} = nabla^2 f(x) (such as diag{g_i''(<a_i, x>)^{1/2}} A for separable generalized linear models or analytic barrier terms) and an independent isotropic sketch matrix S^t in R^{m x n} with E[(S^t)^T S^t] = I_n (drawn from a sub-Gaussian ensemble, a randomized orthonormal system such as the fast Hadamard or Fourier transform with cost O(n d log m), or leverage-score row sampling), the fully sketched Newton update computes x^{t+1} := argmin_{x in C} { (1/2)||S^t nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2}(x - x^t)||_2^2 + <nabla f(x^t), x - x^t> } (Equation 6); for additive decompositions f = f_0 + g where g has a structured or separable Hessian (such as a log-barrier or regularizer), the partially sketched Newton update (Equation 8) sketches only nabla^2 f_0(x^t) via Q^t := (S^t nabla^2 f_0(x^t)^{1/2})^T S^t nabla^2 f_0(x^t)^{1/2} + nabla^2 g(x^t). Under strong convexity gamma and smoothness beta restricted to the tangent cone K at x^* and L-Lipschitz Hessian continuity, a sketch dimension m >= (c/eps^2) max_{x in C} W^2(nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} K) (which is at most O(s log d) << min{n, d} for l_1-constrained problems with an s-sparse optimum, Lemma 8) guarantees local linear-quadratic convergence ||x^{t+1} - x^*||_2 <= eps(beta/gamma)||x^t - x^*||_2 + (4L/gamma)||x^t - x^*||_2^2 (Theorem 1) and super-linear convergence under eps(t) = 1/log(1+t) (Corollary 1). For strictly convex self-concordant functions and self-concordant barrier problems (Algorithms 1-3, Theorems 2-4), backtracking line search on the approximate Newton decrement achieves global affine-invariant iteration complexity T <= (f(x^0) - f(x^*))/nu + 0.65 log_2(1/(16 delta)) completely independent of condition numbers and Lipschitz constants.

Mechanism #full-and-partial-hessian-square-root-newton-sketch-and-ros-complexity
Given a twice-differentiable convex function f: R^d -> R over a convex constraint set C subseteq R^d with an n x d Hessian square-root matrix nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} satisfying (nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2})^T nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} = nabla^2 f(x) for n >= rank(nabla^2 f(x)) (for example, diag{g_i''(<a_i, x>)^{1/2}}_{i=1}^n A for separable objectives g(Ax) = sum_{i=1}^n g_i(<a_i, x>) or diag(1/|b_i - <a_i, x>|) A for polyhedral log-barriers) and an independent isotropic sketch matrix S^t in R^{m x n} satisfying E[(S^t)^T S^t] = I_n, the Newton Sketch update (Equation 6) solves x^{t+1} := argmin_{x in C} { (1/2)||S^t nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2}(x - x^t)||_2^2 + <nabla f(x^t), x - x^t> }, which in the unconstrained case C = R^d reduces to x^{t+1} = x^t - ((nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2})^T (S^t)^T S^t nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2})^{-1} nabla f(x^t) (Equation 7). For an additive decomposition f = f_0 + g, the partially sketched Newton update (Equation 8) sketches only nabla^2 f_0(x^t) while retaining the exact Hessian nabla^2 g(x^t) via Q^t := (S^t nabla^2 f_0(x^t)^{1/2})^T S^t nabla^2 f_0(x^t)^{1/2} + nabla^2 g(x^t). When S^t is drawn from a randomized orthonormal system (Hadamard or Fourier ROS, Section 2.2) with rows s^T = sqrt(n/m) e_j^T H D, forming S^t nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2} requires O(n d log m) operations and each unconstrained step costs O(n d log m + d m^2) rather than O(n d^2) for classical Newton's method.

Mechanism #tangent-cone-gaussian-width-local-linear-quadratic-and-superlinear-convergence
Let x^* = argmin_{x in C} f(x), let K := {Delta in R^d | x^* + t Delta in C for some t > 0} be the tangent cone at x^* (Equation 11), let gamma = inf_{z in K cap S^{d-1}} <z, nabla^2 f(x^*) z> and beta = sup_{z in K cap S^{d-1}} <z, nabla^2 f(x^*) z> be the cone-constrained Hessian extreme eigenvalues (Equation 13), and suppose nabla^2 f is L-Lipschitz continuous (Equation 2). Defining the tangent-cone sketch random variables Z_1(S; x) := sup_{w in nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} K cap S^{n-1}} <w, (S^T S - I) r> and Z_2(S; x) := inf_{w in nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} K cap S^{n-1}} ||S w||_2^2 (Section 6.1, p. 20), whenever ||Delta^t||_2 < gamma/(2L) the error Delta^t = x^t - x^* satisfies ||Delta^{t+1}||_2 <= (6 beta Z_1^t / (gamma Z_2^t)) ||Delta^t||_2 + (4L / (gamma Z_2^t)) ||Delta^t||_2^2 (Equation 27). Under sketch dimension m >= (c / eps^2) max_{x in C} W^2(nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} K) for sub-Gaussian sketches (or with an additional log^4 n factor for ROS sketches over self-bounding cones, Lemma 1) and initialization ||x^0 - x^*||_2 <= delta * gamma / (8L), Theorem 1 (Equation 14) establishes the linear-quadratic recursion ||x^{t+1} - x^*||_2 <= eps (beta/gamma) ||x^t - x^*||_2 + (4L/gamma) ||x^t - x^*||_2^2 with probability at least 1 - c_1 e^{-c_2 m eps^2}, and Corollary 1 proves that iteration-dependent sketching accuracy eps(t) = 1 / log(1 + t) yields super-linear convergence lim_{t -> infty} ||x^{t+1} - x^*||_2 / ||x^t - x^*||_2 = 0. For l_1-constrained generalized linear models with an s-sparse optimum ||x^*||_0 <= s, Lemma 8 (Appendix C, p. 33, Equation 25) bounds max_{x in C} W(nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} K) <= 6 sqrt(s log d) sqrt(psi_max''/psi_min'') max_j ||A_j||_2 / sqrt(gamma_s^-(A)), so a sketch dimension m = O(s log d) << min{n, d} suffices.

Scope #self-concordant-two-phase-global-convergence-and-sketched-interior-point-barrier-complexity
For a strictly convex self-concordant function f: R^d -> R (Algorithm 1, Theorem 2) or f = f_0 + g where f_0 is self-concordant and g is a self-concordant barrier for C whose Hessian nabla^2 g(x) is retained unsketched (Algorithm 2, Theorem 3), if m^t >= (c_3 / eps^2) max_{x in C} W^2(nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} K^t), then with probability at least 1 - c_1 e^{-c_2 m^t eps^2} the sketched Newton step v_NSK and exact Newton step v_NE satisfy ||nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2}(v_NSK - v_NE)||_2 <= eps ||nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} v_NE||_2 (Lemma 2, Equation 37; Lemma 7, Equation 48), and the approximate Newton decrement tilde_lambda_f(x) := (-<nabla f(x), v_NSK>)^{1/2} satisfies (1 - eps) lambda_f(x) <= tilde_lambda_f(x) <= (1 + eps) lambda_f(x) and ||nabla^2 f(x)^{1/2} v_NSK||_2 <= ((1+eps)/(1-eps)) tilde_lambda_f(x) (Lemmas 5-6, Equations 42, 44b, 58). Defining eta = (1/8) (1 - (1/2)((1+eps)/(1-eps))^2 - a) / ((1+eps)/(1-eps))^3 > 0 and nu = a b eta^2 / (1 + ((1+eps)/(1-eps)) eta) > 0 for backtracking line-search parameters (a, b) (Equation 18), Lemma 3 proves two phases: in Phase I (tilde_lambda_f(x) > eta), each backtracking step decreases f by at least nu (Equation 39); in Phase II (tilde_lambda_f(x) <= eta), unit steps satisfy lambda_f(x_NSK) <= ((1+eps) lambda_f(x)^2 + eps lambda_f(x)) / (1 - (1+eps) lambda_f(x))^2 <= (16/25) lambda_f(x) (Equations 44a and 46), yielding global complexity T <= (f(x^0) - f(x^*))/nu + 0.65 log_2(1 / (16 delta)). When embedded as the centering solver inside the log-barrier interior-point method min_{x in R^d} {tau f_0(x) - sum_{j=1}^r log(-g_j(x))} with barrier multiplier mu > 1 (Algorithm 3), Theorem 4 (Equation 22) bounds the total Newton Sketch iterations to reach delta-optimality by ceil(log(r/(tau^0 delta)) / log mu) * (r(mu - 1 - log mu)/nu + 0.65 log_2(1/(16 delta))), accelerating primal and dual interior-point solvers for LPs, portfolio QPs, SVMs, d >> n dual Lasso, and metric learning SDPs (Section 5).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requirement of Accessible n x d Hessian Square-Root Factorization: As noted in Section 3.1 (p. 5), computing the sketched Hessian S^t nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2} without forming the full d x d Hessian nabla^2 f(x^t) requires an explicit n x d matrix square root nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2} (such as diagonal-times-data-matrix structure in generalized linear models and separable analytic barriers).
  • Asymptotic Linear Floor Under Fixed Sketch Size and ROS Polylogarithmic Factor: Under a fixed sketch dimension m, Theorem 1 (Equations 14-15) exhibits linear-quadratic convergence whose asymptotic local rate is linear with factor eps(beta/gamma) once ||x^t - x^*||_2 << 1 unless the sketch size is inflated by log^2(1+t) across iterations (Corollary 1); moreover, randomized orthonormal system (ROS) sketches over self-bounding cones require an extra log^4 n factor in the sufficient sketch dimension (Lemma 1(b), p. 21).
  • Primary-Source Typographical and Domain-Threshold Notes in Pilanci & Wainwright (2017, arXiv:1505.02250v1): Verified five self-correcting typographical/notational details across the 35-page manuscript: (1) Section 3.1 (p. 6) writes 'S^t in R^{m x d}' instead of 'S^t in R^{m x n}' below Equation (6), and Equation (7) on p. 6 and Section 6.2 on p. 23 omit the transpose on the first nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2} factor in ((nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2})^T (S^t)^T S^t nabla^2 f(x^t)^{1/2})^{-1} nabla f(x^t); (2) Section 4.1 (p. 11, Equation 18) states that eta = (1/8)(1 - (1/2)((1+eps)/(1-eps))^2 - a)/((1+eps)/(1-eps))^3 is defined for eps in (0, 1/4), whereas strict positivity eta > 0 (required by Lemma 3, p. 24, and verified at the end of Section 6.2.1 on p. 26 via a <= 1/2 - (1/2)(eps')^2 - eps') holds if and only if (1+eps)/(1-eps) < sqrt(2(1-a)), i.e., eps < (sqrt(2(1-a)) - 1)/(sqrt(2(1-a)) + 1) <= 3 - 2 sqrt(2) approx 0.17157 (and eps < 0.14590 for the standard choice a = 0.1); (3) Algorithms 1-2 (pp. 12-13) and Section 6.2 (p. 23) write the approximate Newton decrement tilde_lambda_f(x) without the square root (and without the minus sign in Algorithms 1-2), whereas Lemma 5 (p. 25, Equation 42), Equation (43) (p. 26), Lemma 6 (p. 26, Equation 44b), and Appendix A.3 (p. 30, Equation 58) use the square-root decrement tilde_lambda_f(x) = (-<nabla f(x), v_NSK>)^{1/2}; (4) Theorem 4 (p. 14, Equation 22) and the combined bound N = N_1 + N_2 at the top of p. 25 print gamma in the denominator instead of the Phase-I decrement parameter nu; and (5) Lemma 3(b) and the Second Phase Analysis on p. 24 print '1 - c_1 e^{c_2 m eps^2}' and '1 - N_2 c_1 e^{c_2 m}' missing the minus sign in the exponent.
