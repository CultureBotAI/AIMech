FedCurv (Fisher-Information-Regularized Federated Learning for Non-IID Data)

Aliases: FedCurv, Fisher-Information-Regularized Federated Learning, Federated Curvature Regularization, EWC-Regularized Federated Learning
A curvature-regularized federated learning algorithm (Shoham et al. 2019, Section 3, Equations 1-5) that mitigates catastrophic forgetting across statistically heterogeneous (non-IID) clients by augmenting each client s's local empirical loss L_s(theta) in round t with an Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) quadratic penalty weighted by the diagonal empirical Fisher information matrices diag(I_{t-1,j}) of all other participating clients j in S \ {s}: L~_{t,s}(theta) = L_s(theta) + lambda * sum_{j in S \ {s}} (theta - theta^hat_{t-1,j})^T diag(I_{t-1,j}) (theta - theta^hat_{t-1,j}) (Equation 4), decoupled for linear communication overhead into aggregated server statistics P_{t-1} = sum_{j in S} diag(I_{t-1,j}) and Q_{t-1} = sum_{j in S} diag(I_{t-1,j}) theta^hat_{t-1,j} via L~_{t,s}(theta) = L_s(theta) + lambda * theta^T (P_{t-1} - diag(I_{t-1,s})) theta - 2*lambda * theta^T (Q_{t-1} - diag(I_{t-1,s}) theta^hat_{t-1,s}) + const (Equation 5).

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from FedProx (AIMech:FedProxProximalHeterogeneousFederatedOptimization, Li et al. 2020), which penalizes isotropic Euclidean distance (mu/2)*||theta - w^{t-1}||_2^2 around the scalar-averaged global model without parameter-wise Fisher curvature weighting or peer-model decoupling; and distinct from FFL-ERL / Fisher-weighted server model fusion, as FedCurv applies the peer-summed diagonal Fisher quadratic regularizer directly during local client SGD optimization via aggregated server vectors (P_{t-1}, Q_{t-1}).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r452-decoupled-fisher-quadratic-penalty-mechanism
Rewrites the pairwise peer Fisher penalty sum_{j in S \ {s}} (theta - theta^hat_{t-1,j})^T diag(I_{t-1,j}) (theta - theta^hat_{t-1,j}) (Equation 4) into the algebraically identical decoupled form theta^T (P_{t-1} - diag(I_{t-1,s})) theta - 2 * theta^T (Q_{t-1} - diag(I_{t-1,s}) theta^hat_{t-1,s}) + const (Equation 5), reducing per-round communication from O(|S|) vectors per client to exactly 3 parameter-sized vectors (model parameters plus two Fisher summary vectors) independent of the number of clients.
  • supports Source: Overcoming Forgetting in Federated Learning on Non-IID Data · Locator: Section 3 Equations 4-5, p. 3
Scope #r452-laplace-posterior-multi-task-scope
Interprets each communication round of non-IID federated learning as distributed multi-task continual learning under a diagonal Laplace approximation of the joint log-posterior log p(theta | D) (Equations 1-3), preventing local SGD steps with large local epoch counts E from overwriting parameters with high Fisher information on other clients' data.
  • supports Source: Overcoming Forgetting in Federated Learning on Non-IID Data · Locator: Section 3 Equations 1-4 & Section 4 Experimental Results, pp. 2-5
Limitation #r452-diagonal-fisher-approximation-and-communication-factor-limitation
Approximates each client's local Hessian by the diagonal of the empirical Fisher information matrix diag(I_{t,s}), omitting off-diagonal parameter correlations, and increases per-round communication bandwidth by a factor of up to 3 compared to vanilla FedAvg (reduced to 2 in round-initial Fisher evaluation variants).
  • supports Source: Overcoming Forgetting in Federated Learning on Non-IID Data · Locator: Section 3 Bandwidth Reduction Discussion & Section 5 Conclusions, pp. 3-5
Application #r452-non-iid-classification-communication-reduction-application
Applied to non-IID federated image classification where each client holds examples from only a strict subset of classes, reducing the communication rounds required to reach target test accuracy under large local epoch budgets.
  • supports Source: Overcoming Forgetting in Federated Learning on Non-IID Data · Locator: Section 4 Experimental Results (Figures 1-3, Table 1), pp. 4-5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Relies on a diagonal empirical Fisher approximation of the local Hessian, neglecting off-diagonal parameter interactions.
  • Transmits two additional parameter-sized Fisher summary vectors per round (3x total communication volume per round in the standard end-of-round Fisher variant).
