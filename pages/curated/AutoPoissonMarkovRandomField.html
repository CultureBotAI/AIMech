Auto-Poisson Markov random field

Model Families AIMech:AutoPoissonMarkovRandomField DRAFT
Aliases: Auto-Poisson model, Auto-Poisson scheme
A finite pairwise Markov-field model for unbounded nonnegative integer counts whose full conditionals are Poisson with log means linear in neighboring counts and whose symmetric interaction coefficients are nonpositive.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the classical unbounded auto-Poisson joint model. A regression using neighboring counts can resemble its site formula while failing joint compatibility or normalizability. Truncated, Winsorized, transformed-statistic and other modified count graphical models require separate identities; the broad phrase Poisson graphical model is therefore not used as an exact alias. Poisson refers to each full conditional, not necessarily each marginal distribution. A finite count field is also distinct from a Poisson point process with random locations. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For finite real alpha_i and symmetric beta_ij=beta_ji, the full conditional is X_i|X_(-i)~Poisson(mu_i), where mu_i=exp[alpha_i+sum_(j!=i) beta_ij*x_j]. The compatible pairwise mass is p(x)=Z^(-1)*exp[sum_i(alpha_i*x_i-log(x_i!))+sum_(i<j) beta_ij*x_i*x_j] on all nonnegative integer configurations, with zero coefficients on nonedges. For this unrestricted Cartesian support and finite site set, Z is finite if and only if every off-diagonal beta_ij is nonpositive. Sufficiency follows by bounding the interaction factor by one and summing the independent Poisson kernels. A positive coefficient fails summability along a two-site growing-count slice even though every proposed site Poisson conditional is individually normalized. With valid beta_ij, increasing a neighbor count cannot increase the site conditional mean when other neighbors are held fixed; zero interactions yield independent Poisson variables.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The nonpositive-interaction restriction concerns this unbounded pairwise model and its conditional dependence. It is not a theorem that every marginal pairwise correlation in every count graphical model must be negative.
  • A finite cutoff makes the state space summable but changes the support and site conditionals to truncated laws. A successful truncated calculation or finite Gibbs run cannot certify the original unbounded positive-interaction model.
  • The factorial base measure is part of the model. Omitting it changes conditional count laws and their existence conditions.
  • The source presents a formal validity restriction, not a generic accuracy or efficiency result for ecological or genomic count prediction. Later modified Poisson families are research leads, not silently absorbed into this record.
  • The graph, site supports and any conditioning boundary values must be specified. The construction assumes compatible positive full conditionals on the stated Cartesian support; arbitrary separately fitted regressions or asymmetric neighbor coefficients do not automatically define a joint model.
  • The finite fixed-site model does not by itself specify a random-location point process, temporal causal mechanism, hidden observation layer or infinite-lattice existence theorem. Conditional association does not establish causation.
  • A closed-form site conditional does not make the joint partition function, maximum-likelihood fit or uncertainty calculation trivial. Estimation, sampling and model diagnostics require their own procedures. No author implementation, empirical fit or benchmark was reproduced.
