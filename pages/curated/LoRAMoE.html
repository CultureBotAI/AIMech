LoRAMoE

Architectures AIMech:LoRAMoE DRAFT
Aliases: LoRAMoE Plugin with Localized Balancing Constraint, Localized-Balanced Mixture of LoRA Experts for World Knowledge Retention, MoE-Style LoRA Plugin for Alleviating World Knowledge Forgetting
A Mixture-of-Experts-style parameter-efficient fine-tuning architecture that freezes the pre-trained backbone feed-forward linear weights W_0 to preserve parametric world knowledge, introduces N parallel low-rank experts Delta W_{E_i} = B_i A_i gated by a trainable softmax router G(x) = Softmax(x W_g) as o = W_0 x + (alpha / r) sum_{i=1}^N omega_i B_i A_i x, and enforces a Localized Balancing Constraint L_lbc = sigma^2(I o Q) / mu(I o Q) during supervised fine-tuning to partition experts into world-knowledge-aligning experts and downstream-task experts while balancing utilization within each group.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from HydraLoRA (AIMech:HydraLoRA, which shares a single down-projection matrix A across multiple up-projection heads B_k without type-partitioned localized balancing), from MoLE (AIMech:MoLEMixtureOfLoRAExperts, which freezes pre-trained LoRAs and gates over their concatenated layer outputs), and from standard sparsely-gated MoE layers (which replace the entire FFN with full-rank experts and apply a single-distribution global coefficient-of-variation balancing loss). Formulated by Dou et al. (ACL 2024, arXiv:2312.09979).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #loramoe-ffn-plugin-forward-routing-mechanism
In each Transformer FFN block, LoRAMoE freezes the pre-trained linear weight matrix W_0 in R^{d_in x d_out} and adds N trainable low-rank experts Delta W_{E_i} = B_i A_i (A_i in R^{d_in x r}, B_i in R^{r x d_out}, r << min(d_in, d_out)) weighted by a router G(x) = Softmax(x W_g) so that the layer output is o = W_0 x + (alpha / r) sum_{i=1}^N omega_i B_i A_i x.
  • supports Source: LoRAMoE: Alleviate World Knowledge Forgetting in Large Language Models via MoE-Style Plugin · Locator: Section 3.1 Architecture, Eq. 1-5 and Figure 4, p. 3-5
Mechanism #loramoe-localized-balancing-constraint-mechanism
Given the sample-level expert importance matrix Q_{n,m} = sum_{j=1}^{T_m} G(x_j)_n for expert n and training sample m and the binary-group affinity coefficient matrix I_{n,m} = 1 + delta when Type_e(n) = Type_s(m) and 1 - delta when Type_e(n) != Type_s(m) (delta in [0, 1]), LoRAMoE minimizes L_total = L + beta * L_lbc with L_lbc = sigma^2(I o Q) / mu(I o Q), softly specializing subsets of experts to world-knowledge vs. downstream tasks while balancing utilization within each expert group and requiring no task-type labels at inference time.
  • supports Source: LoRAMoE: Alleviate World Knowledge Forgetting in Large Language Models via MoE-Style Plugin · Locator: Section 3.2 Localized Balancing Constraint, Eq. 6-9 & Appendix C Eq. 10-12, p. 5-6, 11-13
Application #loramoe-large-scale-sft-knowledge-retention-application
LoRAMoE is applied to large-scale multi-task supervised fine-tuning of LLaMA-2-7B on 3 million instruction samples spanning reading comprehension, NLI, translation, summarization, coreference, and closed-book QA (TriviaQA, Natural Questions, HotpotQA), improving world-knowledge benchmark scores by an average of 35.3% (up to 63.9%) over vanilla SFT and 30.9% over single-LoRA PEFT while matching or exceeding SFT on downstream tasks.
  • supports Source: LoRAMoE: Alleviate World Knowledge Forgetting in Large Language Models via MoE-Style Plugin · Locator: Section 4.1-4.4, Tables 2-3 and Figure 7, p. 6-8
Limitation #loramoe-coarse-binary-task-taxonomy-limitation
During training, the Localized Balancing Constraint requires partitioning training datasets into pre-defined coarse task categories (world-knowledge vs. other downstream tasks) to construct the importance coefficient matrix I_{n,m}, and its extension to finer-grained multi-category expert taxonomies and models larger than 7B parameters remains unverified.
  • supports Source: LoRAMoE: Alleviate World Knowledge Forgetting in Large Language Models via MoE-Style Plugin · Locator: Section 7 Limitations, p. 9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires pre-categorizing training samples into coarse task types (world-knowledge vs. downstream tasks) during training to compute the localized balancing constraint matrix I_{n,m}.
