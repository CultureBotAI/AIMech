Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions (LightConv and DynamicConv)

Aliases: LightConv and DynamicConv, Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions, Softmax-Normalized Lightweight and Dynamic Sequence Convolutions
Lightweight Convolution (LightConv) and Dynamic Convolution (DynamicConv) are linear-time O(n k d) sequence modeling primitives that replace quadratic O(n^2 d) content-based self-attention: LightConv applies a depthwise separable 1D convolution (preceded by a d -> 2d linear projection and Gated Linear Unit) whose kernel weights W in R^{H x k} are tied across every group of d/H contiguous channels, normalized across the temporal dimension k via softmax(W)_{h,j} = exp(W_{h,j}) / sum_{j'=1}^k exp(W_{h,j'}), and regularized with DropConnect; DynamicConv extends LightConv by predicting time-step-dependent convolution kernels f(X_i) = W^Q X_i in R^{H x k} as a learned linear function of the current time-step input X_i alone.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from standard 1D or depthwise separable convolutions (Kaiser et al., 2018), which neither tie weights across d/H channel groups nor apply temporal softmax normalization or per-timestep kernel prediction, and distinct from Lite Transformer (AIMech:LiteTransformerWithLongShortRangeAttention), which combines a self-attention global branch and a convolution local branch in parallel rather than replacing self-attention across all encoder and decoder layers.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #lightconv-channel-group-weight-sharing-and-temporal-softmax
LightConv ties depthwise convolution parameters across every group of d/H contiguous channels—reducing kernel parameters from d^2 * k (standard convolution) and d * k (depthwise convolution) to H * k (e.g., from 7,340,032 to 112 parameters for d = 1024, k = 7, H = 16)—and normalizes weights across the temporal window k via softmax(W)_{h,j} = exp(W_{h,j}) / sum_{j'=1}^k exp(W_{h,j'}) so that LightConv(X, W_{ceil(cH/d),:}, i, c) = DepthwiseConv(X, softmax(W_{ceil(cH/d),:}), i, c).
  • supports Source: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions · Locator: Section 3 (Lightweight Convolutions), p. 3
  • supports Source: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions · Locator: conv_parameter_counts and lightconv_step
Mechanism #dynamicconv-timestep-dependent-linear-kernel-prediction
DynamicConv predicts a separate H x k convolution kernel at each time step i solely from the current token representation X_i via a learned linear module f(X_i)_{h,j} = sum_{c=1}^d W^Q_{h,j,c} X_{i,c} and computes DynamicConv(X, i, c) = LightConv(X, f(X_i)_{h,:}, i, c), achieving position-varying context weights with O(n) complexity in sequence length n.
  • supports Source: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions · Locator: Section 4 (Dynamic Convolutions), p. 4
  • supports Source: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions · Locator: dynamicconv_step and main
Application #dynamicconv-translation-summarization-and-runtime-speedup
Without using self-attention in either encoder or decoder, DynamicConv achieves 29.7 BLEU on WMT'14 En-De (+0.4 BLEU over Transformer-Big), 43.2 BLEU on WMT'14 En-Fr, +0.8 BLEU on IWSLT'14 De-En, 20% faster inference throughput than self-attention, 26.67 test perplexity on Billion Word language modeling, and higher ROUGE on CNN-DailyMail summarization.
  • supports Source: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions · Locator: Section 6.1-6.4, Tables 1-5, pp. 6-8
Limitation #dynamicconv-softmax-normalization-stability-and-receptive-field-limitation
Removing temporal softmax normalization (or replacing it with unnormalized, L1-normalized, squared, or abs weights) causes DynamicConv training to diverge (Table 3 and Appendix A Table 6), and each layer's receptive field is bounded by its fixed kernel width k rather than attending globally in a single hop.
  • supports Source: Pay Less Attention with Lightweight and Dynamic Convolutions · Locator: Section 6.2 (Table 3, p. 7) and Appendix A (Table 6, p. 13)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • DynamicConv training diverges without temporal softmax (or L2/sigmoid/tanh) kernel normalization.
  • Each layer has a finite receptive field bounded by kernel width k, requiring progressive multi-layer kernel expansion (e.g., k = 3, 7, 15, 31) to capture long-range context.
