Probabilistic Principal Component Analysis

Model Families AIMech:ProbabilisticPCA DRAFT
Aliases: PPCA
A linear Gaussian latent-variable model with a standard-normal latent prior and isotropic observation noise, whose maximum-likelihood loading subspace corresponds to leading principal components under the stated covariance-rank conditions.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The single Gaussian observation model is distinct from ordinary PCA as a deterministic projection, diagonal-noise factor analysis, kernel PCA, discrete-latent GTM, generalized component analysis with categorical observations, and supervised PCA. Its eigensolution and EM procedure are fitting alternatives for the same model. Mixtures, missing-data fitting and hierarchical variants require their own scope. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #latent-density
For latent dimension q<d, let x~Normal(0,I_q) and t=Wx+mu+epsilon with independent epsilon~Normal(0,sigma^2 I_d), sigma^2>0. Then t~Normal(mu,C), C=WW^T+sigma^2 I_d. The observed-data model is a normalized full-rank Gaussian; the low-rank loading contribution does not make the positive-noise density singular. Isotropic noise is a stronger restriction than the arbitrary diagonal noise of factor analysis.
  • supports Source: Probabilistic principal component analysis · Locator: Sections 2.1 and 3.1, Equations 1-5, printed pp. 612 and 614 / PDF pp. 2 and 4
Mechanism #likelihood-eigensolution
For sample covariance S=N^(-1)sum_n(t_n-mu)(t_n-mu)^T at the sample-mean mu, take its q leading eigenpairs U_q,Lambda_q. The likelihood solution has sigma^2 equal to the mean of the d-q discarded eigenvalues and W=U_q(Lambda_q-sigma^2 I)^(1/2)R for any orthogonal R. Rank(S)>q ensures positive residual variance. Tied eigenvalues can make loading columns vanish: for spectrum (2,1,1) and q=2, sigma^2=1 and the effective loading rank is one. Neither the columns nor latent coordinates are uniquely identified.
  • supports Source: Probabilistic principal component analysis · Locator: Sections 3.2-3.3, Equations 7-8, printed pp. 614-615 / PDF pp. 4-5; Appendix A, Equations 10-21 and equal-eigenvalue example, pp. 618-621 / PDF pp. 8-11
Mechanism #posterior-reconstruction
With M=W^T W+sigma^2 I, the latent posterior is Normal(M^(-1)W^T(t-mu),sigma^2 M^(-1)). Mapping its mean back through W generally shrinks the observation within the loading subspace; it is not the ordinary orthogonal PCA reconstruction. When W has full column rank, W(W^T W)^(-1)M times the posterior mean, plus mu, recovers the orthogonal projection. The zero-noise limit loses a nonsingular ambient density for q<d.
  • supports Source: Probabilistic principal component analysis · Locator: Equations 6 and 9 and Section 3.4, printed pp. 614-615 / PDF pp. 4-5; curator finite shrinkage and rank qualification
Mechanism #em-fitting
The E-step computes posterior first and second latent moments using the old W and noise variance. The M-step fits W by observation-latent cross-moments times the inverse summed latent second moment, then updates noise from the expected squared residual using the new W and old posterior moments. Exact well-defined EM makes likelihood nondecreasing, but a zero-loading initialization can remain stationary at an inferior isotropic solution. The existence of a principal-eigenspace maximum does not guarantee successful convergence from every initialization.
  • supports Source: Probabilistic principal component analysis · Locator: Appendix B, Equations 25-31, printed pp. 621-622 / PDF pp. 11-12; Appendix A stationary-point analysis, pp. 619-620 / PDF pp. 9-10; curator zero-loading example

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Latent dimension is a model choice. When residual eigenvalues all vanish, the formal noise estimate reaches the zero-variance boundary, outside the nonsingular positive-noise model. Latent rotations and covariance eigenvalue ties prevent unique axes without additional conventions.
  • The conditional mean is a probabilistic latent summary, not automatic recovery of physical factors or minimum input-space reconstruction error. Isotropic Gaussian noise can be misspecified.
  • Missing-at-random integration and mixtures of PPCA are extensions described in the paper; they are not properties of an arbitrary imputation scheme or aliases for this single Gaussian model. This record does not cover supervised PCA.
  • All 12 primary pages were read. Finite arithmetic checks one exact EM step, posterior shrinkage, rotation/rank ambiguity and a stationary initialization. The Tobamovirus illustrations and author software were not reproduced.
