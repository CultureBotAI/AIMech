PointNet

Architectures AIMech:PointNet DRAFT
A neural architecture for unordered point clouds that applies shared pointwise feature extraction and symmetric max pooling to produce a global representation, with a per-point prediction extension for segmentation.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the original PointNet architecture, including its classification and segmentation paths. It is distinct from PointNet++'s hierarchical local grouping and from a generic set-pooling model.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #architecture-mechanism
Shared multilayer perceptrons encode points; max pooling aggregates their features. The segmentation path concatenates the global descriptor with point features before shared prediction layers. Learned input and feature transformations provide alignment modules.
  • supports Source: PointNet: Deep Learning on Point Sets for 3D Classification and Segmentation · Locator: Section 4.2, Figure 2 and equations 1–2, PDF pp. 3–4 / printed pp. 654–655

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Permutation invariance of a global output and permutation equivariance of per-point predictions are different properties.
  • Learned alignment modules do not establish exact invariance to every geometric transformation; no universal accuracy or robustness claim is curated.
