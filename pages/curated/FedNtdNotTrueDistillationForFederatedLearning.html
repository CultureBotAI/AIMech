FedNTD (Federated Not-True Distillation for Global Knowledge Preservation)

Algorithms AIMech:FedNtdNotTrueDistillationForFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedNTD, Federated Not-True Distillation, Not-True Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning
A heterogeneous federated learning algorithm (Lee et al. 2022, Section 3, Equations 10-14, Algorithm 1) that mitigates catastrophic forgetting of out-of-local-distribution classes during client local updates by augmenting the local cross-entropy loss L_CE(q^l, 1_y) with a beta-weighted Not-True Distillation loss L_NTD(q_tilde_tau^l, q_tilde_tau^g) := -sum_{c=1, c != y}^C q_tilde_tau^g(c) log[q_tilde_tau^l(c) / q_tilde_tau^g(c)] (Equations 10-11), where the not-true softmax probabilities q_tilde_tau^l(c) = exp(z_c^l / tau) / sum_{c_tilde != y}^C exp(z_{c_tilde}^l / tau) and q_tilde_tau^g(c) = exp(z_c^g / tau) / sum_{c_tilde != y}^C exp(z_{c_tilde}^g / tau) normalize client and frozen server logits exclusively over the C - 1 non-target classes c != y, yielding identically zero distillation gradient with respect to the ground-truth logit z_y^l while matching the out-local class distribution p_tilde_c = (1 - p_c) / (C - 1) (Definition 1, Propositions 1-2).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from FedLSD (Federated Local Self-Distillation) and full-logit client-side knowledge distillation methods that distill all C logits including the ground-truth class y, which couples the distillation gradient with in-local class adaptation L_KL^true and degrades new local knowledge acquisition under severe non-IID skew; and distinct from server-side ensemble distillation methods such as FedDF (AIMech:FedDfEnsembleKnowledgeDistillationForFederatedModelFusion, Lin et al. 2020) and FedGEN (AIMech:FedGenDataFreeKnowledgeDistillationForHeterogeneousFederatedLearning, Zhu et al. 2021), as FedNTD operates purely during client local training without proxy datasets or generative models.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r458-not-true-softmax-and-out-local-kl-decomposition-mechanism
Excludes the ground-truth class logit z_y from the temperature-scaled softmax normalization over the C - 1 non-true classes c != y (Equation 11), making dL_NTD/dz_y^l = 0 while decomposing the not-true KL divergence into an expectation weighted by the complementary out-local prior p_tilde_c = (1 - p_c) / (C - 1) (Proposition 2, Equation 14) that provably reduces inter-client gradient diversity Lambda(beta) on 0 <= beta <= C/2 - 1 (Proposition 1, Equation 9, Appendix P Equations 20-31).
  • supports Source: Preservation of the Global Knowledge by Not-True Distillation in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 2.2 Proposition 1 (Equation 9), Section 3.1-3.2 Proposition 2 (Equations 10-14), and Appendices P-Q (Equations 20-41), pp. 4-6, 19-22
Scope #r458-data-free-stateless-client-regularization-scope
Applies to multi-class classification across stateless and stateful cross-device or cross-silo federated learning without requiring proxy datasets, generator networks, or extra communication payload beyond the standard global model parameters w^g.
  • supports Source: Preservation of the Global Knowledge by Not-True Distillation in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.1 Federated Not-True Distillation (Algorithm 1, Table 1) & Section 4 Experiments, pp. 5-8
Limitation #r458-forward-teacher-inference-and-multiclass-requirement-limitation
Requires C >= 3 classes so the non-target class set {c != y} is non-trivial (C - 1 >= 2), and incurs an additional forward pass through the frozen global teacher model w^g on every local training mini-batch together with tuning of the distillation weight beta and temperature tau.
  • supports Source: Preservation of the Global Knowledge by Not-True Distillation in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.1 Equations 10-11 & Section 4.2 Effect of Hyperparameters (Figure 5), pp. 5, 8-9
Application #r458-non-iid-image-and-text-classification-application
Applied to heterogeneous federated image and sequential classification on MNIST, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, CINIC-10, Tiny-ImageNet, and Shakespeare under sharding and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) partitioning.
  • supports Source: Preservation of the Global Knowledge by Not-True Distillation in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 Experiments (Tables 2-4, Figures 4-5), pp. 7-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires at least C >= 3 classes so that the not-true class distribution over c != y is non-degenerate.
  • Adds a frozen global-teacher forward pass on every local training batch and requires tuning distillation weight beta and temperature tau.
