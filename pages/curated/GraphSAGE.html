GraphSAGE

Architectures AIMech:GraphSAGE DRAFT
An inductive graph representation architecture that learns neighborhood aggregation functions to generate node embeddings from features.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope GraphSAGE learns an embedding-generating function, unlike individually fitted transductive node embeddings. The broad AIO graph-convolutional category does not require the named sampling-and-aggregation framework.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
At each depth, sampled neighbor representations are aggregated and combined with a node's current representation through learned transformations; parameters are shared across nodes.
  • supports Source: Inductive Representation Learning on Large Graphs · Locator: Section 3.1, Algorithm 1 and Neighborhood definition, PDF/printed pp. 3–4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The paper describes multiple aggregators; no single mean or pooling variant defines the entire framework.
  • Inductive construction does not guarantee accurate embeddings for every unseen graph or feature distribution.
