Informed backtracking repair

Algorithms AIMech:InformedBacktrackingRepair DRAFT
A finite-CSP repair-oriented backtracking controller that uses an existing complete assignment to order repairs while recursively extending a compatible repaired prefix.

5 Claims
Mechanism #complete-assignment-and-repaired-prefix
The procedure starts with an initial value for every variable. VARS-LEFT initially contains all variables and VARS-DONE is empty. Repairs extend a consistent repaired prefix while the assignments of unrepaired variables remain available to guide search; this is a complete-assignment repair controller rather than only a value-selection heuristic.
  • supports Source: Minimizing Conflicts: A Heuristic Repair Method for Constraint-Satisfaction and Scheduling Problems · Locator: Section 3.2, PDF pp. 9–10 / printed pp. 4–5; Figure 2 initialization and recursive arguments.
Mechanism #conflicted-variable-and-ranked-domain
At a nonsolution node, Figure 2 selects a currently conflicted variable from VARS-LEFT, removes it from that list and pushes it onto VARS-DONE. It considers the selected variable’s possible values in ascending number of conflicts with the remaining VARS-LEFT assignments. The figure does not mandate one particular conflicted-variable selector or tie order.
  • supports Source: Minimizing Conflicts: A Heuristic Repair Method for Constraint-Satisfaction and Scheduling Problems · Locator: Figure 2, PDF p. 10 / printed p. 5, variable selection, list movement and VALUES ordering; Section 3.2, PDF p. 9.
Mechanism #repaired-prefix-compatibility-filter
A value may be assigned and recursively explored only if it is compatible with the already repaired variables. Compatibility with the repaired prefix is a hard filter, separate from the conflict score against unrepaired variables. With binary constraints between distinct variables, extending a consistent repaired prefix this way preserves its consistency; a low remaining-variable conflict score alone is insufficient.
  • supports Source: Minimizing Conflicts: A Heuristic Repair Method for Constraint-Satisfaction and Scheduling Problems · Locator: Section 3.2 and Figure 2, PDF pp. 9–10 / printed pp. 4–5: the VARS-DONE admissibility test and recursive repair. The prefix invariant is the direct binary-CSP interpretation of that test.
Scope #success-test-and-exhaustive-backtracking
The success test checks whether all variables are consistent, not merely whether VARS-LEFT is empty. When choices fail, ordinary backtracking returns to earlier choices and tries alternatives. For finite variables and domains, exhaustive branch exploration with restoration of branch state gives the completeness characterized by the source: find a solution if one exists or report failure after exhaustion. This qualification does not cover a run stopped by an early budget.
  • supports Source: Minimizing Conflicts: A Heuristic Repair Method for Constraint-Satisfaction and Scheduling Problems · Locator: Figure 2 and Section 4 opening strategy comparison, PDF p. 10 / printed p. 5; Section 3.2, PDF p. 9. Branch-state restoration is implicit in the stated standard backtracking interpretation.
Scope #repair-ordering-distinct-from-hill-climbing
The controller combines backtracking with min-conflicts information; it does not require each accepted repair to reduce the total conflict count. The report distinguishes this controller from min-conflicts hill climbing and from its later Brelaz-based hybrid variable-ordering example. The later hybrid is not a mandatory step of Figure 2.
  • supports Source: Minimizing Conflicts: A Heuristic Repair Method for Constraint-Satisfaction and Scheduling Problems · Locator: Sections 3.2 and 4, PDF pp. 9–10 / printed pp. 4–5; hybrid and Section 4.4, PDF pp. 18–19 / printed pp. 13–14.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The completeness statement assumes finite domains, complete exploration and ordinary recursive restoration of assignments and list state. The printed pseudocode does not specify every copying, restoration or failure-return convention; it is not an executable implementation specification.
  • Figure 2 leaves the choice among conflicted variables and tied domain-order scores open. Random tie breaking in the separately boxed min-conflicts heuristic is not silently imposed on every backtracking choice.
  • Ranking against current VARS-LEFT assignments and compatibility with VARS-DONE are different operations. An admissible repair can increase total conflicts; no monotone whole-assignment descent or fixed conflict-reduction rate is guaranteed.
  • The direct operational scope is the binary-CSP formulation. General constraints require an explicit interpretation of conflict counts and repairs, as the source notes; arbitrary weighted or higher-order scoring is not fixed by Figure 2.
  • At-most-once repair applies along one recursive branch. Backtracking can reconsider earlier assignments, and exhaustive failure on a large problem can take a very long time. A cutoff without a solution does not establish unsatisfiability.
  • No universal speedup, benchmark reproduction or exhaustion of the backtracking family is claimed. Later hybrid orderings and the report’s simplified problem-specific analysis are not included as mandatory mechanisms of this controller.
