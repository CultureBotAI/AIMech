Layer-wise relevance propagation

Mathematical Methods AIMech:LayerWiseRelevancePropagation DRAFT
Aliases: LRP
A prediction-decomposition framework that redistributes relevance backward through a classifier's computational layers toward its input features.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope LRP is a family of constrained relevance-propagation constructions, demonstrated for neural networks and kernel-based Bag-of-Words classifiers; it is not a single gradient formula or a classifier-training algorithm.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #attribution-method
Starting from an output score, layer-specific rules allocate relevance messages to preceding units and sum incoming messages until the input is reached. The neural-network procedure supplies stabilized and positive/negative contribution rules.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Conservation alone does not uniquely determine meaningful attributions; the original paper gives a counterexample.
  • Bias terms and epsilon stabilization can absorb relevance, so exact conservation is not asserted for every practical rule.
