Binary Multiple Kernel Maximum Margin Clustering

Algorithms AIMech:BinaryMultipleKernelMaximumMarginClustering DRAFT
Aliases: Two-Class Multiple Kernel Clustering
A two-class unsupervised clustering procedure that jointly fits a nonnegative multiple-kernel combination and absolute-margin classifier using cutting-plane constraints and constrained concave-convex updates.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the binary Zhao-Kwok-Zhang2009 procedure with an intercept and aggregate score balancing. It differs from the same paper multiclass winner-versus-runner-up procedure, supervised multiple-kernel SVM training, fixed-kernel maximum-margin solvers and variance-minimizing multiview kernel k-means. The paper uses MKC for both variants, so that ambiguous acronym is not a unique alias. Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #kernel-weight-geometry
Given supplied PSD base kernels K_k and feature maps Phi_k, learn nonnegative weights beta_k with sum_k beta_k^2<=1, not a simplex constraint. The resulting mixed kernel is sum_k beta_k*K_k, consistently represented by concatenated sqrt(beta_k)*Phi_k blocks. The paper introductory sum of feature maps must not be read as an arbitrary vector sum that adds cross terms, especially when feature dimensions differ. Its reparameterization v_k=beta_k*w_k gives a quadratic-over-linear regularizer. At beta_k=0 the closed perspective has cost zero for v_k=0 and infinite cost otherwise. Kernel scaling affects the optimization.

Mechanism #binary-objective
Let f_i=sum_k v_k^T*Phi_k(x_i)+b. Minimize 0.5*sum_k ||v_k||^2/beta_k+(C/n)*sum_i xi_i with |f_i|>=1-xi_i, xi_i>=0, nonnegative L2-bounded beta and |sum_i f_i|<=l. Assign labels by the sign of the fitted scores under an explicit zero-score convention. This optimizes an absolute-margin relaxation of latent-label clustering, not supervised fitting to given labels.
  • supports Source: Multiple Kernel Clustering · Locator: Section 2.1, PDF pp. 1-3 / pp. 638-640, Equations 2.2-2.6
Mechanism #working-set-and-inner-fit
Replace individual slacks by a shared xi with all binary masks c in {0,1}^n constrained by mean_i c_i*|f_i|>=mean_i c_i-xi. The maximal violation selects c_i=1 where |f_i|<1 and zero otherwise, giving mean_i max(0,1-|f_i|). Starting with an empty working set, alternate restricted fitting and adding this most violated constraint until its violation is at most epsilon. Within a restricted fit, freeze z_i as an absolute-value subgradient at the previous score, replace |f_i| by z_i*f_i and solve the convex perspective problem as an SOCP; repeat subject to the chosen stopping rule. For each kernel its epigraph is ||(2*v_k,t_k-beta_k)||<=t_k+beta_k.
  • supports Source: Multiple Kernel Clustering · Locator: Sections 2.2-2.3, PDF pp. 3-5 / pp. 640-642, Theorems 2.1-2.2, Algorithms 1-2 and Equations 2.7-2.17
Limitation #balance-is-a-relaxation
The source replaces |sum_i y_i|<=l with |sum_i f_i|<=l. The latter does not guarantee the former despite nearby equivalence language: with four zero-feature observations, l=1 and b=1/4, all scores are positive and sum to 1, while all predicted labels are +1 and sum to 4. With C=1 this feasible relaxed solution has objective 3/4, whereas every count-balanced labeling has minimum objective 1. Thus the score-balanced formulation and the original hard count-balanced objective are not generally equivalent.
  • supports Source: Multiple Kernel Clustering · Locator: Section 2.1, PDF pp. 1-2 / pp. 638-639, Equations 2.2-2.3; curator exact zero-feature derivation and finite checks
Limitation #stopping-and-guarantee-boundary
The epsilon test bounds omitted constraint violation: increasing the shared slack by epsilon makes the current score solution feasible for the full mask set. It does not bound label error or distance from a global optimum. At zero scores, choosing the valid subgradient zero can make the inner problem return the same zero-scoring point, even though a small feasible bias lowers the actual hinge objective; initialization and tie conventions therefore qualify local-optimum language. The source complexity discussion treats an empirically small CCCP iteration count as constant and contains a linear beta-sum term in Equation 3.21 despite the squared beta constraint in the stated primal. No unconditional end-to-end linear-time or convergence certificate is asserted here.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The full problem is nonconvex. Solving convex second-order cone subproblems does not establish a globally optimal kernel combination, globally optimal labels or universal local-optimum behavior. Initialization, score ties, numeric tolerances and stopping conventions matter.
  • Nonnegative kernel weights preserve PSD when the supplied base kernels are PSD. Feature construction, kernel choice and scaling, regularization C, balance limit l and cutting-plane tolerance epsilon remain supplied choices. No class-count balance or nonempty-cluster guarantee follows solely from aggregate score constraints.
  • All twelve source pages, including experiments and references, were read. The reported comparisons use selected datasets, known class counts, chosen kernels and tuning; their per-cluster majority-label accuracy is not stated as one-to-one cluster matching. The source whole-dataset generalization evaluation includes the fitting subset. No universal performance, independent held-out guarantee, historical implementation or benchmark reproduction is asserted.
