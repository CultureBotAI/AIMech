TTQ (Trained Ternary Quantization)

Algorithms AIMech:TTQTrainedTernaryQuantization DRAFT
Aliases: TTQ, Trained Ternary Quantization
TTQ (Trained Ternary Quantization) is a neural network weight quantization method that compresses full-precision weights into 2-bit asymmetric ternary values {-W_l^n, 0, +W_l^p} per layer l by normalizing latent full-precision weights w_tilde_l into [-1, 1], thresholding by Delta_l = t * max(|w_tilde_l|) (or a constant sparsity ratio r), and jointly training both the asymmetric positive and negative scaling coefficients (W_l^p, W_l^n) via summed intra-bucket gradients (Eq. 7) and the ternary assignments via asymmetrically scaled latent-weight gradients dL/dw_tilde_l in {W_l^p * dL/dw_l^t, 1 * dL/dw_l^t, W_l^n * dL/dw_l^t} (Eq. 8).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from symmetric Ternary Weight Networks (TWN), which tie positive and negative ternary magnitudes to a single analytical scale W_l with heuristic threshold 0.7 * E(|w|), and distinct from uniform k-bit weight quantizers such as DoReFa-Net (AIMech:DoReFaNetTrainingLowBitwidthConvolutionalNeuralNetworksWithLowBitwidthGradients).

Mechanism #ttq-asymmetric-ternary-quantization-and-threshold-rule
TTQ normalizes latent full-precision weights w_tilde_l to [-1, 1] via maximum-magnitude division (Eq. 5) and assigns ternary weights w_l^t = +W_l^p for w_tilde_l > Delta_l, 0 for |w_tilde_l| <= Delta_l, and -W_l^n for w_tilde_l < -Delta_l (Eq. 6), where the threshold Delta_l = t * max(|w_tilde_l|) (Eq. 9, typically t = 0.05) or is set to maintain a constant layer sparsity ratio r.
  • supports Source: Trained Ternary Quantization · Locator: Section 3, Equations 5-6 and 9, pp. 3-4
  • supports Source: Trained Ternary Quantization · Locator: ttq_quantize
Mechanism #ttq-joint-scale-and-latent-assignment-gradient-backpropagation
During backpropagation, TTQ updates the per-layer positive and negative scaling coefficients via dL/dW_l^p = sum_{i in I_l^p} dL/dw_l^t(i) and dL/dW_l^n = sum_{i in I_l^n} dL/dw_l^t(i) (Eq. 7) and updates the latent weights via piecewise scaled gradients dL/dw_tilde_l = W_l^p * dL/dw_l^t for w_tilde_l > Delta_l, 1 * dL/dw_l^t for |w_tilde_l| <= Delta_l, and W_l^n * dL/dw_l^t for w_tilde_l < -Delta_l (Eq. 8).
  • supports Source: Trained Ternary Quantization · Locator: Section 3, Equations 7-8 and Figure 1, pp. 3-4
  • supports Source: Trained Ternary Quantization · Locator: ttq_backward and main
Application #ttq-16x-weight-compression-exceeding-or-matching-fp32-on-cifar-and-imagenet
TTQ reduces weight storage from 32 bits to 2 bits (~16x model compression) with 30%-50% zero-weight sparsity while outperforming full-precision baselines on CIFAR-10 (ResNet-20, 32, 44, 56) and AlexNet on ImageNet (57.5% vs. 57.2% top-1 accuracy) and matching ResNet-18 within 3% top-1 accuracy.
  • supports Source: Trained Ternary Quantization · Locator: Section 4.1-4.2, Tables 1-3 and Figures 2-3, pp. 5-7
Limitation #ttq-full-precision-activations-and-first-last-layer-exclusion
TTQ quantizes only network weights (keeping activations and gradients in 32-bit floating point) and leaves the first convolutional layer and final fully connected layer in full precision across CIFAR-10 and ImageNet experiments.
  • supports Source: Trained Ternary Quantization · Locator: Section 4.1 (p. 5) and Section 4.2 (AlexNet and ResNet-18, pp. 5-7)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Quantizes only weights while keeping activations and gradients in full precision.
  • Leaves the first convolutional layer and the final classification layer in full precision to preserve accuracy.
