Age-Granularity-Net Multi-Granularity Classification-Regression Framework

Architectures AIMech:AgeGranularityNetMultiGranularityClassificationRegressionFramework DRAFT
Aliases: Age-Granularity-Net, Multi-Granularity Classification-Regression Network, Age-Granularity-Net Multi-Task Age Estimator
A multi-head neural network training architecture for continuous/ordinal visual estimation that attaches multiple parallel classification branches quantized at progressively coarser label granularities alongside a continuous mean-squared-error regression branch to a shared feature backbone during training, and prunes all auxiliary coarse and regression heads at inference time to predict from the finest-granularity branch alone.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope No AIO class represents Age-Granularity-Net or multi-granularity classification-regression auxiliary branch training; ArtificialNeuralNetwork (AIO:ArtificialNeuralNetwork) is the closest broad match covering neural network architectures.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #hierarchical-multi-granularity-cross-entropy-and-mse-aggregation
Age-Granularity-Net jointly optimizes a shared backbone under the sum of N-1 cross-entropy losses over progressively coarser target quantizations (1-year, 5-year, 10-year, and 20-year bins) and a lambda-weighted continuous mean-squared-error regression loss, rewarding near-miss predictions that fall within the same coarser interval.
  • supports Source: Using Multiple Losses for Accurate Facial Age Estimation · Locator: Section 3.1-3.2, Figures 1-2 and Equations 1-2, pp. 2-3, 5
Mechanism #single-branch-inference-without-computational-overhead
During inference, Age-Granularity-Net evaluates only the finest-granularity 100-class branch and discards the coarser classification and MSE regression branches, achieving multi-loss representation regularization with zero additional inference latency or parameters.
  • supports Source: Using Multiple Losses for Accurate Facial Age Estimation · Locator: Section 4.3, p. 3; Section 6, p. 4
Application #apparent-facial-age-estimation-under-label-ambiguity-application
Applied to apparent facial age estimation on the CVPR ChaLearn Looking at People dataset across AlexNet, MobileNet-V1, ResNet-50, DenseNet-121, and VGG-16-BN backbones, Age-Granularity-Net reduces test mean absolute error by 17% to 49% relative to single-loss 100-class training.
  • supports Source: Using Multiple Losses for Accurate Facial Age Estimation · Locator: Section 4.1-5, Table 1, pp. 3-4
Limitation #mse-loss-scale-sensitivity-and-learning-rate-instability-limitation
Combining unnormalized continuous MSE loss with multi-class cross-entropy losses introduces gradient magnitude disparities that require tuning the balancing weight lambda or lowering the initial learning rate by two orders of magnitude (from 10^-3 to 10^-5 on AlexNet) to prevent training divergence.
  • supports Source: Using Multiple Losses for Accurate Facial Age Estimation · Locator: Section 3.2, p. 3; Section 5, Table 1 footnote 1, p. 4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Combining unnormalized continuous MSE loss with multi-class cross-entropy losses introduces gradient magnitude disparities that require tuning the balancing weight lambda or lowering the initial learning rate by two orders of magnitude (from 10^-3 to 10^-5 on AlexNet) to prevent training divergence.
