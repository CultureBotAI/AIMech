Gradient-weighted class activation mapping

Algorithms AIMech:GradCAM DRAFT
Aliases: Grad-CAM
A convolutional-network localization method weighting activation maps by spatially averaged gradients of a selected target output.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Grad-CAM computes a coarse activation-space localization map. It differs from architecture-restricted CAM and from Guided Grad-CAM's additional input-space fusion.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #explanation-method
Differentiate the target score with respect to convolutional feature maps, average gradients spatially to obtain channel weights, and apply ReLU to the weighted sum of activation maps.
  • supports Source: Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-based Localization · Locator: Section 3, equations 1–2, PDF pp. 3–4 / printed pp. 620–621

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Map resolution follows the selected convolutional layer, and ReLU retains positive weighted contributions.
  • A localization map is not a proof of causal influence or universal faithfulness; evaluation claims are not curated here.
