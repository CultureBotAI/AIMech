Direct preference optimization

Algorithms AIMech:DirectPreferenceOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: DPO
Preference-based policy fitting using a classification objective on policy-to-reference probability ratios for preferred and dispreferred responses.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope A training algorithm, not an instruction-tuned language-model instance or a synonym for all reinforcement learning from human feedback.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
A reward-to-policy reparameterization yields a logistic preference loss that trains the policy without separately fitting an explicit reward model.
  • supports Source: Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model · Locator: Section 4, equations 4–7, PDF/printed p. 4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The derivation uses a specified preference model and KL-regularized reference-policy objective; it is not a guarantee of universally correct human preferences.
  • No benchmark superiority or general safety claim is curated.
