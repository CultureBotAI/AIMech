Relevance-weighted topic distillation

Algorithms AIMech:RelevanceWeightedTopicDistillation DRAFT
Aliases: Relevance-conscious topic distillation, Chakrabarti relevance-weighted distillation
An iterative web-link ranking procedure that alternates authority and hub score propagation using destination relevance for forward contributions and source relevance for backward contributions, retaining inter-site links and restricting authority eligibility by relevance.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #asymmetric-propagation
For a retained link u to v, forward weight E_F[u,v] is R(v) and backward weight E_B[u,v] is R(u). Authority updates sum E_F[u,v] times hub score h(u); hub updates sum E_B[u,v] times authority score a(v). The procedure alternates these updates with unit-length scaling. Destination and source relevance therefore act on different propagation directions; one unweighted adjacency matrix does not represent both operations.
  • supports Source: Focused crawling: a new approach to topic-specific Web resource discovery · Locator: Section 3.2, printed p. 552 / PDF p. 8
Mechanism #eligibility-and-graph
Construct the graph using links between different sites. A relevance threshold admits potential authorities, described as the most relevant 10–20 percent of nodes, and remains enforced during iteration. Hubs have no corresponding eligibility threshold, although their backward contributions are weighted by their own relevance. Thus a low but positively relevant hub can receive a positive score; lack of an eligibility cutoff is not lack of relevance weighting.
  • supports Source: Focused crawling: a new approach to topic-specific Web resource discovery · Locator: Section 3.2, printed p. 552 / PDF p. 8; finite masked-graph arithmetic in research/discovery/focused-crawling-boundaries.py
Application #acquisition-interface
In the focused crawler this component periodically processes the accumulated graph, identifies hubs to revisit and promotes their unvisited citations. It can help reconsider useful routes lost through text-based misclassification. The ranking component itself is distinct from retrieving pages, training a topic classifier or compressing a teacher model into a student.
  • supports Source: Focused crawling: a new approach to topic-specific Web resource discovery · Locator: Sections 3.2–3.3 and 4.7, printed pp. 552–553,559 / PDF pp. 8–9,15
Limitation #convergence-and-input-boundary
The source prescribes a suitable number of iterations with score normalization but does not provide a universally applicable stopping rule or zero-norm/tie policy for this weighted procedure. Ranking is conditional on the supplied graph, relevance values and authority selection. The classifier supplying those values has a printed Bayes-denominator discrepancy in Equation 1; this record does not infer implementation behavior from it.
  • supports Source: Focused crawling: a new approach to topic-specific Web resource discovery · Locator: Sections 3.1–3.2, printed pp. 551–552 / PDF pp. 7–8
Limitation #evaluation-boundary
The source compares top-ranked pages and server addresses obtained from separate crawls and presents historical resource lists and integration anecdotes. Section 4.4 and Figure 8 axes refer to top 25 resources, but the caption says 100 best servers. These results do not establish a single unambiguous overlap denominator, independently labeled ranking accuracy, complete-web recall or a general semantic-authority guarantee.
  • supports Source: Focused crawling: a new approach to topic-specific Web resource discovery · Locator: Sections 4.4–4.7 and Figures 8–10, printed pp. 557–559 / PDF pp. 13–15

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The record denotes the 1999 relevance-weighted distiller, not generic HITS, all topic-distillation methods, a learned semantic class or a whole focused-crawling system.
  • Authority selection is asymmetric with hub eligibility. Numerical weight definitions do not guarantee that classifier scores are calibrated or that high link authority equals factual correctness.
  • The primary text specifies normalization between updates but not every degenerate graph, initialization, zero-score or stopping case. No blanket convergence or uniqueness claim is made.
  • The same-source classifier equation and ranking-count ambiguities remain explicit. Constructed exact arithmetic illustrates directional weights and eligibility; it does not replicate author code or experiments.
  • All eighteen primary pages and relevant rendered figures/formulas were inspected. The author-hosted PDF pagination differs from publisher metadata, and publisher full-text access failed. No author software, historical URL visits, live crawl, paid service or training was performed; DRAFT remains.
