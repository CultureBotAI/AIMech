Strict Saddle Property and Noisy Stochastic Gradient Saddle Escaping for Orthogonal Tensor Decomposition

Algorithms AIMech:StrictSaddlePropertyAndNoisyStochasticGradientSaddleEscapingForOrthogonalTensorDecomposition DRAFT
Aliases: Strict Saddle Property and Noisy SGD Saddle Escaping (Ge-Huang-Jin-Yuan 2015), Projected Noisy Stochastic Gradient Descent on Strict Saddle Manifolds, Deflation-Free Cross-Correlation Orthogonal Tensor Decomposition
The geometric characterization of $(\theta,\gamma,\zeta)$-strict saddle functions (both unconstrained in $\mathbb{R}^d$ and manifold-constrained via generalized Lagrange multipliers on $\{c(x)=0\}$) and the polynomial-time saddle-escaping convergence guarantee of *Noisy Stochastic Gradient Descent* $w_{t+1} = w_t - \eta(\nabla f(w_t) + \xi_t + n_t)$ (with injected isotropic spherical noise $n_t \sim \mathrm{Unif}(\mathbb{S}^{d-1})$) and *Projected Noisy SGD* $w_{t+1} = \Pi_{\mathcal{X}}(w_t - \eta(\nabla f(w_t) + \xi_t + n_t))$, introduced by Rong Ge, Furong Huang, Chi Jin, and Yang Yuan (2015). A twice-differentiable function $f$ satisfies the $(\theta,\gamma,\zeta)$-strict saddle property (Definition 1 / Definition 5) if every point $x$ obeys at least one of: (1) $\|\nabla f(x)\| \ge \theta$ (or Riemannian tangent gradient $\|\chi(x)\| \ge \theta$), (2) $\lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2 f(x)) \le -\gamma$ (or minimum tangent-space Hessian eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min}(M(x)) \le -\gamma$), or (3) $x$ lies within distance $\zeta$ of a local minimum $x^*$ around which $f$ is locally $\alpha$-strongly convex. Theorems 6 and 16 prove that Noisy SGD escapes every saddle region in $O(\gamma^{-2}\log d)$ iterations via second-order Taylor quadratic growth of the negative-eigenvector projection and outputs an $\epsilon$-close local minimum in $O(\mathrm{poly}(1/\theta, 1/\gamma, d)/\epsilon^2)$ iterations; furthermore, Theorem 13 proves that the fourth-order cross-correlation objective $\min_{\|u^{(i)}\|_2=1} \sum_{i\ne j}\langle T, u^{(i)}\otimes u^{(i)}\otimes u^{(j)}\otimes u^{(j)}\rangle$ for orthogonal tensor decomposition satisfies the manifold strict-saddle property with all local minima equal to signed permutations of the true components $\{\pm a_1,\dots,\pm a_d\}$ without sequential deflation.

Distinct from `AIMech:OrthogonalTensorDecompositionAndRobustTensorPowerMethodForLatentVariableModels` (which relies on sequential tensor power iterations and deflation of an explicit empirical $d\times d\times d$ tensor) and `AIMech:NonconvexMatrixCompletionLandscapeWithoutSpuriousLocalMinima` (which analyzes Burer-Monteiro matrix completion rather than the $(\theta,\gamma,\zeta)$-strict saddle framework, Noisy SGD negative-curvature amplification, and deflation-free fourth-order cross-correlation tensor objectives on $\prod_{i=1}^d \mathbb{S}^{d-1}$).

Mechanism #claim-strict-saddle-definition-and-noisy-sgd-polynomial-escape-theorem
For any $\beta$-smooth, $\rho$-Hessian-Lipschitz function $f:\mathbb{R}^d\to\mathbb{R}$ satisfying the $(\theta,\gamma,\zeta)$-strict saddle property (Definition 1), Theorem 6 and Lemma 9 prove that Noisy SGD $w_{t+1} = w_t - \eta(\nabla f(w_t) + \xi_t + n_t)$ with isotropic perturbation $n_t \sim \mathrm{Unif}(\mathbb{S}^{d-1})$ decreases function value by $\Omega(\eta\theta^2)$ when $\|\nabla f(w_t)\| \ge \theta$ (Lemma 7), amplifies the negative-eigenvector component at rate $(1 + \eta|\lambda_i|)^{2t}$ near any saddle point with $\lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2 f(w_0)) \le -\gamma$ to achieve $\mathbb{E}[f(w_T)] - f(w_0) \le -\eta\sigma^2/(4d)$ in $T = O(\gamma^{-1}\eta^{-1}\log d)$ steps (Lemma 9), and reaches $\|w_t - x^*\|_2 \le \epsilon$ in $O(\eta^{-2}\log(1/\delta))$ total steps with $\eta = \widetilde{O}(\epsilon^2/\mathrm{poly}(d,1/\gamma,1/\theta))$.

Mechanism #claim-manifold-strict-saddle-property-of-deflation-free-orthogonal-tensor-cross-correlation
For an orthogonal symmetric fourth-order tensor $T = \sum_{k=1}^d a_k^{\otimes 4}$, the deflation-free cross-correlation objective $\min_{\|u^{(i)}\|_2=1} \sum_{i\ne j} T(u^{(i)},u^{(i)},u^{(j)},u^{(j)})$ on $(\mathbb{S}^{d-1})^d$ (Equation 13) satisfies the manifold $(\theta,\gamma,\zeta)$-strict saddle property with $\theta = 1/(4d^{3.5}), \gamma = 1/(3d^2), \zeta = 1/(2d^{1.5})$, and $\alpha = 1$: every stationary point where two rows $u^{(i)}, u^{(j)}$ share non-tiny support exhibits a tangent direction $v\in\mathcal{T}(U)$ with quadratic curvature $v^\top M(U) v \le -1/(3d^2)$ (Lemma 44), whereas every point near a signed permutation $V$ of $\{a_1,\dots,a_d\}$ satisfies $v^\top M(V) v \ge 2\|v\|_2^2$ (Lemma 45), so Projected Noisy SGD (Algorithm 2) recovers all $d$ components simultaneously without forming $T$ explicitly (Theorems 13 and 16).

Scope #claim-scope-online-ica-topic-models-gmm-and-community-detection-via-fourth-order-moments
Section 2 and Appendix A show that Independent Component Analysis (fourth-order cumulant $\mathbb{E}[y^{\otimes 4}] - T_{\mathcal{N}}$), Latent Dirichlet Allocation topic models, spherical Gaussian mixture models, and multi-view models admit unbiased online stochastic gradients of the fourth-order tensor objective from $O(1)$ streaming samples per step without ever storing an $O(d^4)$ or $O(d^3)$ moment tensor in memory.

Limitation #claim-limitation-whitened-orthogonality-and-degenerate-higher-order-saddles
Noisy SGD's polynomial escape guarantee requires non-degenerate second-order negative curvature ($\lambda_{\min}(\nabla^2 f(x)) \le -\gamma < 0$) and fails on degenerate higher-order monkey saddles where $\nabla f(x) = 0$ and $\nabla^2 f(x) = 0$; in addition, the tensor objective (Equation 13) assumes orthonormal components $\{a_1,\dots,a_d\}$ after second-order whitening.
  • supports Source: Escaping From Saddle Points — Online Stochastic Gradient for Tensor Decomposition · Locator: Section 1 & Section 3, Definition 1, pp. 2-6; Section 4, Equation (10), p. 11
