HyperPrompt (HyperNetwork-Generated Task-Conditioned Self-Attention Prompts)

Aliases: HyperPrompt, HyperPrompt-Global, HyperPrompt-Share, HyperPrompt-Sep, MTL-Prompt-Share, MTL-Prompt-Sep
A multi-task Transformer architecture that initializes a trainable global task prompt P_tau in R^{l x d} for each task tau and applies local bottleneck projection networks (D^m_{tau,k/v}, U^m_{tau,k/v})—either shared across tasks (HyperPrompt-Share), separate per task (HyperPrompt-Sep), or generated from a layer-aware task embedding I^m_tau = h_t(k_tau, z_m) via shared global HyperNetworks H_k and H_v (HyperPrompt-Global)—to produce layer-specific key and value hyper-prompts P^m_{tau,k}, P^m_{tau,v} prepended directly to the multi-head self-attention keys and values at every Transformer block.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Introduced by Yun He, Huaixiu Steven Zheng, Yi Tay, Jai Gupta, Yu Du, Vamsi Aribandi, Zhe Zhao, YaGuang Li, Zhao Chen, Donald Metzler, Heng-Tze Cheng, and Ed H. Chi (ICML 2022, PMLR 162:8678-8690, arXiv:2203.00759). Distinct from Prefix-Tuning (Li & Liang, ACL 2021; AIMech:PrefixTuning), which optimizes single-task per-layer prefixes without cross-task hypernetworks or global-to-local bottleneck projections, and distinct from HyperFormer (Karimi Mahabadi et al., ACL 2021; AIMech Round 703), which generates post-FFN sequential adapter layers rather than self-attention key/value memory tokens. All three architectural variants (HyperPrompt-Share, HyperPrompt-Sep, and HyperPrompt-Global) are covered within this single record.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #hyperprompt-key-value-attention-injection-and-global-hypernetwork-mechanism
At each Transformer block m for task tau, HyperPrompt-Global fuses a task embedding k_tau in R^{t'} and layer embedding z_m in R^{t'} via a two-layer ReLU MLP I^m_tau = h_t(k_tau, z_m) in R^t, uses shared global HyperNetworks H_k and H_v to generate local bottleneck matrices (U^m_{tau,k}, D^m_{tau,k}) = (W^{U_k}, W^{D_k}) I^m_tau and (U^m_{tau,v}, D^m_{tau,v}) = (W^{U_v}, W^{D_v}) I^m_tau (Eqs. 7-8), projects the task global prompt P_tau in R^{l x d} into P^m_{tau,k} = U^m_{tau,k}(ReLU(D^m_{tau,k}(P_tau))) and P^m_{tau,v} = U^m_{tau,v}(ReLU(D^m_{tau,v}(P_tau))) (Eqs. 5-6), and prepends them to the original keys and values as K'_tau = concat(P^m_{tau,k}, K_tau) and V'_tau = concat(P^m_{tau,v}, V_tau) (Eqs. 1-2) so O_tau = softmax(Q_tau K'_tau^T) V'_tau in R^{L x d} retains sequence length L and skips passing prompt tokens through standard FFN layers.
  • supports Source: HyperPrompt: Prompt-based Task-Conditioning of Transformers · Locator: Section 1 Introduction, pp. 1-2; Section 3.1-3.3, Figure 2 & Eqs. 1-8, pp. 3-5; Section 4.4 Table 2, pp. 6-7
Scope #hyperprompt-sublinear-task-parameter-complexity-scope
HyperPrompt-Global introduces exactly d l T + 4(b d t) + T t' + M t' + (2 t' + t) e additional task-conditioning parameters (where d is model dimension, l is prompt length, T is number of tasks, b is local bottleneck dimension, M is number of layers, t'/t are raw/final task embedding dimensions, and e is MLP hidden dimension), yielding O(d(l T + 4 b t)) space complexity that is independent of layer count M in its dominant terms and scales sub-linearly with T while requiring 9.8 x 10^{12} forward-pass ops on T5-Base versus 3.14 x 10^{13} for HyperFormer++.
  • supports Source: HyperPrompt: Prompt-based Task-Conditioning of Transformers · Locator: Section 3.4 Parameter Efficiency of HyperPrompt, p. 5; Section 4.4 Computational Efficiency, Table 2, pp. 6-7; Appendix A.1, p. 13
Application #hyperprompt-superglue-and-glue-scaling-application
Across T5 model sizes from Base (220M) to XXL (11B), full multi-task fine-tuning with HyperPrompt-Global achieves state-of-the-art multi-task accuracy on GLUE and SuperGLUE, reaching 86.8 GLUE / 78.9 SuperGLUE on T5-Base (vs. 85.5 / 77.2 for MTL and 86.5 / 78.2 for HyperFormer++), 89.4 GLUE / 87.0 SuperGLUE on T5-Large, and 91.3 SuperGLUE on T5-XXL (vs. 90.2 for MTL) with as little as 0.14% additional task-conditioning parameters.
  • supports Source: HyperPrompt: Prompt-based Task-Conditioning of Transformers · Locator: Abstract & Figure 1, p. 1; Section 4.2 Key Results, p. 6; Section 4.5 Ablation Study, Tables 3-4, p. 7; Appendix A.4 Tables 8-11, p. 14
Limitation #hyperprompt-frozen-backbone-superglue-drop-limitation
When the Transformer backbone parameters theta are kept frozen and only task-conditioned parameters are tuned, HyperPrompt-Global suffers a 5.5-point average score drop on SuperGLUE with T5-Large (falling from 87.0 to 81.5 in Table 1), and inserting hyper-prompts into the encoder alone degrades SuperGLUE performance on T5-Base relative to decoder-only insertion (76.5 vs. 78.9 in Table 5).
  • supports Source: HyperPrompt: Prompt-based Task-Conditioning of Transformers · Locator: Section 4.3 Tuning all vs Task-Conditioned Parameters, Table 1, p. 6; Section 4.8 Encoder vs Decoder, Table 5, pp. 8-9; Appendix A.4 Tables 6-7, p. 14

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Freezing the Transformer backbone and updating only task-conditioned HyperPrompt parameters drops average SuperGLUE accuracy by 5.5 points on T5-Large compared to joint backbone-and-hyperprompt fine-tuning.
  • Inserting hyper-prompts only into the T5 encoder underperforms decoder-only insertion on SuperGLUE (76.5 vs. 78.9 on T5-Base).
