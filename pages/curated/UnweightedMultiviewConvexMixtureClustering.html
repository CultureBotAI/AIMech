Unweighted Multiview Convex Mixture Clustering

Aliases: Multiview Convex Mixture Model Clustering, Convex Mixture Models for Multi-view Clustering
A multiview exemplar-clustering procedure that fits a single vector of exemplar weights by minimizing the equally weighted sum of fixed-component negative log likelihoods across aligned data views, then selects a specified number of exemplars.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the unweighted Tzortzis-Likas 2009 procedure. Shared q couples corresponding exemplar indices across views, while each view retains its own fixed component family and scale. Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #shared-weight-fitting
For N observations with V aligned views, fix view-specific component values f_vij=f_vj(x_vi) and common simplex weights q_j. Minimize -(1/(N V))sum_vi log Q_vi, where Q_vi=sum_j q_j f_vij. Set q_j_new=q_j/(N V)*sum_vi f_vij/Q_vi. Each view contributes its average current responsibilities equally. Common factors depending on the view and observation but not the exemplar cancel in this update. With finite positive component values, positive support and exact unpruned iteration, the soft convex problem attains a global optimum; coefficient uniqueness is not implied.
  • supports Source: Convex Mixture Models for Multi-view Clustering · Locator: Sections 3.1-3.2, printed pp. 208-209 / PDF pp. 4-5, Equations 6-9 and support condition
Mechanism #specified-exemplars-and-assignment
Given a requested M, retain the M largest fitted q values as exemplars, each anchoring its own cluster. Assign every other observation to the selected exemplar maximizing sum_v q_j f_vij/Q_vi. This sums per-view posteriors, rather than multiplying densities or taking the maximum over individual views. The source uses strict comparisons without a tie policy. Its implementation stops when the selected top-M indices and their ranking stay unchanged for several consecutive iterations; this can occur before the soft objective is optimized.
  • supports Source: Convex Mixture Models for Multi-view Clustering · Locator: Section 3.2, printed pp. 209-210 / PDF pp. 5-6, stopping discussion and Equation 10
Limitation #scope-and-calibration
View families and beta_v values remain supplied choices. The empirical scale N^2 log(N)/sum_ij d_vij is a search guide and is undefined when its denominator vanishes. The fitting update needs O(N^2 V) dense arithmetic per iteration, apart from distance construction. A fixed-M hard partition has no transferred global-optimum guarantee; stable ranking is not a bound on residual objective error. Equal view contribution also does not guarantee robustness to uninformative or systematically biased views.
  • supports Source: Convex Mixture Models for Multi-view Clustering · Locator: Section 3.2, printed p. 210 / PDF p. 6, Equation 11 and cost; Sections 4-5, printed pp. 210-214 / PDF pp. 6-10; curator stable-ranking example

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The record uses the finite evaluated-component/log-likelihood objective; the source empirical-versus-continuous KL shorthand requires a compatible measure or lift. Gaussian exponents use squared Euclidean distances.
  • The soft fit is a shared-weight specialization of fixed-component EM. The complete multiview data construction, equal-view objective and sum-of-posteriors/top-M policy define this narrower algorithm.
  • Source experiments remove observations lacking required views and select scale multipliers by label entropy. They do not establish an intrinsic missing-view algorithm, fully label-free model selection, or universal performance. No historical experiment was reproduced.
