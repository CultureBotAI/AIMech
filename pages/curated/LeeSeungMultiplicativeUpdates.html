Aliases: Lee-Seung NMF Updates
The Lee-Seung block multiplicative algorithms for nonnegative matrix factorization, using objective-specific elementwise factor rescaling to reduce squared reconstruction error or generalized KL divergence with the other factor held fixed.
Definition Evidence

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #squared-error-update
For squared error and well-defined positive denominators, update H elementwise as H <- H*(W^T V)/(W^T W H) with W fixed. Update W analogously as W <- W*(V H^T)/(W H H^T) with H fixed, evaluating each block rule against its current other factor. Multiplication and division of corresponding entries are elementwise. The proof uses a quadratic auxiliary function whose diagonal curvature majorizes W^T W, with current positive coordinates required in its displayed derivation.
  • supports Source: Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization · Locator: Section 4, Equation 4, PDF p. 3; Section 6, Lemma 2 and Equations 12-19, PDF pp. 4-5
Mechanism #divergence-update
For generalized KL divergence, H_aμ is multiplied by [sum_i W_ia V_iμ/(WH)_iμ]/[sum_i W_ia], and W_ia by [sum_μ H_aμ V_iμ/(WH)_iμ]/[sum_μ H_aμ], with the other block fixed. Jensen majorization of the negative logarithm gives the source auxiliary function. With valid ratios and the stated domain, exact minimization of each auxiliary does not increase the corresponding objective. These are two objective-specific rules, not a universal multiplicative update for arbitrary losses.
  • supports Source: Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization · Locator: Section 4, Equation 5, PDF p. 3; Section 6, Lemma 3 and Equations 20-33, PDF pp. 5-6
Limitation #descent-boundaries
Monotone objective values do not alone prove convergence of factor matrices to a local minimum. In a curator scalar example V=1 and W=H=0.5, updating both factors simultaneously from their old values sets both to 2 and raises squared error from 0.5625 to 9. A separate fixed-W example W=[[1,0],[1,1]], V=[1,2]^T and H=[1.5,0]^T is unchanged by the H rule, although the zero coordinate has half-squared-error derivative -0.5 and increasing it lowers the loss. This is a zero-locked nonoptimal block state with positive denominators, not a claim about an inspected author implementation.
  • supports Source: Algorithms for Non-negative Matrix Factorization · Locator: Sections 4 and 6-7, PDF pp. 3-7; curator finite counterexamples to unrestricted update and local-optimum interpretations

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The rules require defined denominators and an explicit zero policy. The proof for positive current coordinates does not automatically cover arbitrary boundary initializations; multiplicative zeros may remain locked.
  • Sequential block descent is not a license to update both blocks from stale factors. Arbitrary epsilon denominators, clipping, approximate updates or added penalties change the algorithm and require separate analysis.
  • The objective is generally nonconvex jointly in W and H. Descent, bounded objective values and an auxiliary-function proof do not establish unique factors, a global optimum or the source discussion’s unrestricted local-optimum guarantee.
  • Finite arithmetic verifies selected update and boundary claims; no author software, empirical model fitting or benchmark was executed.
