IECO constrained elliptical-mixture classification

Algorithms AIMech:IECOConstrainedMixtureClassification DRAFT
A constrained classification procedure that models each known class by a mixture of elliptical category distributions, marginalizes category likelihoods, and assigns independent observations to distinct class slots by minimizing negative log mixture costs together with specified missing-slot prior costs.

This record is the supplied-density IECO constrained virtual-slot assignment procedure, with categories marginalized before MAP of Phi. It is distinct from POP/BAS/OUT parameter fitting, independent per-object Gaussian QDA/LDA, Naive Bayes feature independence, SeLa learned pseudo-label transport, and iterative conditional estimation. The 1995 singleton-category fixed-count setting is contextual lineage, not an exact alias; the 1996 maximum-over-quality rule differs from the 1997 category sum. Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #category-marginalized-class-density
For each class slot j, provide category densities f_jk with nonnegative category priors p_jk summing to one. Under the stated object/category independence assumptions, the class likelihood for observation x_i is sum_k p_jk*f_jk(x_i). IECO specializes these laws to elliptically symmetric regular and outlier distributions; its radial-family choices are model inputs. The observation-to-class cost is the negative natural logarithm of that mixture sum.
  • supports Source: Outliers in Statistical Pattern Recognition and an Application to Automatic Chromosome Classification · Locator: PDF pp.7–9, Sections3.1–3.4, Eqs2–3 and10; IECO naming paragraph
Mechanism #injective-assignment-and-missing-slots
For m observations and n class slots with m<=n, require an injective assignment of observations to slots. With a missing-subset prior proportional to the product of alpha_j over missing slots and an independent uniform permutation prior, append n−m dummy rows of costs −log(alpha_j). Minimize the total n-by-n assignment cost. Repeated slots can encode known class multiplicities; the generic linear-assignment solver is a component, not the classifier identity.
  • supports Source: Outliers in Statistical Pattern Recognition and an Application to Automatic Chromosome Classification · Locator: PDF pp.7–9, Sections3.1–3.4, Eqs4–11; PDF pp.10–12, Section4 application construction
Scope #missing-weight-interpretation
General alpha_j values are nonnegative subset weights with a normalizing constant at fixed m. In the independent-coin construction they are failure odds g_j/(1−g_j), not generally the marginal probabilities that classes are absent. Only the normalized m=n−1 specialization identifies alpha_j with the probability that slot j is missing.
  • supports Source: Outliers in Statistical Pattern Recognition and an Application to Automatic Chromosome Classification · Locator: PDF p.8, Section3.3, Eqs4–7
Scope #class-versus-category-decision
The decision maximizes the posterior of Φ, the injective assignment to indexed virtual class slots, after marginalizing categories. It does not universally maximize the posterior of a biological labeling obtained by merging equivalent slots or category-dependent identities; that target requires aggregating posterior mass over its compatible realizations. Choosing a maximum-posterior category after the slot assignment also does not in general give the joint slot-and-category MAP decision. The 1996 abstract’s minimum-over-quality scoring therefore cannot be substituted for the 1997 mixture-sum costs.
  • supports Source: Outliers in Statistical Pattern Recognition and an Application to Automatic Chromosome Classification · Locator: PDF p.9, Section3.4, concluding category-estimator paragraph
  • context Source: Outlier Treatment: A New Statistical Method for Automatic Chromosome Classification · Locator: Rendered abstract PDF p.2, minimum-over-quality score before classification
Scope #iec-lineage-special-case
With one category per class and fixed class multiplicities, the1997 construction includes the earlier independent elliptical classifier setting. The1995 cancellation of class-dependent determinant factors relies on fixed multiplicities; it does not justify dropping those factors when comparing different missing-class or count hypotheses. The generic1997 mixed-class construction supports broader densities, while this proposed IECO record retains the authors’ elliptical specialization.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The MAP interpretation concerns Φ, the injective indexed-slot variable of Section 3, conditional on valid normalized component laws, category priors, object independence, the specified missing-subset product prior, an independent uniform permutation prior and feasible slot constraints. Equivalence to MAP over coarser biological labels requires a separate aggregation argument; exchangeable fixed-multiplicity cases have constant representation counts. This general boundary does not establish failure of the paper’s specific 45-chromosome construction. It does not guarantee correct biological labels or calibrated future error.
  • Positive mixture likelihoods and positive weights give finite costs. Zero likelihoods or zero missing weights require infinite-cost constraints; an all-infeasible assignment has no finite optimum and is not silently made valid.
  • The source’s generic section3 permits multiple category types, while IECO denotes the elliptical regular/outlier specialization. A chromosome-specific construction, a general assignment solver and every finite-mixture classifier are not exact aliases.
  • The author-hosted1997 PDF p.11 Eq13 displays a density symbol equated to an expression with the form of a negative log cost, and its radial argument differs from the explicit norm form in the1996 abstract. The proposal uses the valid supplied-density construction of section3, does not certify Eq13 literally and does not silently repair it. No defect in p.9 Eq9 was established.
  • The1995 Pareto radial family has a support cutoff; an unrestricted log-distance score omitting that cutoff is not asserted equivalent to its literal likelihood. This predecessor boundary is retained rather than imported into IECO as an unqualified formula.
  • The1996 two-page conference abstract is context only and its maximum-over-quality decision differs from the1997 sum-over-category rule. The earlier cutREG notation is not used to patch the1997 fitting text.
  • Complete numerical reproduction needs component fitting, cutoff calibration, category priors, covariance treatment, missing-slot conventions and a solver implementation. No author code, historical benchmark, universal complexity or numerical performance advantage is claimed.
  • The three source artifacts are exact author-hosted versions. Journal publication metadata identifies the works but does not establish equivalence to publisher PDF bytes. Source-version comparison remains a retained question.
