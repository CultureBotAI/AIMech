Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #linear-static-linear
For zero-state linear operators R and S and a memoryless function f, the response is y=S(f(Ru)). The first block mixes input history, f acts pointwise on the resulting internal signal, and S filters that nonlinear signal. With causal finite filters R=(1,2), S=(1,3), inputs (1,2,-1), zero prehistory and f(v)=v², the first three outputs are (1,19,57). This is an exact curator example of the specified composition, not a fitted benchmark.

Scope #two-block-special-cases
An identity input block R gives the Hammerstein order, and an identity output block S gives the Wiener order. If f is the identity, the cascade reduces to the linear composition SR. The families therefore overlap through degenerate or linear special cases; their different ordered structures are not interchangeable names, and a three-block representation need not be minimal.

Limitation #internal-gain-ambiguity
For nonzero a and b in the zero-state deterministic formulation, replacing R by aR, f(v) by b*f(v/a), and S by S/b leaves the external response unchanged on the corresponding signal domains. The finite example with a=2,b=3 still gives (1,19,57). External data alone therefore cannot fix these two internal scale choices without normalization or other information; this is not a complete classification of all ambiguities.

Limitation #linear-approximation-boundary
The source’s Gaussian best-linear approximation exposes a scalar multiple of the product RS rather than uniquely assigning dynamics to R and S. An informative nonzero gain is an additional requirement: non-evenness of f alone does not suffice. For iid standard Gaussian input, R=(1,2) gives internal variance 5, and f(q)=q³-15q has zero first input/output correlations despite being a nonconstant odd function. The curator counterexample qualifies the source text after Theorem 1. Estimating or separating blocks is a distinct identification problem.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • This maintained record selects real-valued scalar discrete-time deterministic cascades. Noise models, multivariable, parallel, feedback, time-varying and neural variants require explicit additional scope. Finite examples use causal FIR blocks and zero prehistory; general linear blocks may have infinite impulse-response memory or specified nonzero initial states.
  • A memoryless function is a single-valued map on its stated domain. Forward composition does not in itself require inverse functions, monotonicity, Gaussian inputs, polynomial parameterization or a GP prior. Any use of inverse-coordinate predictors must state the valid domain and stronger assumptions.
  • Representation, minimality, internal identifiability, stable simulation and estimation accuracy are separate questions. Internal gain normalizations alone do not settle all ambiguities; overlapping special cases do not establish family equivalence.
  • The source’s phase-coupled-multisine initialization method has separate excitation, odd-term, steady-state and noise assumptions. Its higher-degree combinatorial formulas need a separate audit; they are not used as model-definition evidence. The reported electronics experiment was not replicated.
  • All pages of the cited version were visually read. No model was trained, no empirical result was replicated and no paid provider was run. This record remains DRAFT; author self-review is not independent scientific approval.
