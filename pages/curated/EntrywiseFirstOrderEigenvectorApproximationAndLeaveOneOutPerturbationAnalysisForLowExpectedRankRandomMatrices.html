Entrywise First-Order Eigenvector Approximation and Leave-One-Out Perturbation Analysis for Low-Expected-Rank Random Matrices

Aliases: Abbe-Fan-Wang-Zhong Entrywise Eigenvector Analysis, First-Order Entrywise Eigenvector Approximation via Leave-One-Out Perturbation, Entrywise Eigenvector Analysis of Random Matrices with Low Expected Rank, Leave-One-Out Entrywise Eigenvector Perturbation Method, First-Order Spectral Approximation for Sparse SBM Exact Recovery
A probabilistic entrywise ($\ell_\infty$ and $2 \to \infty$ norm) eigenvector and eigenspace perturbation framework for symmetric random matrices $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with low-rank expectation $\mathbf{A}^* = \mathbb{E}\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{U}^*\boldsymbol{\Lambda}^*(\mathbf{U}^*)^T$ (introduced by Emmanuel Abbe, Jianqing Fan, Kaizheng Wang, and Yiqiao Zhong in 2020). Rather than bounding the direct entrywise difference $\|\mathbf{u}_k - \mathbf{u}_k^*\|_\infty$ via the deterministic matrix $\ell_\infty$ operator norm $\|\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{A}^*\|_\infty$, the method establishes the first-order linear entrywise approximation $\mathbf{u}_k \approx \mathbf{A}\mathbf{u}_k^* / \lambda_k^* = \mathbf{u}_k^* + (\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{A}^*)\mathbf{u}_k^* / \lambda_k^*$ and, for a general rank-$r$ principal subspace $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times r}$ with eigengap $\Delta^*$, the polar-aligned $2 \to \infty$ approximation $\|\mathbf{U}\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H}) - \mathbf{A}\mathbf{U}^*(\boldsymbol{\Lambda}^*)^{-1}\|_{2\to\infty} \lesssim (\kappa(\gamma + \varphi(1/\sqrt{n})) + \varphi(\gamma)/\bar{\gamma})\|\mathbf{U}^*\|_{2\to\infty}$ where $\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H}) = \bar{\mathbf{U}}\bar{\mathbf{V}}^T$ is the matrix sign function of $\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{U}^T\mathbf{U}^*$ (Theorems 1.1 and 2.1, Corollary 2.1). The proof introduces $n$ auxiliary leave-one-out matrices $[\mathbf{A}^{(m)}]_{ij} = A_{ij}\mathbf{1}_{\{i \ne m, j \ne m\}}$ ($m=1,\dots,n$) whose leading eigenspace $\mathbf{U}^{(m)}$ is statistically independent of the $m$-th row $\mathbf{A}_{m\cdot}$ under row-and-column independence (Assumption A2), decoupling the row-concentration functional $\|(\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{A}^*)_{m\cdot}\mathbf{W}\|_2 \le \Delta^*\|\mathbf{W}\|_{2\to\infty}\varphi(\|\mathbf{W}\|_F / (\sqrt{n}\|\mathbf{W}\|_{2\to\infty}))$ (Assumption A3, Appendix A.1, Lemmas 1–5). Applied to $\mathbb{Z}_2$-synchronization, the Two-Block Stochastic Block Model $\mathrm{SBM}(n, a\log n/n, b\log n/n, J)$, and noisy matrix completion, this first-order entrywise expansion proves that unregularized spectral clustering $\widehat{\mathbf{z}} = \operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{u}_2)$ achieves exact recovery down to the information-theoretic threshold $\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b} > \sqrt{2}$ without trimming or cleaning, even in regimes where Theorem 3.3 proves $\sqrt{n}\|\mathbf{u}_2 - \mathbf{u}_2^*\|_\infty > 1$.

Distinct from `AIMech:LInfinityEigenvectorPerturbationBoundAndRobustFactorCovarianceEstimation` (Fan, Wang, & Zhong 2018, which is a purely deterministic perturbation bound in terms of the matrix $\ell_\infty$ operator norm $\|\mathbf{E}\|_\infty = \max_i \sum_j |E_{ij}|$ requiring $O(r^{5/2}\mu^{3/2}\|\mathbf{E}\|_\infty / (\gamma_0\sqrt{d}))$ and cannot explain exact recovery in sparse logarithmic-degree SBMs where $\|\mathbf{u}_2 - \mathbf{u}_2^*\|_\infty > 1/\sqrt{n}$), and from `AIMech:SCORESpectralClusteringOnRatiosOfEigenvectorsForDegreeCorrectedBlockModels` (Jin 2015, which normalizes eigenvectors row-wise by the leading eigenvector to remove degree heterogeneity in denser graphs rather than establishing leave-one-out first-order entrywise approximations $\mathbf{u}_k \approx \mathbf{A}\mathbf{u}_k^* / \lambda_k^*$ at the $\log n / n$ threshold).

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-first-order-entrywise-eigenspace-approximation-and-leave-one-out
For a symmetric random matrix $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with expectation $\mathbf{A}^* = \mathbb{E}\mathbf{A}$ having leading rank-$r$ eigenspace $\mathbf{U}^*\boldsymbol{\Lambda}^*(\mathbf{U}^*)^T$, eigengap $\Delta^* = (\lambda_r^* \wedge |\lambda_{r_0+r-1}^*|) - \max_{r_0 \le j < r}|\lambda_j^*|$, condition number $\kappa = \max(|\lambda_1^*|, |\lambda_n^*|)/\Delta^*$, and row-wise concentration function $\varphi(t)$ under row-and-column independence (Assumptions A1–A4), Theorem 2.1 proves that with probability at least $1 - \delta$, the polar-aligned sample eigenspace $\mathbf{U}\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H})$ (where $\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H}) = \bar{\mathbf{U}}\bar{\mathbf{V}}^T$ is the matrix sign of $\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{U}^T\mathbf{U}^*$) satisfies the first-order linear approximation $\|\mathbf{U}\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H}) - \mathbf{A}\mathbf{U}^*(\boldsymbol{\Lambda}^*)^{-1}\|_{2\to\infty} \lesssim [\kappa(\gamma + \varphi(1/\sqrt{n})) + \varphi(\gamma)/\bar{\gamma}]\|\mathbf{U}^*\|_{2\to\infty}$ by constructing $n$ auxiliary leave-one-out matrices $[\mathbf{A}^{(m)}]_{ij} = A_{ij}\mathbf{1}_{\{i \ne m, j \ne m\}}$ whose eigenspace $\mathbf{U}^{(m)}$ is independent of the $m$-th row $\mathbf{A}_{m\cdot}$ and satisfies $\|\mathbf{U}^{(m)}\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H}^{(m)}) - \mathbf{U}\operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{H})\|_F \lesssim (\kappa + \gamma)\|\mathbf{U}\|_{2\to\infty}$ (Section 2.2, Theorem 2.1, Corollary 2.1, Appendix A.1, and Appendix B Lemmas 1–5).

Mechanism #claim-sbm-exact-recovery-threshold-and-theorem-3-3-linf-impossibility
In the two-block Stochastic Block Model $\mathrm{SBM}(n, a\log n / n, b\log n / n, J)$ with $a > b > 0$ and ground-truth labels $\mathbf{z} \in \{\pm 1\}^n$, applying Theorem 1.1 with $\varphi(t) \propto (1 \vee \log(1/t))^{-1}$ proves that the unregularized spectral estimator $\widehat{\mathbf{z}} = \operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{u}_2)$ achieves exact recovery $\mathbb{P}(\min_{s \in \{\pm 1\}}\|\widehat{\mathbf{z}} - s\mathbf{z}\|_\infty = 0) = 1 - o(1)$ whenever $\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b} > \sqrt{2}$ (matching the information-theoretic maximum likelihood threshold and settling the conjecture of Abbe et al. 2014b) and achieves the minimax exponential misclassification rate $\mathbb{E}r(\widehat{\mathbf{z}}, \mathbf{z}) \le n^{-(1+o(1))(\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b})^2/2}$ when $\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b} \in (0, \sqrt{2}]$ (Corollary 1.1, Theorem 3.2); moreover, Theorem 3.3 and Corollary 3.1 prove that when $a+b > 2$ and $(a+b-2)^2 < 4ab$, $\sqrt{n}\|\mathbf{u}_2 - \mathbf{u}_2^*\|_\infty \ge a(\eta - 1)/(a - b) - \varepsilon$ with probability $1 - o(1)$ (where $\eta \in (1, 2)$ solves $\eta\log\eta - \eta + 1 = 2/(a+b)$), which exceeds $1$ for sparse regimes above the threshold, demonstrating that direct $\|\mathbf{u}_2 - \mathbf{u}_2^*\|_\infty < 1/\sqrt{n}$ bounds fail and the first-order approximation $\mathbf{u}_2 \approx \mathbf{A}\mathbf{u}_2^* / \lambda_2^*$ is essential.
  • supports Source: Entrywise Eigenvector Analysis of Random Matrices with Low Expected Rank · Locator: Section 1.2 (Corollary 1.1, p. 6), Section 3.2 (Theorems 3.2-3.3, Corollary 3.1, pp. 15-19), Appendix C.2 (Lemmas 6-18, pp. 36-50)
Mechanism #claim-z2-synchronization-and-noisy-matrix-completion-entrywise-rates
In Gaussian $\mathbb{Z}_2$-synchronization $\mathbf{Y} = \mathbf{x}\mathbf{x}^T + \sigma\mathbf{W}$ with $\mathbf{x} \in \{\pm 1\}^n$ and symmetric Gaussian Wigner noise $\mathbf{W}$, Theorem 3.1 shows that the leading eigenvector satisfies $\min_{s \in \{\pm 1\}}\|s\mathbf{u}_1 - \mathbf{Y}\mathbf{x}/(n\sqrt{n})\|_\infty = O((\sigma^2\log n / n + \sigma\sqrt{\log n} / n) / \sqrt{n})$ with probability $1 - O(n^{-1})$, so $\widehat{\mathbf{x}} = \operatorname{sgn}(\mathbf{u}_1)$ achieves exact recovery down to the information-theoretic threshold $\sigma \le \sqrt{n / ((2+\varepsilon)\log n)}$ (Section 3.1, Theorem 3.1); for noisy low-rank matrix completion of $\mathbf{M}^* = \mathbf{U}^*\boldsymbol{\Sigma}^*(\mathbf{V}^*)^T \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_2}$ observed with probability $p$ and noise variance $\sigma^2$, symmetric dilation into an $(n_1+n_2) \times (n_1+n_2)$ random matrix yields the entrywise error bound $\|\mathbf{M} - \mathbf{M}^*\|_{\max} \lesssim (1 + \sigma/\sigma_{\min}^*)\|\mathbf{M}^*\|_{\max}\sqrt{r(n_1+n_2)\log(n_1+n_2) / (p n_1 n_2)}$ (Section 3.3, Theorem 3.4, Appendix C.3).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The leave-one-out decoupling in Assumption A2 requires that for each index m in [n], the entries in the m-th row and column {A_{ij} : i = m or j = m} are statistically independent of the remaining submatrix {A_{ij} : i != m, j != m}; when matrix entries exhibit strong spatial or temporal dependence across rows and columns (such as sample covariance matrices formed from n << p dependent observations without independent entries), the row-and-column leave-one-out independence breaks down and requires sample-splitting or observation-level leave-one-out constructions.
  • Theorem 2.1 requires the signal eigengap Condition A4 (Delta^* >= 4(1 + bar{gamma})||A - A^*||_2 and kappa * gamma <= 1/32); in ultra-sparse constant-degree graphs where p ~ c/n and max degrees grow as log n / log log n without spectral concentration of A - A^*, high-degree vertices localize the top eigenvectors of A and violate Assumption A4 unless degrees are regularized or trimmed.
  • Primary-source typographical audit of Abbe, Fan, Wang, & Zhong (2020, arXiv:1709.09565v2) uncovered five groups of minor typographical errors that are corrected in this record: (1) Section 1 p. 2 line 18 writes 'This is party because' ('party' instead of 'partly'); (2) Section 2.1 p. 10 line 18 spells 'an eigen-gap Delta^* seperating' ('seperating' instead of 'separating'); (3) Section 3.2 p. 16 line 11 writes 'as soon as the signal strength sqrt(a) - sqrt(b) exceed' ('exceed' instead of 'exceeds') and line 26 writes 'whenever it is information-theoretic possible' ('information-theoretic' instead of 'information-theoretically'); (4) Section 3.3 p. 20 line 12 spells 'spectral intialization' ('intialization'), p. 21 line 6 writes 'better portrait the behavior' ('portrait' instead of 'portray'), Section 4.2 p. 22 line 1 writes 'report the the proportion' (duplicate 'the'), and Fig. 6 caption spells 'phase transtion'; and (5) Appendix A.1 p. 26 line 3 writes 'the mth row A_{m.} and u^{(m)} - u^* are independence' ('independence' instead of 'independent'), Appendix B.1 p. 29 line 14 writes 'This will soon leads to' ('leads' instead of 'lead'), Appendix C.2 p. 36 line 18 writes 'adapt our proofs slighted' ('slighted' instead of 'slightly'), and Appendix C.4 p. 50 line 8 writes 'The Lemma 19 below' and p. 51 line 3 spells 'Without loss of geneality'.
