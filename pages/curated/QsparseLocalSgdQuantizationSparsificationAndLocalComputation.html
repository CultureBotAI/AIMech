Qsparse-local-SGD (Distributed SGD with Quantization, Sparsification, and Local Computations)

Algorithms AIMech:QsparseLocalSgdQuantizationSparsificationAndLocalComputation DRAFT
Aliases: Qsparse-local-SGD, Quantized Sparsified Local Stochastic Gradient Descent, Error-Compensated Composed Quantizer-Sparsifier Local SGD
A unified distributed and decentralized optimization algorithm (Basu et al. 2019, Algorithms 1-2) that combines local multi-step SGD updates over synchronization periods of length H >= 1, composite gradient compression via a composed k-sparsifier Comp_k and s-level stochastic quantizer Q_s rescaled as Q_s(Comp_k(x)) / (1 + beta_{k,s}), and local error-memory compensation m_{t+H}^{(r)} = m_t^{(r)} + Delta x_t^{(r)} - Q(m_t^{(r)} + Delta x_t^{(r)}) across both synchronous and asynchronous parameter-server and decentralized topologies.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from uncompressed Local SGD (AIMech:LocalSgdPeriodicAveragingParallelStochasticGradientDescent, Stich 2019), which performs periodic averaging every H steps without quantization, sparsification, or error memory, and from single-step Sparsified SGD with Error Memory (AIMech:SparsifiedSgdWithErrorMemoryForKContractiveGradientCompression) and QSGD (AIMech:QsgdQuantizedStochasticGradientDescentWithEliasCoding), neither of which composes contractive sparsification with unbiased quantization over multi-step local blocks H > 1.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r422-composed-quantizer-sparsifier-rescaled-contraction-lemma
When composing a k-contraction sparsifier Comp_k in R^d and an unbiased stochastic quantizer Q_s with relative variance E||Q_s(u) - u||_2^2 <= beta_{k,s} ||u||_2^2, the unscaled operator Q_s(Comp_k(x)) has contraction factor gamma = (1 - beta_{k,s})(k / d) which fails whenever beta_{k,s} >= 1 (Lemma 1), whereas the rescaled operator Q_s(Comp_k(x)) / (1 + beta_{k,s}) (Lemma 2, Corollary 1) is unconditionally a gamma-contraction for all beta_{k,s} > 0 with strictly larger contraction coefficient gamma = k / (d (1 + beta_{k,s})) > (1 - beta_{k,s})(k / d).
  • supports Source: Qsparse-local-SGD: Distributed SGD with Quantization, Sparsification, and Local Computations · Locator: Section 2 Contraction Operators (Lemmas 1-3, Corollary 1) & Appendix A
Mechanism #r422-block-error-memory-bound-and-nonconvex-strongly-convex-rates
Under synchronization period H and contraction parameter gamma, the local error memory satisfies E||m_t^{(r)}||_2^2 <= 4 (1 - gamma^2) / gamma^2 * eta^2 H^2 G^2 (Lemma 4), yielding smooth non-convex convergence at rate O(1 / sqrt(R T)) + O(H^2 / (gamma^2 T)) (Theorem 1, Corollary 2) and strongly convex convergence at rate O(1 / (R T)) + O(H^2 / (gamma^2 T^2)) (Theorem 2).
  • supports Source: Qsparse-local-SGD: Distributed SGD with Quantization, Sparsification, and Local Computations · Locator: Section 4 Main Results (Theorems 1-6, Corollaries 2-3) & Appendix B (Lemma 4, Proofs of Theorems 1-2)
Limitation #r422-second-moment-gradient-bound-and-quadratic-h-gamma-penalty-limitation
Relies on a uniform second-moment bound E||g(x)||_2^2 <= G^2 to bound the block error accumulator m_t^{(r)}, and the higher-order error term scales quadratically with the ratio H / gamma.
  • supports Source: Qsparse-local-SGD: Distributed SGD with Quantization, Sparsification, and Local Computations · Locator: Section 4 Assumptions & Theorem 1-2 Remarks
Application #r422-resnet50-imagenet-and-cifar10-joint-compression-application
Applied to distributed training of ResNet-50 on ImageNet and ResNet-20/VGG-11 on CIFAR-10, combining Top-k sparsification, 2-bit/4-bit QSGD quantization, and H = 4-8 local SGD steps to achieve up to 1000x communication reduction.
  • supports Source: Qsparse-local-SGD: Distributed SGD with Quantization, Sparsification, and Local Computations · Locator: Section 5 Experiments (Figures 1-4), pp. 14675-14678

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires bounded stochastic gradient second moments E||g(x)||_2^2 <= G^2 to bound local error memory across H local steps.
  • Aggressive joint sparsification and quantization (small gamma) combined with large H increases the transient O(H^2 / (gamma^2 T)) error term.
