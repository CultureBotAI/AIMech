EF-BV Unified Error Feedback and Variance Reduction with Bias-Variance Decomposition

Algorithms AIMech:EfBvUnifiedErrorFeedbackAndVarianceReductionWithBiasVarianceDecomposition DRAFT
Aliases: EF-BV, Error Feedback with Bias-Variance Decomposition, EF-BV Distributed Compressed Gradient Method
A unified distributed compressed proximal gradient algorithm (Algorithm 1) operating over the two-parameter bias-variance compressor class C(eta, omega) (where ||E[C(x)] - x|| <= eta ||x|| and E[||C(x) - E[C(x)]||^2] <= omega ||x||^2, with parallel average variance omega_av <= omega) that decouples the worker control-variate scaling parameter lambda in h_i^{t+1} = h_i^t + lambda C_i^t(nabla f_i(x^t) - h_i^t) from the master gradient-estimate scaling parameter nu in g^{t+1} = h^t + nu (1/n) sum_i C_i^t(nabla f_i(x^t) - h_i^t), recovering EF21 when nu = lambda and DIANA when nu = 1 while achieving faster convergence with optimal lambda^* = min((1 - eta)/((1 - eta)^2 + omega), 1) and nu^* = min((1 - eta)/((1 - eta)^2 + omega_av), 1).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from standalone DIANA (Mishchenko et al., 2019; restricted to unbiased compressors eta = 0 with nu = 1) and standalone EF21 (Richtarik et al., NeurIPS 2021; restricted to nu = lambda and unable to exploit 1/n parallel variance reduction when omega_av = omega/n < omega). EF-BV (Condat, Yi, & Richtarik, NeurIPS 2022, arXiv:2205.04180) unifies both mechanisms and introduces the C(eta, omega) compressor class including mix-(k, k') and comp-(k, k').

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #ef-bv-bias-variance-compressor-class-and-dual-scaling-parameters-mechanism
For compressors C_i^t in C(eta, omega) with relative bias eta in [0, 1), individual variance omega >= 0, and parallel aggregated variance omega_av <= omega (Equation 6), EF-BV (Algorithm 1) updates each worker's control variate as h_i^{t+1} = h_i^t + lambda d_i^t with d_i^t = C_i^t(nabla f_i(x^t) - h_i^t) and forms the master gradient estimator g^{t+1} = h^t + nu d^t before taking x^{t+1} = prox_{gamma R}(x^t - gamma g^{t+1}), where Proposition 2 yields optimal contraction factors r = (1 - lambda^* + lambda^* eta)^2 + (lambda^*)^2 omega and r_av = (1 - nu^* + nu^* eta)^2 + (nu^*)^2 omega_av.
  • supports Source: EF-BV: A Unified Theory of Error Feedback and Variance Reduction Mechanisms for Biased and Unbiased Compression in Distributed Optimization · Locator: Section 2.3-2.5 Proposition 1-2 pp. 4-6 & Section 3 Algorithm 1 p. 7
Scope #ef-bv-kurdyka-lojasiewicz-linear-and-nonconvex-sublinear-convergence-scope
Under the Polyak-Lojasiewicz or Kurdyka-Lojasiewicz condition with constant mu > 0 (Theorems 1-2), EF-BV with step size gamma = 1 / (L + tilde{L} sqrt(r_av / r) / s^*) (or 2L in the composite case) achieves linear convergence E[Psi^t] <= (max(1 - gamma mu, (r+1)/2))^t Psi^0, improving over EF21's complexity O((tilde{L}/mu)(1/(1-r)) log(1/eps)) by the parallel variance reduction factor sqrt(r_av / r) in Eq. (12), and achieves O(1/t) stationarity in smooth nonconvex optimization (Theorem 3).
  • supports Source: EF-BV: A Unified Theory of Error Feedback and Variance Reduction Mechanisms for Biased and Unbiased Compression in Distributed Optimization · Locator: Section 4 Theorems 1-2 & Remark 3 Eq. (12) p. 8, Section 5 Theorem 3 p. 10
  • supports Source: EF-BV: A Unified Theory of Error Feedback and Variance Reduction Mechanisms for Biased and Unbiased Compression in Distributed Optimization (arXiv abstract) · Locator: Abstract
Limitation #ef-bv-strictly-less-than-unit-relative-bias-and-full-worker-participation-limitation
Requires the compressor relative bias to satisfy eta < 1 (since scaling lambda in (0, 1] reduces variance quadratically as lambda^2 omega but cannot reduce relative bias below eta) and maintains a d-dimensional control variate h_i^t at every worker.
  • supports Source: EF-BV: A Unified Theory of Error Feedback and Variance Reduction Mechanisms for Biased and Unbiased Compression in Distributed Optimization · Locator: Section 2.3 p. 4, Section 2.5 Proposition 1-2 pp. 5-6 & Algorithm 1 p. 7
Application #ef-bv-distributed-optimization-with-hybrid-comp-and-mix-compressors-application
Applied to distributed convex and nonconvex regularized logistic regression using hybrid biased-and-random compressors such as comp-(k, k') (composition of Top-k' and Rand-k) and mix-(k, k') (mixture of Top-k and Rand-k').
  • supports Source: EF-BV: A Unified Theory of Error Feedback and Variance Reduction Mechanisms for Biased and Unbiased Compression in Distributed Optimization · Locator: Section 6 p. 10 & Appendices A & C Propositions 4-5 pp. 15-18

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires compressor relative bias eta < 1 since linear pre-scaling cannot reduce bias below eta.
  • Each worker must store and update a full d-dimensional control variate h_i^t across iterations.
