Generalized Alternating Minimization

Algorithms AIMech:GeneralizedAlternatingMinimization DRAFT
Aliases: GAM (generalized alternating minimization)
An information-geometric fitting framework that updates a generating distribution constrained by the observations and model parameters to decrease their joint Kullback-Leibler objective, allowing inexact distribution and parameter minimization.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the Gunawardana-Byrne distribution/parameter KL-descent framework. In its alternating realization both coordinate updates decrease the same paired objective; the general composite formulation can establish overall descent directly. Exact EM and suitable generalized EM are special cases. GAM does not mean every approximate E-step, every alternating loss or the unrelated generalized additive model. Restricted-family variational EM is a specialization, while cyclic incremental EM has a separate cache/sweep realization. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #paired-descent
Let D contain normalized generating laws supported on the complete-data fiber compatible with the observed event, and let P_theta be the normalized complete-data model. Minimize D_KL(Q||P_theta) over the pair. A generalized forward step reduces it in Q at fixed theta; a generalized backward step reduces it in theta at the updated Q. Exact forward minimization over the unrestricted desired family gives the current conditional posterior, and exact backward minimization maximizes expected complete-data log likelihood. A constrained family changes the optimization problem. Finite, continuous KL on the relevant domain is an explicit premise here.
  • supports Source: Convergence Theorems for Generalized Alternating Minimization Procedures · Locator: Sections2.1-2.2, pp2052-2054 / PDF4-6; Eq4 and Figure2
Scope #conditional-limit-set
The inspected convergence theorem requires compact parameter space, an eventually entered compact invariant generating-distribution set, a closed update map, continuous finite objective, nonincrease, and strict decrease outside the set of joint coordinatewise global minimizers. It concludes objective convergence and membership of accumulation points in that set, not necessarily convergence of the whole parameter sequence. For the unrestricted desired family, additional interior differentiability conditions give stationary observed-likelihood accumulation points; stationary does not mean a local maximum. Separate coordinate descent tests need extra hypotheses, such as the unique forward minimizer in Proposition5, to establish the composite equality condition.
  • supports Source: Convergence Theorems for Generalized Alternating Minimization Procedures · Locator: Section3 Definition1, Theorems2-3, Corollary4 and Proposition5, pp2058-2062 / PDF10-14; AppendicesA-B, PDF20-24
Limitation #surrogate-versus-likelihood
D_KL(Q||P_theta)=D_KL(Q||P_theta(.|observed))-log p_theta(observed). Decreasing the paired divergence may lower observed likelihood when the posterior approximation gap also changes. The paper’s constrained two-coin example moves theta from0.3 to0.4, reducing likelihood from0.58 to0.52 and reaching a restricted fixed point that is not likelihood-stationary. Even exact EM can stay at the likelihood minimum theta=0.5 in that example.

Limitation #domain-and-source-conditions
Positive common support alone does not ensure finite KL on an arbitrary countably infinite state space. Continuous-observation extensions also need suitable measures and regularity. The incremental-EM argument applies to the inspected finite cyclic full sweep with unique admissible cached M minimizers and the theorem’s compactness/closedness hypotheses, not every random or unfair schedule. The displayed Section4 GEM comparison has its Q inequality reversed; it is not adopted as an ascent rule.
