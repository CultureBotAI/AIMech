Agnostic Federated Learning via Minimax Distributionally Robust Optimization

Algorithms AIMech:AgnosticFederatedLearningMinimaxDistributionallyRobustOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: Agnostic Federated Learning, AFL, Stochastic-AFL, Minimax Agnostic Federated Optimization
A distributionally robust federated learning framework and stochastic minimax optimization algorithm (Agnostic Federated Learning, AFL) that replaces fixed empirical sample-proportion risk minimization with the agnostic minimax objective min_{h in H} L_{D_Lambda}(h) = min_{h in H} max_{lambda in Lambda} sum_{k=1}^p lambda_k L_{D_k}(h) over a subset Lambda of the domain mixture simplex Delta_p. AFL controls generalization via the weighted Rademacher complexity R_m(G, Lambda) and the chi-squared skewness parameter s(Lambda || m_bar) = 1 + max_{lambda in Lambda} chi^2(lambda || m_bar), and optimizes the empirical saddle-point objective via Stochastic-AFL projected gradient descent-ascent w_{t+1} = Pi_W(w_t - gamma_w * delta_w L(w_t, lambda_t)), lambda_{t+1} = Pi_Lambda(lambda_t + gamma_lambda * delta_lambda L(w_t, lambda_t)) using unbiased PerDomain or Weighted stochastic gradients.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from q-Fair Federated Learning (AIMech:QFairFederatedLearningAlphaFairResourceAllocation, which minimizes a smooth alpha-fair sum of (1/(q+1)) F_k^{q+1}(w) without maintaining dual simplex mixture weights lambda in Delta_p or solving a saddle-point problem) and from standard FedAvg (AIMech:FederatedAveragingCommunicationEfficientDecentralizedDeepLearning, which minimizes the fixed empirical sample-weighted mixture sum_k (m_k / m) L_k(w) and can suffer a constant excess agnostic risk even as m_k -> infinity by Proposition 1).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r398-proposition1-uniform-mixture-excess-agnostic-risk-and-good-intent-fairness
Proposition 1 proves that even in the infinite-sample limit m_k -> infinity with equal domain weights on two binary distributions D_1(x, 1) = 1 and D_2(x, 1) = D_2(x, 0) = 1/2, the uniform-mixture predictor h_U(x) = 3/4 incurs strictly higher worst-case cross-entropy risk than the agnostic minimax predictor h_{D_Lambda}(x) = 1/2 by a constant gap (1/2) log(4/3) > 0, motivating good-intent fairness across protected or heterogeneous domains.
  • supports Source: Agnostic Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.2 Comparison with Standard Federated Learning (Proposition 1) & Section 3.3 Fairness in Learning, pp. 3-4
Mechanism #r398-skewness-weighted-rademacher-generalization-and-stochastic-afl-convergence
Theorem 2, Lemma 3, and Corollary 4 bound the uniform generalization gap between expected and empirical agnostic risk in terms of the weighted Rademacher complexity R_m(G, Lambda), the chi-squared skewness s(Lambda || m_bar) = 1 + max_{lambda in Lambda} chi^2(lambda || m_bar) = max_{lambda in Lambda} sum_{k=1}^p lambda_k^2 / m_bar_k, and an l_1 epsilon-cover Lambda_epsilon, while Theorem 5 and Lemmas 6, 8, and 9 prove O(1/sqrt(T)) saddle-point convergence for Stochastic-AFL under PerDomain and Weighted stochastic gradients.
  • supports Source: Agnostic Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 Learning Guarantees (Theorem 2, Lemma 3, Corollary 4) & Section 5 Algorithm (Theorem 5, Lemmas 6, 8, 9), pp. 4-7
Limitation #r398-large-domain-count-skewness-and-lambda-gradient-variance-limitation
When the number of domains p is large and Lambda = Delta_p is the full simplex, the skewness parameter s(Delta_p || m_bar) = max_k (m / m_k) grows inversely with the smallest client sample fraction, weakening generalization unless Lambda is constrained near m_bar or clients are grouped into clusters, and single-domain sampling for delta_lambda L has O(p M^2) variance (Lemma 6).
  • supports Source: Agnostic Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 Discussion after Lemma 3, Section 5 Lemma 6, and Section 7 Discussion, pp. 5-8
Application #r398-adult-fashion-mnist-and-language-modeling-fairness-application
Applied to federated income classification (Adult), image classification across clothing categories (Fashion-MNIST), and multi-domain language modeling (Cornell Movie + Penn Treebank), consistently reducing worst-domain test loss and perplexity compared to uniform FedAvg.
  • supports Source: Agnostic Federated Learning · Locator: Section 6 Experiments (Tables 1-3, Figure 5), pp. 7-8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Unconstrained simplex optimization over a large number of small-sample clients p inflates the skewness parameter s(Delta_p || m_bar) = max_k (m / m_k), requiring domain clustering or simplex regularization.
  • Uniform single-domain stochastic gradients for lambda have variance scaling linearly with the number of domains p (O(p M^2)).
