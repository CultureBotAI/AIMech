FedNova Normalized Averaging for Heterogeneous Federated Optimization

Algorithms AIMech:FedNovaNormalizedAveragingForHeterogeneousFederatedOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: FedNova, Federated Normalized Averaging, Normalized Averaging for Heterogeneous Federated Optimization
A heterogeneous federated optimization framework and normalized averaging algorithm (FedNova) that models client i's cumulative local model change over tau_i^{(t)} local steps as Delta_i^{(t)} = x_i^{(t,tau_i)} - x^{(t,0)} = -eta * G_i^{(t)} * a_i^{(t)}, where G_i^{(t)} in R^{d x tau_i} stacks the local stochastic gradients and a_i^{(t)} in R^{tau_i} is the local solver's gradient weighting vector (a_i = [1,...,1]^T for vanilla SGD, a_i = [(1-alpha)^{tau_i-1},...,1]^T for proximal SGD, and a_i = [1-rho^{tau_i},...,1-rho]^T/(1-rho) for momentum SGD), normalizes each client's update into an effective local gradient d_i^{(t)} = G_i^{(t)} a_i^{(t)} / ||a_i^{(t)}||_1 = -Delta_i^{(t)} / (eta * ||a_i^{(t)}||_1), and aggregates across clients with weights w_i = p_i and effective global step size tau_eff^{(t)} (defaulting to sum_{i=1}^m p_i ||a_i^{(t)}||_1) via x^{(t+1,0)} = x^{(t,0)} - tau_eff^{(t)} * sum_{i=1}^m p_i * eta * d_i^{(t)}, eliminating the surrogate objective inconsistency chi^2_{p||w} = sum_{i=1}^m (p_i - w_i)^2 / w_i caused by heterogeneous local step counts tau_i or heterogeneous local solvers while achieving O(1/sqrt(m * tau_tilde * T)) convergence to stationary points of the true global objective F(x) = sum_{i=1}^m p_i F_i(x).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from FedProx (AIMech:FedProxProximalHeteratedOptimization / AIMech:FedProxProximalHeterogeneousFederatedOptimization, which adds a proximal penalty (mu/2)||x - x^{(t,0)}||^2 to local objectives but still suffers from surrogate objective inconsistency w_i proportional to p_i ||a_i||_1 when tau_i varies across clients, as proven in Wang et al. 2020 Section 4.3), from SCAFFOLD (AIMech:ScaffoldStochasticControlledAveragingForFederatedLearning, which transmits control variate shifts c_i and requires equal local step counts), and from Local SGD (AIMech:LocalSgdPeriodicAveragingParallelStochasticGradientDescent). No pinned AIO class covers FedNova or objective-inconsistency-eliminating normalized federated averaging.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r386-surrogate-objective-inconsistency-theorem
Theorem 1 and Corollary 1 prove that any federated algorithm aggregating unnormalized local updates with weights w_i proportional to p_i ||a_i||_1 converges to a stationary point of a mismatched surrogate objective F_tilde(x) = sum_{i=1}^m w_i F_i(x) with w_i = p_i ||a_i||_1 / sum_j p_j ||a_j||_1, and Theorem 2 combined with Lemma 4 shows that on strongly convex quadratic local losses F_i(x) = (1/2) x^T H_i x - e_i^T x, standard FedAvg converges as eta -> 0 to x_inf^{FedAvg} = (sum_i p_i tau_i H_i)^{-1} (sum_i p_i tau_i e_i) != x* with a non-vanishing lower bound min_{t in [T]} E||nabla F(x^{(t,0)})||^2 = Omega(chi^2_{p||w} kappa^2 / (chi^2_{p||w} beta^2 + 1)).
  • supports Source: Tackling the Objective Inconsistency Problem in Heterogeneous Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 4.1-4.2 Theorem 1, Corollary 1, Theorem 2, and Appendix B Lemmas 3-4, pp. 4-6, 15-17
Mechanism #r386-normalized-averaging-stationarity-convergence
By normalizing each client's cumulative update by ||a_i^{(t)}||_1 (where ||a_i||_1 = tau_i for vanilla SGD, (1 - (1-alpha)^{tau_i})/alpha for proximal SGD, and (tau_i - rho(1-rho^{tau_i})/(1-rho))/(1-rho) for momentum SGD) and setting w_i = p_i, FedNova achieves exact surrogate consistency (chi^2_{p||w} = 0) and Theorem 3 / Corollary 3 guarantees (1/T) sum_{t=0}^{T-1} E||nabla F(x^{(t,0)})||^2 = O(1/sqrt(m * tau_tilde * T) + m * tau_tilde / T) for L-smooth nonconvex objectives.
  • supports Source: Tackling the Objective Inconsistency Problem in Heterogeneous Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 4.3, Section 5 Theorem 3 & Corollary 3, pp. 6-8
Limitation #r386-local-step-size-and-drift-limitation
While FedNova eliminates the O(chi^2_{p||w}) objective inconsistency term as eta -> 0, Theorem 3 requires local step sizes to satisfy eta * L * max_i ||a_i||_1 <= O(1) so that client-drift variance O(eta^2 L^2 ||a_i||_2^2) remains controlled when local step counts tau_i are large.
  • supports Source: Tackling the Objective Inconsistency Problem in Heterogeneous Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 5 Theorem 3 condition (12) and Lemma 4 finite-eta limit (19), pp. 7, 16
Application #r386-heterogeneous-epoch-federated-training-application
Applied to federated training of VGG-11 and ResNet-18 on non-IID Dirichlet-partitioned CIFAR-10 and synthetic quadratic benchmarks under heterogeneous client local epochs E_i and straggler drop-offs, outperforming FedAvg and FedProx while supporting local momentum and proximal solvers.
  • supports Source: Tackling the Objective Inconsistency Problem in Heterogeneous Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 6 Experimental Results, Table 2 & Figures 3-4, pp. 8-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires each participating client i to report or allow computation of its effective local step weight norm ||a_i^{(t)}||_1 alongside its model update.
  • Eliminates objective inconsistency as eta -> 0 but still incurs O(eta^2 L^2 ||a_i||_2^2) client drift at finite step sizes unless combined with variance reduction.
