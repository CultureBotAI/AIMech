diffGrad

Algorithms AIMech:DiffGrad DRAFT
Aliases: Difference of Gradients Adaptive Optimization, Dynamic Friction Sigmoid Adaptive Moment Estimation, Short-Term Gradient-Change Clamped Adam
A first-order adaptive stochastic gradient optimization algorithm that modulates Adam's bias-corrected parameter update by an element-wise diffGrad Friction Coefficient (DFC) xi_{t,i} = 1 / (1 + exp(-|Delta g_{t,i}|)) in [0.5, 1) driven by the first-order backward difference of consecutive gradients Delta g_{t,i} = g_{t-1,i} - g_{t,i}, updating parameters via theta_{t,i} = theta_{t-1,i} - (alpha_t * xi_{t,i} / (sqrt(v_hat_{t,i}) + epsilon)) * m_hat_{t,i} so that rapid gradient changes incur low friction (xi_{t,i} -> 1) and vanishing gradient changes near optima incur high friction (xi_{t,i} -> 0.5) to prevent overshooting.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Adam and AMSGrad (which rely solely on exponential moving averages of past gradients m_t and squared gradients v_t without immediate short-term gradient-difference friction) and from AdaBelief (AIMech:AdaBelief, which replaces v_t in the denominator with the EMA of (g_t - m_t)^2). diffGrad retains Adam's bias-corrected moments m_hat_t and v_hat_t and multiplies the step size by the bounded logistic friction factor xi_t = 1 / (1 + exp(-|g_{t-1} - g_t|)) in [0.5, 1).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #diffgrad-friction-coefficient-and-update-rule
At step t, diffGrad computes the element-wise absolute difference between consecutive gradients |Delta g_{t,i}| = |g_{t-1,i} - g_{t,i}|, passes it through the abs-sigmoid diffGrad Friction Coefficient xi_{t,i} = 1 / (1 + exp(-|g_{t-1,i} - g_{t,i}|)) bounded strictly in [0.5, 1), and updates parameters via theta_{t,i} = theta_{t-1,i} - (alpha_t * xi_{t,i} / (sqrt(v_hat_{t,i}) + epsilon)) * m_hat_{t,i}.
  • supports Source: diffGrad: An Optimization Method for Convolutional Neural Networks · Locator: Section III, Equations 8-14 and Figure 1, p. 2-4
  • supports Source: diffGrad: An Optimization Method for Convolutional Neural Networks (Crossref metadata) · Locator: Bibliographic metadata for Dubey et al. 2020 (DOI:10.1109/TNNLS.2019.2955777)
Scope #diffgrad-online-convex-regret-bound
Under Theorem 1 of Dubey et al. (2020), for convex online functions f_t with ||nabla f_t(theta)||_2 <= G, ||nabla f_t(theta)||_inf <= G_inf, and diameter ||theta_n - theta_m||_2 <= D, diffGrad with alpha_t = alpha / sqrt(t) and beta_{1,t} = beta_1 * lambda^{t-1} achieves O(sqrt(T)) regret bounded by R(T) <= (D^2 / (alpha * (1 - beta_1))) * sum_{i=1}^d sqrt(T * v_hat_{T,i}) + (alpha * (1 + beta_1) * G_inf / ((1 - beta_1) * sqrt(1 - beta_2) * (1 - gamma)^2)) * sum_{i=1}^d ||g_{1:T,i}||_2 + sum_{i=1}^d (D_inf^2 * G_inf * beta_1) / ((1 - beta_1) * (1 - lambda)^2 * alpha).
  • supports Source: diffGrad: An Optimization Method for Convolutional Neural Networks · Locator: Section III-A (Convergence Analysis), Theorem 1, Lemma 2, and Appendix I, p. 4-5, 10-12
Application #diffgrad-overshoot-prevention-and-cnn-classification-applications
diffGrad prevents Adam's momentum-induced overshooting out of narrow global valleys on non-convex synthetic objectives (F1-F3) and improves top-1 validation accuracy over Adam, AMSGrad, AdaGrad, and RMSprop on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 across ResNet-18, ResNet-50, ResNet-101, DenseNet-121, MobileNet, SENet, ShuffleNet, VGG16, and GoogleNet.
  • supports Source: diffGrad: An Optimization Method for Convolutional Neural Networks · Locator: Section III-B (Optimization Toy Examples), Figure 3, and Section IV (Experimental Results), Tables I-IV, p. 5-9
Limitation #diffgrad-mini-batch-noise-sensitivity-and-previous-gradient-storage
diffGrad must store the previous step's full gradient tensor g_{t-1} in memory (increasing optimizer state from 2P to 3P floats), and under high mini-batch gradient variance the instantaneous difference |g_{t-1,i} - g_{t,i}| can remain large even near a stationary point, keeping xi_{t,i} close to 1.
  • supports Source: diffGrad: An Optimization Method for Convolutional Neural Networks · Locator: Section III, Equations 8-14, and Section IV-B, p. 3-4, 7-8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires storing the previous iteration's gradient vector g_{t-1} alongside m_t and v_t, increasing auxiliary optimizer memory from 2P to 3P floats.
  • Because the friction coefficient depends on the unsmoothed single-step difference |g_{t-1} - g_t|, large stochastic mini-batch variance can keep |g_{t-1} - g_t| large near a minimum and reduce the friction effect.
