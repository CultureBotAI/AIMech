Replicated Softmax

Architectures AIMech:ReplicatedSoftmax DRAFT
An undirected document model with binary hidden units and a length-dependent set of categorical word units that share visible-to-hidden weights, with hidden biases scaled by document length.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the original Replicated Softmax model of Salakhutdinov and Hinton. It specializes a restricted Boltzmann machine to word-count sufficient statistics across documents of different lengths. It is distinct from repeatedly applying the ordinary softmax function, from the expected-count Constrained Poisson update discussed in the paper, and from the directed deterministic-feature Document NADE architecture. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For a document with D word positions, vocabulary counts n_k and binary hidden states h_j, use energy E(V,h)=-sum_jk W_jk h_j n_k-sum_k b_k n_k-D sum_j a_j h_j. The joint probability is proportional to exp(-E) with a partition function for that length. Hidden units are conditionally independent given words, with P(h_j=1|V)=sigmoid(D a_j+sum_k W_jk n_k). Given h, positions independently draw words from softmax(b_k+sum_j W_jk h_j); equivalently, sample D word counts from the corresponding multinomial. Parameter sharing makes the ordered-word probability permutation invariant. A count-vector probability includes the number of distinct ordered realizations. Learning contrasts data and model sufficient statistics; the paper approximates the model term with finite alternating Gibbs steps initialized at data.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Tractable hidden posteriors do not make the partition function or equilibrium model expectation tractable. Finite-step contrastive-divergence updates are not exact maximum-likelihood gradients; deterministic expected counts are not the same as multinomial Gibbs sampling.
  • The model is conditional on document length and supplies no document-length distribution. Its hidden feature vector has independent conditional Bernoulli coordinates, not topic proportions constrained to sum to one.
  • For ordinary counting measure on K^D ordered word assignments and F binary hidden units, the zero-weight, zero-bias partition is K^D times 2^F. The AIS discussion’s printed Z_0=2^F needs a corresponding normalized base-measure convention or visible-state factor; it is not adopted unchanged as that counting-measure partition. This is a mathematical qualification of the displayed derivation, not a claim about author-code behavior.
  • No convergence, topic quality or benchmark advantage is asserted. The later experimental pages and author software were not audited.
