RNN-NADE

Architectures AIMech:RecurrentNeuralNetworkNeuralAutoregressiveDistributionEstimator DRAFT
Aliases: Recurrent neural network neural autoregressive distribution estimator
A neural sequence architecture in which a deterministic recurrent state supplies parameters of a NADE output distribution, combining temporal recurrence with normalized within-frame autoregressive conditionals.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the RNN-NADE identified in the 2012 recurrent-output-estimator paper by substituting NADE for the conditional RBM. Temporal order between vector frames and variable order within a frame are separate. The recurrent state and NADE prefix activations are deterministic computations; there is no stochastic RBM hidden state to integrate out. It is distinct from unconditional NADE, order-agnostic NADE, RNN-RBM and a recurrent predictor with independent Bernoulli outputs. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For the single-layer binary specialization, compute the past-dependent biases b_t=b+P r_(t-1) and c_t=c+Q r_(t-1). Fix an ordering of D bits within the current frame. At position i, compute z_(t,i)=sigmoid(c_t+W[:,<i] v_(t,<i)) and q_(t,i)=sigmoid(b_(t,i)+V[i,:] z_(t,i)). Multiply q_(t,i) raised to v_(t,i) times (1-q_(t,i)) raised to (1-v_(t,i)) over positions, then multiply frame probabilities over time. Reuse a prefix accumulator initialized to c_t, adding W[:,i] v_(t,i) after evaluating each conditional. After the frame is observed or generated, update the separate recurrent state using its own input and recurrent weights. Differentiate the exact sum of frame negative log probabilities through the NADE parameters, the bias projections and the recurrent history. The 2012 paper explicitly contrasts these exact model gradients with CD-based RNN-RBM gradients. Ancestral generation samples bits in the fixed within-frame order before updating the recurrent state.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Exact normalized probabilities and gradients apply to this specified model with fully observed training sequences. They do not imply global optimization, calibration, equality to an RBM distribution or tractable inference for every pattern of missing frames or bits.
  • The within-frame ordering and temporal recurrence serve different purposes. Prefix sharing gives O(DH) NADE evaluation per frame for H hidden units, in addition to the recurrent-state and bias-projection costs; it does not remove sequential generation dependencies.
  • The original NADE paper prints an accumulator index discrepancy and initializes a multiplicatively accumulated probability to zero with an incorrect plus between Bernoulli factors in Algorithm 1. The displayed mechanism follows its normalized chain definition, Equations (9)–(11) and prefix-update rule, retaining the source qualification.
  • Output weights V may be learned separately from W as in the original NADE paper. Sharing prefix parameters across conditionals does not require tying input and output weights, and it does not make within-frame variables independent.
  • The RNN-RBM paper’s negative joint/bias signs concern its RBM formulas and are not a prescription for NADE conditionals. Backpropagation through the separate recurrence still requires dimensionally correct transposes. No author-code or empirical superiority claim is certified.
