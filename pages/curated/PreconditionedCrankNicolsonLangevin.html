Preconditioned Crank–Nicolson Langevin sampling

Algorithms AIMech:PreconditionedCrankNicolsonLangevin DRAFT
Aliases: pCNL
A Metropolis-corrected Gaussian-reference sampler that preconditions the potential-gradient drift by the reference covariance and uses a scalar Crank–Nicolson autoregressive update.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Cotter et al. pCNL. The reference covariance multiplies grad Phi, and the linear denominator is the scalar 2+delta, distinguishing it from the CNL matrix solve. Removing the potential gradient recovers the pCN proposal, but a nonzero gradient changes its acceptance rule. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For finite-dimensional pi(u) proportional to exp(−Phi(u)) Normal(u;0,C), with fixed symmetric positive-definite C, assume differentiable Phi, choose delta>0 and draw w from Normal(0,C). Set v=((2−delta)u−2 delta C grad Phi(u)+sqrt(8 delta)w)/(2+delta). Its Gaussian covariance is 8 delta C/(2+delta) squared. Let q be that proposal; accept with min(1,pi(v)q(u|v)/(pi(u)q(v|u))) and otherwise retain u, using the reverse gradient-dependent mean at v. This finite-dimensional expression specializes the source’s general Metropolis rule and pCNL rho acceptance. The prior-cancelled pCN likelihood ratio alone is generally insufficient for this gradient-directed kernel.
  • supports Source: MCMC Methods for Functions: Modifying Old Algorithms to Make Them Faster · Locator: Sections 4.1 and 4.3, Equation (4.13) and its rho/acceptance expression, reprint/PDF pp. 9 and 11

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The target must be proper relative to the Gaussian reference. The explicit density-ratio implementation here is finite-dimensional with symmetric positive-definite C; function-space extensions require the source’s operator, regularity and absolute-continuity assumptions.
  • Target invariance does not imply independent draws, good exploration or finite-run accuracy. Mesh-robust behavior under stated assumptions is not a universal dimension-free performance guarantee.
  • C and the step size are fixed in the stated kernel. Adaptive covariance or step-size policies need separate validity arguments.
  • The reprint’s later Equation (6.2) reverses signs relative to Equation (6.1) and the explicit Section 4 proposals. This record uses Section 4 and does not claim a published erratum or use the inconsistent expression.
  • The gradient is of the potential relative to the Gaussian reference. Substituting the full log-target gradient without accounting for the already implicit Gaussian drift defines a different proposal.
  • Function-space validity and efficient discretizations need the source’s extra assumptions; the finite-dimensional density ratio is not a definition of an infinite-dimensional Lebesgue density.
