Generative Topographic Mapping

Model Families AIMech:GenerativeTopographicMapping DRAFT
Aliases: GTM
A nonlinear latent-variable density model with a uniform discrete prior on fixed latent grid nodes, mapped through a shared smooth function to the centers of an equal-weight Gaussian mixture with common isotropic covariance.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Scoped to the original fixed-basis GTM construction and its batch EM fitting. The shared latent-to-data map ties Gaussian centers and distinguishes the model from an unconstrained mixture, a conditional mixture-density network, a Kohonen map and topographic ICA energy dependence. Gaussian radial basis functions are a usual map choice, not an identity with every radial-basis network. Learned mixture weights, heteroscedastic extensions and mixtures of GTMs require separate scope. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #constrained-density
Choose K fixed nodes x_i in a latent space of dimension L<D, fixed smooth basis vectors phi(x_i), a weight matrix W and shared precision beta>0. Set y_i=W phi(x_i) and p(t)=K^(-1)sum_i Normal(t;y_i,beta^(-1)I_D). This finite mixture is normalized exactly for finite parameters; the equal node weights do not imply a uniform density over the mapped surface. Mixture centers move together through W rather than being independent free parameters.
  • supports Source: GTM: The Generative Topographic Mapping · Locator: Sections 2-2.1, Equations 1-7, reprint/PDF pp. 3-5
Mechanism #exact-em-fitting
For N observations, the E-step gives R_in proportional to exp(-beta*||t_n-W phi(x_i)||^2/2), normalized over nodes i. With Phi_ij=phi_j(x_i), T containing data rows and G_ii=sum_n R_in, the unregularized M-step solves Phi^T G Phi W_new^T=Phi^T R T. Then beta_new^(-1)=(ND)^(-1)sum_ni R_in||t_n-W_new phi(x_i)||^2, using the old responsibilities. Exact well-defined EM updates make the observed-data log likelihood nondecreasing. Rank deficiency can require a pseudoinverse or another weighted least-squares minimizer; zero residual variance lies outside the finite-positive-precision model.
  • supports Source: GTM: The Generative Topographic Mapping · Locator: Section 2.1, Equations 8-14, reprint/PDF pp. 5-6; Section 2.4, p. 8; curator rank, boundary and EM qualifications
Mechanism #posterior-visualization
Bayes responsibilities define a posterior distribution over latent nodes for each observation. A posterior-mean display uses sum_i R_in x_i; a modal display selects a node with maximum responsibility. Multimodal posteriors can make the mean misleading. Smooth forward mapping supports local continuity, but does not ensure global injectivity, distance preservation or a unique inverse. A curator circular-map example has identical images for separated latent nodes and a posterior mean at a nearly unsupported middle node.
  • supports Source: GTM: The Generative Topographic Mapping · Locator: Section 2.2, Equations 17-19, reprint/PDF p. 6; Sections 2 and 4.2, pp. 4 and 12; curator smooth-map counterexample
Limitation #fitting-and-comparison-scope
EM objective nondecrease is narrower than an unconditional guarantee of convergence to a local maximum or globally optimal density. Gaussian weight regularization changes the objective to MAP and changes the normal equations. The original comparisons with SOM concern explicit mixture probability, soft responsibilities and tied-center regression; they do not establish universal superiority, monotonic posterior concentration or biologically correct topology.
  • supports Source: GTM: The Generative Topographic Mapping · Locator: Section 2.1, Equations 15-16, reprint/PDF p. 6; Sections 4.1-4.2, Equations 21-27 and Figures 6-7, pp. 9-13; curator qualification of the paper’s convergence language

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Latent dimension, nodes, basis functions and smoothness parameters are modeling choices. The source does not establish automatic recovery of the true manifold or globally preserved topology.
  • Likelihood fitting is nonconvex and sensitive to initialization and model choice. Responsibilities require numerically stable evaluation; exact finite-parameter EM statements do not guarantee arbitrary approximate or floating-point implementations.
  • The source’s effective regression smoother can have negative entries; it is not the posterior responsibility matrix. Its row-sum property relies on the stated constant-basis and unregularized algebra.
  • The 16-page author reprint, including simulated oil-flow and toy-curve experiments, was read completely. Finite calculations check a tiny exact update and a topology boundary; no author software, empirical training or benchmark comparison was reproduced.
