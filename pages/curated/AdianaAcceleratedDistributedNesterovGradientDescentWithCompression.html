ADIANA Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent with Compression

Algorithms AIMech:AdianaAcceleratedDistributedNesterovGradientDescentWithCompression DRAFT
Aliases: ADIANA, ACGD, Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent with Compression, Accelerated Compressed Gradient Descent
An accelerated distributed optimization algorithm with unbiased communication compression (ADIANA, together with its single-node counterpart ACGD) for minimizing f(x) = (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n f_i(x) that couples three iterate sequences via x^k = theta_1 * z^k + theta_2 * w^k + (1 - theta_1 - theta_2) * y^k, where w^k is a loopless probabilistic reference point updated via w^{k+1} = y^k with probability p and w^k with probability 1 - p, and each worker i applies two independent omega-unbiased compressors C_i^k(nabla f_i(x^k) - h_i^k) and C_tilde_i^k(nabla f_i(w^k) - h_i^k) to form the global gradient estimator g^k = h^k + (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n C_i^k(nabla f_i(x^k) - h_i^k), update local shifts h_i^{k+1} = h_i^k + alpha * C_tilde_i^k(nabla f_i(w^k) - h_i^k) with alpha = 1 / (omega + 1), and perform Nesterov gradient and mirror steps y^{k+1} = x^k - eta * g^k and z^{k+1} = beta * z^k + (1 - beta) * x^k - (gamma / eta) * (x^k - y^{k+1}), achieving accelerated O((omega + sqrt(L / mu) + sqrt((omega/n + sqrt(omega/n)) * omega * L / mu)) log(1/eps)) communication rounds on L-smooth mu-strongly convex problems.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from non-accelerated DIANA (AIMech:DianaDistributedLearningWithCompressedGradientDifferences, which scales linearly with condition number kappa = L/mu rather than sqrt(L/mu) and updates shifts h_i^k at the current iterate x^k rather than a loopless reference w^k), from Katyusha (AIMech:KatyushaDirectNegativeMomentumAccelerationForStochasticVarianceReducedOptimization, which accelerates centralized finite-sum SVRG without communication compression), and from Linear Coupling AGM (AIMech:LinearCouplingOfGradientAndMirrorDescentForFirstOrderOptimization, which operates on uncompressed oracles). No pinned AIO class covers ADIANA or Nesterov-accelerated compressed distributed optimization.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r390-single-node-acgd-sqrt-kappa-and-sqrt-eps-rates
Theorem 2 proves that coupling Nesterov momentum x^k = theta_k y^k + (1 - theta_k) z^k with an omega-unbiased compressor C(nabla f(x^k)) in ACGD (Algorithm 1) reduces single-node compressed gradient descent complexity from O((1 + omega) * (L/mu) log(1/eps)) to O((1 + omega) * sqrt(L/mu) log(1/eps)) for mu-strongly convex objectives, and Theorem 3 proves an O((1 + omega) * sqrt(L/eps)) rate for general smooth convex objectives.
  • supports Source: Acceleration for Compressed Gradient Descent in Distributed and Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 3 Algorithm 1, Theorem 2, and Theorem 3, pp. 4-5
Mechanism #r390-distributed-adiana-accelerated-complexity-and-free-compression-regime
By decoupling gradient difference compression at x^k from shift-memory updates at the loopless reference w^k (Lemmas 2-3: E_k||g^k - nabla f(x^k)||^2 <= (2*omega/n)*(2*L*D_f(x^k, w^k) + H^k) and E_k[H^{k+1}] <= (1 - alpha) H^k), Theorem 4 proves that ADIANA (Algorithm 2) converges in O((omega + sqrt(L/mu) + sqrt((omega/n + sqrt(omega/n)) * omega * L / mu)) log(1/eps)) communication rounds—matching uncompressed Nesterov accelerated gradient descent O(sqrt(L/mu) log(1/eps)) whenever the compression variance satisfies omega <= O(min{n^{1/3}, sqrt(L/mu)}).
  • supports Source: Acceleration for Compressed Gradient Descent in Distributed and Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 4 Algorithm 2, Theorem 4, Table 1, and Section 5 Lemmas 2-5, pp. 2, 5-8
Limitation #r390-dual-compression-and-convexity-requirement-limitation
ADIANA requires each worker i to evaluate two gradients (nabla f_i(x^k) and nabla f_i(w^k)) and transmit two compressed difference vectors (m_i^k and c_i^k) per iteration, and its Nesterov three-sequence coupling relies on convexity (mu >= 0) and exact/full-batch local gradients.
  • supports Source: Acceleration for Compressed Gradient Descent in Distributed and Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 4 Algorithm 2 Steps 4-6 and Theorem 4 assumptions, pp. 6-7
Application #r390-ill-conditioned-distributed-regularized-classification-application
Applied to ill-conditioned distributed L2-regularized logistic regression on LIBSVM benchmarks (a9a, w8a, mushrooms, phishing) with natural dithering and random sparsification compressors, achieving orders-of-magnitude faster convergence than DIANA, QSGD, TernGrad, and EF-SGD.
  • supports Source: Acceleration for Compressed Gradient Descent in Distributed and Federated Optimization · Locator: Section 6 Experiments, Figures 1-2, pp. 8-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires two gradient evaluations and two compressed vector transmissions per worker at each iteration.
  • Restricted to convex and mu-strongly convex objectives with unbiased compression operators.
