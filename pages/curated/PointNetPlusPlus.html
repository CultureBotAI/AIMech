PointNet++

A hierarchical point-set neural architecture that repeatedly samples centroids, groups metric neighborhoods and applies local PointNet feature extraction, with multi-scale or multi-resolution constructions for variable sampling density.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: PointNet++: Deep Hierarchical Feature Learning on Point Sets in a Metric Space · Locator: Sections 3.2–3.3, Figures 2–3, PDF/printed pp. 3–5
Identity Notes & Concept Scope PointNet++ adds hierarchical local set abstraction to PointNet. Multi-scale and multi-resolution grouping are distinct variants within this architecture; the paper also evaluates a single-scale ablation.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #architecture-mechanism
Farthest-point sampling chooses centroids; neighborhood grouping and local-coordinate PointNet encoders produce features for successive abstraction levels. Multi-scale grouping concatenates features from different radii, while multi-resolution grouping combines lower-level summaries with directly encoded regional points.
  • supports Source: PointNet++: Deep Hierarchical Feature Learning on Point Sets in a Metric Space · Locator: Sections 3.2–3.3, Figures 2–3, PDF/printed pp. 3–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Neighborhood scale, sampling density and the selected grouping variant affect the representation; density-adaptive construction is not a guarantee for arbitrary missing data.
  • The single-scale ablation, multi-scale grouping and multi-resolution grouping are not identical procedures. No universal benchmark advantage is curated.
