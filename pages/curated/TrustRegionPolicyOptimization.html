Trust region policy optimization

Algorithms AIMech:TrustRegionPolicyOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: TRPO
A policy-optimization method that optimizes a surrogate objective while constraining the change in policy by a trust region based on Kullback–Leibler divergence.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope TRPO's constrained policy update differs from PPO's clipped surrogate or adaptive penalty variants. It is an optimization procedure, not a trained policy instance.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #learning-method
The practical method estimates a surrogate objective from sampled trajectories and constrains the average divergence from the old policy, using an approximate constrained update rather than evaluating a maximum-divergence constraint at every state.
  • supports Source: Trust Region Policy Optimization · Locator: Sections 4–6, PDF pp. 3–5; equations 12–15 and practical-approximation discussion

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The paper distinguishes its theoretical improvement scheme from practical approximations; this record does not guarantee monotonic improvement for every practical update.
  • Single-path and vine sampling have different access requirements; the latter assumes the ability to restore sampled states.
