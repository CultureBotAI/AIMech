Decorrelated Score Function and One-Step Estimator for Sparse High-Dimensional Inference

Aliases: Decorrelated Score Test, High-Dimensional Decorrelated Score Function, Ning-Liu Decorrelated Score and One-Step Estimator, Generalized Decorrelated Score Test, High-Dimensional One-Step Estimator via Decorrelated Score
A general hypothesis-testing and confidence-region methodology (introduced by Yang Ning and Han Liu in 2017) for a low-dimensional or growing parameter of interest $\boldsymbol{\theta} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$ in the presence of a high-dimensional sparse nuisance parameter $\boldsymbol{\gamma} \in \mathbb{R}^{d-d_0}$ ($d \gg n$) under smooth empirical risk or negative log-likelihood $\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma}) = n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n \ell_i(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma})$. By projecting the target score $\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma})$ onto the nuisance tangent space via $\mathbf{w}^* = (\mathbf{I}_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}\boldsymbol{\gamma}}^*)^{-1}\mathbf{I}_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}\boldsymbol{\theta}}^*$ where $\mathbf{I}^* = \mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{\beta}^*}(\nabla^2\ell(\boldsymbol{\beta}^*))$, the method defines the population *decorrelated score function* $S(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma}) = \nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma}) - \mathbf{w}^{*T}\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma})$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{\beta}^*}[\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}^*, \boldsymbol{\gamma}^*)S^T(\boldsymbol{\theta}^*, \boldsymbol{\gamma}^*)] = \mathbf{0}$ (Equation 2.4). Given an initial penalized M-estimator $\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}} = (\widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})$ (Equation 2.5) and a Dantzig-selector or penalized quadratic projection estimator $\widehat{\mathbf{w}}$ (Equations 2.6–2.7), the plug-in decorrelated score $\widehat{S}(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}) = \nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}) - \widehat{\mathbf{w}}^T\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})$ converts first-order nuisance estimation bias into the second-order Taylor product $(\widehat{\mathbf{w}} - \mathbf{w}^*)^T\nabla^2_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}\boldsymbol{\gamma}}\ell(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \widetilde{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}} - \boldsymbol{\gamma}^*)$ without inverting the full $d \times d$ Hessian. This yields the asymptotically normal score statistic $\widehat{U}_n = \sqrt{n}\widehat{S}(\mathbf{0}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})\widehat{I}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}}^{-1/2} \rightsquigarrow N(0, 1)$ where $\widehat{I}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}} = \nabla^2_{\theta\theta}\ell(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}) - \widehat{\mathbf{w}}^T\nabla^2_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}\theta}\ell(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})$ (Algorithm 1, Theorem 3.7), the semiparametrically efficient *one-step estimator* $\widetilde{\theta} = \widehat{\theta} - \widehat{S}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})/\widehat{I}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}}$ (Equation 2.9, Corollary 3.10), the Gaussian multiplier bootstrap supremum test $\widehat{T}_n = \|\sqrt{n}\widehat{S}(\mathbf{0}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})\|_\infty$ for high-dimensional nulls $H_0: \boldsymbol{\theta}^* = \mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$ (Section 6.1, Theorem 6.1), and sandwich-variance-adjusted tests under model misspecification and general non-likelihood losses (Sections 6.2–6.3, Theorems 6.4 and 6.8).

Mechanism #claim-decorrelated-score-asymptotic-normality-and-local-power
Under Assumptions 3.1–3.4 and product sparsity rate $\eta_1(n) + \eta_2(n) + s^*\eta_1(n)\eta_2(n) + (s^* \vee s^{\prime *})\eta_1(n)\sqrt{\log d / n} = o(n^{-1/2})$ (yielding $(s^* \vee s^{\prime *})\log d = o(\sqrt{n})$ when $\eta_1(n) \asymp \sqrt{s^*\log d / n}$ and $\eta_2(n) \asymp \sqrt{s^{\prime *}\log d / n}$), the decorrelated score statistic $\widehat{U}_n = \sqrt{n}\widehat{S}(0, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})\widehat{I}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}}^{-1/2}$ converges in distribution to $N(0, 1)$ under $H_0: \theta^* = 0$ (Theorem 3.7) and to $N(-c(I^*_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}})^{1/2}, 1)$ under local alternatives $H_{1n}: \theta^* = c n^{-1/2}$ (Theorem 3.15), while the one-step estimator $\widetilde{\theta} = \widehat{\theta} - \widehat{S}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})/\widehat{I}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}}$ satisfies $\sqrt{n}(\widetilde{\theta} - \theta^*)(\widehat{I}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}})^{1/2} \rightsquigarrow N(0, 1)$ attaining the semiparametric efficiency bound $(I^*_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}})^{-1}$ (Corollary 3.10) and uniform validity over parameter classes $\Omega_0, \Omega_1$ (Theorems 3.18 and 3.22).

Mechanism #claim-linear-and-glm-decorrelated-score-instantiation
In high-dimensional linear models $Y_i = \theta Z_i + \boldsymbol{\gamma}^T\mathbf{X}_i + \varepsilon_i$, the one-step estimator $\widetilde{\theta}$ equals the exact closed-form root of $\widehat{S}(\theta, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}) = 0$ and coincides algebraically with the Zhang–Zhang low-dimensional projection estimator when $\widehat{\mathbf{w}}$ is estimated via Lasso $n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n (Z_i - \mathbf{w}^T\mathbf{X}_i)^2 + \lambda'\|\mathbf{w}\|_1$ (Section 4.1, Equations 4.5–4.6), whereas in sparse generalized linear models with log-partition $b(\eta_i)$, $\widetilde{\mathbf{w}}$ is estimated directly by the single weighted Lasso regression $\arg\min_{\mathbf{w}} (2n)^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n b''(\mathbf{Q}_i^T\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})(Z_i - \mathbf{w}^T\mathbf{X}_i)^2 + \lambda'\|\mathbf{w}\|_1$ without computing the full $d \times d$ inverse weighted Gram matrix required by van de Geer et al. (Section 5, Equation 5.2, Theorem 5.3).

Mechanism #claim-multiplier-bootstrap-and-misspecified-generalized-score
For high-dimensional null hypotheses $H_0: \boldsymbol{\theta}^* = \mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_0}$ with $\log d_0 = o(n^{1/7})$, the supremum statistic $\widehat{T}_n = \|\sqrt{n}\widehat{S}(\mathbf{0}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})\|_\infty$ is consistently calibrated by the Gaussian multiplier bootstrap $\widehat{\mathbf{N}}_e = n^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^n e_i(\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}\ell_i(\mathbf{0}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}) - \widehat{\mathbf{W}}^T\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}\ell_i(\mathbf{0}, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}}))$ with $e_i \sim N(0,1)$ satisfying $\sup_{\alpha \in (0,1)}|\mathbb{P}_{H_0}(\widehat{T}_n > c_N(\alpha)) - \alpha| = o(1)$ (Section 6.1, Theorem 6.1); under model misspecification or general non-likelihood loss functions $L(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\gamma})$, the generalized score statistic $\widehat{U}_n^{\prime} = \sqrt{n}\widehat{S}(0, \widehat{\boldsymbol{\gamma}})(\widehat{\Sigma}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}})^{-1/2}$ replaces the information identity with the sandwich variance estimator $\widehat{\Sigma}_{\theta|\boldsymbol{\gamma}} = \widehat{J}_{\theta\theta} - 2\widehat{\mathbf{w}}^T\widehat{\mathbf{J}}_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}\theta} + \widehat{\mathbf{w}}^T\widehat{\mathbf{J}}_{\boldsymbol{\gamma}\boldsymbol{\gamma}}\widehat{\mathbf{w}}$ where $\widehat{\mathbf{J}} = n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n \nabla L_i(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})\nabla L_i(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\beta}})^T$ (Sections 6.2–6.3, Theorems 6.4 and 6.8).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Asymptotic normality of the decorrelated score statistic and one-step estimator requires both the initial parameter sparsity s* = ||gamma*||_0 and the score-projection sparsity s'^* = ||w*||_0 to satisfy the ultra-sparse product rate (s* v s'^*) log d = o(sqrt(n)) (Theorems 3.7, 4.3, and 5.3); if the inverse Fisher information direction w* = (I*_{gamma,gamma})^{-1} I*_{gamma,theta} is dense, the remainder term sqrt(n)(w_hat - w*)^T nabla^2_{gamma,gamma} ell (gamma_hat - gamma*) need not vanish.
  • The Gaussian multiplier bootstrap for high-dimensional null hypotheses H_0: theta* = 0 in R^{d_0} requires log d_0 = o(n^{1/7}) together with sub-exponential score and Hessian moment bounds (Assumptions 6.1-6.3, Theorem 6.1).
  • Primary-source typographical audit of Ning & Liu (2017, arXiv:1412.8765v2) uncovered five minor notation and typographical issues that are corrected in this record: (1) Section 3.1 Assumption 3.4 (p. 10) writes 'sqrt(n) v^{*T} nabla ell(0, gamma*) / sqrt(v^T I* v) -> N(0,1)' omitting the superscript '*' on 'v*' inside the denominator (corrected in Assumption 3.12 on p. 12); (2) Section 3.3 Remark 3.24 Eq. (3.14) (p. 15) writes '|P_{beta*}(T_n = 1) - psi_alpha)| = 0' with an unmatched closing parenthesis after 'psi_alpha'; (3) Section 5.3 p. 24 line 11 writes 'Bases on these assumptions' instead of 'Based on these assumptions'; (4) Section 6.1 display for N_hat_e (p. 26) and Appendix D.1 Proof of Theorem 6.1 (p. 49) write 'N_hat_e = n^{-1/2} sum_{i=1}^n e_i(nabla_theta ell_i(0, gamma_hat) - W_hat^T nabla_gamma ell(0, gamma_hat))' omitting the sample subscript 'i' on the second 'ell_i(0, gamma_hat)'; and (5) Appendix C.3 Lemma C.5 Eq.
