Degree-Corrected Stochastic Blockmodel and Edge-Stub Kullback-Leibler Divergence Profile Log-Likelihood

Mathematical Methods AIMech:DegreeCorrectedStochasticBlockmodelAndEdgeStubKLDivergenceProfileLikelihood DRAFT
Aliases: Degree-Corrected Stochastic Blockmodel (DC-SBM), Karrer-Newman Degree-Corrected Stochastic Blockmodel, Edge-Stub Kullback-Leibler Profile Log-Likelihood for Community Detection, Poisson Multigraph Degree-Corrected Blockmodel with Stub Normalization
Degree-Corrected Stochastic Blockmodel and Edge-Stub Kullback-Leibler Divergence Profile Log-Likelihood is a generative random multigraph model and maximum-likelihood community-inference formulation introduced by Karrer and Newman (2011, Physical Review E 83(1):016107, DOI:10.1103/PhysRevE.83.016107, arXiv:1008.3926v1) that augments the $K$-block Poisson stochastic blockmodel with within-group-normalized vertex degree propensity parameters $\theta_i$ ($\sum_i \theta_i \delta_{g_i, r} = 1$ for each group $r \in \{1, \dots, K\}$, Eq. 15) so that off-diagonal edge counts satisfy $A_{ij} \sim \operatorname{Poisson}(\theta_i \theta_j \omega_{g_i g_j})$ and self-edge counts satisfy $A_{ii}/2 \sim \operatorname{Poisson}(\frac{1}{2}\theta_i^2 \omega_{g_i g_i})$ (Eqs. 14, 16). Maximizing the log-likelihood $\log P(G \mid \theta, \omega, g) = 2\sum_i k_i \log \theta_i + \sum_{rs}(m_{rs}\log \omega_{rs} - \omega_{rs})$ (Eq. 17) over $(\theta, \omega)$ in closed form yields $\hat{\theta}_i = k_i / \kappa_{g_i}$ and $\hat{\omega}_{rs} = m_{rs}$ (Eq. 18)—where $k_i = \sum_j A_{ij}$ is vertex degree, $m_{rs} = \sum_{ij} A_{ij}\delta_{g_i, r}\delta_{g_j, s}$ is the inter-group edge count, and $\kappa_r = \sum_s m_{rs} = \sum_i k_i \delta_{g_i, r}$ is the total number of edge stubs in group $r$—which exactly preserves both expected block edge counts $m_{rs}$ and every vertex's expected degree $\langle k_i \rangle = k_i$ (Eqs. 19-20) and reduces to the unnormalized profile log-likelihood $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DC}}(G \mid g) = \sum_{r,s=1}^K m_{rs} \log \frac{m_{rs}}{\kappa_r \kappa_s} \propto D_{\text{KL}}(p_K \parallel p_{\text{degree}})$ (Eqs. 23-24), measuring the Kullback-Leibler divergence between empirical block edge fractions $p_K(r,s) = m_{rs}/(2m)$ and the degree-preserving configuration-model null $p_{\text{degree}}(r,s) = (\kappa_r/2m)(\kappa_s/2m)$.

Distinct from `AIMech:TensorSpectralDecompositionForDirichletMixedMembershipCommunityModels` (Anandkumar, Ge, Hsu, & Kakade 2014, JMLR 15(66):2239-2312), which models fractional simplex community memberships $\pi_u \sim \operatorname{Dir}(\alpha)$ without vertex-specific degree heterogeneity parameters $\theta_i$ and recovers $(\Pi, P)$ via 5-way vertex-partitioned $\alpha_0$-centered 3-star subgraph count tensors, whereas Karrer & Newman (2011) formulate hard group assignments $g_i \in \{1, \dots, K\}$ with explicit per-vertex degree propensities $\theta_i$, derive closed-form MLEs $\hat{\theta}_i = k_i / \kappa_{g_i}$ and $\hat{\omega}_{rs} = m_{rs}$ that preserve the exact expected degree sequence $\langle k_i \rangle = k_i$, prove equivalence of the profile log-likelihood $\sum_{rs} m_{rs}\log(m_{rs}/(\kappa_r \kappa_s))$ to the edge-end Kullback-Leibler divergence $D_{\text{KL}}(p_K \parallel p_{\text{degree}})$ against the configuration-model stub null rather than the Erdős-Rényi vertex-count null $p_1(r,s) = n_r n_s / n^2$, and establish an $O(K + \langle k\rangle)$ single-vertex shift identity $\Delta \mathcal{L}$ for Kernighan-Lin-style local search.

Mechanism #poisson-multigraph-degree-corrected-likelihood-closed-form-mles-and-exact-degree-preservation
In an $n$-vertex undirected multigraph $G$ with adjacency matrix $A_{ij}$ (where $A_{ii}$ is twice the number of self-edges at vertex $i$), group assignments $g_i \in \{1, \dots, K\}$, symmetric group affinity matrix $\omega_{rs}$, and vertex degree parameters $\theta_i$ normalized within each community via $\sum_i \theta_i \delta_{g_i, r} = 1$ (Eq. 15), modeling off-diagonal edge counts as independent $\operatorname{Poisson}(\theta_i \theta_j \omega_{g_i g_j})$ and self-edge counts as $\operatorname{Poisson}(\frac{1}{2}\theta_i^2 \omega_{g_i g_i})$ yields the factorized graph probability $P(G \mid \theta, \omega, g) = \frac{1}{\prod_{i<j} A_{ij}! \prod_i 2^{A_{ii}/2} (A_{ii}/2)!} \prod_i \theta_i^{k_i} \prod_{rs} \omega_{rs}^{m_{rs}/2}\exp(-\frac{1}{2}\omega_{rs})$ (Eqs. 14, 16). Maximizing $\log P(G \mid \theta, \omega, g) = 2\sum_i k_i \log \theta_i + \sum_{rs}(m_{rs}\log \omega_{rs} - \omega_{rs})$ (Eq. 17) subject to Eq. (15) gives the closed-form maximum-likelihood estimates $\hat{\theta}_i = \frac{k_i}{\kappa_{g_i}}$ and $\hat{\omega}_{rs} = m_{rs}$ where $\kappa_r = \sum_s m_{rs} = \sum_i k_i \delta_{g_i, r}$ is the total number of stubs in group $r$ (Eqs. 12, 18), under which the fitted ensemble preserves both expected inter-group edges $\sum_{ij} \langle A_{ij}\rangle \delta_{g_i, r}\delta_{g_j, s} = m_{rs}$ (Eq. 19) and every vertex's expected degree $\sum_j \langle A_{ij}\rangle = k_i$ (Eq. 20), whereas the uncorrected stochastic blockmodel $\hat{\omega}_{rs} = m_{rs}/(n_r n_s)$ (Eq. 5) assigns every vertex in group $r$ the uniform expected degree $\kappa_r / n_r$.

Mechanism #edge-stub-kullback-leibler-divergence-profile-log-likelihood-and-local-vertex-move-identity
Substituting the closed-form MLEs $(\hat{\theta}_i, \hat{\omega}_{rs})$ into Eq. (17) and dropping assignment-independent constants $2\sum_i k_i \log k_i - 2m$ (Eqs. 21-22) yields the unnormalized profile log-likelihood $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DC}}(G \mid g) = \sum_{r,s=1}^K m_{rs} \log \frac{m_{rs}}{\kappa_r \kappa_s}$ (Eq. 23), which replaces the vertex counts $n_r n_s$ of the uncorrected SBM objective $\mathcal{L}_{\text{SBM}}(G \mid g) = \sum_{r,s} m_{rs} \log \frac{m_{rs}}{n_r n_s}$ (Eq. 6) with stub counts $\kappa_r \kappa_s$ and is affine-equivalent to the Kullback-Leibler divergence $\sum_{r,s} p_K(r,s)\log \frac{p_K(r,s)}{p_{\text{degree}}(r,s)}$ (and mutual information) between empirical edge-end group probabilities $p_K(r,s) = m_{rs}/(2m)$ and the degree-preserving configuration-model null $p_{\text{degree}}(r,s) = \frac{\kappa_r}{2m}\frac{\kappa_s}{2m}$ (Eqs. 11, 24). Moving a single vertex $i$ (with self-edge count $u_i = A_{ii}/2$ and non-self edge counts $k_{it}$ to group $t$) from group $r$ to group $s$ updates only $\kappa_r, \kappa_s, m_{rt}, m_{st}$, allowing $\Delta \mathcal{L}$ to be evaluated in $O(K + \langle k\rangle)$ average time via Eq. (25) within a Kernighan-Lin-style greedy sweep algorithm (Section III, pp. 5-6).

Scope #degree-split-distortion-in-uncorrected-sbm-versus-degree-corrected-recovery-on-heterogeneous-networks
In networks with broad or bimodal degree distributions, the uncorrected SBM objective $\mathcal{L}_{\text{SBM}}$ fits degree variation $P_{ij} \propto k_i k_j$ by partitioning vertices into high-degree and low-degree groups whenever the degree-split likelihood exceeds the community-split likelihood, failing on empirical heterogeneous graphs (splitting Zachary's karate club into high- and low-degree groups and achieving $\operatorname{NMI} = 0.0001$ vs. $0.72$ for DC-SBM on the 1222-vertex political blogs network, Section IV.A, Figs. 1-2, pp. 6-7) and failing on two-group bimodal-degree synthetic benchmarks $\omega_{rs} = \lambda \omega_{rs}^{\text{planted}} + (1 - \lambda) \frac{\kappa_r \kappa_s}{2m}$ (Eqs. 27-28, degrees 10 and 30) for all $\lambda \lesssim 0.6$ even when initialized at the true planted assignment (whereas DC-SBM achieves $\operatorname{NMI} > 0.7$ at $\lambda = 0.5$ from both random and planted initializations, Section IV.C, Fig. 3, pp. 8-9).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Assumes the number of communities $K$ is known a priori; because the number of degree propensity parameters $\theta_i$ scales linearly with the network size $n$, maximum profile likelihood alone cannot select $K$ without additional model-selection penalties or marginalization over conjugate Dirichlet-Gamma priors (Section V, p. 10).
  • Uses a Poisson multigraph generative model with independent edge counts (including multi-edges and self-edges), which can generate an unrealistic fraction of zero-degree vertices when expected degrees are small and does not model higher-order motifs, triangles, or overlapping/mixed memberships (Section II, p. 2; Section V, p. 10).
  • Primary-source typographical audit of `arXiv:1008.3926v1` (Phys. Rev. E 83:016107, 2011): in Equation (25) (p. 5), the single-vertex log-likelihood change $\Delta \mathcal{L}$ when moving vertex $i$ from community $r$ to community $s$ prints $a(m_{rt} + k_{it}) - a(m_{rt})$ instead of $a(m_{rt} - k_{it}) - a(m_{rt})$ in the sum over $t \ne r, s$ (since vertex $i$ leaves group $r$, the edge count between group $r$ and group $t$ decreases from $m_{rt}$ to $m_{rt} - k_{it}$, whereas the edge count between group $s$ and group $t$ increases from $m_{st}$ to $m_{st} + k_{it}$).
