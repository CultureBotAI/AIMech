Natural Compression and Natural Dithering for Distributed Optimization

Algorithms AIMech:NaturalCompressionAndNaturalDitheringForDistributedOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: Natural Compression, Natural Dithering, C_nat and D_nat Power-of-Two Gradient Quantization
A family of unbiased power-of-two randomized quantization operators for distributed and in-network gradient aggregation (Horváth et al. 2022): Natural Compression C_nat (Definition 1) rounds any non-zero scalar t = (-1)^s 2^e (1 + m) stochastically to sign(t) 2^{floor(log_2 |t|)} with probability p(t) = (2^{ceil(log_2 |t|)} - |t|) / 2^{floor(log_2 |t|)} = 1 - m and to sign(t) 2^{ceil(log_2 |t|)} with probability 1 - p(t) = m (dropping the IEEE 754 mantissa and transmitting only the sign and 8-bit exponent), while Natural Dithering D_nat^{p,s} (Definition 6) quantizes normalized vector coordinates |x_i| / ||x||_p onto s exponentially spaced power-of-two levels {0, 2^{1-s}, 2^{2-s}, ..., 2^{-1}, 1}.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from QSGD / Standard Random Dithering D_sta^{p,s} (AIMech:QSGDQuantizedStochasticGradientDescent, Alistarh et al. 2017, which uses uniformly spaced linear levels {0, 1/s, 2/s, ..., 1} rather than geometric power-of-two levels {2^{1-s}, ..., 1} and requires float multiplication rather than integer bit-shift addition), from TernGrad (AIMech:TernGradTernaryGradientsForDistributedDeepLearning, which uses a single l_infinity ternary level), and from biased Top-k / SignSGD compressors requiring error-feedback memory.

Mechanism #r415-c-nat-exact-one-eighth-relative-variance-and-composition-theorem
Theorem 3 proves that Natural Compression C_nat is unbiased (E[C_nat(x)] = x) and belongs to B(1/8) with tight worst-case relative variance omega = 1/8 attained at |t| = (4/3) 2^a (where E[C_nat(t)^2]/t^2 = max_{1 <= theta <= 2} (3 theta - 2)/theta^2 = 9/8), whereas unbiased integer rounding C_int has omega = infinity (Theorem 4); furthermore, Theorem 5 proves that composing any C_1 in B(omega_1) and C_2 in B(omega_2) yields C_1 o C_2 in B(omega_1 omega_2 + omega_1 + omega_2), so composing C_nat with any compressor C in B(omega) incurs only C_nat o C in B((9/8) omega + 1/8).
  • supports Source: Natural Compression for Distributed Deep Learning · Locator: Section 2.2-2.4 (Theorems 3, 4, and 5) & Appendix C.1-C.3 (Eqs. 6-9)
Mechanism #r415-natural-dithering-variance-bound-and-exponential-level-efficiency
Theorem 7 proves that Natural Dithering D_nat^{p,s} with s geometric levels satisfies D_nat^{p,s} in B(omega) with omega = 1/8 + d^{1/r} 2^{1-s} min{1, d^{1/r} 2^{1-s}} (r = min{p, 2}), and Theorem 8 proves that D_nat^{p,s} is a subset-level coarsening of standard linear dithering D_sta^{p,2^{s-1}} with E[||D_nat^{p,s}(x) - x||^2] <= (9/8) E[||D_sta^{p,2^{s-1}}(x) - x||^2] + (1/8) ||x||^2, reducing the number of quantization levels from u = 2^{s-1} to s for comparable variance.
  • supports Source: Natural Compression for Distributed Deep Learning · Locator: Section 3 (Definitions 6-7, Theorems 7 and 8, Figure 5) & Appendix C.4-C.5
Limitation #r415-non-vanishing-asymptotic-variance-floor-and-stochastic-rounding-rng-limitation
Because D_nat^{p,s} converges to C_nat rather than the identity operator as the number of levels s -> infinity, its worst-case relative variance cannot drop below the floor omega = 1/8 (Appendix A.2.3), and generating uniform random bits for stochastic mantissa rounding introduces a 10-15% CPU/GPU compression overhead relative to deterministic rounding (Appendix B).
  • supports Source: Natural Compression for Distributed Deep Learning · Locator: Appendix A.2.3 (Figure 18) & Appendix B Implementation Optimization
Application #r415-bidirectional-compressed-sgd-and-in-network-aggregation-application
Integrated into Distributed SGD with bidirectional worker (C_{W_i}) and master/switch (C_M) compression (Algorithm 1, Theorem 9) and SwitchML-style integer In-Network Aggregation on CIFAR-10, ImageNet (ResNet-50, AlexNet), and Neural Collaborative Filtering.
  • supports Source: Natural Compression for Distributed Deep Learning · Locator: Section 4 Distributed SGD (Algorithm 1, Theorem 9) & Section 5 Experiments (Figures 8-12)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Natural dithering has a non-vanishing asymptotic relative variance floor omega = 1/8 as s -> infinity.
  • Stochastic mantissa rounding incurs a 10-15% random-number generation overhead compared to deterministic power-of-two rounding.
