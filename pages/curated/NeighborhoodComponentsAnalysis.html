Neighbourhood components analysis

Algorithms AIMech:NeighborhoodComponentsAnalysis DRAFT
Aliases: Neighborhood components analysis, NCA
A supervised metric-learning algorithm that learns a linear transformation by maximizing the expected number of correctly classified training examples under stochastic leave-one-out neighbor selection.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope NCA is Goldberger et al.’s transformation-learning procedure, not ordinary k-nearest-neighbor voting or an unsupervised local-coordinate reconstruction. Its main objective sums correct-class probabilities; the paper also discusses a sum-of-log-probabilities alternative, which is a different objective. A rectangular transformation enables supervised dimensionality reduction. The AIO MetricLearning node is explicitly a deep neural network, so the original linear NCA procedure is not treated as its subclass. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For labeled vectors x_i, choose a matrix A, possibly rectangular, and use squared transformed distance d_ij=||A(x_i−x_j)||², with Q=A^T A positive semidefinite. For j!=i set p_ij=exp(−d_ij)/sum_(k!=i)exp(−d_ik), and set p_ii=0. Define p_i=sum_(j:c_j=c_i)p_ij. Optimize f(A)=sum_i p_i by a gradient-based method; this is the expected number of correct labels when each training point selects one other point as its neighbor according to p_ij. For dimensionality reduction use A with d rows, store Ax_i and classify a new projected point using ordinary Euclidean k-nearest-neighbor voting. Learning the scale of A changes the softness of the training neighbor distribution; it does not uniquely determine the later integer k.
  • supports Source: Neighbourhood Components Analysis · Locator: Sections 2–3, PDF pp. 1–4, Equations (1)–(7); rectangular projection and test-time classification at pp. 3–4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Training uses class labels and a smooth stochastic surrogate, not the discontinuous deterministic k-nearest-neighbor accuracy. Improving its training objective does not certify held-out classification accuracy.
  • The objective is nonconvex in A; initialization and the optimizer affect its result. A=0 is a stationary point of the displayed gradient, so a claim that any initialization learns a useful metric would be unwarranted.
  • At least two observations are needed for the leave-one-out denominator. A class represented by a singleton has p_i=0 and contributes no correct-class reward; the paper’s logarithmic alternative is undefined for that point.
  • Rectangular or rank-deficient A yields a positive-semidefinite squared distance with possible collapsed directions. It need not define a strictly positive distance between distinct original points. No global optimum, absence of overfitting or benchmark advantage is asserted.
