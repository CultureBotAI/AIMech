Order-agnostic NADE training

Algorithms AIMech:OrderAgnosticNADETraining DRAFT
Aliases: Order-agnostic training of NADE
A stochastic training procedure that shares neural conditional parameters across variable orderings and estimates the order-averaged NADE log-likelihood objective using a sampled observed prefix and losses for all remaining variables.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Uria, Murray and Larochelle’s order-agnostic training procedure, applicable to shallow or deep NADE and its real-valued extension. It is distinct from the original fixed-order NADE architecture, the existence of additional deterministic hidden layers alone, and an ensemble mixture evaluated after training. Parameter sharing across orderings does not assert that every ordering defines the same joint distribution. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Treat a variable ordering o as uniformly distributed over permutations and minimize the mean over data and o of the negative log probability assigned by the corresponding autoregressive NADE. For a training example, sample an ordering and a prefix length d-1 uniformly from zero through D-1. Feed the observed prefix values to the shared neural conditional network, set unobserved input values to zero and append a binary mask identifying observed dimensions so that observed zeros remain distinguishable from missing inputs. Evaluate the conditional loss for each of the D-d+1 remaining variables under that same prefix, sum those losses and multiply by D/(D-d+1). The resulting loss and, under the differentiability conditions, its gradient estimate the order-averaged objective; one masked-prefix forward/backward computation serves all missing outputs. Additional deterministic hidden layers can be used, while full joint evaluation for a chosen ordering still requires all ordered conditionals. After training, a selected ordering defines one normalized autoregressive model. A separate ensemble averages the joint probabilities of selected orderings, and samples by selecting a member then generating from that member. This probability average is distinct from the order-average log-likelihood training objective.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Shared parameters do not force order-specific joint distributions to agree. Choosing an ordering convenient for a conditioning query performs inference under that chosen member, not necessarily under another ordering or the ensemble mixture.
  • Conditioning an ensemble generally reweights its members by their evidence probabilities. Uniformly choosing a member after observing evidence does not automatically sample the ensemble posterior; a finite curator example checks this distinction.
  • The unbiased prefix estimator depends on the stated uniform ordering/prefix sampling and D/(D-d+1) weighting. Arbitrary masks, unweighted missing-output losses or nonuniform prefix sampling require a corresponding derivation.
  • The presence mask is additional input information, not a sequence-timestep skipping layer or a guarantee that missing-data mechanisms have been modeled. The paper distinguishes its missing-output loss from ordinary denoising-autoencoder reconstruction.
  • The training objective is an average of log probabilities rather than the log of an average probability. Ensemble density evaluation costs and deep full-joint evaluation differ from one stochastic training update; selecting a convenient model is not dimension-independent numerical inference.
  • No agreement of all conditionals with one joint distribution, universal performance gain, global optimization guarantee or complete implementation audit is asserted.
