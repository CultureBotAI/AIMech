Exact PoClustering

Algorithms AIMech:ExactPoClustering DRAFT
Aliases: Maximal-clique PoClustering
A dissimilarity-based clustering construction that accumulates maximal cliques of pairwise-distance threshold graphs and organizes their sets by inclusion with diameter labels, allowing overlapping clusters and multiple parents.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

6 Claims
Mechanism #threshold-maximal-cliques
At each distinct dissimilarity threshold, form the graph whose edges connect pairs at or below that value and enumerate its maximal cliques. Accumulate distinct clique sets across thresholds, label them by their diameters and order them by inclusion. Maximal means not contained in a larger clique at that threshold; it does not mean a single largest clique.
  • supports Source: Clustering Pair-wise Dissimilarity Data into Partially Ordered Sets · Locator: Section 3, Definitions 3.1–3.2 and Property 3.1, pp. 3–4; Section 4 and Algorithm 1, p. 4
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Sections 3–4, Equation 3.1 and Theorem 4.1, PDF pp. 3–4
Scope #diameter-labelled-representation
Exact threshold edge coverage supplies a cluster containing each pair at its own dissimilarity threshold. Its dissimilarity is therefore recoverable as the minimum diameter among retained clusters containing that pair. The diameter labels are essential: scaling every nonzero distance preserves the unlabelled inclusion family while changing the numerical distances. The 2007 paper defers its broader correspondence-theorem proof to an uninspected technical report.
  • supports Source: Clustering Pair-wise Dissimilarity Data into Partially Ordered Sets · Locator: Section 3, Definitions 3.1–3.2 and Property 3.1, PDF pp. 3–4
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Sections 3–4 and Theorem 4.1, PDF pp. 3–4; reference 12, p. 6
Limitation #inclusion-and-zero-distance
A smaller cluster diameter does not imply inclusion between arbitrary clusters, despite the biconditional wording in the 2007 abstract. The main construction uses inclusion as a separate relation. Also, allowing zero dissimilarity between distinct objects conflicts with simultaneously requiring every singleton and strictly increasing diameter along inclusion. A positive off-diagonal restriction or an explicit treatment of zero-distance equivalence is needed for those additional conditions.
  • supports Source: Clustering Pair-wise Dissimilarity Data into Partially Ordered Sets · Locator: Section 3 and Property 3.1, PDF pp. 3–4
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Abstract, p. 1; Sections 3–4, pp. 3–4
Scope #exact-versus-heuristic
Enumerating all maximal cliques can produce exponentially many sets. The later edge-clique-cover heuristic retains a potentially smaller family intended to cover graph edges, rather than enumerating every maximal clique. Pairwise-distance information and full cluster-family recovery are different requirements, so this exact construction is kept distinct from that heuristic.
  • supports Source: Clustering Pair-wise Dissimilarity Data into Partially Ordered Sets · Locator: Sections 3–4, PDF pp. 3–4
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Sections 4–5, PDF pp. 4–5
Limitation #pseudocode-boundaries
The 2006 printed driver updates a threshold-specific edge set while its loop condition names the original edge set, and leaves some initial collections implicit. Its incremental clique-update deletion test compares raw intersections with endpoint cliques; without adding the new edge endpoints that test does not perform the stated containment pruning. Equal-weight edges also require careful threshold-level handling. The mathematical threshold construction is not a certification of that literal pseudocode.
  • supports Source: Clustering Pair-wise Dissimilarity Data into Partially Ordered Sets · Locator: Algorithms 1–2 and accompanying text, PDF pp. 4–5
Scope #evaluation-scope
The 2006 Gene Ontology study derives gene dissimilarities from annotation categories and assesses recovery of those categories, rather than independently predicting function from expression alone. Its best-match Jaccard score is directional. Table values qualify conflicting prose summaries of accuracy and recovery, and they do not establish universal biological or clustering superiority.
  • supports Source: Clustering Pair-wise Dissimilarity Data into Partially Ordered Sets · Locator: Section 5, Tables 2–4 and evaluation definitions, PDF pp. 5–6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • This record covers the exact maximal-clique construction, not the later PoClustering edge-clique-cover heuristic or every abstract partially ordered set.
  • Numerical distance recovery requires retained diameter information and threshold edge coverage. The cited UNC technical report was not located in the bounded search and was not read; no general theorem certification is claimed.
  • Zero off-diagonal distances, tied thresholds and literal pseudocode require the stated qualifications. No unqualified NP-completeness claim is adopted from wording that lacks an explicit decision problem.
  • Finite exact examples illustrate representation, scaling, zero-distance and clique-cover boundaries; they are not an author implementation or benchmark.
  • All six pages of each 2006 and 2007 paper were read, including figures, algorithms, tables and references. No author code, datasets or alternative revisions were audited or executed. This record remains DRAFT.
