SAWB Statistics-Aware Weight Binning Quantization

Algorithms AIMech:SAWBStatisticsAwareWeightBinningQuantization DRAFT

Aliases: SAWB, Statistics-Aware Weight Binning, SAWB Weight Quantizer
A low-bitwidth neural network weight quantization algorithm that determines the symmetric clipping threshold alpha_w* minimizing weight quantization mean-squared error in closed form as a linear combination of the empirical second-moment root sqrt(E[w^2]) and first absolute moment E[|w|] using bitwidth-specific regression coefficients calibrated across canonical weight distributions.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #two-moment-linear-regression-optimal-clipping-scale
SAWB computes the optimal symmetric weight clipping threshold alpha_w* = c_1 sqrt(E[w^2]) - c_2 E[|w|] using bitwidth-specific constants (c_1, c_2) calibrated across Gaussian, uniform, Laplacian, gamma, Weibull, and logistic distributions, avoiding per-iteration grid search.
  • supports Source: PACT: Parameterized Clipping Activation for Quantized Neural Networks · Locator: Section 2.2, Equations 4-5, Figure 3, and Table 2, pp. 3-4
Mechanism #outlier-resilient-uniform-weight-binning
By clipping weights outside [-alpha_w*, +alpha_w*] before uniform 2^k-level binning and rescaling to [-alpha_w*, +alpha_w*], SAWB prevents extreme weight outliers from expanding the quantization step size across the bulk of the weight distribution.
  • supports Source: PACT: Parameterized Clipping Activation for Quantized Neural Networks · Locator: Section 2.2, Equation 4 and Figure 3(a), pp. 3-4
Application #ultra-low-bitwidth-cnn-and-rnn-weight-quantization-application
SAWB is combined with PACT activation clipping to quantize convolutional and recurrent neural network weights down to 2-bit and 4-bit precision with full-precision accuracy on ImageNet and Penn Treebank.
  • supports Source: PACT: Parameterized Clipping Activation for Quantized Neural Networks · Locator: Section 3, Figures 4-5 and Table 1, pp. 5-8
Limitation #symmetric-zero-mean-distribution-assumption-limitation
Because the SAWB regression coefficients (c_1, c_2) are calibrated on zero-mean symmetric distributions, strongly skewed or multimodal weight distributions can deviate from the predicted MSE-optimal clipping threshold.
  • supports Source: PACT: Parameterized Clipping Activation for Quantized Neural Networks · Locator: Section 2.2, Figure 3(b) and Table 2, p. 4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Because the SAWB regression coefficients (c_1, c_2) are calibrated on zero-mean symmetric distributions, strongly skewed or multimodal weight distributions can deviate from the predicted MSE-optimal clipping threshold.
