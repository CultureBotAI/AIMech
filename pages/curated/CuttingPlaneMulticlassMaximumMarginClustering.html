Cutting Plane Multiclass Maximum Margin Clustering

Algorithms AIMech:CuttingPlaneMulticlassMaximumMarginClustering DRAFT
Aliases: CPM3C
A fixed-kernel multiclass clustering procedure that fits latent winning-class margins by adding violated aggregate constraints and repeatedly solving quadratic programs with frozen class winners.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Zhao-Wang-Zhang2008 CPM3C, with a supplied feature map or kernel and supplied class count. Its scalar beta is regularization, not learned kernel-combination weights. It differs from the later multiple-kernel procedure, the binary intercept-bearing CPMMC solver, semidefinite label-matrix relaxations and supervised structured-output training. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #fixed-kernel-score-objective
For supplied k classes and feature vectors x_i, use intercept-free scores g_ip=w_p^T*x_i and regularization beta>0. Fit beta/2*sum_p ||w_p||^2+mean_i xi_i with latent winner-versus-competitor margins. For a unique winner p_i and runner-up r_i, the optimal slack at fixed scores is max(0,1-(g_i,p_i-g_i,r_i)). Impose |sum_i(g_ip-g_iq)|<=ell for every class pair; these aggregate score bounds do not impose class-count balance or ensure every class is occupied. Kernel-PCA coordinates are one suggested representation of a supplied kernel, not learned multiple-kernel weights.
  • supports Source: Efficient MultiClass Maximum Margin Clustering · Locator: Sections2.1-2.3, PDF pp.2-4, Equations1-17
Mechanism #cutting-plane-and-quadratic-fit
Aggregate slacks into a shared xi using masks c_i in {0,e_1,...,e_k}. Under a unique winner, the most violated constraint selects the runner-up one-hot vector when the top-runner gap is below 1 and the zero vector otherwise. Starting with an empty working set, fit its constraints, add the most violated mask and stop when violation is at most epsilon. Within a restricted fit, freeze previous winning-class indicators and solve the quadratic program with those indicators and the score-balance constraints; repeat under the stopping rule. The source uses a relative objective-change threshold of 0.01 percent in its experiments.
  • supports Source: Efficient MultiClass Maximum Margin Clustering · Locator: Sections2.2-2.4, PDF pp.3-5, Equations11-20 and Algorithms1-2
Limitation #indicator-and-convexity-limits
The strict winner product makes every indicator zero at a tied maximum, yielding label 0 outside {1,...,k}; tie handling is not fully specified. Moreover, the source claimed convex term includes a winner-dependent indicator and need not be convex: for one observation, mask e_1 and scores (x,0), it is max(x,0)+I[x>0]. At x=-0.2 and 0.2, a 3/4 mixture gives x=0.1 with value 1.1, exceeding the convex interpolation bound 0.9. These points have unique winners. Thus convexity of the frozen-indicator QP does not justify an unconditional global constrained concave-convex convergence or monotonicity claim for the printed nonconvex procedure.
  • supports Source: Efficient MultiClass Maximum Margin Clustering · Locator: Sections2.2 and2.4, PDF pp.2-5, strict indicators and Equations18-20; curator exact Jensen check
Limitation #tolerance-and-runtime-scope
With correct constraint separation and a feasible restricted solution, the epsilon test permits feasibility for all masks after increasing the shared slack by epsilon. This does not itself bound misclassification error or the gap to a global optimum. The stated per-iteration scaling assumes a constant working-set size and supplied feature sparsity; the source iteration-count and linear-runtime theorems explicitly concern k=2, while multiclass scaling is empirical. Nonconvex restricted fitting, feature construction and stopping assumptions prevent an unconditional end-to-end linear-time or optimality guarantee.
  • supports Source: Efficient MultiClass Maximum Margin Clustering · Locator: Sections2.5-3, PDF pp.5-6, Theorems4-7; Section4.4, PDF pp.7-8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • This record describes the printed working-set and frozen-winner procedure with explicit tie and nonconvexity limits. No unmentioned tie repair, globally optimal restricted solver, global optimum or universal convergence guarantee is silently supplied.
  • The class count, fixed kernel/features, regularization beta, score-balance bound and epsilon are supplied choices. Pairwise score balance is not class-count balance; the displayed formulation has no intercept.
