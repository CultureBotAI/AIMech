EKFAC (Eigenvalue-Corrected Kronecker Factorization)

Algorithms AIMech:EKFACEigenvalueCorrectedKroneckerFactorizationNaturalGradient DRAFT
Aliases: EKFAC, EKFAC-ra, Eigenvalue-Corrected K-FAC, Eigenvalue-Corrected Kronecker Factorization, Kronecker-Factored Eigenbasis Natural Gradient Descent
An approximate natural gradient optimization and Fisher information matrix factorization method that projects layer-wise parameter gradients into the orthogonal Kronecker-Factored Eigenbasis (KFE) U_A (x) U_B derived from input activation and backpropagated gradient covariances, and preconditions them using the Frobenius-optimal diagonal second-moment vector s*_i = E[((U_A (x) U_B)^T grad_theta)_i^2] tracked at every mini-batch.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from K-FAC (AIMech:KFacKroneckerFactoredApproximateCurvature), which approximates each layer's Fisher block as G_KFAC = A (x) B = (U_A (x) U_B)(S_A (x) S_B)(U_A (x) U_B)^T with eigenvalues restricted to the Kronecker product S_A (x) S_B; EKFAC keeps the Kronecker-Factored Eigenbasis U_A (x) U_B but replaces S_A (x) S_B with the unconstrained diagonal second-moment matrix S* in KFE coordinates, provably achieving ||G - G_EKFAC||_F <= ||G - G_KFAC||_F and enabling cheap per-iteration updates of S* without recomputing the eigendecomposition.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #ekfac-kfe-projection-and-eigenvalue-correction-mechanism
For each neural network layer l with input activation covariance A = E[h h^T] = U_A S_A U_A^T and backpropagated pre-activation gradient covariance B = E[delta delta^T] = U_B S_B U_B^T, EKFAC forms the orthogonal Kronecker-Factored Eigenbasis (KFE) U_A (x) U_B and defines the preconditioning matrix G_EKFAC = (U_A (x) U_B) S* (U_A (x) U_B)^T, where the diagonal entries S*_{ii} = s*_i = E[((U_A (x) U_B)^T grad_theta)_i^2] track the exact second moments of the gradient coordinates in the KFE basis, computing basis projections efficiently via (U_A (x) U_B)^T vec(C) = vec(U_B^T C U_A).
  • supports Source: Fast Approximate Natural Gradient Descent in a Kronecker-factored Eigenbasis · Locator: Section 3.1-3.2, pages 3-5, and Algorithm 1 with Footnote 1, page 6
Mechanism #ekfac-amortized-eigenbasis-partial-update-mechanism
While recomputing the eigendecompositions of A and B is amortized every n mini-batches, EKFAC (and its exponential moving average variant EKFAC-ra) updates the diagonal scaling vector s* in the fixed KFE basis at every mini-batch, preventing the eigenspectrum of the curvature estimate from drifting away from the true empirical Fisher matrix between basis recomputations.
  • supports Source: Fast Approximate Natural Gradient Descent in a Kronecker-factored Eigenbasis · Locator: Section 3.2 Figure 3, Algorithm 1, and Section 4.1 Figure 5, pages 4-8
Scope #ekfac-frobenius-optimality-over-kfac-scope
Lemma 1 and Theorems 2-3 prove that for any positive semi-definite matrix G = E[grad_theta grad_theta^T] and orthogonal KFE basis Q = U_A (x) U_B, the diagonal matrix D with entries D_{ii} = (Q^T G Q)_{ii} = E[(Q^T grad_theta)_i^2] uniquely minimizes ||G - Q D Q^T||_F over all diagonal matrices D, guaranteeing ||G - G_EKFAC||_F <= ||G - G_KFAC||_F everywhere.
  • supports Source: Fast Approximate Natural Gradient Descent in a Kronecker-factored Eigenbasis · Locator: Section 3.2 page 4 and Appendix A.1 Lemma 1, Theorem 2, and Theorem 3, page 12
Application #ekfac-deep-autoencoder-and-cnn-optimization-application
Applied to training an 8-layer deep MNIST auto-encoder and VGG11 and ResNet-34 convolutional neural networks on CIFAR-10 (using Spatially Uncorrelated Activations for convolutional layers), EKFAC and EKFAC-ra achieve lower training loss per epoch and faster wall-clock convergence than amortized KFAC and batch-normalized SGD with momentum.
  • supports Source: Fast Approximate Natural Gradient Descent in a Kronecker-factored Eigenbasis · Locator: Section 4.1-4.2 Figures 4-7, pages 7-9
Limitation #ekfac-damping-sensitivity-and-small-batch-overhead-limitation
EKFAC optimization performance is sensitive to the choice and initialization of the damping hyperparameter epsilon and learning rate eta, requires careful variance-preserving weight initialization when training deep residual networks without batch normalization, and shows diminished wall-clock speedup over KFAC at small mini-batch sizes on smaller networks.
  • supports Source: Fast Approximate Natural Gradient Descent in a Kronecker-factored Eigenbasis · Locator: Section 4.1 Figure 4(d) page 7, Section 4.2 page 9, and Appendices B, C, and D.1, pages 13-14

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Sensitive to the damping hyperparameter epsilon and learning rate eta, requiring factored eigenvalue damping initialization.
  • Training deep residual networks without batch normalization under EKFAC requires specialized variance-scaling initialization.
  • Per-iteration KFE projection overhead can offset optimization gains in wall-clock time when using small mini-batches on compact networks.
