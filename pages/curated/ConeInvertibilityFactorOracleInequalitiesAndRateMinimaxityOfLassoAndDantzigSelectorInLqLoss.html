Cone Invertibility Factor Oracle Inequalities and Rate Minimaxity of LASSO and Dantzig Selector in Lq Loss

Aliases: Cone Invertibility Factor (CIF) and Sign-Restricted CIF (SCIF), Ye-Zhang Cone Invertibility Factor and Lq Minimax Oracle Inequalities, Sign-Restricted Cone Invertibility Factor (SCIF) for LASSO and Dantzig Selector, Thresholded LASSO and Thresholded Dantzig Selector via L_infinity CIF Bounds
A sharp non-asymptotic Gram-matrix invertibility and minimax estimation framework (introduced by Fei Ye and Cun-Hui Zhang in 2010) that unifies and sharpens $\ell_q$-loss ($1 \le q \le \infty$) oracle inequalities for the LASSO and Dantzig selector across $\ell_0$ and weak/strong $\ell_r$ balls ($0 \le r \le 1$) via the Cone Invertibility Factor (CIF) $F_q(\xi, S) = \inf\left\{\frac{|S|^{1/q}\|\Sigma u\|_\infty}{\|u\|_q} : u \in \mathscr{C}(\xi, S) \setminus \{0\}\right\}$ and the Sign-Restricted Cone Invertibility Factor (SCIF) $SCIF_q(\xi, S) = \inf\left\{\frac{|S|^{1/q}\|\Sigma u\|_\infty}{\|u\|_q} : u \in \mathscr{C}_-(\xi, S) \setminus \{0\}\right\}$ on the sign-restricted cone $\mathscr{C}_-(\xi, S) = \{u : \|u_{S^c}\|_1 \le \xi\|u_S\|_1,\, u_j(\Sigma u)_j \le 0\, \forall j \notin S\}$ ($\Sigma = X^\top X / n$). Because the KKT and Dantzig score-dominance condition $\|z^*\|_\infty \le \eta$ ($z^* = X^\top(y - X\beta^*)/n$) automatically forces the estimation error $\delta = \hat{\beta} - \beta^*$ of both LASSO (with $\xi = (\lambda + \eta)/(\lambda - \eta)$) and the Dantzig selector (with $\xi = (\lambda_D + \eta)/\lambda_D$) into $\mathscr{C}_-(\xi, S)$ while bounding $\|\Sigma\delta\|_\infty \le \lambda + \eta$, SCIF yields the direct single-factor oracle bound $\|\hat{\beta} - \beta^*\|_q \le \frac{(\lambda + \eta)|S|^{1/q}}{SCIF_q(\xi, S)}$ (Theorem 3, Eq. 20) without squaring a restricted eigenvalue or multiplying two separate Gram invertibility constants, and matches the universal design-independent minimax lower bound $R_q^*(\mathscr{B}_r(\rho)) \asymp \rho^{q(1-1/t)}(\sigma^2\log(p/\tau_*)/n)^{q/(2t)}$ ($1/t = 1 - r/q$) across all $0 \le r \le 1 \le q \le \infty$ alongside thresholded LASSO ($\hat{\beta}^{TL}$) and Gauss-Dantzig ($\hat{\beta}^{GD}$) sign consistency under $q = \infty$.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Resolved against all 442 pinned AIO concepts and 659 maintained AIMech concepts. Distinct from `AIMech:RestrictedEigenvalueConditionAndBickelRitovTsybakovSimultaneousLassoDantzigOracleBounds` (Bickel, Ritov, & Tsybakov 2009, which defines the $\ell_2$ quadratic-form Restricted Eigenvalue $\kappa(s, c_0) = \min_{\delta \in \mathscr{C}(c_0, S)} \|X\delta\|_2 / (\sqrt{n}\|\delta_S\|_2)$ whose $\ell_q$ error bounds scale with $1/\kappa^2$ and an extra $(1+c_0)^{2-2/q}$ factor, whereas Ye & Zhang 2010 introduce the $\ell_\infty$-score-to-$\ell_q$ Cone Invertibility Factor $F_q(\xi, S)$ and Sign-Restricted Cone Invertibility Factor $SCIF_q(\xi, S)$ on $\mathscr{C}_-(\xi, S)$, proving $SCIF_2(\xi, S) \ge \psi(\xi, S)^2 \ge \kappa^2$ and eliminating the two-factor product gap as well as establishing universal design-free minimax lower bounds in $\ell_r$ balls for $0 \le r \le 1 \le q \le \infty$), distinct from `AIMech:CompatibilityConditionAndOracleInequalitiesForLassoInHighDimensionalGeneralizedLinearModels` (van de Geer 2007/2008, which introduces the $\ell_1$ compatibility constant $\phi_{\mathrm{comp}}(S)$ for empirical-process excess risk and $\ell_1$ loss), and distinct from `AIMech:DantzigSelectorLinearProgrammingSparseEstimator` (Candes & Tao 2007, which relies on uniform uncertainty principle / restricted isometry constants $\delta_{2s} + \theta_{s,2s} < 1$).

Mechanism #universal-design-free-minimax-lower-bounds-in-l0-and-lr-balls
For any design matrix $X \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times p}$ normalized to $\max_{j \le p} \|X_j\|_2^2 / n \le 1$ under $\varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 I_n)$, the minimax $\ell_q$ risk ($1 \le q \le \infty$) over the $\ell_0$ ball $\mathscr{B}_0(k) = \{\beta : \|\beta\|_0 \le k\}$ ($1 \le k < p/2$) is bounded below for every $X$ by $\inf_{\hat{\beta}} \sup_{\beta \in \mathscr{B}_0(k)} \mathbb{E}_{\beta}\|\hat{\beta} - \beta\|_q^q \ge a_q k(\sigma^2\log(p/k - 1)/n)^{q/2}$ with explicit constant $a_q = \sup_{0 < \varepsilon < 1} \left[(1 - \varepsilon)(1 - \sqrt{\varepsilon})^q / 2^{q/2 + 1}\right]$ ($a_2 = 1/4$ at $\varepsilon = 1/4$), and over $\ell_r$ balls $\mathscr{B}_r(\rho) = \{\beta : \|\beta\|_r \le \rho\}$ ($0 < r \le 1$) by $\inf_{\hat{\beta}} \sup_{\beta \in \mathscr{B}_r(\rho)} \mathbb{E}_{\beta}\|\hat{\beta} - \beta\|_q^q \ge a_q\min\left\{\rho^{q(1-1/t)}(\sigma^2\log(p/\tau_* - 1)/n)^{q/(2t)} + o(1),\, \rho^q\right\}$ where $1/t = 1 - r/q$ and $\tau_* = \rho^r(\sigma^2\log p / n)^{-r/2}$ (Theorem 1, Eqs. 7-8, p. 3522; Corollary 1, Eqs. 9-10, p. 3523; Appendix A, pp. 3532-3535).

Mechanism #sign-restricted-cone-invertibility-factor-and-unified-lasso-dantzig-oracle-inequalities
On the noise domination event $\|z^*\|_\infty \le \eta$ ($z^* = X^\top(y - X\beta^*)/n$), the estimation error $u = \hat{\beta} - \beta^*$ of both the LASSO ($\lambda > \eta$, $\xi = (\lambda + \eta)/(\lambda - \eta)$) and the Dantzig selector ($\lambda_D > 0$, $\xi = (\lambda_D + \eta)/\lambda_D$) belongs to the sign-restricted cone $\mathscr{C}_-(\xi, S) = \{u : \|u_{S^c}\|_1 \le \xi\|u_S\|_1,\, u_j(\Sigma u)_j \le 0\, \forall j \notin S\}$ and satisfies $\|\Sigma u\|_\infty \le \lambda + \eta$ (for LASSO) or $\lambda_D + \eta$ (for Dantzig), yielding the unified $\ell_q$ oracle inequality $\|\hat{\beta} - \beta^*\|_q \le \frac{(\lambda + \eta)|S|^{1/q}}{SCIF_q(\xi, S)}$ (Theorem 3, Eq. 20, p. 3526). Because $SCIF_q(\xi, S) \ge F_q(\xi, S)$ for all $q \ge 1$ and $SCIF_2(\xi, S) \ge \psi(\xi, S)^2$ where $\psi(\xi, S) = \inf_{u \in \mathscr{C}_-(\xi, S)} \sqrt{|S| u^\top \Sigma u}/(\|u_S\|_1 \|u\|_2)^{1/2}$ exceeds the Bickel-Ritov-Tsybakov restricted eigenvalue $\kappa(S, \xi)$ (Proposition 1, Eq. 18, p. 3525), Theorem 3 strictly improves upon two-factor RE bounds and extends to weak/strong $\ell_r$ balls with many small nonzero entries via the interpolation function $g_q(u; \xi) = \frac{(1 - u_S^\top \Sigma u / \|u\|_\Sigma^2)_+}{(\xi\|u_S\|_1/\|u_{S^c}\|_1 - 1)_+}$ and $CIF_q^*(\xi, S)$ (Theorem 4, Eqs. 33-34, p. 3529; Theorem 5, Eqs. 36-39, p. 3530).

Mechanism #linfinity-cif-variable-selection-consistency-of-thresholded-lasso-and-gauss-dantzig
Setting $q = \infty$ in Theorem 3 yields the $\ell_\infty$ error bound $\|\hat{\beta} - \beta^*\|_\infty \le \frac{\lambda + \eta}{SCIF_\infty(\xi, S)}$ without assuming the strong irrepresentable condition $\|\Sigma_{S^c, S}\Sigma_{S,S}^{-1}\operatorname{sign}(\beta_S^*)\|_\infty < 1$. Consequently, for any threshold level $t_* \ge \frac{\lambda + \eta}{SCIF_\infty(\xi, S)}$ and minimum signal strength $\min_{j \in S} |\beta_j^*| > t_* + \frac{\lambda + \eta}{SCIF_\infty(\xi, S)}$, both the Thresholded LASSO ($\hat{\beta}_j^{TL} = \hat{\beta}_j^L\mathbf{1}_{|\hat{\beta}_j^L| > t_*}$), the Thresholded Dantzig Selector ($\hat{\beta}_j^{TD} = \hat{\beta}_j^D \mathbf{1}_{|\hat{\beta}_j^D| > t_*}$), and the two-step Gauss-Dantzig estimator ($\hat{\beta}^{GD} = \arg\min_{\beta : \beta_j = 0 \forall j \notin \hat{S}^{TD}} \|y - X\beta\|_2^2$) achieve exact sign recovery $\operatorname{sign}(\hat{\beta}) = \operatorname{sign}(\beta^*)$ and exact oracle OLS refitting on $S$ (Section 3.3, Corollary 2, Eqs. 27-28, pp. 3527-3528).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Computing the exact Cone Invertibility Factor $F_q(\xi, S)$ or Sign-Restricted Cone Invertibility Factor $SCIF_q(\xi, S)$ over all subsets $S$ of cardinality $k$ is combinatorial in $p$, and for $q > 2$ the quantity $SCIF_q(\xi, S)$ can depend on $|S|$ for strongly correlated designs unless the Gram matrix $\Sigma = X^\top X / n$ has bounded off-diagonal coherence or sparse $\ell_\infty$ invertibility.
  • For weak/strong $\ell_r$ balls ($0 < r \le 1$) with many small nonzero coefficients where the threshold level is smaller than the universal $\sigma\sqrt{2\log p / n}$ threshold ($\tau_* = \rho^r(\sigma^2\log p / n)^{-r/2} \ge 2$), rate minimaxity in Theorem 5 requires the stronger interpolation condition $CIF_q^*(\xi, S) > 0$ (Eq. 34) together with a sparse maximal eigenvalue upper bound $\varphi_{\max}(m) \le C_{\max} < \infty$ on sub-Gram blocks of size $m \asymp \tau_*$.
  • Primary-source typographical audit of Ye & Zhang (2010, *JMLR* 11:3519-3540): (1) Section 3.3 heading (p. 3527) prints `'3.3 Vaiable Selection'` instead of `'3.3 Variable Selection'`; (2) Section 5 line 2 (p. 3531) cites `'Greenshtein and Rotiv, 2004'` instead of `'Greenshtein and Ritov, 2004'`; (3) Appendix A line 2 (p. 3532) prints `'Lemms are stated and proved as needed'` instead of `'Lemmas'`; and (4) Appendix A Proof of Theorem 1 after Eq. (57) (p. 3533, line 15) writes `$\{k/(1-\varepsilon)^r\}\mu^p/\lambda_{mm}^p = k(1-\varepsilon)^{q-r} \le k$' with exponent $p$ instead of $q$ in $\mu^q/\lambda_{mm}^q$.
