Ditto Fair and Robust Personalized Federated Multi-Task Learning

Algorithms AIMech:DittoFairAndRobustPersonalizedFederatedMultiTaskLearning DRAFT
Aliases: Ditto, Ditto Personalized Federated Learning, Global-Regularized Personalized Federated Multi-Task Learning
A decoupled bi-level personalized federated multi-task learning framework (Ditto) that resolves the tension between cross-device performance fairness (uniformity/low variance of benign device test errors) and Byzantine robustness (resistance to data/model poisoning) under statistical heterogeneity: min_{v_k in R^d} h_k(v_k; w^*) := F_k(v_k) + (lambda / 2) ||v_k - w^*||^2 subject to w^* in argmin_w G(F_1(w), ..., F_K(w)). Unlike coupled personalization schemes, the global objective G(.) is optimized for w^t completely independently of the local personalized models {v_k^t} while each device k interleaves local stochastic gradient steps on h_k(v_k; w^t) = F_k(v_k) + (lambda / 2)||v_k - w^t||^2, interpolating between pure local training (lambda = 0) and the global model (lambda -> infinity).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from pFedMe (AIMech:PFedMePersonalizedFederatedLearningWithMoreauEnvelopes, where the global model w minimizes the sum of Moreau envelopes F_i(w) and therefore depends directly on the local personalized iterates theta_i, making w vulnerable to local personalization state changes and requiring extra inner-loop steps before updating w) and from q-FFL (AIMech:QFairFederatedLearningAlphaFairResourceAllocation, which learns a single global model w whose fairness-oriented reweighting of high-loss devices degrades robustness under Byzantine corruption).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r397-bayes-optimal-fair-and-robust-lambda-characterization
In the hierarchical Bayesian linear/point estimation setting where benign parameters follow v_k ~ N(theta, tau^2), K_a corrupted devices follow v_k ~ N(theta, tau_a^2) with tau_a^2 > tau^2, and n local samples have noise variance sigma^2, Theorem 8 (and Lemmas 4, 6, 7) proves that the exact Bayes-optimal and maximally fair (zero cross-device test-error variance) regularization parameter is lambda^* = (sigma^2 K) / (tau^2 K + (tau_a^2 - tau^2) K_a + sigma^2 / n), showing that lambda^* decreases monotonically with stronger heterogeneity tau^2 or stronger corruption (K_a, tau_a^2).
  • supports Source: Ditto: Fair and Robust Federated Learning Through Personalization · Locator: Section 3.3 Theoretical Analysis of Ditto (Lemmas 4, 6, 7 and Theorem 8) and Appendix A, pp. 5-6, 13-17
Mechanism #r397-modularity-and-linear-speedup-convergence
Because the global subproblem w^* in argmin_w G(F_1(w),...,F_K(w)) is completely decoupled from {v_k}, Ditto preserves the exact communication footprint and privacy/robust-aggregation compatibility of the base global solver G, and Theorem 1 (Appendix B) proves that when w^t converges at rate O(1/t) on strongly convex F_k, the personalized iterates v_k^t converge to v_k^* at rate E||v_k^t - v_k^*||^2 = O(1/t) with linear speedup in local steps.
  • supports Source: Ditto: Fair and Robust Federated Learning Through Personalization · Locator: Section 3.2 Solver: Modularity and Efficiency (Algorithm 1, Theorem 1) and Appendix B, pp. 4-5, 19-21
Limitation #r397-local-state-persistence-and-lambda-tuning-limitation
Requires each participating device k to maintain a persistent local personalized model state v_k across rounds (or warm-start from w^*) and to tune the regularization hyperparameter lambda >= 0 based on the unknown degree of statistical heterogeneity and adversarial corruption.
  • supports Source: Ditto: Fair and Robust Federated Learning Through Personalization · Locator: Section 3.2 Algorithm 1, Section 3.3 Theorem 8, and Appendix C.2 Tuning lambda, pp. 4-6, 22
Application #r397-fairness-and-byzantine-defense-benchmarks-application
Evaluated on FEMNIST, CelebA, StackOverflow, and Fashion-MNIST under benign heterogeneity and three Byzantine attacks (A1 label poisoning, A2 random Gaussian updates, A3 model replacement), simultaneously reducing benign device test-accuracy variance (improving fairness) and boosting robust test accuracy by up to 40% when paired with Krum, Multi-Krum, or median aggregation.
  • supports Source: Ditto: Fair and Robust Federated Learning Through Personalization · Locator: Section 4 Experiments (Figures 3-8, Tables 1-2), pp. 6-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires stateful devices capable of storing personalized parameters v_k across communication rounds.
  • Optimal trade-off between local and global information requires tuning or estimating the regularization parameter lambda.
