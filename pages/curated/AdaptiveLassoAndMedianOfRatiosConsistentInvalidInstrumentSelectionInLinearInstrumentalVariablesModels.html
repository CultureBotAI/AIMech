Adaptive Lasso and Median-of-Ratios Consistent Invalid Instrument Selection in Linear Instrumental Variables Models

Aliases: Windmeijer Median-of-Ratios Adaptive Lasso Invalid IV Selection, Adaptive Lasso for Instrumental Variables with Invalid Instruments, Median-of-Ratios Initial Estimator and Adaptive sisVIVE, Consistent Invalid Instrument Selection via Median Estimator and Adaptive Lasso
A two-step consistent invalid-instrument selection and structural parameter estimation method (introduced by Frank Windmeijer, Helmut Farbmacher, Neil Davies, and George Davey Smith in 2019) for linear instrumental variables models $y = d\beta + Z\alpha + u$, $d = Z\gamma + v$ with $s < L/2$ invalid instruments ($\alpha_1 \ne 0$, $\alpha_2 = 0$). Standard unweighted $\ell_1$-penalized IV (sisVIVE, Kang et al. 2016) minimizes $\frac{1}{2n}\|P_Z(y - d\beta - Z\alpha)\|_2^2 + \lambda_n\|\alpha\|_1 = \frac{1}{2n}\|\widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}y - \widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}Z\alpha\|_2^2 + \lambda_n\|\alpha\|_1$. Proposition 1 proves that the projected instrument design matrix $C = \text{plim}\,n^{-1}Z'\widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}Z = Q - Q\gamma(\gamma'Q\gamma)^{-1}\gamma'Q$ has rank $L - 1$ with regression block $C_{21}C_{11}^{-1} = (I_{L-s} - \frac{\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2\gamma_2'}{\gamma_2'\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2})Q_{21}Q_{11}^{-1} - \frac{\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2\gamma_1'}{\gamma_2'\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2}$ where $\widetilde{Q}_{22} = Q_{22} - Q_{21}Q_{11}^{-1}Q_{12}$ (Equation 18). Proposition 2 establishes the exact structural identity $\gamma_2'C_{21}C_{11}^{-1} = -\gamma_1'$, which implies that for orthogonal instruments ($Q = I_L$), the Lasso irrepresentable condition $\|C_{21}C_{11}^{-1}\text{sign}(\alpha_1)\|_\infty < 1$ reduces to $\|\gamma_2\|_\infty |\gamma_1'\text{sign}(\alpha_1)| < \|\gamma_2\|_2^2$ and necessarily fails whenever invalid instruments have relatively large first-stage strengths $\gamma_1$ (Equations 19–22). Theorem 1 proves that under the plurality/majority rule $s < L/2$, the Median-of-Ratios estimator $\widehat{\beta}_m = \text{median}(\{\widehat{\Gamma}_j / \widehat{\gamma}_j\}_{j=1}^L)$ from the OLS reduced-form ratios $\pi_j = \Gamma_j / \gamma_j = \beta + \alpha_j / \gamma_j$ is $\sqrt{n}$-consistent with $\sqrt{n}(\widehat{\beta}_m - \beta) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_{m(l)}^2)$ where $l$ is the median order-statistic index. Plugging the initial consistent invalidness estimate $\widehat{\alpha}_m = \widehat{\Gamma} - \widehat{\gamma}\widehat{\beta}_m$ into the Adaptive Lasso objective $\widehat{\alpha}_{\text{ad}}^n = \arg\min_\alpha \frac{1}{2}\|\widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}y - \widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}Z\alpha\|_2^2 + \lambda_n\sum_{j=1}^L \frac{|\alpha_j|}{|\widehat{\alpha}_{m,j}|^\nu}$ (Theorem 2) achieves selection consistency $\mathbb{P}(\text{supp}(\widehat{\alpha}_{\text{ad}}^n) = \text{supp}(\alpha)) \to 1$ and oracle asymptotic normality $\sqrt{n}(\widehat{\beta}_{\text{post}} - \beta) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_u^2 / (\gamma_2'\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2))$ without any irrepresentable restriction on $C$.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-schur-complement-irrepresentable-condition-failure
For the projected instrument covariance $C = Q - Q\gamma(\gamma'Q\gamma)^{-1}\gamma'Q$ partitioned into $s$ invalid instruments ($Z_1$) and $L-s$ valid instruments ($Z_2$), Proposition 1 proves $C_{21}C_{11}^{-1} = (I_{L-s} - \frac{\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2\gamma_2'}{\gamma_2'\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2})Q_{21}Q_{11}^{-1} - \frac{\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2\gamma_1'}{\gamma_2'\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2}$ with Schur complement $\widetilde{Q}_{22} = Q_{22} - Q_{21}Q_{11}^{-1}Q_{12}$ (Equation 18), and Proposition 2 proves $\gamma_2'C_{21}C_{11}^{-1} = -\gamma_1'$, so for standardized orthogonal instruments ($Q = I_L$) the Lasso irrepresentable condition $\|C_{21}C_{11}^{-1}\text{sign}(\alpha_1)\|_\infty < 1$ simplifies to $|\gamma_1'\text{sign}(\alpha_1)| < \|\gamma_2\|_2^2 / \|\gamma_2\|_\infty$ and fails whenever the invalid instruments have strong first-stage effects $\gamma_1$ (Section 3.2, Propositions 1–2, Equations 18–22).
  • supports Source: On the Use of the Lasso for Instrumental Variables Estimation with Some Invalid Instruments · Locator: Section 3.2, Propositions 1-2, Equations (18)-(22), Appendix A.1
Mechanism #claim-median-of-ratios-root-n-consistency-under-majority-rule
Under the majority validity rule $s < L/2$ with $\gamma_j \ne 0$ for all $j \in \{1,\dots,L\}$, Theorem 1 proves that the Median-of-Ratios estimator $\widehat{\beta}_m = \text{median}(\{\widehat{\pi}_j\}_{j=1}^L)$ where $\widehat{\pi}_j = \widehat{\Gamma}_j / \widehat{\gamma}_j$ is the ratio of the $j$-th OLS reduced-form coefficient to the $j$-th OLS first-stage coefficient is $\sqrt{n}$-consistent for $\beta$ and converges in distribution to the normal order-statistic limit $\sqrt{n}(\widehat{\beta}_m - \beta) \xrightarrow{d} q_{[l],L-s}$ (Section 4.1, Theorem 1, Equations 23–24, Appendix A.2).
  • supports Source: On the Use of the Lasso for Instrumental Variables Estimation with Some Invalid Instruments · Locator: Section 4.1, Theorem 1, Equations (23)-(24), Appendix A.2
Mechanism #claim-adaptive-lasso-oracle-instrument-selection-and-post-lasso-2sls
Using $\widehat{\alpha}_m = \widehat{\Gamma} - \widehat{\gamma}\widehat{\beta}_m$ as the $\sqrt{n}$-consistent initial estimator in the Adaptive Lasso objective $\widehat{\alpha}_{\text{ad}}^n = \arg\min_\alpha \frac{1}{2}\|\widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}y - \widehat{M}_{\widehat{d}}Z\alpha\|_2^2 + \lambda_n^a\sum_{j=1}^L |\alpha_j| / |\widehat{\alpha}_{m,j}|^\nu$ with $\lambda_n^a / \sqrt{n} \to 0$ and $\lambda_n^a n^{(\nu-1)/2} \to \infty$ for $\nu > 0$, Theorem 2 proves $\mathbb{P}(\text{supp}(\widehat{\alpha}_{\text{ad}}^n) = \{1,\dots,s\}) \to 1$ without any irrepresentable condition on $C$, and the resulting post-Adaptive-Lasso two-stage least squares estimator $\widehat{\beta}_{\text{post}}$ achieves the oracle asymptotic distribution $\sqrt{n}(\widehat{\beta}_{\text{post}} - \beta) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_u^2 / (\gamma_2'\widetilde{Q}_{22}\gamma_2))$ (Section 4.2, Theorem 2, Equation 25).
  • supports Source: On the Use of the Lasso for Instrumental Variables Estimation with Some Invalid Instruments · Locator: Section 4.2, Theorem 2, Equation (25)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Consistency of the initial Median-of-Ratios estimator beta_hat_m (Theorem 1) requires the strict majority rule s < L/2 (more than 50% of candidate instruments must be valid); when s >= L/2 and only a plurality of instruments are valid, beta_hat_m is asymptotically biased unless replaced by a mode-based or voting-based initial estimator.
  • Theorem 1 and Theorem 2 are fixed-L asymptotic results assuming individually strong instruments (gamma_j != 0 fixed as n -> infty); when invalid instruments are locally invalid (alpha_j = O(1/sqrt(n))) or first-stage effects gamma_j are weak, consistent model selection fails and post-Adaptive-Lasso 2SLS confidence intervals can under-cover.
