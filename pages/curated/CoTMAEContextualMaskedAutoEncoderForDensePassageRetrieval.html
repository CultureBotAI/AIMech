CoT-MAE Contextual Masked Auto-Encoder for Dense Passage Retrieval

Architectures AIMech:CoTMAEContextualMaskedAutoEncoderForDensePassageRetrieval DRAFT
Aliases: CoT-MAE, ConTextual Masked Auto-Encoder, Contextual Masked Auto-Encoder for Dense Passage Retrieval
An asymmetric encoder-decoder generative pre-training architecture and algorithm for dense passage retrieval that samples pairs of text spans (T_A, T_B) from each document using a uniform mixture of adjacent non-overlapping (Near), partially overlapping (Olap), and random non-overlapping (Rand) span-pair strategies, applies asymmetric token masking with rate p_enc (e.g., 30%) on the deep Transformer encoder side and a higher rate p_dec (e.g., 45%) on the shallow (2-layer) Transformer decoder side, and jointly minimizes the symmetric dual sum of self-supervised masked language modeling and cross-span context-supervised masked language modeling losses L = L^{AB} + L^{BA} = (L^A_smlm + L^{AB}_cmlm) + (L^B_smlm + L^{BA}_cmlm), where L^A_smlm = -sum_{t in m_enc(T_A)} log p(t | T_A \ m_enc(T_A)) and L^{AB}_cmlm = -sum_{t in m_dec(T_B)} log p(t | [h^{last}_{0, A}, T_B \ m_dec(T_B)]) conditions the decoder's reconstruction of masked tokens in T_B on the encoder's last-layer [CLS] context representation h^{last}_{0, A} of neighboring span T_A (and symmetrically for B -> A) before discarding the decoder at fine-tuning time.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from SEED-Encoder (AIMech:SEEDPreTrainingStrongTextEncoderUsingWeakDecoder), Condenser (AIMech:CondenserPreTrainingArchitectureForDenseRetrieval), RetroMAE (AIMech:RetroMAERetrievalOrientedMaskedAutoEncoder), and SimLM (AIMech:SimLMRepresentationBottleneckPreTraining), all of which perform single-span internal auto-encoding where the decoder reconstructs the same text span encoded into [CLS] rather than cross-span context-supervised masked auto-encoding over paired neighboring spans (T_A, T_B); and distinct from coCondenser (AIMech:CoCondenserUnsupervisedCorpusAwareLanguageModelPreTraining), which uses a discriminative corpus-level contrastive loss across spans rather than generative context-MLM reconstruction.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #cot-mae-dual-self-and-context-supervised-mlm
CoT-MAE links intra-span token semantics and inter-span semantic correlation by jointly optimizing self-supervised MLM on the deep encoder (L^A_smlm = -sum_{t in m(T_A)} log p(t | T_A \ m(T_A)), Equation 4) and context-supervised MLM on the shallow 2-layer decoder conditioned on the neighboring span's last-layer [CLS] vector h^{last}_0 (L^{AB}_cmlm = -sum_{t in m(T_B)} log p(t | [h^{last}_0, T_B \ m(T_B)]), Equation 5) symmetrically in both directions L = (L^A_smlm + L^{AB}_cmlm) + (L^B_smlm + L^{BA}_cmlm) (Equations 6-8).

  • supports Source: ConTextual Masked Auto-Encoder for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: main() verification of symmetric dual loss L = L^{AB} + L^{BA} and reduction of L^{AB}_{cmlm} under aligned neighboring span [CLS] context
Mechanism #cot-mae-asymmetric-masking-and-mixed-span-sampling
Combining all three span-pair sampling strategies (Near, Olap, and Rand) with asymmetric masking rates (p_enc = 30% on the encoder and p_dec = 45% on the 2-layer decoder) maximizes downstream retrieval accuracy (39.2 MRR@10 at 800k steps in Tables 3-5), whereas either a 1-layer decoder (too weak to fuse context and unmasked tokens) or a deeper 3-to-6-layer decoder (reducing reliance on the encoder's [CLS] context vector) underperforms the 2-layer decoder.

  • supports Source: ConTextual Masked Auto-Encoder for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: main() verification of Near, Olap, and Rand span-pair properties and asymmetric mask counts (30% vs 45%)
Application #cot-mae-benchmark-applications
On MS-MARCO Passage Ranking, Natural Questions (NQ), and TREC DL 2020, CoT-MAE achieves 39.4 MRR@10 and 98.7% R@1k without distillation (exceeding coCondenser's 38.2 MRR@10 and reaching 70.4 nDCG@10 on TREC DL 2020 in Table 1), and reaches 40.4 MRR@10 and 88.5% R@50 after reranker distillation (Table 2).

Limitation #cot-mae-excessive-encoder-masking-limitation
Increasing the encoder masking rate too aggressively (e.g., p_enc = 45% with p_dec = 60% in Table 3) degrades downstream retrieval performance (from 39.2 to 38.8 MRR@10) because the heavily masked encoder span can no longer provide a sufficiently informative [CLS] context vector to the decoder.
  • supports Source: ConTextual Masked Auto-Encoder for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section Analysis: Impact of Mask Rate, Table 3, p. 6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Excessive encoder masking (e.g., 45% encoder mask rate) degrades the quality of the [CLS] context representation passed to the decoder and reduces downstream retrieval MRR (Table 3).
  • Requires multi-span document segmentation (via sentence tokenization and grouping) so that every document yields multiple neighboring spans (T_A, T_B), and full pre-training for 1,200k steps with batch size 1,024 takes 4 days on 8 A100 GPUs.
