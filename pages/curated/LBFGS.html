Limited-memory BFGS

Algorithms AIMech:LBFGS DRAFT
Aliases: L-BFGS, Limited-memory Broyden-Fletcher-Goldfarb-Shanno
A limited-memory quasi-Newton optimization method using a bounded history of parameter and gradient differences to compute approximate inverse-Hessian search directions.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope L-BFGS is an optimization algorithm, not a neural architecture; bounded curvature history distinguishes it from full-memory BFGS.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #update-rule
The implementation retains a bounded set of curvature pairs and applies a two-loop recursion to obtain a direction from the gradient without storing a dense inverse Hessian.
