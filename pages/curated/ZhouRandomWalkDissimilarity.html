Zhou random-walk distance-profile dissimilarity

Aliases: Zhou neighbor dissimilarity index
A pairwise graph dissimilarity that compares the mean first-passage-time profiles of two adjacent vertices by taking the Euclidean norm of their differences to all other vertices and dividing that norm by N-2, for a graph of N vertices.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

6 Claims
Scope #walk-and-profile-domain
The maintained domain is a finite connected undirected graph with N>2, symmetric nonnegative adjacency weights and positive vertex strengths. A walker moves from i to j with probability A_ij/sum_l A_il. For each distinct adjacent pair (i,j), the functional compares d_ik and d_jk for every k other than i and j, where d is the mean first-hitting-time matrix on the entire input graph. Unweighted graphs are a special case. The endpoint coordinates are omitted, so diagonal self-return conventions do not affect this index.
  • supports Source: Distance, dissimilarity index, and network community structure · Locator: Section II, PDF p. 2; preceding distance construction in the inspected Zhou attractor paper, Equation 1, p. 2
  • supports Source: Network Landscape from a Brownian Particle’s Perspective · Locator: Graph and transition specification, PDF p. 1; Equation 1 and first-passage linear system, p. 2
Mechanism #exact-source-normalization
In the inspected arXiv v1, Lambda(i,j)=sqrt(sum_{k not in {i,j}}(d_ik-d_jk)^2)/(N-2). The denominator lies outside the square root. Root-mean-square normalization would divide the same norm by sqrt(N-2), yielding a value sqrt(N-2) times larger. On a four-cycle the source index squared is 1/2, whereas the root-mean-square value squared is 1. No equivalence between these numerical threshold scales is asserted.
  • supports Source: Distance, dissimilarity index, and network community structure · Locator: Section II, Equation 1, PDF p. 2; four-cycle arithmetic in research/discovery/zhou-walk-boundaries.py
Limitation #symmetric-nonseparating-score
The index is symmetric and nonnegative even though its input passage times are generally asymmetric. It need not be positive for different vertices: two vertices with identical hitting profiles to all remaining vertices have value zero, including any pair in an unweighted complete four-vertex graph. The source defines it on adjacent pairs; extending it to all pairs or asserting metric separation or a triangle inequality requires additional justification.
  • supports Source: Distance, dissimilarity index, and network community structure · Locator: Section II, PDF p. 2; exact complete-graph calculation in research/discovery/zhou-walk-boundaries.py
Mechanism #global-dynamics-not-local-overlap
The compared profiles depend on whole-graph random-walk passage, not only shared-neighbor counts, shortest-path lengths or stationary visit probabilities. Multiplying every adjacency weight by one common positive factor leaves the transition matrix, all mean passage times and the index unchanged. Computing full-network distances before later subdivision is distinct from recomputing them within each induced subgraph.
  • supports Source: Distance, dissimilarity index, and network community structure · Locator: Section II and opening of Section III, PDF p. 2; transition specification in the Zhou attractor paper, PDF pp. 1–2; common-weight rescaling calculation in research/discovery/zhou-walk-boundaries.py
  • supports Source: Network Landscape from a Brownian Particle’s Perspective · Locator: Graph and transition specification, PDF p. 1; Equation 1 and first-passage linear system, p. 2
Limitation #hierarchy-algorithm-is-separate
The source’s separate hierarchy procedure thresholds these indices into friend sets and simultaneously reassigns unstable vertices by total interaction. This functional alone defines neither a partition nor guaranteed hierarchy termination. A curator complete four-vertex weighted graph with within-pair weights 1 and cross-pair weights 2 has zero within-pair indices and cross-pair index squared 25/288. A threshold below the latter index yields two friend pairs; each node has own-group weight 1 and other-group weight 4. Simultaneous moves swap all group labels, leaving every node unstable and preventing the printed step-4 stopping condition. This is a scoped counterexample to unconditional termination of that printed adjustment rule, not an execution of author software.
  • supports Source: Distance, dissimilarity index, and network community structure · Locator: Section III, steps 2–5, PDF p. 2; exact synchronous-update construction in research/discovery/zhou-walk-boundaries.py
Limitation #interpretation-and-version-limits
The source interprets small index values as similar graph perspectives and uses them in historical synthetic, social and yeast-network clustering examples. Its performance and protein-function hypotheses concern the full hierarchy pipeline and specific data, not independent validation of this pairwise functional or causal biology. This record follows the inspected arXiv v1 normalization; journal-byte equivalence and version-specific numerical changes were not established.
  • supports Source: Distance, dissimilarity index, and network community structure · Locator: Section II, p. 2; applications, pp. 3–9; conclusions, pp. 7 and 9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The maintained identity is the adjacent-pair dissimilarity functional, not the complete threshold hierarchy, attractor communities, a learned graph representation or a node centrality.
  • N>2 and connected positive-strength input are explicit. Disconnected cross-component passage, zero denominators and directed generalizations are outside scope.
  • The source denominator is outside the radical. Numerical thresholds cannot be copied from a root-mean-square convention without rescaling.
  • Tie policy, friendless vertices, exhausted thresholds and synchronous reassignment termination require separate algorithm assessment. The finite cycling example preserves the source’s simultaneous update rule.
  • All ten source pages were read as text, with the defining equation and algorithm steps inspected visually; other displays remain text-only. No author code, raw benchmark replication, journal-byte equivalence or biological hypothesis validation. DRAFT remains.
