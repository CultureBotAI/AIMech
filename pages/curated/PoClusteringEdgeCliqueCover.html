PoClustering edge-clique-cover heuristic

An approximate PoClustering algorithm that incrementally extends and prunes clique sets to cover the edges of dissimilarity-threshold graphs, retaining a potentially smaller overlapping cluster family than exhaustive maximal-clique enumeration.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

6 Claims
Mechanism #incremental-edge-cover
Process dissimilarity-ordered edge insertions while maintaining a family of maximal cliques that covers the current graph edges. The described heuristic extends newly affected sets into maximal cliques and sequentially removes cliques whose edges are already covered. The retained sets support an overlapping cluster-inclusion structure.
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Section 5, Definition 5.1 and Algorithm 1 with accompanying prose, PDF pp. 4–5
Scope #heuristic-not-minimum-solver
The stated goal is a small edge-clique cover, but the described maximal extension and sequential redundancy removal are greedy. They are not an exact minimum-cardinality cover solver and do not enumerate every maximal clique. Distinct choices can affect which cluster sets are retained.
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Section 5 and Algorithm 1, PDF pp. 4–5
Scope #coverage-versus-family-recovery
Covering every threshold-graph edge with retained clique sets can preserve each pair’s dissimilarity when the corresponding cluster diameters and threshold witnesses are retained. This is distinct from recovering the complete exact PoCluster family. A constructed six-vertex graph has eight maximal triangles but a four-triangle cover of the same twelve edges; the example is not a claimed output of the published greedy routine.
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Section 4, pairwise coverage discussion, p. 3; Section 5 and Definition 5.1, pp. 4–5
Limitation #printed-pseudocode
The displayed routine forms intersections of old endpoint cliques without explicitly adding the new edge endpoints, and its argmin constraint names the original cover rather than the candidate subset. Read literally, those expressions do not specify the prose’s extension and coverage-preserving pruning. The record follows the described heuristic while declining conformance claims for the literal pseudocode or an uninspected implementation.
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Algorithm 1 and its explanatory paragraphs, PDF p. 5
Scope #complexity-conditions
The paper gives an edge-count-based polynomial accounting for its intended greedy updates, with at most O(n²) edge insertions and costs depending on the maintained cover size. This is an analysis of the stated restricted update procedure, not the complexity of an exact minimum-cover solver or a reproduced wall-clock guarantee.
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Section 5, complexity paragraphs, PDF p. 5
Scope #evaluation-scope
Reported experiments compare the heuristic with hierarchical and pyramidal clustering on generated overlapping set families. The match score averages each reference set’s best Jaccard match and is directional; it does not by itself measure precision of all output sets. Exact-method timings are omitted because of long runs, the heuristic is slower than the plotted baselines, and real-data experiments are deferred to an uninspected technical report.
  • supports Source: PoClustering: Lossless Clustering of Dissimilarity Data · Locator: Section 6, match score and Figure 4, PDF pp. 5–6; reference 12, p. 6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • This is the Liu et al. PoClustering ECC heuristic, not arbitrary edge-clique-cover optimization, exact PoClustering, or error-correcting coding.
  • The record does not certify a minimum cover, an approximation ratio, order independence or literal pseudocode correctness. Coverage preservation requires the stated threshold/diameter information.
  • The conference paper’s broader bijection proof and real-data experiments are deferred to an unread technical report; no universal lossless-family or biological performance claim is adopted.
  • The synthetic generator leaves some details, including the treatment of normally distributed overlap probabilities outside [0,1], unspecified. No author code or experimental data were inspected or executed.
  • All six pages of the primary 2007 paper were read, including figures, algorithm and references; the 2006 exact method was read as context. Finite constructed checks are not author implementation runs or benchmark replication. This record remains DRAFT.
