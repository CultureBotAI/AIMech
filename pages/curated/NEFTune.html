NEFTune

Aliases: Noisy Embedding Instruction Finetuning, NEFT Noisy Embedding Finetuning, Scaled Uniform Embedding Noise Instruction Finetuning
A regularization algorithm for language model instruction fine-tuning that perturbs input token embedding tensors X_emb in R^{B x L x d} during each training forward pass by adding i.i.d. uniform noise epsilon ~ Uniform(-1, 1) scaled by alpha / sqrt(L d), where L is sequence length, d is embedding dimension, and alpha > 0 is a tunable noise magnitude.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from adversarial embedding perturbation methods such as FreeLB (which require inner-loop gradient ascent steps to compute worst-case adversarial perturbations) and from parameter-space noise injection or dropout. NEFTune (Noisy Embedding Instruction Fine Tuning; Neel Jain, Ping-yeh Chiang, Yuxin Wen, John Kirchenbauer, Hong-Min Chu, Gowthami Somepalli, Brian R. Bartoldson, Bhavya Kailkhura, Avi Schwarzschild, Aniruddha Saha, Micah Goldblum, Jonas Geiping, & Tom Goldstein, ICLR 2024, arXiv:2310.05914) samples a single zero-overhead random vector epsilon ~ Uniform(-1, 1)^{B x L x d} per forward pass and computes X'_emb = X_emb + (alpha / sqrt(L d)) epsilon (Algorithm 1). Because E[epsilon_{t,j}^2] = 1/3, the perturbation has expected Euclidean norm alpha / sqrt(3) independent of sequence length L and embedding dimension d, reducing overfitting to the exact phrasing and length formatting of small instruction datasets without degrading factual knowledge or token-level nearest-neighbor semantics.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #neftune-length-and-dimension-invariant-uniform-embedding-noise-scaling
For a token sequence of length L and embedding dimension d, sampling epsilon ~ Uniform(-1, 1)^{L x d} i.i.d. and forming X'_emb = X_emb + (alpha / sqrt(L d)) epsilon produces a non-adversarial perturbation with expected Euclidean norm alpha / sqrt(3) independent of L and d and zero additional backward passes.
  • supports Source: NEFTune: Noisy Embeddings Improve Instruction Finetuning · Locator: Section 2 (NEFTune: Noisy Embedding Instruction Finetuning) & Algorithm 1, pp. 2-3
Mechanism #neftune-instruction-overfitting-mitigation-and-intra-token-preservation
NEFTune increases training loss on the instruction dataset while lowering held-out test loss and training-prompt ROUGE-L/BLEU memorization scores (preventing verbatim lock-in to training responses), while maintaining <= 0.4% nearest-neighbor vocabulary token flips and preserving n-gram log-diversity.
  • supports Source: NEFTune: Noisy Embeddings Improve Instruction Finetuning · Locator: Section 5.1-5.2 (Figures 4-5, Table 4) & Appendix A.3 (Figures 6-8), pp. 7-8, 14-16
Application #neftune-application-llama2-opt-qlora-instruction-tuning
Applied to LLaMA-2-7B across Alpaca, Evol-Instruct, ShareGPT, and OpenPlatypus, NEFTune boosts GPT-4 AlpacaEval win rate by +15.1 percentage points on average (including 29.79% -> 64.69% on Alpaca), improves RLHF-tuned LLaMA-2-Chat-7B (+7.30%) and LLaMA-2-70B (+13.78%), and improves QLoRA fine-tuning while preserving OpenLLM Leaderboard accuracy on MMLU, ARC, HellaSwag, and TruthfulQA.
  • supports Source: NEFTune: Noisy Embeddings Improve Instruction Finetuning · Locator: Section 4 (Tables 1-3, Figures 1-3), pp. 1, 4-6
Limitation #neftune-limitation-verbosity-shift-and-safety-refusal-tradeoff
NEFTune induces substantially longer, more verbose completions (which correlate with LLM-judge preferences on AlpacaEval), yields smaller gains under 4-bit QLoRA than under full-parameter fine-tuning, and can affect safety-aligned checkpoints' refusal behavior against toxic outputs if applied without safety re-alignment.
  • supports Source: NEFTune: Noisy Embeddings Improve Instruction Finetuning · Locator: Section 4 (p. 4-5), Section 5.2-5.3 (pp. 7-8), Section 6-7 (pp. 9-10)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Significantly increases output response length and verbosity, which can confound length-sensitive automatic evaluators such as GPT-4 AlpacaEval.
  • Further fine-tuning RLHF-aligned chat models with NEFTune on instruction datasets without safety data can degrade refusal behavior against toxic prompts.
  • Provides milder improvements under 4-bit QLoRA quantization than under full-parameter fine-tuning.
