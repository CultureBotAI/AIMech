DoReFa-Net (Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients)

Algorithms AIMech:DoReFaNetTrainingLowBitwidthConvolutionalNeuralNetworksWithLowBitwidthGradients DRAFT
Aliases: DoReFa-Net, DoReFa-Net Low-Bitwidth Gradient Quantization, Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients
DoReFa-Net (Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients) is a low-bitwidth neural network training and inference framework that executes both forward and backward passes via bitwise AND and popcount convolution kernels (Eq. 3) by deterministically quantizing weights to k_w bits via tanh-normalized uniform quantization f_omega^k(r_i) = 2 * quantize_k(tanh(r_i) / (2 * max(|tanh(r_i)|)) + 0.5) - 1 (or layer-mean scaling E(|r_i|) * sign(r_i) for 1-bit weights), deterministically quantizing [0, 1]-bounded activations to k_a bits via f_alpha^k(r) = quantize_k(r), and stochastically quantizing backpropagated activation gradients dr to k_g bits via per-instance max-abs scaling and additive uniform noise N(k) = sigma / (2^k - 1) with sigma ~ Uniform(-0.5, 0.5).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from PACT (AIMech:PACTParameterizedClippingActivationForQuantizedNeuralNetworks) which learns a parameterized activation clipping bound alpha with full-precision gradients rather than fixed [0, 1] clipping with stochastic low-bitwidth gradient quantization, and distinct from TTQ (AIMech:TTQTrainedTernaryQuantization) which learns asymmetric ternary weight scales {-W_l^n, 0, +W_l^p}.

Mechanism #dorefa-net-bitwise-dot-product-and-tanh-weight-activation-quantization
DoReFa-Net decomposes fixed-point dot products between M-bit and K-bit vectors into bitwise AND and bitcount operations sum_{m=0}^{M-1} sum_{k=0}^{K-1} 2^{m+k} bitcount[and(c_m(x), c_k(y))] (Eq. 3), deterministically quantizes k-bit weights via f_omega^k(r_i) = 2 * quantize_k(tanh(r_i) / (2 * max(|tanh(r_i)|)) + 0.5) - 1 (Eq. 9; or sign(r_i) * E(|r_i|) for k = 1, Eq. 7), and quantizes [0, 1]-clipped activations via f_alpha^k(r) = quantize_k(r) (Eq. 11) with Straight-Through Estimator backpropagation (Eqs. 5-6).
  • supports Source: DoReFa-Net: Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients · Locator: Section 2.1-2.3.2, Equations 1-11, pp. 2-4
  • supports Source: DoReFa-Net: Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients · Locator: bitwise_dot_uint2 and dorefa_weight_quantize
Mechanism #dorefa-net-stochastic-unbiased-gradient-quantization-and-maxpool-fusion
Because backpropagated activation gradients dr are unbounded and span a wide dynamic range, DoReFa-Net maps dr into [0, 1] per sample via max_0(|dr|), injects uniform dithering noise N(k) = sigma / (2^k - 1) with sigma ~ Uniform(-0.5, 0.5) to yield unbiased k-bit stochastic gradients f_gamma^k(dr) = 2 * max_0(|dr|) * [quantize_k(dr / (2 * max_0(|dr|)) + 0.5 + N(k)) - 0.5] (Eqs. 12-14), and commutes activation quantization past max-pooling via quantize_k(max(a, b)) = max(quantize_k(a), quantize_k(b)) (Eq. 15).
  • supports Source: DoReFa-Net: Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients · Locator: Section 2.3.3-2.5, Equations 12-15 and Algorithm 1, pp. 4-6
  • supports Source: DoReFa-Net: Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients · Locator: dorefa_grad_quantize and main
Application #dorefa-net-1bit-weight-2bit-activation-6bit-gradient-imagenet-training
On SVHN, CIFAR-10, and ImageNet (AlexNet), DoReFa-Net trains convolutional networks with 1-bit weights, 2-bit activations, and 6-bit stochastic gradients (or 8-bit gradients with <4-bit weights/activations) while maintaining competitive classification accuracy and enabling bitwise acceleration on CPU, FPGA, and ASIC platforms.
  • supports Source: DoReFa-Net: Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients · Locator: Section 3 (Experiment Results), Tables 1-3 and Figures 2-4, pp. 6-9
Limitation #dorefa-net-first-last-layer-full-precision-and-sub-4bit-gradient-collapse
DoReFa-Net keeps the first convolutional layer and final classification layer unquantized because quantizing them significantly degrades accuracy (Section 2.6), and quantizing backpropagated gradients below 4 bits (e.g., 2-bit gradients) causes substantial accuracy degradation.
  • supports Source: DoReFa-Net: Training Low Bitwidth Convolutional Neural Networks with Low Bitwidth Gradients · Locator: Section 2.6 (First and the Last Layer, p. 5) and Section 3.1 (Table 1, p. 7)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires leaving the first convolutional layer and the final classification layer in higher precision to avoid substantial accuracy drops.
  • Backpropagated gradient quantization below 4 bits causes significant accuracy degradation even with stochastic dithering.
