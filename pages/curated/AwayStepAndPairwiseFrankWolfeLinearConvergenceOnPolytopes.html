Away-Step and Pairwise Frank-Wolfe Linear Convergence on Polytopes

Algorithms AIMech:AwayStepAndPairwiseFrankWolfeLinearConvergenceOnPolytopes DRAFT
Aliases: Lacoste-Julien and Jaggi Global Linear Convergence of Frank-Wolfe Variants, Away-Steps Frank-Wolfe (AFW) and Pairwise Frank-Wolfe (PFW), Pyramidal Width Affine-Invariant Polytope Conditioning for Conditional Gradient
Away-Step and Pairwise Frank-Wolfe Linear Convergence on Polytopes is a projection-free conditional gradient framework and affine-invariant geometric convergence theory introduced by Lacoste-Julien and Jaggi (2015) for minimizing a mu-strongly convex, L-smooth function f over a compact polytope M = conv(A) subseteq R^d. Whereas classical Frank-Wolfe (conditional gradient) zig-zags when the constrained optimum x^* lies on a boundary face of M (suffering an unavoidable Omega(1 / t^{1+epsilon}) sublinear rate), Away-Steps Frank-Wolfe (AFW, Algorithm 1) and Pairwise Frank-Wolfe (PFW, Algorithm 2) maintain an explicit active atom set S^{(t)} subseteq A with strict convex weights alpha_v^{(t)} > 0 for x^{(t)} = sum_{v in S^{(t)}} alpha_v^{(t)} v and query both the standard FW atom s_t = argmin_{v in A} <grad f(x^{(t)}), v> and the worst active away atom v_t = argmax_{v in S^{(t)}} <grad f(x^{(t)}), v>. AFW chooses the larger of the FW gap <grad f(x^{(t)}), x^{(t)} - s_t> and the away gap <grad f(x^{(t)}), v_t - x^{(t)}> (with maximum step gamma_max = alpha_{v_t}^{(t)} / (1 - alpha_{v_t}^{(t)})), while PFW moves weight directly along d_t^{PFW} = s_t - v_t (with gamma_max = alpha_{v_t}^{(t)}). By establishing the Geometric Strong Convexity bound h_t <= <grad f(x^{(t)}), v_t - s_t>^2 / (2 mu_f^G) in terms of the polytope's Pyramidal Width delta = PWidth(A) > 0 (Theorem 3, Lemma 4, and Theorem 6) and bounding the number of non-halving 'drop steps' (which remove an atom from S^{(t)}) by at most half the total steps, Theorem 1 and Theorem 8 prove that both AFW and PFW achieve global linear convergence h_t = f(x^{(t)}) - f(x^*) <= h_0 (1 - rho)^t regardless of whether x^* lies in the interior or on a low-dimensional boundary face of M.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from standard Frank-Wolfe / Conditional Gradient, which moves only toward vertices s_t in A and stagnates at O(1/t) when the optimum lies on a facet, and from projected gradient methods such as Catalyst or Soft-Impute, which require Euclidean or Bregman projections onto M at every step rather than linear minimization oracle (LMO) calls. Specifically captures the active-set Away-Steps (Algorithm 1) and Pairwise (Algorithm 2) conditional gradient updates, the drop-step amortisation bound, the Pyramidal Width PWidth(A) and Geometric Strong Convexity mu_f^G = mu * delta^2 (Theorem 6), and the exact simplex/cube pyramidal widths (Section 3.1 and Lemma 4).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-01
For any finite atom set A subseteq R^d, the Pyramidal Width PWidth(A) > 0 is strictly positive and lower-bounded by the minimal width over all subsets of atoms (Theorem 3); for the unit d-dimensional probability simplex conv(e_1, ..., e_d), PWidth(A) = 2 / sqrt(d) if d is even and 2 / sqrt(d - 1/d) if d is odd (Section 3.1), while for the unit hypercube [0, 1]^d, PWidth({0, 1}^d) = 1 / sqrt(d) and eccentricity(M) = d (Lemma 4); furthermore, any mu-strongly convex function f satisfies the Geometric Strong Convexity inequality mu_f^A >= mu * (PWidth(A))^2 (Theorem 6).

  • supports Source: On the Global Linear Convergence of Frank-Wolfe Optimization Variants (arXiv abstract) · Locator: Abstract: Global linear convergence rate characterized via the affine-invariant curvature and pyramidal width of the polytope
Mechanism #claim-02
At every non-drop step of Away-Steps Frank-Wolfe (Algorithm 1), the objective suboptimality h_t = f(x^{(t)}) - f(x^*) decreases geometrically by at least h_{t+1} <= (1 - rho_f^{AFW}) h_t where rho_f^{AFW} = mu_f^A / (4 C_f^A) <= 1/2 (or h_{t+1} <= h_t / 2 when gamma_t = gamma_max > gamma_t^B), and since each drop step removes an active atom from S^{(t)} while at most one atom can be added per step, the number of good steps after t iterations is at least k(t) >= t / 2, yielding the global linear convergence bound h_t <= h_0 (1 - rho_f^{AFW})^{max(0, floor((t - |S^{(0)}| + 1)/2))} (Theorem 1 and Theorem 8).
  • supports Source: On the Global Linear Convergence of Frank-Wolfe Optimization Variants · Locator: Section 2, Theorem 1, p. 4; Appendix D, Theorem 8 and Proof of Linear Convergence (Equations 24-31), pp. 14-16
  • supports Source: On the Global Linear Convergence of Frank-Wolfe Optimization Variants (Crossref metadata) · Locator: Abstract: Global linear convergence for Away-Steps, Pairwise, and Fully-Corrective Frank-Wolfe variants without strict complementarity
Limitation #claim-03
Standard Frank-Wolfe without away or pairwise steps zig-zags across active boundary faces whenever x^* lies on the boundary of a polytope M, exhibiting sublinear O(1/t) convergence on strongly convex objectives where Away-Steps and Pairwise Frank-Wolfe converge linearly via drop steps.
  • supports Source: On the Global Linear Convergence of Frank-Wolfe Optimization Variants · Locator: Section 1, Introduction and Figure 1 (Zig-Zagging of Standard FW vs Away/Pairwise FW), pp. 1-2

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires explicitly maintaining the active convex combination weights alpha_v^{(t)} over S^{(t)} subseteq A (unlike memoryless standard Frank-Wolfe), and for pairwise Frank-Wolfe the worst-case bound on consecutive swap/drop steps can depend on the combinatorial vertex count |A|!.
