Alpha-entmax

Mathematical Methods AIMech:AlphaEntmax DRAFT
Aliases: Entmax transformation, Tsallis entmax
A score-to-probability transformation for alpha at least one that maximizes the score inner product plus Tsallis alpha-entropy over the probability simplex, with Shannon entropy defining the alpha=1 case.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the transformation family, not a complete sequence model, its associated training loss, or a particular implementation. Entmax and DSelect-k name different constructions. Softmax and Sparsemax are special cases, rather than aliases for the whole family. The ignored-inclusive repository identity search and open-PR inspection are recorded in the accompanying discovery ledger. AIO:SoftmaxFunction covers only one case; AIO:MathematicalFunction supplies the broader mapping.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #thresholded-probabilities
For alpha greater than one, a shared threshold truncates low scores to zero; positive residuals are raised to 1/(alpha-1), with the threshold chosen so probabilities sum to one. Alpha=2 recovers Sparsemax and alpha=1 recovers Softmax.
  • supports Source: Sparse Sequence-to-Sequence Models · Locator: Sections 3.2-3.3, equations 10 and 13, printed p. 1507 (PDF p. 4)
Limitation #computation-scope
The paper gives an exact sorting algorithm for alpha=1.5 and a finite-iteration bisection approximation for general alpha greater than one. Sparse outputs are possible; not every input must have a zero component.
  • supports Source: Sparse Sequence-to-Sequence Models · Locator: Section 3.3, algorithms 1-2 and Figure 3, printed pp. 1507-1508 (PDF pp. 4-5)
