HeteroPCA Iterative Diagonal Imputation and Robust Subspace Perturbation for Heteroskedastic PCA

Algorithms AIMech:HeteroPCAIterativeDiagonalImputationAndRobustSubspacePerturbationForHeteroskedasticPCA DRAFT
Aliases: HeteroPCA, Heteroskedastic Principal Component Analysis via Iterative Diagonal Imputation, Zhang-Cai-Wu Robust sin Theta Subspace Perturbation under Diagonal Corruption
An iterative spectral estimation algorithm and deterministic subspace perturbation framework (introduced by Anru R. Zhang, T. Tony Cai, and Yihong Wu in 2022) for recovering the principal rank-$r$ subspace $\boldsymbol{U}$ of a low-rank signal matrix $\boldsymbol{M} = \boldsymbol{U}\boldsymbol{\Lambda}\boldsymbol{U}^\top$ from observations $\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}} = \boldsymbol{M} + \boldsymbol{Z}$ corrupted by severe diagonal heteroskedasticity $\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{Z})$. Whereas classical PCA fails when diagonal noise variances $\sigma_i^2$ are non-uniform and one-step diagonal deletion ($\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}})$ setting the diagonal to zero) incurs a non-vanishing rank-$r$ diagonal bias $\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{M})\| \asymp \frac{I(\boldsymbol{U})r}{p}\lambda_1$, HeteroPCA (Algorithm 1) initializes $\boldsymbol{N}^{(0)} = \boldsymbol{\Delta}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}})$ and iteratively updates $\widetilde{\boldsymbol{N}}^{(t)} = \mathrm{SVD}_r(\boldsymbol{N}^{(t)})$ (best rank-$r$ SVD approximation) followed by $\boldsymbol{N}^{(t+1)} = \boldsymbol{\Delta}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}) + \boldsymbol{D}(\widetilde{\boldsymbol{N}}^{(t)})$ for $t=0,\dots,T-1$. By establishing the operator-norm contraction identities $\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{P}_U\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{A}))\| \le \frac{I(\boldsymbol{U})r}{p}\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{A})\|$ and $\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{P}_U\boldsymbol{A})\| \le \sqrt{\frac{I(\boldsymbol{U})r}{p}}\|\boldsymbol{A}\|$ under incoherence $I(\boldsymbol{U}) = \frac{p}{r}\max_i \|\boldsymbol{U}^\top\boldsymbol{e}_i\|_2^2$ (Lemma 1) alongside the sharp factor-2 off-diagonal operator bound $\sup_{\boldsymbol{M}\ne 0}\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{M})\|/\|\boldsymbol{M}\| = 2 - 2/p < 2$ (Lemma 4), Theorem 3 proves the deterministic robust $\sin\Theta$ bound $\|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}},\boldsymbol{U})\| \lesssim \min\{\frac{\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{Z})\|}{\sigma_r(\boldsymbol{M})}, 1\}$ completely independent of the diagonal corruption $\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{Z})$.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Resolved against all 442 pinned AIO concepts and 689 maintained AIMech concepts. Distinct from `AIMech:HeteroskedasticPCAPrincipalSubspaceConfidenceRegionsAndSpikedCovarianceInferenceWithMissingData` (Yan, Chen, & Fan 2024, which develops distributional asymptotic normality and $\chi_r^2$ confidence regions for subspace rows and linear forms by building upon the HeteroPCA estimator) and from `AIMech:EntrywiseFirstOrderEigenvectorApproximationAndLeaveOneOutPerturbationAnalysisForLowExpectedRankRandomMatrices` (Abbe, Fan, Wang, & Zhong 2020, which analyzes un-imputed symmetric random matrices).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-incoherent-projection-diagonal-contraction-and-factor-two-off-diagonal-bound
For any rank-$r$ subspace $\boldsymbol{U}\in\mathbb{O}_{p,r}$ with incoherence parameter $I(\boldsymbol{U}) = \frac{p}{r}\max_{i\in[p]}\|\boldsymbol{P}_U\boldsymbol{e}_i\|_2^2$, Lemma 1 proves that for any matrix $\boldsymbol{A}$ the diagonal operator $\boldsymbol{D}(\cdot)$ satisfies $\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{P}_U\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{A}))\| \le \frac{I(\boldsymbol{U})r}{p}\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{A})\|$ and $\|\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{P}_U\boldsymbol{A})\| \le \sqrt{\frac{I(\boldsymbol{U})r}{p}}\|\boldsymbol{A}\|$, while Lemma 4 proves that the zero-diagonal operator $\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{M}) = \boldsymbol{M} - \boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{M})$ satisfies the sharp extremal bound $\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{M})\| \le 2\|\boldsymbol{M}\|$ for general matrices and $\sup_{\boldsymbol{M}=\boldsymbol{M}^\top\ne 0} \frac{\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{M})\|}{\|\boldsymbol{M}\|} = 2 - \frac{2}{p}$ attained by $\boldsymbol{M} = \mathbf{1}_p\mathbf{1}_p^\top - \frac{p}{2}\boldsymbol{I}_p$ (Section 5, Lemmas 1 and 4).
  • supports Source: Heteroskedastic PCA: Algorithm, Optimality, and Applications · Locator: Section 5 (Lemma 1, p. 68; Lemma 4, p. 71), Supplement Proof of Lemma 4
Mechanism #claim-deterministic-robust-sin-theta-theorem-independent-of-diagonal-perturbation
Under the incoherence condition $\max\{\frac{p}{r}\max_i\|\boldsymbol{P}_U\boldsymbol{e}_i\|_2^2, \frac{q}{r}\max_j\|\boldsymbol{P}_V\boldsymbol{e}_j\|_2^2\} \le c_0$, Theorem 3 proves that HeteroPCA (Algorithm 1) run for $T$ iterations on any perturbed matrix $\boldsymbol{N} = \boldsymbol{M} + \boldsymbol{Z}$ satisfies $\max\{\|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}(\boldsymbol{U}^{(T)},\boldsymbol{U})\|, \|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}(\boldsymbol{V}^{(T)},\boldsymbol{V})\|\} \le \min\{\Gamma_T, 1\}$ where $\Gamma_t \le \frac{\|\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{Z})\|}{c_1\sigma_r(\boldsymbol{M})} + 2^{-(t+3)}$, converging geometrically to exact recovery when $\boldsymbol{\Delta}(\boldsymbol{Z}) = \boldsymbol{0}$ regardless of the magnitude of the diagonal perturbation $\boldsymbol{D}(\boldsymbol{Z})$ (Section 3, Theorem 3, Section 5, Proof of Theorem 3).
  • supports Source: Heteroskedastic PCA: Algorithm, Optimality, and Applications · Locator: Section 2 (Algorithm 1, p. 57), Section 3 (Theorem 3, pp. 61-62), Section 5 (pp. 68-73)
Application #claim-minimax-optimal-subspace-recovery-under-heteroskedastic-spiked-covariance-and-poisson-pca
For the heteroskedastic spiked covariance model $\boldsymbol{Y}_k = \boldsymbol{X}_k + \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_k$ with $\mathrm{Cov}(\boldsymbol{X}_k) = \boldsymbol{U}\boldsymbol{\Lambda}\boldsymbol{U}^\top$ and $\mathrm{Cov}(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_k) = \mathrm{diag}(\sigma_1^2,\dots,\sigma_p^2)$, Theorems 1 and 2 establish that HeteroPCA achieves the minimax-optimal subspace estimation rate $\mathbb{E}\|\sin\boldsymbol{\Theta}(\widehat{\boldsymbol{U}},\boldsymbol{U})\| \asymp \min\{\sqrt{\frac{p}{n}}\frac{\lambda_1^{1/2}\sigma_{\max}}{\lambda_r} + \frac{\sqrt{p}\sigma_{\mathrm{ave}}\sigma_{\max}}{\sqrt{n}\lambda_r}, 1\}$ where $\sigma_{\mathrm{ave}}^2 = \frac{1}{p}\sum_{i=1}^p \sigma_i^2$, strictly improving over standard SVD and diagonal-deletion SVD as well as extending to Poisson PCA and SVD with heteroskedastic missing data (Sections 2.2 and 4, Theorems 1, 2, 4, and 5).
  • supports Source: Heteroskedastic PCA: Algorithm, Optimality, and Applications · Locator: Section 2.2 (Theorems 1-2, pp. 58-60), Section 4 (Theorems 4-5, pp. 63-66)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires the true principal subspace U to be incoherent (I(U) * r / p <= c_0 < 1); if a singular vector is localized on a single coordinate (spiky basis vector e_i), its diagonal entry cannot be separated from an arbitrary diagonal noise spike D(Z)_{i,i}.
  • Each HeteroPCA iteration requires computing a truncated rank-r SVD of the p x p (or p_1 x p_2) matrix N^(t), incurring O(T * p^2 * r) computational cost.
