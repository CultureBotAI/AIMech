Retentive network

Architectures AIMech:RetNet DRAFT
Aliases: RetNet
An autoregressive neural sequence architecture that stacks pre-normalized residual blocks combining gated multi-scale retention with feedforward transformations, with parallel, recurrent and chunkwise forms of retention.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope RetNet denotes Sun and colleagues' architecture, not a generic recurrent network or an attention-based Transformer language model. Its retention operation is a component, while the architecture also specifies gating, normalization, residual paths and feedforward blocks.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
Retention projects position-modulated queries, keys and values and combines causal query-key interactions with exponential distance decay. Different heads use fixed decay scales; headwise normalization and a swish gate combine their outputs. Recurrent evaluation updates a decayed key-value state, and chunkwise evaluation combines parallel within-chunk interactions with recurrent cross-chunk state.
  • supports Source: Retentive Network: A Successor to Transformer for Large Language Models · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.3, equations 5–9 and Figures 3–4, PDF/printed pp. 3–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Constant recurrent decoding cost is with respect to prior sequence length at fixed model dimensions, not constant total work for a whole sequence or arbitrary model size.
  • The mathematical parallel, recurrent and chunkwise representations do not by themselves establish identical finite-precision outputs for every implementation; numerical score-normalization details matter.
  • This record does not generalize the paper's reported benchmark or hardware results.
