Nonsmooth Convex-Composite Optimization, Weak Convexity, Sharpness, and Prox-Linear Low-Rank Matrix Recovery

Aliases: Convex-Composite Low-Rank Matrix Recovery, Weakly Convex and Sharp Nonsmooth Low-Rank Matrix Estimation, Prox-Linear and Polyak Subgradient Low-Rank Matrix Recovery under Gross Outliers
A unified nonsmooth convex-composite optimization framework (introduced by Vasileios Charisopoulos, Yudong Chen, Damek Davis, Mateo Díaz, Lijun Ding, and Dmitriy Drusvyatskiy in 2021) for recovering low-rank matrices $\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp = \boldsymbol{W}_\sharp\boldsymbol{W}_\sharp^\top$ (symmetric) or $\boldsymbol{X}_\sharp\boldsymbol{Y}_\sharp^\top$ (asymmetric) from linear, quadratic, or bilinear measurements corrupted by arbitrary gross outliers on a fraction $p_{\mathrm{fail}} = |\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{fail}}|/m < 1/2$ of observations. Formulating estimation as minimizing the nonsmooth composite objective $f(\boldsymbol{X}) = h(F(\boldsymbol{X})) = \|\mathcal{A}(\boldsymbol{X}) - \boldsymbol{b}\|_1$ (where $h = \|\cdot\|_1$ is $L$-Lipschitz convex and $F(\boldsymbol{X}) = \mathcal{A}(\boldsymbol{X}) - \boldsymbol{b}$ is a quadratic map with Linearized Taylor model $f_{\boldsymbol{X}}(\boldsymbol{Z}) = h(F(\boldsymbol{X}) + \nabla F(\boldsymbol{X})(\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{X}))$, Equations 1.4–1.5), Propositions 4.1 and 4.6 prove that under rank-$2r$ $\ell_1/\ell_2$ Restricted Isometry Property (Assumptions 1–2), $f$ satisfies two regularities: (1) global *weak convexity / quadratic model accuracy* $|f(\boldsymbol{Z}) - f_{\boldsymbol{X}}(\boldsymbol{Z})| \le \rho\|\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{X}\|_F^2$ with $\rho = \kappa_2$ independent of $\sigma_1(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)$, and (2) exact *sharpness* $f(\boldsymbol{X}) - f(\boldsymbol{X}_\sharp) \ge \mu\,\mathrm{dist}(\boldsymbol{X}, \mathcal{X}_\sharp)$ with $\mu \asymp (1 - 2p_{\mathrm{fail}})\sqrt{\sigma_r(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)}$. Consequently, the effective condition number $\rho/\mu \asymp \sqrt{\kappa(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)}$ improves by a square root over smooth squared-loss formulations, enabling linear convergence via Polyak/geometrically decaying subgradient methods (Theorems 5.2–5.3) and local **quadratic convergence** $\mathrm{dist}(\boldsymbol{X}_{k+1},\mathcal{X}_\sharp) \le \frac{\rho}{\mu}\,\mathrm{dist}^2(\boldsymbol{X}_k,\mathcal{X}_\sharp)$ via the prox-linear algorithm $\boldsymbol{X}_{k+1} = \arg\min_{\boldsymbol{Z}}\{f_{\boldsymbol{X}_k}(\boldsymbol{Z}) + \frac{\beta}{2}\|\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{X}_k\|_F^2\}$ (Equation 1.7, Theorem 5.4).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-two-sided-quadratic-taylor-accuracy-and-weak-convexity-under-l1-l2-rip
For the nonsmooth composite objective $f(\boldsymbol{X}) = \|\mathcal{A}(\boldsymbol{W}\boldsymbol{W}^\top) - \boldsymbol{b}\|_1$ (symmetric) or $\|\mathcal{A}(\boldsymbol{X}\boldsymbol{Y}^\top) - \boldsymbol{b}\|_1$ (asymmetric, with or without nuclear-norm-style factor penalty $\tilde{f}$), Propositions 4.1, 4.2, and 4.3 prove that under the rank-$2r$ $\ell_1/\ell_2$ Restricted Isometry Property $\kappa_1\|\boldsymbol{M}\|_F \le \|\mathcal{A}(\boldsymbol{M})\|_1 \le \kappa_2\|\boldsymbol{M}\|_F$, the linearized convex model $f_{\boldsymbol{X}}(\boldsymbol{Z}) = h(F(\boldsymbol{X}) + \nabla F(\boldsymbol{X})(\boldsymbol{Z}-\boldsymbol{X}))$ satisfies the two-sided quadratic error bound $|f(\boldsymbol{Z}) - f_{\boldsymbol{X}}(\boldsymbol{Z})| \le \kappa_2\|\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{X}\|_F^2$ for all factors $\boldsymbol{X},\boldsymbol{Z}$, certifying global $2\kappa_2$-weak convexity with parameter independent of $\sigma_1(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)$ (Section 4.1, Assumptions 1–2, Propositions 4.1–4.3).
  • supports Source: Low-Rank Matrix Recovery with Composite Optimization: Good Conditioning and Rapid Convergence · Locator: Section 1.2 (Eqs. 1.4-1.6, pp. 1510-1513), Section 4.1 (Assumptions 1-2, Propositions 4.1-4.3, pp. 1528-1531)
Mechanism #claim-exact-sharpness-and-local-conditioning-under-arbitrary-outlier-corruption
Under the $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{fail}}$-outlier concentration bound $\|\mathcal{A}(\boldsymbol{M})\|_1 - 2\|\mathcal{A}_{\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{fail}}}(\boldsymbol{M})\|_1 \ge \kappa_3\|\boldsymbol{M}\|_F$ holding for outlier fraction $p_{\mathrm{fail}} < 1/2$ (Assumption 2, Theorem 3.1), Propositions 4.6, 4.7, and 4.8 prove that $f$ is sharp around the ground-truth solution set $\mathcal{X}_\sharp$: $f(\boldsymbol{W}) - f(\boldsymbol{W}_\sharp) \ge \kappa_3\sqrt{2(\sqrt{2}-1)\sigma_r(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)}\,\mathrm{dist}(\boldsymbol{W},\mathcal{D}^*(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp))$ in the symmetric case (and analogously with $\mu \asymp \kappa_3\sqrt{\sigma_r(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)}$ in the balanced asymmetric case), yielding a local basin of attraction of radius $\mathrm{dist}(\boldsymbol{X}_0,\mathcal{X}_\sharp) < \mu/\rho \asymp \frac{\kappa_3}{\kappa_2}\sqrt{\sigma_r(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)}$ free of spurious stationary points (Section 3, Theorem 3.1; Section 4.2, Lemma 4.4, Propositions 4.6–4.8, Corollary 4.9).
  • supports Source: Low-Rank Matrix Recovery with Composite Optimization: Good Conditioning and Rapid Convergence · Locator: Section 3 (Theorem 3.1, pp. 1521-1524), Section 4.2 (Lemma 4.4, Propositions 4.6-4.8, Corollary 4.9, pp. 1532-1537)
Application #claim-polyak-subgradient-linear-convergence-and-prox-linear-quadratic-convergence
For any $\rho$-weakly convex and $\mu$-sharp composite function $f = h\circ F$ initialized within $\mathrm{dist}(x_0,\mathcal{X}_\sharp) \le \frac{\alpha\mu}{2\rho}$ ($\alpha \in (0,1)$), Theorems 5.2 and 5.3 prove that the Polyak and geometrically decaying subgradient methods contract linearly with iteration complexity $O(\frac{L^2}{\mu^2}\log(1/\varepsilon)) = O(\kappa(\boldsymbol{M}_\sharp)\log(1/\varepsilon))$, while Theorem 5.4 proves that the prox-linear iteration $x_{k+1} = \arg\min_x\{f_{x_k}(x) + \frac{\beta}{2}\|x - x_k\|_2^2\}$ achieves local **quadratic convergence** $\mathrm{dist}(x_{k+1},\mathcal{X}_\sharp) \le \frac{\rho + \beta}{\mu}\,\mathrm{dist}^2(x_k,\mathcal{X}_\sharp)$, reaching accuracy $\varepsilon$ in $O(\log\log(1/\varepsilon))$ outer convex subproblems (Section 5, Algorithms 1–3, Lemma 5.1, Theorems 5.2–5.4).
  • supports Source: Low-Rank Matrix Recovery with Composite Optimization: Good Conditioning and Rapid Convergence · Locator: Section 5.1 (Algorithms 1-2, Lemma 5.1, Theorems 5.2-5.3, pp. 1538-1542), Section 5.2 (Algorithm 3, Theorem 5.4, pp. 1543-1544)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The Polyak step size (Eq. 5.2) requires knowing the optimal objective value f(x_sharp) (which is zero in the noiseless/outlier-free regime, or estimated via geometric step-size decay in Algorithm 2 when outliers are present).
  • Each step of the prox-linear method (Algorithm 3) requires solving a convex linearly constrained L1 / quadratic program of size m x (d*r), trading higher per-iteration cost for quadratic O(log log(1/epsilon)) iteration complexity.
