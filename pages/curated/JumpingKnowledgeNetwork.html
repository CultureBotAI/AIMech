Jumping Knowledge network

Architectures AIMech:JumpingKnowledgeNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: JK-Net, Jumping Knowledge networks
A graph neural architecture that sends intermediate node representations from multiple neighborhood-aggregation depths to a final layer-aggregation mechanism.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Jumping Knowledge adds aggregation across layer depths and can be combined with GCN, GraphSAGE or GAT neighborhood operators. It is distinct from ordinary adjacent-layer skip connections and from any one of those base operators.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #architecture-mechanism
Retain each node's intermediate neighborhood-aggregation representations and combine them at the final stage using concatenation, element-wise maximum, or an LSTM-based attention mechanism over depths.
  • supports Source: Representation Learning on Graphs with Jumping Knowledge Networks · Locator: Section 4 and Figure 4, PDF pp. 5–6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Concatenation followed by a node-shared linear transformation is not node-adaptive in its layer weighting; max pooling and the attention variant provide node-dependent selection or weights.
  • The influence-distribution result is conditional on activation assumptions. No general guarantee of optimal neighborhood range or benchmark superiority is asserted.
  • The record describes the layer-aggregation family. The paper's attention prose refers to both weighted layer features and weighted bidirectional-LSTM features, so an exact attention implementation would require a separately pinned implementation review.
