Graph isomorphism network

Architectures AIMech:GraphIsomorphismNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: GIN
A graph neural architecture using multilayer perceptrons on sums of neighboring representations and a separately weighted self-representation.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope GIN is the named sum-and-MLP architecture, not the graph-isomorphism decision problem, all graph neural networks, or all architectures as expressive as a Weisfeiler–Lehman test.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
Each update applies an MLP to the sum of neighbor states and (1 + epsilon) times the current node state, with epsilon fixed or learned.
  • supports Source: How Powerful are Graph Neural Networks? · Locator: Section 4.1, Corollary 6 and equation 4.1, PDF/printed p. 5 of arXiv v3

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The equation is taken from revised v3; the earlier v1 expression lacks the separately parameterized self weighting.
  • No claim that GIN solves arbitrary graph isomorphism or that every trained finite network attains the theoretical expressivity bound is curated.
