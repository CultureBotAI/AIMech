Path integral clustering

Algorithms AIMech:PathIntegralClustering DRAFT
Aliases: Agglomerative clustering via maximum incremental path integral, PIC (path integral clustering)
An agglomerative clustering algorithm that merges clusters with the greatest increase in their size-normalized sums of discounted random-walk paths when paths are allowed through the proposed union.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Zhang, Zhao and Wang’s 2013 maximum-incremental-path-integral clustering procedure. The qualified PIC alias denotes path integral clustering, not unrelated power-iteration clustering or generic physical path integration. Its affinity uses conditional path descriptors, not simply the merged cluster’s descriptor minus the two original descriptors. GDL instead uses products of degree averages; JULE is a joint neural-learning procedure. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Build a weighted directed nearest-neighbor graph and row-normalize its adjacency W to obtain transition matrix P=D^−1 W. Keep that global P fixed. Initialize small clusters by linking each sample to its nearest neighbor and repeatedly merging overlapping groups. For nonempty C and 0<z<1, let P_C be the principal submatrix of P, without renormalizing its rows inside C, and define S_C=1_C^T(I−z P_C)^−1 1_C/|C|^2. This sums geometrically discounted within-C transition paths, including the length-zero identity contribution. For U=A union B, define S_(A|U)=e_A^T(I−z P_U)^−1 e_A/|A|^2, where e_A is the indicator of A among the vertices of U; its paths stay in U and start and end in A. Merge the pair maximizing (S_(A|U)−S_A)+(S_(B|U)−S_B), then repeat to the requested count. Linear systems can evaluate the descriptors without explicitly forming a dense inverse.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The distance/similarity, neighborhood size, bandwidth, discount z and target count are inputs or choices. Row normalization needs positive row sums; 0<z<1 and a substochastic principal submatrix make the geometric series converge.
  • Initial nearest-neighbor components and later greedy merges are irreversible. The algorithm cannot split an initial component to reach a larger requested cluster count. No semantic recovery or global partition optimum is certified.
  • Conditional path descriptors retain each original cluster’s |C|^2 normalization and endpoint indicator. Replacing the affinity by S_(A union B)−S_A−S_B changes the method. Principal submatrices are not independently renormalized transition matrices.
  • The paper’s efficient-computation discussion relies on sparse graphs, iterative solves and chosen conditioning/discount. An exact resolvent formula is not a claim of exact finite-iteration numerical solves or an unconditional linear bound for the whole clustering algorithm. No runtime or benchmark reproduction was performed.
