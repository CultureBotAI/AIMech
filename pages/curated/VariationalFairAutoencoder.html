Variational Fair Autoencoder

Architectures AIMech:VariationalFairAutoencoder DRAFT
Aliases: VFAE
A variational autoencoder architecture for learning representations with reduced sensitive or nuisance information, combining sensitive-variable-conditioned inference and generation with an MMD penalty on group-conditional aggregated latent distributions and a semi-supervised predictive component.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope VFAE denotes the MMD-regularized semi-supervised variational architecture introduced by Louizos et al. It is distinct from the paper’s unregularized VAE baseline, prototype-based LFR, ordinary autoencoding and the model-dependent FAIRLEARNING algorithm. Prior independence and aggregated-posterior matching do not establish causal counterfactual fairness. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method-scope
Use neural inference and generative models conditioned on the observed sensitive variable, with latent structure separating that variable from the representation in the generative model. In the semi-supervised construction, introduce a second latent layer and label variable and jointly train the inference, reconstruction and predictive components. Penalize differences between aggregated latent distributions across sensitive groups with maximum mean discrepancy, approximated using random Fourier features in the reported implementation.
  • supports Source: The Variational Fair Autoencoder · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.4, Figures 1–2 and equations (1)–(10), PDF pp. 2–4; Section 3.2, PDF pp. 5–6 for prediction sampling

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Independence in the generative prior does not alone ensure independence of inferred representations; the MMD regularizer addresses remaining group dependence.
  • Finite data, optimization, kernel choice and random-feature approximation limit how strongly matching can be claimed; no exact population independence or universal fairness guarantee is asserted.
  • The encoder conditions on the sensitive variable. Semi-supervised here allows missing task labels, not arbitrary absence of sensitive-attribute information.
  • The paper reports sampling the inferred latent representation for prediction rather than replacing it by its mean. Numerical objective-sign and bandwidth conventions are not certified by this conceptual record.
