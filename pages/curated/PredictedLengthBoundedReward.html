Bounded word reward with predicted length

Mathematical Methods AIMech:PredictedLengthBoundedReward DRAFT
A sequence rescoring method that adds a fixed word reward only up to an input-specific length predicted from encoder states, replacing a corpus-level generation-ratio cap with Lpred(x).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Yang et al.’s predicted-length bounded word-reward variant. It changes the cap, while retaining a separately tuned reward rate r. It is not the beam-statistic adaptive reward or the brevity-penalty normalized score, and it is not a standalone trained length-predictor instance. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Feed the mean source encoder hidden state to a two-layer multilayer perceptron to predict a target/source generation ratio, then multiply it by the source length to obtain Lpred(x). Score candidate y by S(x,y)+r·min(|y|,Lpred(x)), where S is cumulative log probability. The predictor is evaluated from the input and the reward cap is fixed for that input during decoding.
  • supports Source: Breaking the Beam Search Curse: A Study of (Re-)Scoring Methods and Stopping Criteria for Neural Machine Translation · Locator: Sections 4.2–4.2.1, Equations (8)–(9), printed p. 3056 / PDF p. 3

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The method still requires a learned length predictor and a tuned reward rate; replacing the corpus ratio does not make the complete system parameter-free.
  • Predicted length is a reward cap, not automatically an enforced maximum sequence length or an accurate target for every input.
  • A usable nonnegative length prediction, tokenizer conventions and integration with the decoder must be specified. The paper passage does not provide a complete predictor training or calibration protocol.
  • Finite beam width still prunes hypotheses. Neither accurate length prediction nor a stopping certificate implies globally optimal output or universally better translation quality.
  • The empirical source scope is Chinese-to-English news translation; no numerical result is curated.
