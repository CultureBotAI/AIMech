Real-valued neural autoregressive density estimator

Architectures AIMech:RealValuedNeuralAutoregressiveDensityEstimator DRAFT
Aliases: RNADE, Real-valued autoregressive density estimator
A fully visible neural autoregressive density architecture for real-valued vectors that shares input-to-hidden parameters across ordered scalar conditional mixture-density models.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes Uria, Murray and Larochelle’s original RNADE family. It extends binary NADE by replacing Bernoulli outputs with real-valued mixture conditionals. Deterministic prefix features and NADE-style input sharing distinguish it from separate untied conditional networks and from a stochastic latent RBM. Mixture-of-Gaussians and mixture-of-Laplace outputs are reported variants, not identity aliases for every Gaussian or Laplace mixture. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For an ordered real-valued vector x, evaluate p(x)=product_d p(x_d|x_<d). Start a_1=c and update a_(d+1)=a_d+x_d W[:,d] when another conditional remains. At each position, a learned scalar rho_d rescales the shared prefix preactivation; sigmoid or rectified-linear units produce deterministic hidden features h_d. Separate position-specific output matrices map those features to scalar-mixture parameters. In RNADE-MoG, softmax gives component weights, linear outputs give means, and exponentials give positive standard deviations. Summing the normalized scalar Gaussian components gives each conditional density; multiplying the conditionals yields a normalized joint without a global partition-function estimate. Complete-vector log densities and their derivatives are directly evaluable; prefix recurrence and fixed hidden/component sizes give cost linear in the number of dimensions, with output work O(DHK). Ancestral generation samples each scalar from the mixture conditioned on previously generated values. The experimental mean-output gradient rescaling by component standard deviation is a training heuristic, separate from the exact log-density derivative. The paper also substitutes Laplace mixture conditionals.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The variable ordering, conditional family, hidden capacity, component count and optimization restrict the fitted density. Exact density evaluation does not make arbitrary missing-variable posterior inference a forward pass or establish data-model agreement.
  • Linear scaling in dimensionality holds with fixed hidden and mixture sizes; it does not omit their cost. The final prefix update is unnecessary, consistent with the declared H by (D-1) input-weight matrix.
  • The supplement labels several log-density derivative formulas as derivatives of p rather than log p. Its Algorithm 2 also uses a log-shaped expression with a positive squared-residual term as if it were a component density in responsibilities, and has prefix/index ambiguities. A literal finite example produces invalid responsibilities. The curated mechanism follows the main-paper normalized densities and prefix equations; this is not a claim about author-code behavior.
  • The experimental standard-deviation gradient rescaling is not the raw likelihood gradient or Newton’s variance scaling. Exponential scales remain susceptible to approaching zero; regularization and model selection matter.
  • No performance ranking, convergence guarantee, exact posterior claim or full executable implementation validation is included.
