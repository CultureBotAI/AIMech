Wake-sleep algorithm

Algorithms AIMech:WakeSleepAlgorithm DRAFT
Aliases: Wake-sleep
An alternating learning procedure that trains a generative network from observed data and recognition-sampled hidden states during wake, and trains a recognition network from generative-model samples during sleep.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the original wake-sleep procedure with separate generative and recognition parameters. It is a training algorithm for a paired probabilistic model, distinct from the Helmholtz-machine architecture, exact EM, joint ELBO-gradient training in NVIL, and reweighted wake-sleep. The original binary-sigmoid construction supplies local delta rules; the general phase distinction does not require every later model variant to use those same units. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
During wake, present an observed input and sample its hidden representation layer by layer through the bottom-up recognition distribution q_phi(h|x). Hold recognition parameters fixed for this update and ascend log p_theta(x,h) in the generative parameters. For the original Bernoulli-sigmoid units, a generative weight receives a learning-rate-scaled product of its sampled parent state and the difference between the sampled target state and the generative activation probability; biases use a constant parent. During sleep, sample a full configuration (x,h) ancestrally from the current top-down generative model, then ascend log q_phi(h|x) in the recognition parameters while holding generative parameters fixed. The generated lower-layer states serve as inputs and their known sampled upper-layer causes as targets. This also gives local Bernoulli delta rules in the original parameterization. The wake update follows the generative-parameter variational-bound gradient at fixed q; the sleep update instead fits recognition on model-generated inputs and targets the posterior-to-recognition KL direction averaged under model inputs. The two phases are not generally gradients of the same data ELBO.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Recognition samples are exact for q, not necessarily the model posterior. The generative wake update need not be an unbiased marginal-likelihood gradient or even have the same direction; a curator binary example demonstrates this distinction.
  • The sleep input distribution is the current model distribution rather than the empirical data distribution. Poor model samples can therefore allocate recognition capacity differently from data-conditioned training.
  • The original recognition distribution factors within each layer conditional on the preceding layer. This can miss explaining-away dependencies in the posterior; it does not assert unconditional independence of every hidden variable across layers.
  • The original paired-model sources explicitly do not establish a common descent objective for both phases. Exact posterior recovery, global convergence and monotonic likelihood improvement are not guaranteed.
  • Local binary-sigmoid rules depend on the stated conditional parameterization. No biological implementation, author-code execution or benchmark advantage is certified.
