Late Dropout

Algorithms AIMech:LateDropout DRAFT
A neural-network training procedure that begins without dropout, activates it after a selected training cutoff and continues optimization with dropout for the remaining steps.

Retain one delayed-onset dropout procedure, accounting for the 2017 Switch-Curriculum neuron-mask configuration and the 2023 Late Dropout/stochastic-depth study. The sources share the no-dropout-then-dropout phase ordering but differ in masks, models, tasks and results; no claim of later invention or identical experimental protocols is made. Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #delayed-onset-rule
The schedule leaves dropout inactive during the initial training phase and introduces it at a selected cutoff while model fitting continues. The 2023 study uses a constant late-phase stochastic-depth rate after the onset. The 2017 paper’s Switch-Curriculum comparison already describes the same no-dropout-then-dropout phase ordering for neuron masking; it is retained here as a precursor configuration rather than a duplicate concept with a separate ID.
  • supports Source: Dropout Reduces Underfitting · Locator: Figure 2, PDF p. 2; Section 4, p. 5; Section 5.3, p. 8
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 3, PDF p. 4/printed 3547; Section 5, p. 8/printed 3551
Scope #onset-versus-smooth-curriculum
A delayed step from no suppression to a selected dropout rate differs from Curriculum Dropout’s gradual increase in suppression. In the 2017 Switch-Curriculum experiment, dropout starts at one of three selected epochs on Double MNIST. Its abrupt insertion can change the objective and transient behavior; that source’s adverse observations do not establish that every delayed schedule is inferior under other architectures and training recipes.
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 3, PDF p. 4/printed 3547; Figure 4, p. 7/printed 3550; Section 5, p. 8/printed 3551
Scope #timing-and-mask-granularity
The 2023 paper uses dropout as a term that can include stochastic depth. Its principal late experiments disable stochastic depth for the first 50 epochs, then use a constant rate selected from the standard stochastic-depth baseline. This temporal rule does not make residual-block masking identical to neuron masking, prescribe identical rates across depth or establish an inference-scaling convention for all implementations.
  • supports Source: Dropout Reduces Underfitting · Locator: Section 2, PDF p. 3; Section 5.3, p. 8; Appendix A, p. 13
Limitation #phase-order-boundary
Early and late dropout reverse which phase contains stochastic masks. Equal numbers of masked and unmasked steps do not guarantee equal optimization trajectories: even simple expected-gradient update maps can fail to commute when their order is reversed. This boundary is independent of any claim that one schedule has universally better accuracy.
  • supports Source: Dropout Reduces Underfitting · Locator: Figure 2, PDF p. 2; Section 4, p. 5; separate curator affine-map calculation
Limitation #finite-vision-evidence
The 2023 late-schedule study evaluates configured supervised vision models and reports dependence on onset, rate and architecture. It did not find a significantly improved setting for ConvNeXt-B or Swin-B in the reported search, and some tested rates or onset times worsened the result. Its results and the earlier Switch-Curriculum comparisons therefore do not establish universal generalization gains, a universally optimal cutoff or superiority over smooth schedules.
  • supports Source: Dropout Reduces Underfitting · Locator: Section 5.3, PDF p. 8; Appendix B and Figures 15–16, p. 14; Appendix G, p. 16

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The stable local identity covers the delayed-onset schedule described as late dropout in 2023 and the neuron-masking Switch-Curriculum configuration described in 2017. It does not claim priority for the later name or identical masks and experimental protocols across sources.
  • Generic dropout, smooth Curriculum Dropout, Early Dropout and the original depth-dependent stochastic-depth procedure have distinct scopes. Layer-depth probability schedules and temporal onset are separate choices.
  • The onset does not terminate training or permanently prune units. Reversing the order of equally many masked and unmasked steps does not preserve optimization in general.
  • All sixteen PMLR pages and nine primary ICCV pages were visually inspected. Author implementations and alternate full-text versions were not inspected. No model fitting, hardware measurements or empirical replication were executed.
