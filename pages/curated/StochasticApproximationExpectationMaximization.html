Stochastic Approximation Expectation Maximization

Aliases: SAEM, Stochastic Approximation EM
An incomplete-data fitting method that recursively averages simulated complete-data log objectives or sufficient statistics using a step-size sequence, then maximizes the accumulated objective to update the model parameters.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: Convergence of a stochastic approximation version of the EM algorithm · Locator: Section 3 Eq6, pp.97-98 / PDF4-5; Section 6 Eq30, p.107 / PDF14
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The Delyon-Lavielle-Moulines SAEM construction fits a fixed observed data set while stochastic approximation controls integration noise. It is distinct from fresh-batch MCEM, stochastic-gradient parameter steps, parameter-iterate averaging, and online learning from newly arriving observations. A persistent MCMC simulation step is a separately scoped extension because exact conditional draws and Markovian perturbations require different arguments. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #recursive-expectation
Simulate latent completions under the current conditional law and update Q_k(theta)=(1-gamma_k)Q_(k-1)(theta)+gamma_k times the current sample mean of complete log objectives. Maximize Q_k to obtain the next parameter. For the stated curved exponential family, store sufficient statistics instead: s_k=s_(k-1)+gamma_k(S(z_k)-s_(k-1)), followed by theta_k=theta_hat(s_k). The one-draw construction can therefore retain past information without storing every prior completion.
  • supports Source: Convergence of a stochastic approximation version of the EM algorithm · Locator: Section 3 Eq6, pp.97-98 / PDF4-5; M1-M5, pp.99-100 / PDF6-7; Section 6 Eq30, p.107 / PDF14
Scope #step-sizes-and-stationary-set
The single-draw stationary-set theorem requires a smooth curved-exponential complete model and maximizer map, exact conditional simulation given the past, finite continuous second moments, step sizes in [0,1] with divergent sum and finite sum of squares, and almost-sure containment of the statistic iterates in a compact subset of their domain. Under those hypotheses the distance to the stated stationary sets tends to zero. Step-size conditions alone do not supply stability, point convergence, a global maximum or observed-likelihood ascent at every iteration.
  • supports Source: Convergence of a stochastic approximation version of the EM algorithm · Locator: Section 6 SAEM1-SAEM4 and Theorem 5, pp.107-109 / PDF14-16
Scope #averaging-and-estimation-boundaries
Recursive averaging of the expected objective precedes its maximization. Optional averaging of fitted parameter iterates is a separate operation with additional local stability, moment and step-size conditions. Averaging statistics before a nonlinear maximizer need not equal averaging parameter values. Simulation-iteration uncertainty around a fixed-data solution is distinct from sampling uncertainty of the statistical estimator.
  • supports Source: Convergence of a stochastic approximation version of the EM algorithm · Locator: Sections 5.1 and 7, pp.104-106 and 110-113 / PDF11-13 and 17-20; Eq26 and Eq48-55
Limitation #source-sign-and-zero-step
In Eq6, gamma=0 freezes the accumulated objective, while gamma=1 replaces it by the current Monte Carlo objective; the paragraph identifying gamma=0 with ordinary stochastic EM is inconsistent with that recursion. The 1999 covariance prose also reverses the Hessian sign: its H=G-Delta Delta^T estimates the observed log Hessian, while observed information is -H, as explicitly used in the 2004 follow-up. These printed statements are not used as implementation rules.
  • supports Source: Convergence of a stochastic approximation version of the EM algorithm · Locator: Section 3 Eq6 and following paragraph, p.97 / PDF4; Section 3 Eq11 and Hessian prose, p.99 / PDF6
  • supports Source: Coupling a stochastic approximation version of EM with an MCMC procedure · Locator: Section 2.3 Eq8-10, p.119 / PDF5; compare 1999 Section 3 Eq6 and Eq11, pp.97,99 / PDF4,6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The core mechanism is supported independently of the source’s stronger local-maximizer and averaging claims. Those results need additional isolated-stationary-point, curvature, excitation and local-rate hypotheses. Their printed exposition contains sign/wording inconsistencies; no blanket theorem repair or global convergence certification is claimed.
  • A finite stochastic update need not increase the observed likelihood. The finite Gaussian example lowers it even with a positive averaging weight; stabilization and convergence need separate arguments.
  • The source’s expanding-compact reset scheme, approximate conditional maximizations and stochastic-gradient extensions introduce additional assumptions. They are not silently covered by the core exact-maximizer theorem.
  • The source’s Eq27 average-noise expression omits a Jacobian factor present in its later proof, and its Section 8 Newton direction and convexity wording conflict with concave maximization. These extension formulas are not implemented or endorsed by this record.
  • Independent exact conditional draws are an assumption of the inspected base theorem, not a property of a short MCMC run. The separately scoped MCMC-SAEM extension handles a different noise structure.
  • All 52 pages of the two primary papers were read. No author software, historical fit, empirical speed comparison or uncertainty-calibration study was reproduced.
