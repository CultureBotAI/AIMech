Auto-exponential Markov random field

Model Families AIMech:AutoExponentialMarkovRandomField DRAFT
Aliases: Auto-exponential model, Auto-exponential scheme
A finite pairwise Markov-field model for nonnegative continuous site values with exponential full conditionals whose rates are positive baseline terms plus nonnegative symmetric linear neighbor interactions.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The record uses the classical positive-baseline auto-exponential construction. Exponential refers to the site conditional probability law, not a generic exponential-family graphical model, an exponential activation or exponential smoothing over time. The rate-interaction sign convention is explicit: nonnegative gamma increases conditional rates and decreases conditional means. These gamma coefficients enter the joint log density with a minus sign, unlike the beta sign used in the auto-Poisson natural parameter. Gamma-shaped conditionals and alternative boundary parameter regimes need separate assessments. Interacting marginal distributions need not themselves be exponential. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
On a finite graph with X_i>=0, choose alpha_i>0 and symmetric gamma_ij=gamma_ji>=0, with gamma_ij=0 on nonedges. The full-conditional density is r_i*exp(-r_i*x_i), where r_i=alpha_i+sum_(j!=i) gamma_ij*x_j and the conditional mean is 1/r_i. Combining this conditional form with the source pairwise representation gives p(x)=Z^(-1)*exp[-sum_i alpha_i*x_i-sum_(i<j) gamma_ij*x_i*x_j]. Each edge is counted once. The nonnegative interaction penalty bounds the unnormalized joint density by the product of integrable exp(-alpha_i*x_i) terms, establishing a finite positive Z in this regime. Increasing a neighbor value cannot increase the site conditional mean with other neighbors fixed. Zero interactions reduce the law to independent exponentials.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The stated positive-baseline, nonnegative-rate-interaction regime is the source-supported construction. It is not asserted to classify every zero-baseline boundary case or every transformed, truncated or gamma-conditional variant.
  • Reversing the rate-interaction sign can make a conditional rate nonpositive somewhere on the unbounded support and invalidate its exponential density. Sign restrictions cannot be transferred unchanged between rate and log-density parameterizations.
  • The full conditional is memoryless as a univariate exponential law at fixed neighbors; this does not supply a temporal evolution model or imply an exponential marginal law after averaging over neighbors.
  • The paper explicitly questions the practical interpretability of this construction and supplies no detailed auto-exponential empirical example. The mathematical model is not a validated mechanism of spatial competition or universal positive-data predictor.
  • The graph, site supports and any conditioning boundary values must be specified. The construction assumes compatible positive full conditionals on the stated Cartesian support; arbitrary separately fitted regressions or asymmetric neighbor coefficients do not automatically define a joint model.
  • The finite fixed-site model does not by itself specify a random-location point process, temporal causal mechanism, hidden observation layer or infinite-lattice existence theorem. Conditional association does not establish causation.
  • A closed-form site conditional does not make the joint partition function, maximum-likelihood fit or uncertainty calculation trivial. Estimation, sampling and model diagnostics require their own procedures. No author implementation, empirical fit or benchmark was reproduced.
