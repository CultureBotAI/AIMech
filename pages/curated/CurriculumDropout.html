Curriculum Dropout

A neural-network training procedure that starts without unit suppression and gradually increases Bernoulli dropout by decreasing the probability of retaining each unit toward a specified layer-dependent limit.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #retention-schedule
The demonstrated schedule is theta(t)=(1-theta_bar)*exp(-gamma*t)+theta_bar, where theta is retention probability, t counts gradient updates and gamma is positive. It starts at one and approaches theta_bar; suppression probability is 1-theta(t). The paper uses terminal retention values between 0.5 and 0.9. Its heuristic gamma=10/T depends on an anticipated training length T and approaches, rather than exactly reaches, the limit at finite T.
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 3, main PDF pp.3–4, Equations 1–2; direct schedule calculation
Scope #mask-and-scaling-scope
Independent Bernoulli masks suppress individual units during training; D units have expected retained count D*theta(t). This schedules noise rather than sorting the observed examples or learning a separate data-dependent gate. The paper’s mathematical analysis uses unscaled binary masks, with prediction scaling described separately. An inverted-dropout implementation rescales retained values during training and requires reconciling the convention before transferring the formulas.
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Sections 1–3, main PDF pp.1–4; Section 4, pp.5–6
  • supports Source: Supplementary Material to Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 1.1, supplemental PDF pp.2–4
Limitation #linear-loss-regularization-boundary
For a linear predictor with squared loss and independent unscaled Bernoulli masks, the exact expected loss equals the squared loss of the theta-scaled mean prediction plus theta*(1-theta)*sum_j(w_j^2*sum_i X_ij^2). Thus the data-fit term also changes. The scalar factor grows along the stated retention path above one half; this identity does not establish a fixed additive penalty for every nonlinear deep network or guarantee better generalization.
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 3, main PDF pp.4–5, Equation 3
  • supports Source: Supplementary Material to Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 1.1, supplemental PDF pp.2–4, Theorem 1 and Equations 3–19; direct finite-mask enumeration
Limitation #curriculum-proof-caveats
The source’s formal curriculum argument needs qualifications. Its binomial coefficient aggregates masks with the same zero count, rather than giving the probability of a specific corrupted image. The supplement multiplies entropies for an independent product distribution, whose joint entropy instead adds. Its proposed ratio Q_lambda/P can exceed the stated unit bound, and zero-valued inputs can make different masks yield the same image. These direct counterexamples prevent adopting a general bounded-weight or strictly increasing observed-image-entropy theorem; they do not invalidate the explicitly defined dropout schedule.
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 4, main PDF pp.5–6, Equations 4–9
  • supports Source: Supplementary Material to Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 1.2, supplemental PDF pp.4–6, Equations 20–27; constructed probability, ratio and entropy counterexamples
Limitation #evaluation-and-protocol-boundary
The reported evaluation uses selected image-classification datasets, architectures and subsets. Results summarize each run’s ten highest observed accuracies and then aggregate ten runs, so they are selected trajectory summaries rather than fixed final-checkpoint estimates. Universal improvement is not established. The supplement specifies a different CIFAR learning rate from the main text’s fixed-rate description; exact reproduction must resolve that discrepancy. Recurrent-network and connection-drop extensions remain future work in the inspected paper.
  • supports Source: Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Section 5 and conclusions, main PDF pp.6–8, Figure 3 and Table 1
  • supports Source: Supplementary Material to Curriculum Dropout · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.3, supplemental PDF pp.6–7, Tables 1–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The local identity is the increasing-suppression curriculum procedure, distinct from decreasing-suppression annealed dropout, an abrupt switch schedule, Gaussian fast dropout, ensemble-objective dropout boosting and confidence-based training-signal annealing.
  • The source’s schedule is retained while probability, entropy and curriculum-weight proof problems are explicit. No universal optimization, calibration or generalization guarantee is asserted.
  • Finite endpoint, retention-versus-suppression and mask-rescaling conventions must be distinguished. The planning horizon T is an input to the demonstrated rate heuristic.
  • All nine primary and eight supplementary pages were visually inspected. Author code, other versions, training and empirical replication were not inspected or executed.
