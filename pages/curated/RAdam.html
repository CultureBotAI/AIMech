Rectified Adam

Algorithms AIMech:RAdam DRAFT
Aliases: RAdam
An Adam-family optimizer that rectifies its adaptive update using a time-dependent estimate of the effective history of the second-moment statistic.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope RAdam's rectification and early-step branch distinguish it from unmodified Adam and from weight-decay decoupling alone.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #update-rule
The cited implementation computes a rectification term when its effective-history statistic exceeds five; before that threshold it takes a bias-corrected first-moment step without adaptive second-moment scaling.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The implementation exposes coupled and decoupled weight-decay choices; these are distinct from the rectification mechanism.
  • The record asserts the update procedure, not a universal variance reduction, convergence result or benchmark advantage.
