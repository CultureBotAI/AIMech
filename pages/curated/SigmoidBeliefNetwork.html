Sigmoid belief network

Architectures AIMech:SigmoidBeliefNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: SBN
A directed acyclic probabilistic network of binary variables whose forward conditional probabilities are logistic functions of weighted parent states and a bias.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the sigmoid conditional family defined by Neal, including equivalent zero/one and minus-one/plus-one encodings. It is not a symmetric Boltzmann machine, a sigmoid activation function alone, or an arbitrary neural network containing sigmoid units. Hidden variables are permitted but not required; the fully visible autoregressive specialization is not treated as an alias for the whole family. Wake-sleep and other inference or learning procedures do not define the model identity. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Choose a topological ordering of binary nodes and directed connections from earlier parents to later nodes. In zero/one encoding let a_i=b_i+sum_j w_ij s_j over parents; then P(s_i=1|parents)=sigmoid(a_i) and P(s_i=0|parents)=1-sigmoid(a_i). Multiplying these locally normalized factors gives the joint distribution without an undirected global partition function. Ancestral sampling visits nodes in topological order. For the equivalent minus-one/plus-one encoding, divide each non-bias weight by two and add half the original incoming-weight sum to the original bias. Marginalizing hidden variables still requires a sum over their configurations, and a Gibbs full conditional uses both the node factor and factors of its children, rather than merely its forward conditional. In the inspected maximum-likelihood learning derivation, gradients of a visible-data log likelihood are posterior expectations of joint log-probability scores; Gibbs sampling is one approximation for those expectations, not a mandatory part of the architecture.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Local normalization and exact ancestral draws do not make arbitrary posterior inference or hidden-variable marginal likelihood tractable. Clamping later observed nodes and using only forward conditionals does not generally sample the desired posterior.
  • The two binary encodings require the stated weight and bias transformation; copying identical numerical parameters between them changes the distribution.
  • The gradient identity requires appropriate posterior expectations. A finite Gibbs run need not provide exact equilibrium samples, rapid mixing or an unbiased implemented learning update.
  • Acyclic probabilistic dependence is not by itself evidence for intervention-level causal interpretation, and the learned units need not represent human-interpretable propositions.
  • No global training optimum, biological implementation or benchmark ranking is asserted. Only the declared portions of the 43-page source were inspected.
