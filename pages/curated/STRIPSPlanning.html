STRIPS planning method

Algorithms AIMech:STRIPSPlanning DRAFT
A symbolic planning method that uses means–ends analysis and theorem proving over world models to construct a sequence of operators satisfying a goal.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record denotes the planning procedure described in the 1971 STRIPS paper, not a particular executable or every later STRIPS-style action representation.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #search-procedure
Operators specify preconditions and additions/deletions to a logical world model. The search identifies operators relevant to an unmet goal, treats their preconditions as subgoals, applies an applicable operator and revisits the original goal.
  • supports Source: STRIPS: A New Approach to the Application of Theorem Proving to Problem Solving · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.2, printed pp. 191–193 / PDF pp. 3–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The original paper's predicate-calculus world models and theorem-proving procedure should not be equated automatically with every later propositional STRIPS formalism.
  • No completeness, runtime or robotic performance guarantee is curated.
