Aliases: Breakout
A finite constraint-satisfaction search method that takes strictly improving local assignment changes under a weighted nogood cost and increases the weights of all currently violated nogoods at a non-solution local minimum to change the search landscape.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Morris’s Breakout method, using weights on forbidden assignment tuples, not the Breakout game. It is distinct from the idealized Fill algorithm, which raises only the current state’s stored cost, and from GLS, which selects features by penalty utility. Shared adaptive-penalty ideas do not make Breakout, GENET and GLS exact synonyms. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Represent forbidden assignment tuples as nogoods, give each weight1, and score a complete assignment by the sum of weights of the nogoods it matches. Until a satisfying assignment is reached, make any local assignment change that strictly lowers this weighted cost when one exists. At a non-solution local minimum, increment every currently matched nogood’s weight by1 and repeat the search/weight-increase cycle until an improving move becomes available. Plateau points are treated as local minima rather than traversed by unweighted sideways moves. The method leaves initialization and the choice among improving changes open; the reported implementation uses random initial assignments and the first improving change found in a left-to-right scan. Weight increases persist and can also raise costs of neighboring states sharing the same nogoods.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The practical Breakout method is incomplete. The paper supplies a satisfiable four-variable CNF counterexample where repeated reweighting can oscillate without reaching the all-true solution. No finite-budget success or unsatisfiability certificate is implied.
  • The paper’s completeness theorem applies to the distinct idealized Fill algorithm, which stores and raises the cost of the current state alone on the finite assignment space. It must not be transferred to Breakout’s shared nogood weights.
  • Representation affects the penalty landscape: one constraint can contain several forbidden tuples, and shared nogoods couple costs of different assignments. A constraint-weight implementation requires evidence that its intended representation matches this scope.
  • Random initialization is an experimental choice, not a required random restart after each local minimum. Favorable results on constructed distributions do not establish general efficiency. The finite curator calculation checks a prefix of the published counterexample, not an independent proof for infinite executions.
