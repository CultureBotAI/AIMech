DSelect-k

Mathematical Methods AIMech:DSelectK DRAFT
A differentiable mixture-of-experts gate that combines k binary-encoded selectors through softmax weights and relaxes their bits with a smooth-step function; binary selectors yield at most k selected experts.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The gate of Hazimeh and colleagues (2021), not a complete mixture model or a trained checkpoint. Ignored-inclusive identity searches found no exact record; the separate discovery ledger records scope and nearby concepts.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Limitation #conditional-sparsity
Fractional relaxed bits can activate more than k experts. Entropy regularization encourages one-hot selectors; sparsity must be checked.
  • supports Source: DSelect-k: Differentiable Selection in the Mixture of Experts with Applications to Multi-Task Learning · Locator: Section 3.1.3, page 7, opening paragraph and entropy regularization
Mechanism #input-conditioning
The per-example version makes selector codes and combination weights functions of the input. Binary selector outputs give the k-expert bound for that example; an average bound is weaker than an every-example bound.
  • supports Source: DSelect-k: Differentiable Selection in the Mixture of Experts with Applications to Multi-Task Learning · Locator: Section 3.2, page 8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The presented binary encoding assumes a power-of-two expert count; the referenced arbitrary-count extension was not assessed here.
  • Binary initialization can freeze selector gradients; training starts with fractional selector bits.
  • No benchmark reproduction or independent scientific review was performed.
