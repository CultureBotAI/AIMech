Fast Wasserstein Barycenters and Smoothed Dual Subgradient Descent

Algorithms AIMech:FastWassersteinBarycentersAndSmoothedDualSubgradientDescent DRAFT
Aliases: Entropic Smoothed Wasserstein Barycenter Optimization, Cuturi-Doucet Fast Wasserstein Barycenter Algorithm, Smoothed Dual Sinkhorn Subgradient Barycenter Descent
Fast Wasserstein Barycenters and Smoothed Dual Subgradient Descent is a computational optimal transport framework introduced by Cuturi and Doucet (2014) for computing Fréchet means (Wasserstein barycenters) of empirical probability measures nu_1, ..., nu_N under p-Wasserstein distances by minimizing the weighted sum f(mu) = (1/N) sum_{i=1}^N D(mu, nu_i) over a discrete support X = [x_1, ..., x_n] and probability simplex weight vector a in Sigma_n. To overcome the non-smoothness and O(n^3 log n) linear-programming cost of the unregularized dual optimal transport polytope, the method introduces the entropically smoothed primal objective p_lambda(a, b; M) = min_{T in U(a,b)} <T, M> - (1/lambda) h(T) and its smoothed Legendre dual d_lambda(a, b; M) = max_{alpha, beta} alpha^T a + beta^T b - (1/lambda) sum_{i,j} exp(-lambda(m_{ij} - alpha_i - beta_j)). Proposition 2 and Algorithm 3 establish that the zero-sum centered optimal dual scaling logarithm alpha_lambda^* = (1/lambda) log(u) - (log(u)^T 1_n / (lambda n)) 1_n recovered from Sinkhorn-Knopp matrix scaling T_lambda^* = diag(u) exp(-lambda M) diag(v) is the exact gradient nabla_a p_lambda(a, b; M) on the probability simplex, enabling entropic mirror descent / exponentiated gradient updates for fixed-support weight optimization (Algorithm 1) and alternating closed-form Newton support updates X <- (1/N) sum_{i=1}^N Y_i (T_i^*)^T diag(a^{-1}) for free-support barycenters (Equation 8, Algorithm 2).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from pairwise Sinkhorn distance evaluation (`AIMech:SinkhornDistancesAndEntropicRegularizedOptimalTransport`) and from Iterative Bregman Projections (`AIMech:IterativeBregmanProjectionsAndBregmanDykstraRegularizedOptimalTransport`). Specifically captures the Cuturi & Doucet (2014) primal-dual entropic smoothing of Wasserstein barycenters (Propositions 1 and 2, Equations 6-10), the Algorithm 3 Sinkhorn primal-dual gradient extractor, the Algorithm 1 entropic mirror descent simplex optimizer for fixed-support weights, and the Equation 8 / Algorithm 2 alternating Newton step for free-support atom locations under squared Euclidean ground cost.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-01
For the entropically regularized transport problem p_lambda(a, b; M) = min_{T in U(a,b)} <T, M> - (1/lambda) h(T), Proposition 2 proves that the Legendre dual d_lambda(a, b; M) is strictly concave on {alpha in R^n : alpha^T 1_n = 0} and its unique maximizer alpha_lambda^* is related to the Sinkhorn-Knopp optimal coupling T_lambda^* = diag(u) exp(-lambda M) diag(v) via alpha_lambda^* = (1/lambda)(log u - (log u^T 1_n / n) 1_n) (Algorithm 3), providing the exact zero-sum tangent gradient of p_lambda(a, b; M) with respect to the barycenter weight vector a in Sigma_n.

Scope #claim-02
For squared Euclidean ground cost M_{X Y_i} = [||x_k - y_{ij}||_2^2], Section 4.3 (Equation 8 and Algorithm 2) shows that for fixed transport couplings T_1^*, ..., T_N^* and weight vector a, the objective sum_{i=1}^N <T_i^*, M_{X Y_i}> is a strictly convex quadratic function of the support matrix X whose exact Newton step updates X in closed form via X <- (1/N) sum_{i=1}^N Y_i (T_i^*)^T diag(a^{-1}).

Limitation #claim-03
In the unregularized LP formulation (lambda -> infinity), Proposition 1 establishes that a dual optimal vector alpha^* is a genuine gradient of p(a, b; M) only when the primal optimal coupling T^* has a connected bipartite support graph (a single spanning tree of n + m - 1 positive entries), and in the free-support problem f(X, a) is jointly non-convex in (X, a) so alternating optimization converges only to a stationary point.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Free-support Wasserstein barycenter optimization is jointly non-convex in support locations X and weights a, and entropic smoothing parameter lambda trades off blurring of the barycenter at small lambda against numerical underflow of exp(-lambda M) at very large lambda.
