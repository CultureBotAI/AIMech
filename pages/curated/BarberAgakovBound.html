Barber–Agakov mutual information bound

Mathematical Methods AIMech:BarberAgakovBound DRAFT
Aliases: Barber-Agakov bound, BA bound
A variational lower bound on mutual information formed by adding input entropy to the expected log probability assigned by a normalized conditional decoder to the input given the output.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The Barber–Agakov bound is the mathematical objective, distinct from Barber and Agakov’s alternating IM optimization procedure. It requires a normalized decoder; TUBA and NWJ provide different parameterizations using unnormalized critics. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For a joint distribution p(x,y) and normalized conditional decoder q(x|y), define L_BA=H(X)+E_p(x,y)[log q(X|Y)], using differential entropy for continuous variables when the required quantities are finite. The identity I(X;Y)−L_BA=E_p(y)[KL(p(X|Y)||q(X|Y))] gives L_BA<=I(X;Y). Equality holds when the decoder matches the true conditional almost everywhere. For representation learning with fixed p(x), an encoder p_theta(y|x) and decoder q_phi(x|y) can be trained to increase the expected decoder log probability; H(X) is constant with respect to their parameters. Dropping that constant for optimization does not supply a numerical information estimate when the entropy is unknown.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The decoder must be normalized and assign compatible support. An arbitrary real score substituted for log q is not this bound; zeros or divergent entropies require extended-value care.
  • Increasing a loose lower bound does not by itself prove that true mutual information increased. Decoder expressiveness and optimization quality determine the gap.
  • A sample average is an estimate of the expected objective and is not a certified lower bound for every realized sample. Unknown H(X) prevents reporting its numerical value without additional information.
  • Only declared theoretical passages and example context were inspected; no decoder implementation, training or benchmark was reproduced.
