Regularized information maximization

Algorithms AIMech:RegularizedInformationMaximization DRAFT
Aliases: RIM, Regularised information maximisation
A discriminative clustering framework that fits conditional class probabilities by maximizing empirical input–assignment mutual information penalized by classifier complexity, with linear and kernel softmax configurations.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope RIM denotes Gomes et al.’s named clustering framework. This record keeps its linear, kernel, nonuniform-prior and semi-supervised configurations explicit. It differs from IMSAT’s augmentation-consistency regularizer and from IIC’s information between paired assignment variables. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For N unlabeled inputs let p_theta(y|x_i) be conditional cluster probabilities and q(y)=N^−1 sum_i p_theta(y|x_i). Maximize H(q)−N^−1 sum_i H(p_theta(.|x_i))−R(theta). The linear configuration uses softmax(w_y^T x+b_y), R=lambda sum_y ||w_y||², and unpenalized biases, optimized with L-BFGS. Initialize by oversegmenting the data, fitting a multinomial logistic classifier to the resulting pseudo-labels, then optimizing the regularized information objective. Redundant categories can shrink toward zero marginal probability as their weights decay and biases become negative. The kernel configuration replaces linear scores with sum_i alpha_yi K(x_i,x)+b_y and penalizes RKHS squared norms alpha_y^T K alpha_y, with a regularization coefficient. A nonuniform-prior variant instead maximizes −mean conditional entropy−KL(q||D)−R for a chosen class prior D. A semi-supervised extension adds labeled log likelihood and weights the unlabeled information term separately.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The information objective is nonconcave. Finite L-BFGS optimization and a regularization sweep do not guarantee a global optimum or a true cluster count. Softmax probabilities are positive at finite parameters; exact class deletion is an ideal limiting description.
  • The uniform empirical input distribution and class-balance preference implicit in marginal entropy may not fit the desired population. The nonuniform-prior variant changes the objective and requires compatible prior support.
  • Kernel scores require an appropriate positive-semidefinite kernel for the stated RKHS penalty. Equation (8) omits the regularization coefficient while the following derivative includes it; this record makes that coefficient explicit.
  • The paper reports ground-truth adjusted-Rand scores across regularization settings. Those plots are not evidence for an automatic label-free hyperparameter or true-cluster-count selection rule.
  • Primary methods and declared experimental context were inspected; no RIM implementation, optimizer behavior or benchmark was reproduced.
