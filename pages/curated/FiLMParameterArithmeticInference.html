FiLM Parameter-Arithmetic Inference

Algorithms AIMech:FiLMParameterArithmeticInference DRAFT
A post-training visual-question-answering procedure that uses a parser to construct related questions and combines their generated FiLM scale/shift vectors by addition and subtraction before running the conditioned visual network.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #generated-parameter-combination
The method obtains FiLM gamma/beta vectors from three related questions and forms theta(q1)+theta(q2)-theta(q3) to supply the target question’s conditioning parameters. In the described analogy, q1 changes one attribute, q2 changes the other and q3 changes both. Arithmetic acts on generated conditioning values, not on question tokens, answer logits or the learned weights of separate complete models.
  • supports Source: FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer · Locator: Section 4.5, PDF pp. 8–9, worked analogy and Figure 10
Scope #parser-dependency
The reported evaluation uses a parser to automatically generate the appropriate question combination. It therefore assumes a supported question structure and suitable related questions. Replacing that parser with a learned model and training the conditioned network explicitly for analogies are proposed extensions, not demonstrated parts of this procedure.
  • supports Source: FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer · Locator: Section 4.5, PDF p. 9, evaluation and final paragraph
Scope #post-training-inference-stage
The arithmetic procedure is applied after the FiLM visual-reasoning model has been trained. It does not update the learned generator or visual-network weights for each analogy. The paper evaluates it on models before and after a separate Condition B fine-tuning phase; those are different model states. Post-training parameter manipulation does not imply that the underlying model needed no supervised training.
  • supports Source: FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer · Locator: Section 4.5, PDF pp. 8–9, Figure 9 and training discussion
Limitation #linear-parameter-nonlinear-output
A linear combination of generated FiLM parameters does not generally imply the same combination of final network outputs or correct semantic composition. For one affine layer with a fixed incoming feature, the corresponding parameter/output arithmetic identity holds. In a network with several modulated layers, interactions and nonlinearities can break that identity; deeper incoming features can themselves depend on the conditioning.
  • supports Source: FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer · Locator: Section 4.5, PDF p. 9, qualified parameter-interpolation observations; Sections 2.1–2.2, pp. 2–3; direct two-layer counterexample
Limitation #restricted-generalization-evaluation
The source evaluates the procedure on CLEVR-CoGenT, whose conditions change color-shape combinations, and reports that its parser-based construction applies to only one third of Condition B questions. In Figure 9, the Condition B validation-accuracy curve excludes the 30000 examples used for Condition B fine-tuning. This does not establish general zero-shot prediction of unseen answer classes, universal compositional reasoning, or applicability to arbitrary questions and images.
  • supports Source: FiLM: Visual Reasoning with a General Conditioning Layer · Locator: Section 4.5, PDF pp. 8–9, Figure 9 caption and applicability paragraph

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The local name is descriptive and is scoped to the Section 4.5 procedure, not every use of FiLM, neural parameter arithmetic or analogy-based reasoning.
  • The method depends on a parser and related-question construction. A learned parser replacement and explicit analogy training remain future work in the inspected source.
  • Constructed algebra does not certify semantic analogies or replicate the reported visual-question-answering results. The AIO no-match finding is limited to the inspected snapshot and targets.
