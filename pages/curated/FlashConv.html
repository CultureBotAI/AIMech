FlashConv

Algorithms AIMech:FlashConv DRAFT
An execution algorithm for state-space sequence convolutions that fuses FFT-based convolution in GPU on-chip memory, uses block FFT matrix multiplications and passes state between chunks for longer sequences.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope FlashConv is the convolution-execution contribution in the H3 paper, distinct from the H3 layer and from FlashAttention's attention-computation algorithm.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
Fuse the FFT, pointwise spectral product and inverse FFT to avoid intermediate off-chip transfers; factor the FFT into block matrix multiplications suited to specialized hardware. When the sequence exceeds the chosen on-chip working capacity, compute chunk convolutions and propagate the state-space boundary state between chunks.
  • supports Source: Hungry Hungry Hippos: Towards Language Modeling with State Space Models · Locator: Sections 4.1–4.2 and Algorithm 2, PDF/printed pp. 6–7

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The paper's 8K on-chip threshold concerns its stated A100 setting; no device-independent cutoff or wall-clock speedup is asserted.
  • Block FFT may use more arithmetic than a conventional FFT while exploiting specialized matrix hardware.
  • The printed Algorithm 2 mixes chunk variable names and its exact boundary indexing was not implemented or verified here. This record supports the execution strategy, not a certified literal transcription of the pseudocode.
