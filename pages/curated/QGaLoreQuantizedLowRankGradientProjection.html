Q-GaLore (Quantized GaLore with INT4 Projection and Layer-Adaptive Low-Rank Gradients)

Algorithms AIMech:QGaLoreQuantizedLowRankGradientProjection DRAFT
Aliases: Q-GaLore, Quantized GaLore, INT4-Projection Quantized Low-Rank Gradient Optimizer, Layer-Adaptive Quantized Gradient Low-Rank Projection
A memory-efficient quantized low-rank gradient-projection optimization algorithm for pre-training and fine-tuning large language models that stores model weights and low-rank first- and second-order optimizer moments in INT8, quantizes the low-rank singular-vector projection matrices to INT4, accumulates low-magnitude weight updates into INT8 master weights via unbiased stochastic rounding, and dynamically doubles each layer's SVD subspace update interval whenever consecutive projection matrices exceed a cosine similarity threshold.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from GaLore (AIMech:GaLoreGradientLowRankProjection), which maintains full BF16/FP16 master weights, stores projection matrices in 16-bit floating point (or 8-bit in Q-GaLore baseline), and recomputes SVD projections at a fixed uniform interval across all layers. Distinct from QLoRA (AIMech:QloraQuantizedLowRankAdaptation), which freezes 4-bit NormalFloat base weights and optimizes only additive low-rank adapter matrices rather than updating the full-rank weight matrix in INT8 via quantized low-rank gradient projections.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #qgalore-layer-adaptive-subspace-cosine-schedule-mechanism
Rather than recomputing SVD projections at a fixed uniform step interval across all layers, Q-GaLore evaluates the subspace cosine similarity CosSim(P_t, P_{t - Delta_t}) = (1/r) sum_{i=1}^r sigma_i(P_t^T P_{t - Delta_t}) between consecutive rank-r singular-vector projection matrices and doubles the layer's update interval Delta_t <- 2 * Delta_t whenever CosSim(P_t, P_{t - Delta_t}) > gamma (default gamma = 0.4), reducing SVD overhead by over 80%.
  • supports Source: Q-GaLore: Quantized GaLore with INT4 Projection and Layer-Adaptive Low-Rank Gradients · Locator: Section 3.1 Theorem 3.1, Equation (5), Figure 2, and Algorithm 1 lines 5-13, pages 3-5
Mechanism #qgalore-int4-projection-and-int8-stochastic-rounding-mechanism
Exploiting the low kurtosis and outlier-free range of unitary singular vectors in P_t, Q-GaLore quantizes the projection matrix P_t to 4-bit integers (INT4, cutting projection memory by 75% vs BF16), maintains low-rank first and second moments (M_t^Q, V_t^Q) in INT8, and updates INT8 master weights via W_t^Q <- W_{t-1}^Q + xi(alpha * P_t^Q * G_tilde_t) using unbiased stochastic rounding xi(.) to preserve small-gradient update information.
  • supports Source: Q-GaLore: Quantized GaLore with INT4 Projection and Layer-Adaptive Low-Rank Gradients · Locator: Section 3.2, Figure 3, and Algorithm 1 lines 7-8, 12, 16-20, pages 4-5
Scope #qgalore-stationary-gradient-subspace-convergence-scope
Theorem 3.1 establishes that for linear layers y = W x with batch input X and output gradient D_y, once weights enter a stationary region W_{t+T_1} approx W_{t+T_2}, the weight gradient G = (1/B) D_y X^T decomposes as A B C^T in the principal singular-vector subspaces of X X^T and D_y D_y^T, explaining why different layers converge to stable low-rank gradient subspaces at heterogeneous rates during training.
  • supports Source: Q-GaLore: Quantized GaLore with INT4 Projection and Layer-Adaptive Low-Rank Gradients · Locator: Section 3.1 Theorem 3.1 and Equations (3)-(4), pages 3-4
Application #qgalore-llama7b-single-16gb-gpu-pretraining-application
Q-GaLore enables pre-training a LLaMA-7B language model from scratch on a single NVIDIA RTX 4060 Ti GPU with 16 GB VRAM, reducing total weight and optimizer-state memory by up to 61.9% compared to BF16 Adam and 30.0% compared to GaLore while matching full-precision perplexity across LLaMA-60M to LLaMA-7B on C4 and fine-tuning benchmarks on GLUE and MMLU.
  • supports Source: Q-GaLore: Quantized GaLore with INT4 Projection and Layer-Adaptive Low-Rank Gradients · Locator: Abstract, Section 1 Figure 1, and Section 4.1-4.3 Tables 1-3, pages 1-2, 5-7
Limitation #qgalore-simulated-weight-quantization-throughput-limitation
Because Q-GaLore retains activations in 16-bit floating point and dequantizes INT8 weights during forward and backward passes (simulated weight-only quantization), its end-to-end training throughput depends on fused dequantization kernel efficiency and does not yet accelerate activation-weight matrix multiplications using native INT8 tensor cores.
  • supports Source: Q-GaLore: Quantized GaLore with INT4 Projection and Layer-Adaptive Low-Rank Gradients · Locator: Section 3.2 and Section 4.4 Figure 4, pages 4-5, 8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Relies on simulated weight-only INT8 quantization with 16-bit activations and stochastic rounding, requiring custom fused dequantization and rounding kernels to avoid latency overhead.
  • Adds an additional hyperparameter gamma (subspace cosine similarity threshold, default 0.4) alongside initial SVD update interval Delta_t_0 and rank r.
