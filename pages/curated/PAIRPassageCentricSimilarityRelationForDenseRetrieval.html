PAIR Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Dense Passage Retrieval

Algorithms AIMech:PAIRPassageCentricSimilarityRelationForDenseRetrieval DRAFT
Aliases: PAIR, PAIR Passage-Centric Dense Passage Retrieval, Leveraging Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Improving Dense Passage Retrieval
A dense passage retrieval training algorithm that jointly enforces query-centric similarity relations (pulling query q toward positive passage p^+ and pushing q away from negative passages p^- in N_P via L_Q(q, p^+, N_P) = -log(exp(f(q, p^+)) / (exp(f(q, p^+)) + sum_{p^- in N_P} exp(f(q, p^-))))) and passage-centric similarity relations (pushing positive passage p^+ away from negative passages p^- in N_P via L_P(q, p^+, N_P) = -log(exp(-f(p^+, p^+)) / (exp(-f(p^+, p^+)) + sum_{p^- in N_P} exp(f(p^+, p^-))))) under a combined objective L(q, p^+, N_P) = (1 - alpha) L_Q(q, p^+, N_P) + alpha L_P(q, p^+, N_P) trained in two stages: a pre-training stage on denoised synthetic pseudo-queries with decaying weight alpha > 0 followed by a fine-tuning stage on human-labeled queries with alpha = 0.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from DPR (AIMech:DensePassageRetrieval) and RocketQA (AIMech:RocketQADenoisedHardNegativeSampling), which optimize only query-passage similarity f(q, p) without explicitly penalizing passage-passage inner products f(p^+, p^-); and distinct from GPL (AIMech:GPLGenerativePseudoLabelingForDenseRetrieval), which distills cross-encoder query-passage margins on synthetic queries rather than optimizing a passage-centric contrastive repulsion loss L_P.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #pair-passage-centric-contrastive-loss
Under the geometric separation condition (Assumption 1: max(||q - p^+||, ||p^+ - p^-||) < ||q - p^-||), query-centric similarity alone cannot prevent hard negative passages p^- from clustering near p^+; PAIR introduces the passage-centric contrastive loss L_P(q, p^+, N_P) = -log(exp(-f(p^+, p^+)) / (exp(-f(p^+, p^+)) + sum_{p^- in N_P} exp(f(p^+, p^-)))) (Equation 2) and combines it with L_Q via L = (1 - alpha) L_Q + alpha L_P (Equation 3) to explicitly minimize positive-negative passage inner products f(p^+, p^-).

  • supports Source: PAIR: Leveraging Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Improving Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: main() verification of query_centric_loss L_Q, passage_centric_loss L_P, and combined PAIR loss under clustered vs. separated hard negatives
Mechanism #pair-two-stage-pseudo-query-training
To overcome data sparsity when estimating both query-centric and passage-centric similarity relations, PAIR generates synthetic queries from passages via a T5/BART generator, filters out noisy pseudo-labels and false negatives with a cross-encoder, pre-trains the dual-encoder with dynamic decay of alpha from alpha_0 > 0, and then fine-tunes on labeled target queries with alpha = 0 (Section 2.4).
  • supports Source: PAIR: Leveraging Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Improving Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section 2.4 Two-Stage Training Procedure, pp. 3-4
Application #pair-benchmark-applications
PAIR achieves 37.9 MRR@10 and 86.4% Recall@50 on MS MARCO Passage Ranking Dev and 74.9% Recall@5 / 83.5% Recall@20 on Natural Questions test, outperforming RocketQA and prior dense retrievers while reducing positive-negative passage cosine similarity (Tables 1, 3, and 4).
  • supports Source: PAIR: Leveraging Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Improving Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section 3.2 Main Results & Section 3.3 Detailed Analysis, Tables 1-4, pp. 5-7
  • supports Source: PAIR: Leveraging Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Improving Dense Passage Retrieval (Crossref metadata) · Locator: BibTeX citation record for arXiv:2108.06027 (Findings of ACL-IJCNLP 2021)
Limitation #pair-unit-norm-and-pseudo-query-limitation
When passage embeddings are not L2-normalized, the exp(-f(p^+, p^+)) term in L_P can encourage growing the norm ||p^+|| unless unit-norm constraints or careful decay of alpha are applied, and the two-stage pipeline requires generating and cross-encoder-filtering millions of synthetic queries.
  • supports Source: PAIR: Leveraging Passage-Centric Similarity Relation for Improving Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Sections 2.2-2.4 & Section 3.1 Implementation Details, pp. 3-5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires generating millions of synthetic pseudo-queries and scoring them with a cross-encoder to provide sufficient training coverage for passage-centric similarity estimation.
  • Sensitive to the weighting hyperparameter alpha and its decay schedule between Stage 1 pre-training and Stage 2 fine-tuning.
