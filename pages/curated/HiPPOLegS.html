Scaled Legendre HiPPO operator

Mathematical Methods AIMech:HiPPOLegS DRAFT
Aliases: HiPPO-LegS
The HiPPO projection operator using the uniform measure over the full elapsed interval from zero to the current time, with scaled-Legendre coefficients evolving under explicitly time-dependent linear memory dynamics.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope HiPPO-LegS is the scaled-Legendre instantiation of the broader HiPPO framework. Its time-dependent operator differs from a fixed state matrix used to initialize a later S4 model and from the fixed-width translated-Legendre window.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
Use the normalized uniform measure on [0,t] and the corresponding Legendre basis. For positive time, the coefficient derivative is minus A times the current coefficient vector divided by t, plus B times the current signal divided by t; the source specifies a lower-triangular A and a fixed B and derives discrete recurrences from these dynamics.
  • supports Source: HiPPO: Recurrent Memory with Optimal Polynomial Projections · Locator: Section 3, Theorem 2, equations 3–4 and footnote 3, with Sections 2.1–2.4 for projection context; PDF/printed pp. 3–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The displayed differential formula contains 1/t and applies for positive t; an executable initialization at the time origin is not supplied by this record.
  • Equation 4 illustrates an Euler discretization; the paper also uses bilinear and zero-order-hold schemes. The operator's approximation properties do not guarantee arbitrary downstream-network accuracy.
