Aliases: RAP
A harmonium model family coupling unbounded visible Poisson counts to finite binomial hidden variables through bilinear interactions that shift conditional log rates and binomial logits.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: The Rate Adapting Poisson Model for Information Retrieval and Object Recognition · Locator: Section 2.1, PDF pp.2-3, Equations 1-3 and 6
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Scoped to Gehler, Holub and Welling's ICML2006 RAP construction with fixed finite integer binomial trial counts. This differs from a Gaussian-hidden count harmonium, a two-unbounded-Poisson-layer construction, categorical word-bin EFH and replicated-token Softmax. Baseline Poisson rates and hidden logits are conditional parameters, not generally marginal means or independent hidden priors. The latent count model is distinct from its CD fitting algorithm or a trained retrieval system. Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #compatible-count-conditionals
With baseline log rates a_i, hidden thresholds beta_j, interaction weights W_ij and finite integer trial counts M_j, visible counts conditioned on h are independent Poisson with rates exp(a_i+sum_j W_ij h_j). Hidden variables conditioned on x are independent Binomial(M_j,sigmoid(sum_i W_ij x_i-beta_j)). The joint retains visible factorial and hidden binomial base measures. Summing all visible counts first leaves a finite sum over bounded hidden configurations, proving a finite joint normalizer for finite parameters without truncating visible counts.
  • supports Source: The Rate Adapting Poisson Model for Information Retrieval and Object Recognition · Locator: Section 2.1, PDF pp.2-3, Equations 1-3
Mechanism #marginal-and-complement-symmetry
Marginalizing hidden units gives visible log mass sum_i(a_i x_i-log(x_i!))+sum_j M_j softplus(sum_i W_ij x_i-beta_j), up to a global constant. The hard threshold interpretation is an approximation to softplus, not exact zero influence below a threshold. Complementing a hidden count to M_j-h_j, reversing its weight column and threshold, and adding M_j W_ij to each visible log baseline preserves the normalized visible law. This discrete symmetry does not establish arbitrary orthogonal rotation invariance or full parameter identifiability.
  • supports Source: The Rate Adapting Poisson Model for Information Retrieval and Object Recognition · Locator: Sections 2.1-2.2, PDF pp.3-4, Equations 6-9
Mechanism #conditional-moment-learning-and-features
Likelihood gradients contrast observed and equilibrium sufficient statistics. Conditional hidden expectations M_j sigmoid(W_j^T x-beta_j) replace sampled hidden counts in the displayed gradient statistics. The reported CD1 procedure draws hidden counts from each observed vector, reconstructs visible counts and uses short-chain negative expectations, introducing approximation bias. Retrieval uses cosine similarity of the linear projection W^T x, which is distinct from the nonlinear conditional-binomial mean and from exact joint likelihood scoring.
  • supports Source: The Rate Adapting Poisson Model for Information Retrieval and Object Recognition · Locator: Section 2.3, PDF p.4, Equation 10 and Algorithm 1; Sections 3.1-3.2, pp.5-6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The normalizer is finite but its exact finite hidden sum can grow exponentially with the number and ranges of hidden units. CD, minibatches and momentum do not certify exact maximum likelihood or chain convergence. No noninteger binomial trial-count extension is asserted.
  • The document-retrieval topic dimension is selected using test-set one-nearest-neighbor performance, explicitly stated in the Figure 4 caption. Reported curves therefore do not constitute an independently tuned held-out comparison. The split used for class-informed newsgroup vocabulary selection is insufficiently specified to assert a separate leakage mechanism.
  • RAP is compared with LSI and PLSI on selected text corpora and bag-of-visual-words image tasks. LDA is omitted from the reported comparison. Different retrieval measures and dataset configurations yield different rankings; no universal performance advantage is curated.
  • Finite checks verify 96 hidden conditional probabilities, exact visible marginalization, a likelihood gradient and complement symmetry. No author code, coordinate-mode implementation, empirical benchmark, hierarchical extension or negative-binomial replacement was executed or certified.
