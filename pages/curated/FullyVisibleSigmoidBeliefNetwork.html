Fully visible sigmoid belief network

Architectures AIMech:FullyVisibleSigmoidBeliefNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: FVSBN, Logistic autoregressive Bayesian network
A fully observed binary autoregressive probabilistic model whose conditional probability at each position is a logistic function of a bias and a weighted sum of preceding visible variables.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the fully visible sigmoid model discussed by Larochelle and Murray, with no stochastic hidden variables. Frey and colleagues call their binary logistic special case a fully visible belief network; that local usage does not make all fully visible Bayesian networks synonyms of the sigmoid family. The record specializes the broader sigmoid-belief-network family and is distinct from NADE’s nonlinear deterministic hidden features and DARN’s stochastic latent architecture. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Fix an ordering of D binary visible variables. Parameterize P(x_i=1|x_<i)=sigmoid(b_i+sum_(j<i) w_ij x_j). Multiply the corresponding Bernoulli probabilities over positions to evaluate the normalized observed joint. All variables in that product are visible, so complete-data likelihood evaluation has no hidden-state marginalization or recognition-model posterior approximation. Its negative log likelihood is the sum of the positionwise binary logistic losses. Generate a sample by drawing each bit from the conditional determined by already drawn predecessors. Full connectivity makes all earlier visible variables available as parents; zero coefficients can remove particular dependencies. A fixed-order conditional’s log odds are affine in its predecessors, unlike the more general nonlinear neural conditional used by NADE.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The chain-rule factorization is general, but restricting each conditional to a logistic-linear function limits the distribution family. A given fixed-order conditional table with a nonzero logit interaction cannot be represented by that affine logit; this is not a claim about every alternative ordering or latent extension.
  • Exact complete-observation likelihood and forward generation do not provide arbitrary missing-variable posteriors for free. Conditioning on a suffix while earlier variables are unknown still requires inference or summation.
  • Changing the variable order changes the restricted model family and can affect fitting. The absence of stochastic latent variables is part of this specialization, not a property of all sigmoid belief networks.
  • The source’s FVBN label is interpreted within its binary logistic model setting. No assertion that every fully visible Bayesian model uses logistic factors, no universal performance ranking and no trained-model result is included.
