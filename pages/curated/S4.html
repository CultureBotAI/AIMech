Structured state space sequence model

Model Families AIMech:S4 DRAFT
Aliases: S4
A structured linear state-space sequence model whose normal-plus-low-rank state matrix is transformed to diagonal-plus-low-rank form for efficient convolution-kernel computation and recurrent sequence evaluation.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope S4 denotes Gu, Goel and Ré's specific structured state-space construction, not every state-space model, S4D, an exponential-smoothing model, or Mamba's input-dependent selective state updates. Deep S4 networks compose these linear models with channel mixing and nonlinearities.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
Bilinear discretization gives recurrent and zero-initial-state convolutional views. A unitary basis change produces a diagonal-plus-low-rank state matrix; Cauchy-kernel evaluations, a low-rank Woodbury correction and an inverse FFT compute the finite convolution kernel.
  • supports Source: Efficiently Modeling Long Sequences with Structured State Spaces · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.4 and 3.1–3.4, equations 1–5, Algorithm 1 and Theorem 1, PDF/printed pp. 3–7

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • This record follows arXiv v3's original diagonal-minus-PQ* parameterization. Section 3.4 notes possible numerical instability with right-half-plane eigenvalues and points to a later diagonal-minus-PP* revision; stability is not asserted unconditionally.
  • The core S4 model is linear. A deep nonlinear S4 network adds channel mixing and nonlinear transformations; kernel computation is not the complete network.
  • No universal accuracy, memory or wall-clock advantage is asserted.
