Adan (Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Algorithm)

Algorithms AIMech:AdanAdaptiveNesterovMomentumAlgorithm DRAFT
Aliases: Adan Optimizer, Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Estimation, NME Adaptive Nesterov Optimizer
An adaptive first-order stochastic optimization algorithm for deep neural networks that reformulates Nesterov accelerated gradient via Nesterov Momentum Estimation (NME) so that lookahead extrapolation requires zero additional gradient oracle evaluations. At step k, Adan updates a first-order gradient EMA m_k = (1 - beta_1) m_{k-1} + beta_1 g_k, a first-order gradient-difference EMA v_k = (1 - beta_2) v_{k-1} + beta_2 (g_k - g_{k-1}), a second-order composite moment n_k = (1 - beta_3) n_{k-1} + beta_3 [g_k + (1 - beta_2)(g_k - g_{k-1})]^2, an elementwise adaptive step size eta_k = eta / (sqrt(n_k) + eps), and a proximal weight-decay iterate update theta_{k+1} = (1 + lambda_k eta)^{-1} (theta_k - eta_k circ (m_k + (1 - beta_2) v_k)).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Nadam (Dozat 2016), AdamW (Loshchilov and Hutter 2019), Lion (AIMech:LionEvoSignMomentumOptimizer), and AdEMAMix (AIMech:AdEMAMixDualExponentialMovingAverageOptimizer): Adan derives its update from an algebraic rearrangement of Nesterov's extrapolated gradient (Lemma 1, Eq. 4) that maintains a dedicated gradient-difference EMA v_k with its own decay coefficient beta_2, preconditioning the combined vector m_k + (1 - beta_2) v_k by the RMS of the composite Nesterov perturbation g_k + (1 - beta_2)(g_k - g_{k-1}) and applying exact proximal scaling (1 + lambda_k eta)^{-1} for decoupled L2 regularization.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #nesterov-momentum-estimation-lemma-1-equivalence
Lemma 1 (Section 2.1, p. 3) proves that the lookahead Nesterov accelerated gradient recursion theta'_k = theta_k - eta (1 - beta_1) m_{k-1}, m_k = (1 - beta_1) m_{k-1} + g'_k, theta_{k+1} = theta_k - eta m_k is algebraically identical to defining u_k := theta'_k with update u_{k+1} = u_k - eta m'_k, where m'_k = (1 - beta_1) m'_{k-1} + [g'_k + (1 - beta_1)(g'_k - g'_{k-1})] = sum_{t=0}^k (1 - beta_1)^t [g'_{k-t} + (1 - beta_1)(g'_{k-t} - g'_{k-t-1})], eliminating the need to compute gradients at a separate extrapolated point.
  • supports Source: Adan: Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Algorithm for Faster Optimizing Deep Models · Locator: Section 2.1, Eqs. 3-4 and Lemma 1, p. 3
Mechanism #proximal-weight-decay-fixed-point-and-separability
Adan derives its weight-decay step theta_{k+1} = (1 + lambda_k eta)^{-1} (theta_k - eta_k circ (m_k + (1 - beta_2) v_k)) as the exact first-order approximation to the scaled proximal operator argmin_theta { (lambda_k eta / 2) ||theta||_2^2 + (1/2) ||theta - (theta_k - eta_k circ u_k)||_{1/sqrt(eta_k)}^2 } (Eq. 5, p. 4), and Proposition 3 (p. 5) proves that every fixed point theta* of Adan satisfies nabla F(theta*) + lambda (c_l + eps) theta* = 0, dynamically decoupling the optimization objective from weight-decay scaling.
  • supports Source: Adan: Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Algorithm for Faster Optimizing Deep Models · Locator: Section 2.1, Eq. 5, p. 4; Section 2.2, Proposition 3, p. 5
Scope #smooth-and-holder-nonconvex-stochastic-complexity-theorems-1-and-2
Under L-smooth nonconvex stochastic objectives with coordinate-wise bounded gradient norms ||g_k||_infty <= c_infty and variance E||g_k - nabla F(theta_k)||_2^2 <= sigma^2 (Assumptions 1-3), Theorem 1 (Section 2.3, p. 5) proves that Adan achieves an epsilon-approximate first-order stationary point in O(c_infty sigma^2 epsilon^{-4}) stochastic gradient evaluations, matching the lower bound under weakened coordinate-wise boundedness, while Theorem 2 (p. 6) establishes convergence under Holder-continuous gradients with parameter nu in (0, 1) without global Lipschitz smoothness.
  • supports Source: Adan: Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Algorithm for Faster Optimizing Deep Models · Locator: Section 2.3, Assumptions 1-3, Theorem 1, and Theorem 2, p. 5-6
Application #vision-nlp-and-rl-training-acceleration
Across ViT, Swin, ConvNeXt, ResNet, MAE, GPT-2, Transformer-XL, BERT, and MuJoCo PPO benchmarks, Adan matches or exceeds the test accuracy and reward of Adam, AdamW, LAMB, and AdaBelief while using half the training epochs on ImageNet vision backbones.
  • supports Source: Adan: Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Algorithm for Faster Optimizing Deep Models · Locator: Section 3, Tables 2-12, p. 6-11
Limitation #extra-gradient-difference-state-and-three-beta-hyperparameters
Adan stores three running state tensors per parameter (m_k, v_k, n_k) plus the previous step gradient g_{k-1}—increasing optimizer state memory relative to two-state AdamW—and introduces three momentum decay hyperparameters (beta_1, beta_2, beta_3) that require coordinated selection.
  • supports Source: Adan: Adaptive Nesterov Momentum Algorithm for Faster Optimizing Deep Models · Locator: Section 2.1, Algorithm 1, p. 4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Maintains an additional first-order gradient-difference state tensor v_k and previous gradient g_{k-1} beyond standard AdamW's two-moment state.
  • Introduces three momentum decay coefficients (beta_1, beta_2, beta_3) whose finite-time stability depends on beta_2 controlling the weight of high-variance stochastic gradient differences g_k - g_{k-1}.
