High-order polynomial projection operators

Mathematical Methods AIMech:HiPPOProjection DRAFT
Aliases: HiPPO (polynomial projection)
A family of time-dependent operators that represents a signal's past by coefficients of its optimal finite-dimensional polynomial approximation under a chosen time-varying measure.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope HiPPO denotes the general projection-and-coefficient framework, not one fixed state matrix, a complete recurrent neural network, or only the scaled-Legendre instantiation HiPPO-LegS. The qualified acronym distinguishes this operator family from the unrelated highlight-replay PPO method.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
At each time, project the available signal history onto a polynomial subspace by minimizing the measure-weighted squared approximation error, then extract coefficients in the associated orthogonal basis. For the derived measure families, differentiating those coefficients yields linear differential dynamics that can be discretized as online recurrences.
  • supports Source: HiPPO: Recurrent Memory with Optimal Polynomial Projections · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.4, Definition 1 and Figure 1, PDF/printed pp. 2–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Optimality is relative to the chosen measure and finite polynomial subspace, not lossless recovery of arbitrary history or optimal downstream prediction.
  • A tractable closed-form recurrence is not asserted for every arbitrary measure. The translated-Legendre sliding-window update additionally approximates the unavailable boundary value; numerical discretization adds its own approximation.
