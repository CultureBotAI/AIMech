FedETF (Neural-Collapse-Inspired Federated Learning with Synthetic Fixed Simplex ETF Classifier)

Algorithms AIMech:FedEtfNeuralCollapseSimplexEquiangularTightFrameClassifierForFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedETF, Simplex Equiangular Tight Frame Federated Learning, Neural-Collapse Synthetic Fixed ETF Classifier for Heterogeneous Federated Learning
A neural-collapse-inspired federated learning algorithm (Li et al. 2023, Sections 3.2-4.2, Definition 3.1, Equations 3-11, Algorithms 1-2) that eliminates local classifier bias under non-IID data by replacing learnable linear classifiers with a synthetic, globally fixed Simplex Equiangular Tight Frame (ETF) classifier matrix V_ETF = sqrt(C / (C - 1)) U (I_C - (1 / C) 1_C 1_C^T) in R^{d x C} (d >= C - 1, U^T U = I_C) whose columns satisfy unit l_2-norm and maximal equiangular separation v_i^T v_j = (C / (C - 1)) delta_{i,j} - 1 / (C - 1) (Equations 3-4), projecting client backbone features onto the unit hypersphere mu = g(p; f(u; x)) / ||g(p; f(u; x))||_2 (Equation 5), training {u, p, beta} globally via a class-frequency-balanced feature loss ell^g(w, V_ETF; x, y) = -log[(n_{k,y}^gamma exp(beta v_y^T mu)) / sum_{c in [C]} n_{k,c}^gamma exp(beta v_c^T mu)] with learnable scalar temperature beta (Equations 6-8), and adapting locally via two-stage alternating personalized finetuning (Equations 9-11).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from CCVR (AIMech:CcvrClassifierCalibrationWithVirtualRepresentationsForFederatedLearning, Luo et al. 2021), which trains standard unconstrained classifiers during federated learning and recalibrates the classifier post-hoc using Gaussian virtual features; and distinct from FedBABU (AIMech:FedBabuFederatedLearningWithBodyAggregationAndBodyUpdate, Oh et al. 2022), which freezes a randomly initialized orthogonal or uniform classifier head rather than synthesizing an exact Simplex Equiangular Tight Frame V_ETF with a projection layer, learnable temperature scaling beta, and power-law balanced feature loss.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r461-synthetic-simplex-etf-and-balanced-feature-loss-mechanism
Synthesizes a fixed C-simplex ETF classifier V_ETF = sqrt(C / (C - 1)) U (I_C - (1 / C) 1_C 1_C^T) with pairwise cosine similarity -1 / (C - 1) (Definition 3.1, Equations 3-4) and trains l_2-normalized projected features mu alongside a learnable temperature scalar beta using the balanced feature loss ell^g(w, V_ETF; x, y) weighted by local class sample counts n_{k,c}^gamma (Equations 5-7), aligning all clients' class representations toward the optimal Neural Collapse (NC1-NC3) geometry without classifier drift.
  • supports Source: No Fear of Classifier Biases: Neural Collapse Inspired Federated Learning with Synthetic and Fixed Classifier · Locator: Section 3.2 (Equations 3-4) & Section 4.1 Improving Generalization by ETF Classifier (Equations 5-8), pp. 3-5
Scope #r461-two-stage-alternating-personalized-adaptation-scope
Supports both global model generalization and local personalization via a two-stage post-training adaptation protocol (Section 4.2, Equations 9-11, Algorithm 2) that first adapts the backbone {u, beta} with {p, V_ETF} frozen and then alternates finetuning {V_ETF, beta} and {p, beta} under unweighted cross-entropy loss to match skewed local test distributions.
  • supports Source: No Fear of Classifier Biases: Neural Collapse Inspired Federated Learning with Synthetic and Fixed Classifier · Locator: Section 4.2 Personalized Adaptation by Local Finetuning (Equations 9-11) & Appendix A Algorithm 2, pp. 5-6, 13
Limitation #r461-feature-dimension-lower-bound-and-projection-layer-limitation
Requires the projection feature dimension d to satisfy d >= C - 1 so that an orthonormal frame U in R^{d x C} with U^T U = I_C can embed a C-class Simplex ETF, and introduces an extra projection MLP g(p; h) plus hyperparameter gamma for balanced loss weighting.
  • supports Source: No Fear of Classifier Biases: Neural Collapse Inspired Federated Learning with Synthetic and Fixed Classifier · Locator: Section 3.2 Definition 3.1 & Section 4.1 Equations 5-6, pp. 3-5
Application #r461-long-tailed-and-heterogeneous-federated-vision-application
Applied to heterogeneous and long-tailed federated visual recognition on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and Tiny-ImageNet across Dirichlet label skew and partial client participation.
  • supports Source: No Fear of Classifier Biases: Neural Collapse Inspired Federated Learning with Synthetic and Fixed Classifier · Locator: Section 5 Experiments (Tables 1-4, Figures 3-6), pp. 6-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires feature embedding dimension d >= C - 1 to construct an exact C-class Simplex ETF.
  • Adds an explicit projection layer g(p; h) and two-stage alternating finetuning epochs for personalized adaptation.
