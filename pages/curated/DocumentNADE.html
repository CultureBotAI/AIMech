Document NADE

Architectures AIMech:DocumentNADE DRAFT
Aliases: DocNADE
A neural autoregressive document model that predicts successive words from shared additive embeddings of the preceding words, using normalized word conditionals and a deterministic document representation.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Larochelle and Lauly’s original Document NADE. The final construction uses binary-tree word probabilities, with a flat-softmax intermediate derivation. It is inspired by Replicated Softmax but is a separately normalized directed model with deterministic hidden features, not the same undirected latent-variable model. Its full-document representation is invariant to word permutation, while its unsymmetrized sequence probability need not be. Supervised and distributional-prior extensions require separate scope decisions. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For a document of given length D, form h_i=sigmoid(c+sum_(k<i) W[:,v_k]); the same word embedding and output parameters are reused across positions, and the preactivation is updated by adding the newly observed word’s embedding. A flat softmax can normalize the next-word distribution. The paper’s final model replaces it with a binary tree whose leaves are vocabulary words: each internal node predicts the probability of taking its right branch, and a word probability is the product of its observed left/right branch probabilities. Multiply all observed-word conditionals to obtain a normalized probability over length-D sequences. Train by differentiating their negative log likelihood. After all words, h(v)=sigmoid(c+sum_i W[:,v_i]) is an order-invariant document representation; tanh is also tested. For count-vector input, the paper samples word occurrences without replacement to produce uniformly random distinct orderings and trains on those sequences. Its separate symmetrized sequence model averages probabilities over distinct permutations; training on sampled order log losses is a surrogate for that log mixture. A count-vector probability instead sums the unsymmetrized sequence probabilities over its distinct permutations.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Order invariance of the summed document representation does not imply exchangeability of the sequence likelihood. The symmetrized probability of each sequence differs from its count-vector probability by the number of distinct permutations; duplicate words change that number.
  • For a word whose tree path has length L, conditional output work is O(HL). The claimed logarithmic vocabulary scaling requires suitable tree depth or average depth; a full binary tree alone can have linear depth. The record does not assert a logarithmic worst case for every full tree.
  • The main paper’s text below Equation (11) retains vi=1 in the joint product although words are categorical. The curated likelihood uses the observed word and its branch path, consistent with Equations (9) and (11) and the supplement’s probability computation.
  • For Bernoulli tree training, the negative-log-likelihood derivative is the probability of the right branch minus the observed branch indicator. The supplement’s p(actual branch)-indicator notation must not be read literally as the probability of the observed left branch when that indicator is zero. No author implementation behavior is inferred.
  • The stated model is conditional on document length and does not supply a learned document-length distribution. Shared additive prefix features discard order within the observed prefix. Hidden topic features are deterministic representations, not posterior latent-topic probabilities.
  • No classification/retrieval advantage, semantic quality guarantee, exact symmetrized-mixture training gradient or implementation certification is asserted.
