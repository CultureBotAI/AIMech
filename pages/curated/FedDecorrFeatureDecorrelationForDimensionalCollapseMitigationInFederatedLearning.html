FedDecorr (Federated Feature Decorrelation Regularization for Mitigating Dimensional Collapse)

Algorithms AIMech:FedDecorrFeatureDecorrelationForDimensionalCollapseMitigationInFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedDecorr, Federated Feature Decorrelation Regularization, Dimensional Collapse Mitigation via Feature Correlation Frobenius Regularization
A heterogeneous federated representation learning algorithm (Shi et al. 2023, Sections 3.3-4, Theorem 1, Proposition 1, Equations 6-9, Algorithm 1) that counteracts heterogeneity-induced dimensional collapse of feature representations by adding a local feature-decorrelation regularizer L_FedDecorr(w, X) := (1 / d^2) ||K||_F^2 to the client classification loss min_w ell(w, X, y) + beta * L_FedDecorr(w, X) (Equations 8-9), where K = (1 / N) sum_{i=1}^N z_hat_i z_hat_i^T in R^{d x d} is the empirical sample correlation matrix of z-score standardized d-dimensional penultimate features z_hat_i = (z_i - z_bar) / sqrt(Var(z)), exploiting the algebraic identity sum_{i=1}^d (lambda_i - (1/d) sum_{j=1}^d lambda_j)^2 = ||K||_F^2 - d (Proposition 1, Equation 7) to minimize singular-value variance in O(N d^2) time without computing singular value decompositions.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from self-supervised cross-view invariance and redundancy-reduction losses such as Barlow Twins (AIMech:BarlowTwinsSelfSupervisedLearningViaRedundancyReduction, Zbontar et al. 2021) and VICReg (AIMech:VicRegVarianceInvarianceCovarianceRegularization, Bardes et al. 2022), which operate on paired augmented views in self-supervised pretraining rather than mitigating non-IID client label-skew dimensional collapse (Theorem 1) in supervised federated learning; and distinct from parameter-space proximal or control-variate federated optimizers such as FedProx and SCAFFOLD.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r459-correlation-frobenius-singular-value-variance-identity-mechanism
Proves that under cross-entropy gradient flow, skewed local class frequencies mu_c reduce the rank of the driving matrix G(t), causing local and aggregated representations to collapse onto a low-dimensional subspace (Theorem 1, Equations 3-5), and mitigates this collapse by penalizing (1 / d^2) ||K||_F^2 on z-score normalized features z_hat_i, which equals (1 / d^2)(sum_{i=1}^d (lambda_i - 1)^2 + d) and therefore uniformizes the singular values lambda_1, ..., lambda_d of K without requiring an explicit SVD (Proposition 1, Equations 7-9).
  • supports Source: Towards Understanding and Mitigating Dimensional Collapse in Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.3 Theorem 1, Section 4 Proposition 1 (Equations 6-9), and Appendices A-B (Equations 32-51), pp. 4-6, 13-18
Scope #r459-plug-and-play-local-feature-regularizer-scope
Acts as a plug-and-play client-side representation regularizer compatible with standard federated optimization backbones (including FedAvg, FedProx, MOON, and FedRS) with zero communication overhead and negligible O(N d^2) mini-batch computation.
  • supports Source: Towards Understanding and Mitigating Dimensional Collapse in Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 Algorithm 1 & Section 5.1-5.2 Experimental Results (Tables 1-3), pp. 6-8
Limitation #r459-minibatch-size-sensitivity-for-correlation-estimation-limitation
Relies on mini-batch sample mean and variance normalization to estimate the d x d correlation matrix K, so very small local mini-batch sizes N produce noisy correlation estimates and degraded regularization effectiveness.
  • supports Source: Towards Understanding and Mitigating Dimensional Collapse in Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 Equations 7-8 & Section 5.3 Ablation Study on Mini-Batch Size (Figure 5b), pp. 6, 9
Application #r459-heterogeneous-federated-vision-classification-application
Applied to heterogeneous federated visual classification on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, Tiny-ImageNet, and PACS across Dirichlet label skew and multi-domain shift.
  • supports Source: Towards Understanding and Mitigating Dimensional Collapse in Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 5 Experiments (Tables 1-3, Figures 3-5) & Appendix E, pp. 7-9, 20-22

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Sensitive to very small local mini-batch sizes N because z-score standardization and correlation matrix estimation K require sufficient batch statistics.
  • Addresses penultimate feature-space dimensional collapse but does not directly debias the final linear classifier layer under missing classes.
