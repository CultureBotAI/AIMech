Volume Sampling for Matrix Approximation and Projective Clustering

Mathematical Methods AIMech:VolumeSamplingForMatrixApproximationAndProjectiveClustering DRAFT
Aliases: Deshpande-Rademacher-Vempala-Wang Simplex Volume Sampling, k-Simplex Volume-Squared Row Subset Selection, Adaptive Volume Sampling for Low-Rank Matrix Approximation
Volume Sampling for Matrix Approximation and Projective Clustering is a joint geometric row-subset sampling principle and adaptive low-rank approximation framework introduced by Deshpande, Rademacher, Vempala, and Wang (2006) that selects a subset S subseteq {1, ..., m} of k rows of a matrix A in R^{m x n} with probability p_S = Delta_S^2 / sum_{|T|=k} Delta_T^2 = det(A_S A_S^T) / sum_{|T|=k} det(A_T A_T^T) proportional to the squared k-dimensional simplex volume Delta_S = (1 / k!) sqrt(det(A_S A_S^T)) spanned by {a_i : i in S} and the origin. By combining the base-times-height simplex identity (k+1)^2 Delta_{S cup {j}}^2 = d(a_j, H_S)^2 Delta_S^2 (Lemma 3.1) with the Cauchy-Binet elementary symmetric polynomial identity sum_{|S|=k} (k!)^2 Delta_S^2 = e_k(sigma_1^2, ..., sigma_n^2) (Lemma 3.3, Equation 3.5), Theorem 1.2 proves the exact expected residual formula E_S[||A - pi_{H_S}(A)||_F^2] = (k + 1) e_{k+1}(sigma_1^2, ..., sigma_n^2) / e_k(sigma_1^2, ..., sigma_n^2) <= (k + 1)||A - A_k||_F^2, establishing the first multiplicative row-subset existence bound (matching the asymptotic lower bound of Proposition 4.1) and, when combined with adaptive residual length-squared sampling (Theorem 1.3 & Theorem 1.4), yielding PTAS (1 + epsilon) algorithms for rank-k matrix approximation and projective clustering.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Relative-Error CUR Matrix Decomposition via Subspace Leverage Sampling (`AIMech:RelativeErrorCURMatrixDecompositionViaSubspaceLeverageSampling`), which samples O(k log k / epsilon^2) columns/rows i.i.d. using precomputed SVD leverage scores rather than joint k-subset volume sampling of exactly k rows with multiplicative factor (k + 1), and from k-Determinantal Point Processes for Fixed-Size Subset Selection (`AIMech:KDeterminantalPointProcessesForFixedSizeSubsetSelection`), which addresses exact two-phase sampling and conditional L-ensemble inference in machine learning rather than the Cauchy-Binet ratio bound E[||A - pi_{H_S}(A)||_F^2] <= (k+1)||A - A_k||_F^2, Proposition 4.1 simplex lower bound, and projective clustering PTAS of Deshpande et al. (2006).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-01
For any matrix A in R^{m x n} with singular values sigma_1 >= ... >= sigma_n, Lemma 3.1 ((k+1)^2 Delta_{S cup {j}}^2 = d(a_j, H_S)^2 Delta_S^2) and Lemma 3.3 (sum_{|S|=k} (k!)^2 Delta_S^2 = e_k(sigma_1^2, ..., sigma_n^2), Equation 3.5) prove that sampling a k-row subset S with probability p_S = Delta_S^2 / sum_{|T|=k} Delta_T^2 achieves the exact expected squared Frobenius projection residual E_S[||A - pi_{H_S}(A)||_F^2] = (k + 1) e_{k+1}(sigma_1^2, ..., sigma_n^2) / e_k(sigma_1^2, ..., sigma_n^2) <= (k + 1)||A - A_k||_F^2 (Theorem 1.2, Equations 3.6-3.8).

  • supports Source: Matrix Approximation and Projective Clustering via Volume Sampling (arXiv abstract) · Locator: Abstract: volume sampling yields (k+1) multiplicative factor and PTAS for rank-k approximation and projective clustering
Scope #claim-02
Iterating k rounds of residual length-squared row sampling approximates k-simplex volume sampling up to a factor of k! to achieve E[||A - pi_{H_S}(A)||_F^2] <= (k + 1)! ||A - A_k||_F^2 in k passes (Theorem 1.3), and following volume sampling with O(k / epsilon) adaptive residual sampling rounds yields a subset of O(k^2 / epsilon + k log k) rows whose span contains a rank-k matrix A'_k with ||A - A'_k||_F^2 <= (1 + epsilon)||A - A_k||_F^2 (Theorem 1.4).
  • supports Source: Matrix Approximation and Projective Clustering via Volume Sampling · Locator: Section 1.2, Theorems 1.3 and 1.4, p. 228; Section 5, Algorithms and Proofs of Theorems 1.3-1.4, pp. 234-239
  • supports Source: Matrix Approximation and Projective Clustering via Volume Sampling (Crossref metadata) · Locator: Theory of Computing 2(12):225-247 bibliographic record
Limitation #claim-03
Proposition 4.1 proves that the multiplicative factor (k + 1) in Theorem 1.2 is asymptotically tight for ANY k-row subset selection method: for a (k+1) x (k+1) matrix whose rows form a regular k-simplex in R^k lifted by epsilon in the (k+1)-st coordinate, every k-row subset S incurs projection error ||A - pi_{H_S}(A)||_F^2 -> (k + 1)||A - A_k||_F^2 as epsilon -> 0, so selecting O(k / epsilon) > k rows is information-theoretically necessary to achieve (1 + epsilon) relative error.
  • supports Source: Matrix Approximation and Projective Clustering via Volume Sampling · Locator: Section 4, Proposition 4.1 and Proof, pp. 233-234

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Any subset of exactly k rows suffers an unavoidable worst-case multiplicative factor of (k + 1) relative to ||A - A_k||_F^2 (Proposition 4.1), and adaptive residual volume sampling alone incurs a (k + 1)! factor before O(k / epsilon) extra rows are sampled.
