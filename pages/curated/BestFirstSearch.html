Best-first search

Algorithms AIMech:BestFirstSearch DRAFT
A search strategy that repeatedly expands the open node with the best value under a specified evaluation rule, adding eligible successors to the open frontier.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record describes the canonical global minimum-f form, with lower values defined as better. A-star, heuristic-only greedy best-first search and weighted A-star choose different evaluation rules. Broader literature also calls restricted-candidate-set policies best-first; their extra selection rules are not part of this core procedure. BFS is not an alias here because it identifies breadth-first search in AIMech. Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #search-procedure
Maintain an open frontier of generated nodes eligible for expansion. Select a node with minimum evaluation value, test the selected node for a goal, and otherwise expand it and update the frontier under the chosen duplicate policy. The evaluation rule and duplicate handling determine the specific search algorithm.
  • supports Source: Linear-Space Best-First Search: Summary of Results · Locator: Introduction, printed p. 533 / PDF p. 1: open/closed lists, minimum-f expansion and special cases
Scope #family-boundary
Global frontier selection differs from choosing only a current node’s neighbor or discarding nodes to enforce a beam width. Some named best-first variants additionally restrict selection to a privileged subset of the frontier.
  • supports Source: A Comparison of Greedy Search Algorithms · Locator: Introduction and Best-First Search, PDF pp. 1–2

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • A minimum evaluation value alone does not guarantee minimum solution cost; the meaning and assumptions of the evaluation rule matter.
  • Maintaining the full frontier and explored-state information can require substantial memory. Recursive or bounded-memory variants change what is retained.
  • Tie-breaking, duplicate updates and termination conditions are additional algorithm choices; the generic strategy does not settle them or guarantee completion on an infinite space.
