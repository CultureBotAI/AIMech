Kernel SHAP

Algorithms AIMech:KernelSHAP DRAFT
A model-agnostic Shapley-attribution estimator using weighted linear regression on feature-presence subsets.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Kernel SHAP is a specific estimator within the SHAP framework. Its Shapley kernel and constraints distinguish it from an arbitrary LIME regression surrogate.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #explanation-method
Fit an additive explanation model to subset-evaluation outputs with the Shapley weighting kernel and constraints fixing the empty-subset baseline and full-input prediction; sampled subsets give an approximate estimate.
  • supports Source: A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions · Locator: Section 4.1, Kernel SHAP; Theorem 2 and endpoint constraints, PDF/printed pp. 5–6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Results depend on how missing features are evaluated and on sampling; exact Shapley recovery in the full weighted problem is not a guarantee for every finite sample or background choice.
  • Independence approximations do not in general reproduce a dependence-aware conditional expectation.
