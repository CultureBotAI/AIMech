Model-X anytime-valid conditional independence testing via e-processes

Mathematical Methods AIMech:ModelXAnytimeValidConditionalIndependenceTesting DRAFT
Aliases: Model-X Conditional Independence E-Statistics, Sequential E-CRT (e-Conditional Randomization Test), Monte Carlo Plus-One Model-X E-Process, Conditional GRO E-Variable for Model-X Testing
A sequential conditional independence testing framework (Grünwald, Henzi, & Lardy 2024, Journal of the American Statistical Association 119(546):1554-1565, arXiv:2209.12637; Shaer, Maman, & Romano 2023, NeurIPS 36, arXiv:2210.00354) for testing H_0: X _||_ Y | Z under the Model-X assumption (where the conditional law Q_{X|Z} is known or estimated while the outcome conditional law P_{Y|X,Z} is completely unspecified) that constructs conditional e-variables E(X_n; Y_n, Z_n) = f(X_n, Y_n, Z_n) / int f(x, Y_n, Z_n) dQ_{X|Z=Z_n}(x) (or exact finite-sample Monte Carlo plus-one approximations E^{(M)}(X_n; Y_n, Z_n) = f(X_n, Y_n, Z_n) / ((1/(M+1))(f(X_n, Y_n, Z_n) + sum_{i=1}^M f(tilde{X}_n^{(i)}, Y_n, Z_n))) using M >= 1 conditional randomization draws tilde{X}_n^{(i)} ~ Q_{X|Z=Z_n}) whose product M_n = prod_{i=1}^n E_i forms a non-negative test martingale under H_0 regardless of the serial dependence of (Y_n, Z_n), achieves Growth-Rate Optimality (GRO) equal to the conditional mutual information I(X; Y | Z) via a conditional Kullback-Leibler divergence regret decomposition, and admits nonasymptotic Total Variation type-I error inflation bounds under Model-X misspecification.

Mechanism #conditional-e-variable-monte-carlo-plus-one-exchangeability-and-test-martingale
For any non-negative measurable test function f(x, y, z) >= 0 with 0 < int f(x, y, z) dQ_{X|Z=z}(x) < infty, the normalized ratio E(X; Y, Z) = f(X, Y, Z) / int f(x, Y, Z) dQ_{X|Z=z}(x) satisfies E_P[E(X; Y, Z) | Y, Z] = 1 for every joint distribution P in H_0: X _||_ Y | Z with conditional law Q_{X|Z} (Proposition 1, Equation 2); when the integral denominator is intractable, drawing M >= 1 conditional randomization copies tilde{X}^{(1)}, ..., tilde{X}^{(M)} ~_{i.i.d.} Q_{X|Z} conditionally independent of (X, Y) given Z and including the observed X in the denominator yields the Monte Carlo plus-one e-variable E^{(M)}(X; Y, Z) = f(X, Y, Z) / ((1/(M+1))(f(X, Y, Z) + sum_{i=1}^M f(tilde{X}^{(i)}, Y, Z))), which satisfies E_P[E^{(M)}(X; Y, Z) | Y, Z] = 1 exactly by conditional exchangeability of (X, tilde{X}^{(1)}, ..., tilde{X}^{(M)}) given (Y, Z) while the naive Monte Carlo estimator without the observed X in the denominator violates E[E] <= 1 (Proposition 2, Equation 3); sequentially multiplying E_n with predictable functions f_n measurable wrt sigma(X^{(n-1)}, Y^{(n)}, Z^{(n)}) forms a non-negative test martingale M^{(n)} = prod_{i=1}^n E_i with E_P[M^{(n)}] = 1 even under arbitrary temporal dependence of (Y_n, Z_n), provided X_n _||_ (X^{(n-1)}, Y^{(n)}, Z^{(n-1)}) | Z_n (Proposition 3, Equation 4).

Mechanism #gro-conditional-mutual-information-and-conditional-kl-regret-identity
Under a point alternative P_1 = P_{Z,Y} x P_{X|Y,Z} dominated by H_0 with positive densities p(x | y, z) and q(x | z), the Growth-Rate Optimal (GRO) conditional e-variable maximizing E_{P_1}[log E(X; Y, Z) | Y, Z] is uniquely E^{GRO}(X; Y, Z) = p(X | Y, Z) / q(X | Z), whose expected log-growth rate under P_1 equals the conditional mutual information I(X; Y | Z) = E_{P_1}[D_KL(P_{X|Y,Z} || Q_{X|Z})] >= 0 (Section 3.1, Theorem 4, Equations 5–6); furthermore, for any non-negative test function f(x, y, z) defining the tilted conditional density r_f(x | y, z) = f(x, y, z) q(x | z) / int f(x', y, z) q(x' | z) dx', the conditional expected log-growth satisfies the exact identity E_{X~P_{X|Y=y,Z=z}}[log E_f(X; y, z)] = D_KL(P_{X|Y=y,Z=z} || Q_{X|Z=z}) - D_KL(P_{X|Y=y,Z=z} || R_{f,X|Y=y,Z=z}) (Section 3.1, Proposition 4, Equation 7), and when P_{Y|X,Z} belongs to a natural exponential family with density exp(theta^T T(X, Z) Y - A(theta | X, Z)), the plug-in test function f_hat(x, y, z) = p_{hat{theta}}(y | x, z) matches the oracle conditional density ratio up to estimation error in hat{theta} because baseline factors depending only on (y, z) cancel identically between numerator and denominator (Section 3.2, Equations 8–9).

Mechanism #total-variation-type-i-error-bound-under-model-x-misspecification
When the true covariate law Q^*_{X^{(n)}|Z^{(n)}} differs from the assumed or estimated law hat{Q}_{X^{(n)}|Z^{(n)}} used to normalize the e-variables hat{E}_i, if hat{E}_i is uniformly bounded by B > 0 then E_{Q^*}[hat{E}_i | Y_i, Z_i] <= 1 + B * d_TV(Q^*_{X_i|Z_i}, hat{Q}_{X_i|Z_i}) (Proposition 5); crucially, for the stopped product process hat{M}_tau = prod_{i=1}^tau hat{E}_i at any stopping time tau <= n and level alpha in (0, 1), coupling Q^* and hat{Q} prior to Ville's maximal inequality proves that the anytime type-I error under the true null distribution satisfies P_{Q^*}(exists k <= n : hat{M}_k >= 1/alpha | Y^{(n)}, Z^{(n)}) <= alpha + d_TV(Q^*_{X^{(n)}|Z^{(n)}}, hat{Q}_{X^{(n)}|Z^{(n)}}) <= alpha + sqrt((1/2) sum_{i=1}^n D_KL(Q^*_{X_i|Z_i} || hat{Q}_{X_i|Z_i})) without requiring any boundedness assumption on hat{E}_i (Section 4, Theorem 6).

Mechanism #truncation-protection-against-zero-lock-in-and-sequential-e-crt-betting
Because a single outlier step with f_i(X_i, Y_i, Z_i) approx 0 can drive an untruncated product martingale close to zero and permanently stall evidence accumulation during burn-in, mixing or lower-truncating the raw conditional e-variable via E_{i,eps} = (1 - eps) E_i + eps or f_{i,eps}(x, y, z) = max(f_i(x, y, z), eps * int f_i(x', y, z) dQ_{X|Z=z}(x')) with eps in (0, 1) (e.g., eps = 0.05) preserves exact conditional expectation E_{H_0}[E_{i,eps} | Y_i, Z_i] = 1 while lower-bounding every single-step factor by eps (Section 3.2, Section 5, Equation 10); similarly, the sequential Model-X e-CRT (Shaer, Maman, & Romano 2023, NeurIPS 36, arXiv:2210.00354) constructs sequential betting e-variables E_t = 1 + lambda_t (s_t(X_t, Y_t, Z_t) - E_{Q_{X|Z}}[s_t(tilde{X}_t, Y_t, Z_t) | Y_t, Z_t]) with predictable bounded betting fractions lambda_t and arbitrary online black-box conformity scores s_t.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Valid Type-I error control relies on the Model-X assumption that the conditional distribution Q_{X|Z} is known or estimated with vanishing cumulative Total Variation / Kullback-Leibler error over the horizon n; when hat{Q}_{X_i|Z_i} has a persistent non-vanishing estimation bias d_TV(Q^*_{X_i|Z_i}, hat{Q}_{X_i|Z_i}) >= delta > 0 at every step, the product TV distance d_TV(Q^*_{X^{(n)}|Z^{(n)}}, hat{Q}_{X^{(n)}|Z^{(n)}}) approaches 1 as n -> infty, rendering the Theorem 6 robustness bound vacuous over long horizons (Section 4, p. 1559).
  • When the conditional normalization integral int f(x, Y_n, Z_n) dQ_{X|Z=Z_n}(x) is approximated by the Monte Carlo plus-one estimator E^{(M)} (Proposition 2), each step is deterministically upper-bounded by M + 1, so achieving near-optimal GRO logarithmic growth under strong alternatives requires drawing M >> 1 conditional samples per time step (Section 2.1, p. 1556).
  • Sequential plug-in estimation of the outcome model p_{hat{theta}_{n-1}}(y | x, z) from past samples requires O(n) cumulative refits (or online updates) and suffers low growth during initial burn-in when sample sizes are small relative to covariate dimension, requiring explicit truncation eps in (0, 1) to prevent early mispredictions from driving the product martingale near zero (Section 3.2, Section 5).
  • All 636 lines of Grünwald, Henzi, & Lardy (2024, JASA 119(546):1554-1565, arXiv:2209.12637v2) were read.
