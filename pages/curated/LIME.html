Local interpretable model-agnostic explanations

Algorithms AIMech:LIME DRAFT
Aliases: LIME
A prediction-explanation method fitting an interpretable surrogate to locally weighted samples of a model's behavior.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope LIME is a local surrogate explanation procedure, not an inherently explainable model, a guarantee of global fidelity, or the separate SP-LIME representative-selection method.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
Perturbed interpretable inputs are mapped back to model inputs and labeled by model outputs; fitting balances a locality-weighted fidelity loss against surrogate complexity.
  • supports Source: Why Should I Trust You?: Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier · Locator: Sections 3.1–3.3 and equation 1, PDF/printed p. 3

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The source explicitly distinguishes local fidelity from global fidelity.
  • Choice of interpretable representation, neighborhood and surrogate family is part of the method's scope; explanations are not a general certification of trustworthiness.
