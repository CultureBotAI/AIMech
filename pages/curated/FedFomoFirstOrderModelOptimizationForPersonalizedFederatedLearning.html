FedFomo (Personalized Federated Learning with First-Order Model Optimization)

Algorithms AIMech:FedFomoFirstOrderModelOptimizationForPersonalizedFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedFomo, First-Order Model Optimization for Personalized Federated Learning, FOMO Personalized Federated Model Aggregation
A personalized federated learning algorithm (Zhang et al. 2021, Section 3.1 & Appendix A.1, Equations 1-13, Algorithm 1) in which each client c_i maintains a personalized model theta_i^{l(t)} and computes client-specific model aggregation weights w = <w_1, ..., w_N> over M downloaded peer models {theta_n^{l(t)}}_{n=1}^N by taking a first-order Taylor expansion along the convex interpolation path phi_n(w_n) = w_n theta_n^{l(t)} + (1 - w_n) theta_i^{l(t-1)} on a local validation split D_i^{val} to obtain closed-form directional improvement scores w_n = (L_i(theta_i^{l(t-1)}) - L_i(theta_n^{l(t)})) / ||theta_n^{l(t)} - theta_i^{l(t-1)}|| (Equations 3 & 12), ReLU-clipping and normalizing w_n^* = max(w_n, 0) / sum_{m=1}^N max(w_m, 0) (Equation 13) to update theta_i^{l(t)} <- theta_i^{l(t-1)} + sum_{n in [N]} w_n^* (theta_n^{l(t)} - theta_i^{l(t-1)}), and updating a server-side K x K client-to-client affinity matrix P via p_{i,n} <- p_{i,n} + w_n for epsilon-greedy peer model selection.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from pFedMe (AIMech:PFedMePersonalizedFederatedLearningWithMoreauEnvelopes, T. Dinh et al. 2020) and Ditto (AIMech:DittoFairAndRobustFederatedLearningThroughPersonalization, Li et al. 2021), which regularize personalized models toward a single shared global model via proximal Moreau envelopes; and distinct from FedAMP (AIMech:FedAmpFederatedAttentiveMessagePassingForPersonalizedCrossSiloFederatedLearning, Huang et al. 2021), which computes server-side pairwise attention weights from parameter-space distances A'(||w_i - w_j||^2) without downloading M candidate models to evaluate local validation-loss differences L_i(theta_i) - L_i(theta_n).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r464-first-order-validation-slope-and-relu-simplex-weights-mechanism
Approximates the gradient of client c_i's validation loss L_i with respect to model combination weight w_n along phi_n(w_n) = w_n theta_n^{l(t)} + (1 - w_n) theta_i^{l(t-1)} via the finite-difference secant slope w_n = (L_i(theta_i^{l(t-1)}) - L_i(theta_n^{l(t)})) / ||theta_n^{l(t)} - theta_i^{l(t-1)}|| (Equations 3, 10-12) and normalizes positive weights w_n^* = max(w_n, 0) / sum_{m=1}^N max(w_m, 0) (Equation 13), defaulting to theta_i^{l(t-1)} whenever all candidate models increase validation loss.
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning with First Order Model Optimization · Locator: Section 3.1 Equations 3-4, p. 4, and Appendix A.1 Equations 5-13, pp. 12-13
Scope #r464-affinity-matrix-epsilon-greedy-and-loo-privacy-scope
Maintains a server-side K x K client affinity matrix P updated by signed validation improvements p_{i,n} <- p_{i,n} + w_n to sample M high-affinity peer models per round via epsilon-greedy exploration (Section 3.1, p. 5), and extends to a privacy-preserving leave-one-out average variant w_n propto L_i((1/(N-1)) sum_{j != n} theta_j) - L_i((1/N) sum_j theta_j) so clients only receive aggregated models (Appendix A.4, Equation 14, p. 13).
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning with First Order Model Optimization · Locator: Section 3.1 Sampling Relevant Models, p. 5, and Appendix A.4 Preserving Client Privacy with FOMO (Equation 14), p. 13
Limitation #r464-downlink-bandwidth-and-validation-split-limitation
Requires each active client to download M peer models per communication round (increasing server-to-client downlink bandwidth by a factor of M) and perform M forward passes on a held-out local validation split D_i^{val}, which can be noisy when local datasets are very small.
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning with First Order Model Optimization · Locator: Section 3.1 Local Validation Split & Sampling Relevant Models, pp. 4-5, and Appendix B.1 Effect of Validation Split Size, p. 14
Application #r464-personalized-non-iid-and-out-of-client-distribution-application
Applied to personalized non-IID image classification on MNIST, CIFAR-10, and CIFAR-100 across 15 to 100 clients, as well as out-of-client target validation distributions where a client's target task differs from its local training split.
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning with First Order Model Optimization · Locator: Section 4 Experiments (Tables 1-2, Figures 2-4), pp. 5-8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Increases server-to-client downlink communication by a factor of M models per round and requires M local validation forward passes.
  • Requires holding out a local validation dataset D_i^{val} on each client, which reduces training sample size on data-scarce clients.
