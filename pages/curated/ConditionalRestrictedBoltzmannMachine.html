Conditional restricted Boltzmann machine

Architectures AIMech:ConditionalRestrictedBoltzmannMachine DRAFT
Aliases: CRBM, Conditional RBM
A conditional neural probability model that represents an output vector with an RBM whose biases or interactions are determined by a supplied conditioning vector.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the general input-conditioned RBM family described by Mnih, Larochelle and Hinton, with their affine-bias binary formulation as the explicitly curated mechanism. The conditioning vector need not be temporal history. Autoregressive CRBM fixes it to earlier frames and RNN-RBM derives it from a recurrent state; these are narrower constructions, not aliases for the entire family. HashCRBM and CD-PercLoss are separate training or prediction constructions and do not define every conditional RBM. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Hold conditioning vector u fixed. In the paper’s binary affine-bias specialization, set a(u)=b_v+(W_uv)^T u and c(u)=b_h+(W_uh)^T u, with visible-by-hidden interaction matrix W_vh. Define E(v,h;u)=-v^T W_vh h-a(u)^T v-c(u)^T h. Marginalizing binary h gives F(v,u)=-a(u)^T v-sum_j log(1+exp(c_j(u)+(W_vh^T v)_j)). Normalize exp(-F(v,u)) over output configurations v to obtain p(v|u), with a partition depending on u. Hidden units given v,u and visible units given h,u have factorial logistic conditionals, but outputs after hidden marginalization can be dependent. Conditional likelihood gradients contrast data-posterior and model expectations at the same u. The paper discusses finite CD as an approximation and separate alternatives for restricted output support or prediction-based training. Prediction may seek the joint conditional mode or coordinate-wise marginal modes according to the loss; those are different decisions.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The model specifies p(v|u), not the input marginal p(u). Normalizing outputs for one conditioning vector does not supply a shared partition for every other input or a joint model over arbitrary inputs.
  • Factorial block conditionals do not make the output marginal factorial or its partition, joint mode and coordinate marginals generally tractable. Tiny output spaces or deliberately restricted support are special cases.
  • A small data-started CD update can reflect poor mixing near the observed output rather than accurate predictions from u alone. Finite CD is not generally the exact conditional likelihood gradient or the gradient of a single fixed objective.
  • Persistent samples from one conditioning input cannot simply be treated as equilibrium samples for another. The paper’s discussion of persistent-chain impracticality is scoped to its input-specific distributions and training setting; it is not a proof that every conditional persistent method is impossible.
  • The general family can condition biases or interactions, while the displayed mechanism holds W_vh fixed and changes biases affinely. Gaussian outputs, input-dependent interactions, retrieved-support HashCRBM and perceptron-style training need separate conventions. No historical-priority, universal prediction-quality or author-code claim is asserted.
