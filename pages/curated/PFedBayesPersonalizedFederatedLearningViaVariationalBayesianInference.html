pFedBayes (Personalized Federated Learning via Variational Bayesian Inference)

Algorithms AIMech:PFedBayesPersonalizedFederatedLearningViaVariationalBayesianInference DRAFT
Aliases: pFedBayes, Personalized Federated Variational Bayesian Inference, Bi-Level Variational Bayesian Personalized Federated Learning
A personalized federated Bayesian neural network algorithm (Zhang, Li, Li, Guo, & Shao 2022, Section 3, Equations 8-20, Algorithm 1, Theorems 4.4-4.5) that models both the global parameters w ~ P_theta(w) = N(mu, diag(sigma^2)) and each client i's personalized parameters w_i ~ P_{theta_i}(w_i) = N(mu_i, diag(sigma_i^2)) (with reparameterization sigma = log(1 + exp(rho))) as mean-field Gaussian random variables and solves the bi-level variational Bayesian optimization problem min_{theta} (1/N) sum_{i=1}^N F_i(theta), where F_i(theta) = min_{theta_i} { zeta * KL(P_{theta_i}(w_i) || P_theta(w)) - E_{w_i ~ P_{theta_i}(w_i)} [log P(D_i | w_i)] } (Equations 8-9), by alternating R local reparameterized Monte Carlo gradient updates on personalized posterior parameters theta_i = (mu_i, rho_i) (Equation 15) and K local closed-form KL gradient updates on localized global prior parameters theta_g^i = (mu_g^i, rho_g^i) (Equation 17), followed by server momentum-style aggregation theta^{t+1} = (1 - beta) theta^t + (beta / |S^t|) sum_{i in S^t} theta_g^i (Equation 20).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from pFedMe (AIMech:PFedMePersonalizedFederatedLearningWithMoreauEnvelopes, T. Dinh et al. 2020), which regularizes deterministic point-estimate weights via an L2 Moreau envelope (zeta/2) ||w_i - w||_2^2 without modeling posterior weight uncertainty sigma_i^2 or optimizing an evidence lower bound; and distinct from FedPA (AIMech:FedPaFederatedPosteriorAveragingViaLocalMcmcAndShrinkageCovariance, Al-Shedivat et al. 2021) and FedBE (AIMech:FedBeFederatedBayesianModelEnsembleViaStochasticWeightAverageDistillation, Chen & Chao 2021), which construct a single global posterior rather than bi-level personalized variational Bayesian posteriors P_{theta_i}(w_i) coupled to a learned global variational prior P_theta(w).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r479-bilevel-gaussian-elbo-and-reparameterized-update-mechanism
Each client i minimizes its negative evidence lower bound zeta * KL(N(mu_i, diag(sigma_i^2)) || N(mu_g^i, diag((sigma_g^i)^2))) - (n / b) sum_{j in D_i} E_{epsilon ~ N(0,I)}[log P(D_j | mu_i + sigma_i odot epsilon)] (Equations 12-14) via R inner reparameterized stochastic gradient steps on theta_i = (mu_i, rho_i) (Equation 15) and K outer steps on the localized prior theta_g^i = (mu_g^i, rho_g^i) using the exact diagonal-Gaussian KL gradient (Equation 17), before the server aggregates theta^{t+1} = (1 - beta) theta^t + (beta / |S^t|) sum_{i in S^t} theta_g^i (Zhang et al. 2022, Section 3.3, Algorithm 1).
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning via Variational Bayesian Inference · Locator: Section 3.3 (Equations 10-20, Algorithm 1), pp. 4-5
Scope #r479-minimax-nonparametric-generalization-rate-scope
Under beta-Holder smooth underlying client functions f_i(x) on [0, 1]^d and sparse Bayesian neural networks, Theorems 4.4 and 4.5 prove that the client-averaged squared Hellinger / L2 estimation error of pFedBayes is bounded by O(C_zeta (r_n / n) log^3 L + C_zeta xi_n^2) and achieves the minimax-optimal rate n^{-2 beta / (2 beta + d)} up to polylogarithmic factors while preventing overfitting in small-data regimes (Zhang et al. 2022, Section 4, Lemmas 4.1-4.3 & Theorems 4.4-4.5, pp. 5-7).
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning via Variational Bayesian Inference · Locator: Section 4 (Lemmas 4.1-4.3, Theorems 4.4-4.5, Equations 21-31), pp. 5-7
Application #r479-small-data-non-iid-personalized-classification-application
Applied to non-IID personalized federated classification under small, medium, and large local sample sizes on MNIST, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and Sent140 (Zhang et al. 2022, Section 5, Tables 1-2, Figures 1-3).
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning via Variational Bayesian Inference · Locator: Section 5 (Experimental Results, Tables 1-2, Figures 1-3), pp. 7-9
Limitation #r479-doubled-parameter-communication-and-mc-sampling-limitation
Representing every weight by both a mean mu and variance parameter rho doubles the memory footprint and per-round communication payload relative to deterministic FedAvg/pFedMe and requires M Monte Carlo forward passes per minibatch step (Zhang et al. 2022, Section 3.3, Equations 10-16).
  • supports Source: Personalized Federated Learning via Variational Bayesian Inference · Locator: Section 3.3 (Equations 10-16, Algorithm 1), pp. 4-5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Doubles the number of communicated parameters per round (mean mu_g^i and variance parameter rho_g^i) compared to point-estimate federated learning.
  • Assumes a mean-field (diagonal covariance) Gaussian variational family that ignores posterior correlations across weights.
