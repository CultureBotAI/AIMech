Rejection-driven independence Metropolis sampling

Algorithms AIMech:RejectionDrivenIndependenceMetropolis DRAFT
An independence Metropolis sampler whose proposals are generated by a rejection procedure with a fixed possibly non-dominating envelope, followed by a Metropolis correction for the resulting clipped proposal law.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Tierney’s rejection sampling chain in Section 2.3.4. It specializes independence Metropolis by an inner proposal generator; it is not ordinary rejection sampling unless the envelope dominates everywhere. It is distinct from adaptive rejection Metropolis sampling, because h and c are fixed here. 2 Claims
Mechanism #method
For a proper target pi and a normalized proposal density h covering its support, fix c>0. Repeatedly draw z from h and u uniformly on (0,c h(z)) until u<pi(z). This yields a proposal y with density f proportional to min(pi,c h). At current state x apply independence Metropolis acceptance min(1,pi(y)f(x)/(pi(x)f(y))); retain x if rejected. Let C={x:pi(x)≤c h(x)}. The published acceptance cases are 1 when x is in C; c h(x)/pi(x) when x is outside C and y is in C; and min(1,pi(y)h(x)/(pi(x)h(y))) when both are outside C. Normalizing constants of f cancel. When c h dominates pi everywhere, this reduces to independent rejection sampling.

Scope #earlier-report-discrepancy
The inspected 1991 technical report prints pi(x)/(c h(x)) in the mixed acceptance case and an uncapped ratio in the both-outside case. The 1994 published Section 2.3.4 instead gives c h(x)/pi(x) and a minimum with one, matching the independence Metropolis ratio used in this record.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The inner proposal must have positive acceptance mass and cover the target support required for irreducibility. A poor choice of h or c can make either stage inefficient.
  • The inner rejected trials are discarded, while rejection by the outer Metropolis test repeats the chain state; these two rejection events have different roles.
  • A non-dominating envelope without the outer correction generally gives the clipped density rather than pi. Correcting it does not guarantee fast mixing.
  • The inspected 1991 technical report prints the reciprocal of the mixed-case factor and omits the minimum with one in the final case. This record uses the visually verified 1994 published expressions, consistent with the general independence ratio; no separate erratum is asserted.
  • Adapting h or c from chain history is outside the fixed-proposal definition and needs its own validity argument.
