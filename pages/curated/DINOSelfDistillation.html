DINO self-distillation

Aliases: Self-distillation with no labels
A self-supervised representation-learning procedure that matches cross-view student probabilities to centered and sharpened probabilities from a momentum teacher, without a separate prediction head or explicit negative-pair objective.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Caron et al.’s 2021 DINO self-distillation method, supporting both vision-transformer and convolutional backbones. It is not the later DINO object detector, DINOv2/v3, or a particular checkpoint. The bare acronym is not asserted as an unqualified alias. Its same-architecture student/teacher branches differ from BYOL’s online-only predictor and normalized vector regression. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Initialize teacher parameters from the student and a center c=0. Generate two global crops and optional local crops from each image; the teacher sees the global crops and the student sees all crops. For raw K-dimensional outputs g, use P_s(v)=softmax(g_s(v)/tau_s) and P_t(v)=softmax((g_t(v)−c)/tau_t), with positive temperatures and a sharpened teacher distribution. Average cross-entropy −sum_i P_t(v)_i log P_s(v′)_i over teacher global crops and student crops of the same image, excluding the identical view, and average over images in the minibatch. Stop gradients through P_t, update student parameters by the loss, and update teacher parameters by theta_t←lambda theta_t+(1−lambda)theta_s, with lambda in [0,1]. Update c by an exponential moving average with center momentum in [0,1) of the raw teacher outputs over all global views in the distributed batch for use on subsequent batches. Both branches share backbone and projection-head architecture; downstream features are taken from the backbone before the head.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The centering prose on printed p. 9653 says to add c, whereas Algorithm 1 subtracts the running center. The pinned author implementation also subtracts it; this record follows the algorithm and implementation and preserves the discrepancy.
  • The teacher is updated from the student, not supplied as an independently trained larger model. Its output coordinates are learned representation probabilities, not verified semantic class labels.
  • Centering and sharpening are empirical collapse-control choices, not a proof that all initializations, temperatures, momentum schedules or datasets avoid collapsed representations.
  • A larger number of local crops changes the cross-view objective. The two-view pseudocode is a specialization; losses exclude the teacher/student pair from the identical crop.
  • The pinned implementation uses zero center initialization, distributed raw-output averaging and matched initial student/teacher weights. Its specific default temperature schedule differs from the paper’s reported experimental schedule; this record does not equate all defaults.
  • No DINO detector mechanism, later DINO variant, segmentation accuracy claim, trained checkpoint or executed benchmark is asserted.
