Aliases: ACS
An ant-colony optimization algorithm that combines a greedy-or-proportional construction rule, local pheromone updates during construction, and global pheromone updates restricted to a selected best tour.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For a traveling-salesman tour, at each construction decision draw q uniformly in [0,1]. If q<=q0, choose an unvisited city maximizing tau_ij*eta_ij^beta; otherwise sample proportionally to those same weights over the unvisited set. Apply the selected local rule tau_ij_new=(1−rho)*tau_ij+rho*tau0 to traversed edges during construction. After all tours finish, update edges belonging to the shortest tour found so far with tau_ij_new=(1−alpha)*tau_ij+alpha/L_best. The scope of this global step follows Section III’s explicit best-tour-edge restriction; edges outside that tour do not undergo this global update. Figure 3 organizes construction into steps. Appendix A has all ants choose a step before its local-update loop, so sequential whole-tour construction is not assumed. Retain the best-so-far tour and reset per-tour feasibility state before the next iteration. Parameter settings, tie handling and repeated updates to an edge must be declared for an executable implementation.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The local update moves a trail toward tau0: it decreases a trail only when that trail exceeds tau0, increases it below tau0, and leaves equality unchanged. It is not unconditional evaporation. At q0=1 the displayed decision uses only maximization, apart from tie handling, so positive trails alone do not supply probabilistic exploration.
  • There is a source presentation ambiguity: Equation 4 defines zero deposit off the best tour, while Section III explicitly restricts the global update to best-tour edges. Appendix A’s global loop uses stale ant-indexed endpoints inside a generic edge loop, and its repeat jump does not explicitly reset all tour state. The record follows the main-text scope and supplies the necessary state-reset qualification; it does not claim the appendix is directly executable or that an official erratum was checked.
  • Random initial cities, heuristic powers, q0, alpha, rho and tau0 are configuration choices. Repeated local updates and their ordering affect trails; independent construction of entire tours is not an equivalent schedule. Zero distances, zero denominators, constraints and numerical underflow need handling.
  • The paper’s task-specific comparisons, optional local search and qualitative improvement claims do not establish universal superiority or finite-run global optimality. Ant-Q’s reinforcement-learning connection does not make the selected tau0 rule a Bellman update.
