Parametric UMAP

Algorithms AIMech:ParametricUMAP DRAFT
Aliases: Parametric Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection
A parametric dimensionality-reduction algorithm that trains a neural mapping from input points to embedding coordinates using sampled attractive and repulsive losses derived from a UMAP neighborhood graph.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The core distinction from AIMech:UMAP is optimization of a shared neural input-to-coordinate mapping, rather than one free coordinate vector per training point. A parametric t-SNE objective and a generic autoencoder are different constructions; the UMAP/autoencoder and supervised hybrids are optional variants. Claims & Grounded Evidence

2 Claims
Mechanism #method-scope
Construct locally scaled UMAP graph affinities, then optimize neural-network weights so the network outputs serve as embedding coordinates. Positive graph edges and sampled negative pairs supply a minibatch binary cross-entropy-style training loss; the learned mapping can embed new inputs by a forward pass. Reconstruction, classification or global-distance regularizers are optional extensions rather than necessary parts of the base algorithm.
  • supports Source: Parametric UMAP embeddings for representation and semi-supervised learning · Locator: Sections 2.1–2.4, PDF pp. 4–10; Section 4 and Figure 2, pp. 11–14; discussion, pp. 36–37
Limitation #parametric-sampling-scope
The sampling analysis distinguishes Parametric UMAP’s within-batch negative samples and gives a batch-size-dependent effective objective with degree-product repulsive weights. This differs from its nonparametric symmetrized objective and from nominal all-pairs cross-entropy; the result concerns the analyzed procedure, not every implementation.
  • supports Source: On UMAP’s True Loss Function · Locator: Section 5, footnote 3 and Theorem 5.1, equation (13), PDF pp. 6–7

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The graph metric, neighborhood structure, network capacity, optimization and sampling affect the learned representation; a reusable mapping does not ensure reliable extrapolation to new distributions.
  • Minibatch negative sampling changes the effective loss relative to the nominal all-pairs fuzzy cross-entropy. The analyzed parametric loss has batch-size and degree-product factors, distinct from the nonparametric symmetrized loss.
  • A classifier regularized by graph structure requires a graph relevant to its labels; the paper does not establish universal semi-supervised improvement.
  • The specified paper and sampling analysis were inspected; current software, all supplementary results and universal topology preservation were not certified.
