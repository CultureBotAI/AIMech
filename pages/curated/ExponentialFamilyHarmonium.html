Exponential-family harmonium

Model Families AIMech:ExponentialFamilyHarmonium DRAFT
Aliases: EFH
A bipartite undirected model family coupling visible and hidden exponential-family sufficient statistics through bilinear interactions, with variables conditionally independent within either layer given the other and a finite global joint normalizer.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Scoped to Welling, Rosen-Zvi and Hinton's exponential-family extension of the harmonium. The family generalizes binary restricted Boltzmann machines to supported exponential-family variables and sufficient statistics. It is broader than a particular categorical/Gaussian document model, the native GCA heterogeneous construction or binary-hidden Replicated Softmax, and is distinct from the contrastive-divergence fitting algorithm. EFH is the source acronym; proper joint parameter domains are part of the model definition. Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #sufficient-statistic-coupling
Choose visible and hidden exponential-family base measures and unary natural parameters, then add bilinear interactions between sufficient statistics from opposite layers. Retain base measures, whether written explicitly or absorbed as fixed-coefficient features, and normalize the complete joint. Given the opposite layer, each variable keeps its exponential-family form with shifted natural parameters. Integrating hidden variables contributes their log-partition functions to the visible log mass. Conditional factorization does not imply marginal independence or make the global partition function easy to compute.
  • supports Source: Exponential Family Harmoniums with an Application to Information Retrieval · Locator: Section 2, PDF p.3, Equations 1-7 and normalization discussion
Mechanism #normalization-and-learning
Admissible interaction parameters must produce a finite joint normalizer. Proper-looking local conditionals are insufficient: a scalar Gaussian/Gaussian example with unit conditional variances and coupling 1.2 has an indefinite joint precision, while a positive Poisson/Poisson interaction can make diagonal joint terms diverge. When the model is proper, exact likelihood gradients contrast data and model expected sufficient statistics; derivatives of hidden log-partitions provide conditional hidden expectations. Short-chain contrastive divergence approximates the equilibrium expectation and does not certify exact likelihood optimization.
  • supports Source: Exponential Family Harmoniums with an Application to Information Retrieval · Locator: Section 2, PDF p.3, restrictions on interactions; Section 2.1, pp.3-4, Equations 8-9
Mechanism #categorical-count-bin-specialization
The demonstrated latent-semantic model uses one categorical count-bin variable per vocabulary word and unit-Gaussian hidden variables. If S is the sum of selected category weight columns, h given all visible observations has distribution N(S,I), and visible mass is proportional to exp(alpha dot x+||S||^2/2). This finite-category construction differs from replicated token positions and from binary hidden units. Common orthogonal rotations preserve visible probabilities; category-constant shifts can also be compensated by visible biases. Centering can change cosine retrieval geometry, while a common orthogonal rotation alone preserves cosine similarity. Missing words require inference rather than claiming the all-visible conditional remains exact.
  • supports Source: Exponential Family Harmoniums with an Application to Information Retrieval · Locator: Section 3, PDF pp.4-5, Equations 10-14; Section 4, pp.6-7, missing-word mean-field inference

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Normalizability and shifted natural-parameter domains depend on the chosen exponential families and interactions. Finite categorical/Gaussian examples do not validate arbitrary unbounded or mixed-family constructions. The source's broad causal or marginal-independence rhetoric about directed models is not adopted as a universal DAG property.
  • Conditional hidden inference is simple when all modeled visible variables are observed. Missing modalities can induce mixtures or require approximate inference. Decorrelation is not full independence, and empirical covariance of posterior means is distinct from marginal hidden covariance including conditional noise.
  • Selected reduced-newsgroup experiments use cosine retrieval and approximate mean-field completion. The NIPS experiment bins counts into twelve categories and compares overlap with TF-IDF retrieval because ground-truth relevance labels are absent; that overlap is not a relevance-accuracy guarantee.
  • Finite checks verify visible-law rotation and compensated-shift invariance and illustrate joint-normalization failures. No author code, complete theorem for every exponential family, training convergence or empirical benchmark is certified.
