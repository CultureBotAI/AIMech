Variational message passing

Algorithms AIMech:VariationalMessagePassing DRAFT
Aliases: VMP
A variational inference algorithm that organizes conjugate-exponential coordinate updates as local messages carrying expected sufficient statistics and natural-parameter contributions through a Bayesian network.

VMP supplies graph-local sufficient-statistic message routing and closed conjugate coordinate updates, beyond a mean-field family and objective alone. It differs from BBVI stochastic gradients, NCVMP expected-log-gradient fixed-point proposals, VEM model-parameter alternation and ICM point-state maximization. This record covers the original conjugate-exponential core; auxiliary-bound, sampling and structured extensions need their own assumptions. Absence is bounded to the inspected pin and corpus.

7 Claims
Mechanism #variational-coordinate-objective
For fixed observed variables v and a proper Bayesian-network joint p(h,v)=product_i p(x_i|pa_i) with positive finite evidence p(v), choose normalized variational factors q(h)=product_j q_j(h_j) over disjoint hidden blocks. Maximize L(q)=E_q[log p(h,v)]+H(q), so log p(v)-L(q)=KL(q||p(h|v)) where expectations are finite and supports compatible. Holding all other factors fixed, the normalized update q_j_new(h_j) proportional to exp(E_q_-j[log p(h,v)]) is the exact coordinate optimum when this distribution exists in the permitted family. Vector-valued node blocks can retain within-block dependence.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Sections 2–3, Equations 6 and 18–20, Algorithm 1, PDF pp. 3–10 / printed pp. 663–670
Mechanism #local-markov-blanket
The coordinate log update depends only on the updated node’s own conditional and its children’s conditionals: its parents, children and the children’s other parents supply the required local information. Nonadjacent log terms are constant in the updated block. The graph’s conditional-independence structure is used to compute the variational update; it does not assert that the true posterior factors like q.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Section 2, Equation 7 and Figure 1, PDF pp. 4–5 / printed pp. 664–665
Mechanism #conjugate-message-routing
In the conjugate-exponential core, write the updated node’s conditional log density as phi(pa)^T*u(x)+f(x)-kappa(phi(pa)). Parent-to-child messages provide current expected sufficient statistics. Each child-to-parent message supplies the expected natural-parameter coefficient of u(x) from that child’s log conditional, using the child’s moments and its other parents’ current moments. The node’s new natural parameter is E[phi(pa)] plus the sum of its child contributions; retain the carrier f(x) once and normalize the resulting factor. Recompute its sufficient-statistic moments from the log-partition derivative or equivalent exact formulas. Observed nodes send their actual sufficient statistics. The needed expectations must be closed under the selected conjugate families.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Sections 3.1–3.3, Equations 8–20 and Algorithm 1, PDF pp. 5–10 / printed pp. 665–670
Mechanism #schedule-and-guarantee
Initialize proper factors with consistent moments and update one block at a time using current incoming information, recomputing its outgoing moments before later affected updates. In exact arithmetic, each valid conjugate coordinate optimum cannot decrease the fixed-model variational lower bound. A bound threshold or iteration cap is a stopping rule, not a posterior-accuracy certificate. Bound monotonicity does not imply global optimality, convergence of all parameter iterates or even that every fixed point is a local optimum; synchronous updates using stale interacting neighbors do not inherit the sequential guarantee.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Algorithm 1 and discussion, PDF pp. 10–13 / printed pp. 670–673
Mechanism #gaussian-mean-precision
For observations y_n conditionally N(mu,tau^-1), independent priors mu~N(m0,lambda0^-1) and tau~Gamma(a0,b0) in shape-rate form, and q(mu)q(tau), the conjugate updates are v_mu=(lambda0+N*E[tau])^-1, m_mu=v_mu*(lambda0*m0+E[tau]*sum_n y_n), a_tau=a0+N/2 and b_tau=b0+0.5*sum_n((y_n-E[mu])^2+Var(mu)), with each update using current moments. E[tau]=a_tau/b_tau. The variance term in the precision-rate update is required; replacing E[(y_n-mu)^2] by its squared-mean residual alone changes the method. These are coordinate updates of an approximate product posterior.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Section 3.4, Equations 21–22, Figure 3 and unnumbered message updates, PDF pp. 10–12 / printed pp. 670–672
Mechanism #bound-accounting
The variational bound sums the expected log conditional of every model node, including observed nodes, plus the entropy of each hidden variational factor. Node normalizers and carrier terms must be retained for the bound even when constants cancel from parameter updates. The resulting quantity is a lower bound on model evidence; comparing such bounds does not in general reproduce exact Bayes-factor rankings, because different models can have different approximation gaps.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Section 3.6, Equation 23 and nodewise bound contributions, PDF pp. 13–15 / printed pp. 673–675; Appendix A, PDF pp. 27–32 / printed pp. 687–692
Scope #extension-boundaries
Deterministic transformations require appropriate moment closure and consistent substitution; they are not arbitrary independent variational point masses. Mixture indicators weight natural-parameter contributions by responsibilities rather than replacing the update by an arithmetic mixture of child densities. Multivariate node blocks can retain internal correlation while separate factors remain independent. Nonconjugate auxiliary bounds, point-estimate heuristics and sampling of message expectations are extensions whose objectives, normalization and progress guarantees require separate analysis.
  • supports Source: Variational Message Passing · Locator: Sections 4.2–4.4, PDF pp. 16–20 / printed pp. 676–680; Section 6, PDF pp. 22–26 / printed pp. 682–686

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The conjugate-exponential core requires a normalizable coordinate distribution, computable current sufficient-statistic expectations, fixed model and declared factorization. Sparse graph structure alone does not make all moments cheap. No Fisher-covariance inverse is needed for these analytic conjugate updates, even when a convenient sufficient-statistic representation is redundant.
  • Mean-field restrictions omit dependence between blocks. Initialization, update order and partition can affect the result. A curator example p(x,y) proportional to exp(2*x*y) on {-1,+1}^2 has the uniform-product exact coordinate fixed point but an indefinite ELBO Hessian with eigenvalues 1 and -3; therefore fixed-point convergence is not a universal local-optimality certificate. The source’s stronger local-minimum prose is not adopted.
  • The Gaussian example’s displayed normalizing constant in Equation 22 uses -log(2*pi) rather than -0.5*log(2*pi); the latter is required for absolute evidence although the constant cancels from local parameter updates. The source calls Gamma b a scale in one passage, while its displayed density and updates use a rate. The record follows the density and states the convention.
  • Nonconjugate logistic-bound updates optimize an additional bound, not necessarily the original ELBO. Continuous point-mass approximations have singular entropy issues. The gamma-shape sampling extension’s unconditional transformed log-concavity claim has a counterexample preserved in the source notes; no adaptive-rejection sampler is certified here.
  • VIBES is the paper’s historical implementation context. Its code was not downloaded, inspected or executed. The Gaussian-mixture demonstration and reported bounds do not establish general accuracy, runtime or exact model-selection performance. A positive-parameter Dirichlet mean is not exactly zero merely because a component is described as pruned.
  • The full 34-page primary paper was read, including all figures, Table 1, Algorithm 1, Appendix A and references. The saved rational and analytic examples check message equivalence, uncertainty propagation and stationary-point boundaries; no source experiment, fitted posterior or benchmark was reproduced. The record remains DRAFT; author self-review is not independent approval.
