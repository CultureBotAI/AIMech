Blahut-Arimoto Rate-Distortion Algorithm

Algorithms AIMech:BlahutArimotoRateDistortionAlgorithm DRAFT
Aliases: Blahut Rate-Distortion Algorithm
An alternating channel-and-marginal minimization algorithm for a fixed-source rate-distortion problem at a specified nonnegative distortion multiplier.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record is the rate-distortion branch: the source distribution is fixed and the reproduction channel is optimized. The channel-capacity branch instead fixes the channel and maximizes information over its input law; it is not an alias here. The IM algorithm maximizes a variational information bound, while this procedure minimizes information plus a distortion penalty. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #gibbs-channel-and-marginal
For finite source/reproduction alphabets, fixed source p(x), finite nonnegative distortion rho(x,y), multiplier beta>=0 and positive initial reproduction marginal q(y), set W(y|x)=q(y)exp(-beta*rho(x,y))/sum_z q(z)exp(-beta*rho(x,z)). Then set q_new(y)=sum_x p(x)W(y|x) and repeat. These are exact coordinate minima of F(W,q)=sum_x,y p(x)W(y|x)log(W(y|x)/q(y))+beta*E[rho]. At q=pW, F equals I(X;Y)+beta*E[rho]. Unused zero-probability source rows can be omitted.
  • supports Source: Information Geometry and Alternating Minimization Procedures · Locator: Section5C, pp233-234 / PDF29-30; explicit normalized row minimum of the displayed double-minimum objective
Scope #fixed-multiplier-convergence
For the stated finite-alphabet, finite-distortion and positive-initial-marginal setting, the alternating procedure converges to a channel attaining the fixed-multiplier minimum. This solves one supporting-slope problem; choosing beta to attain a requested distortion or tracing the whole rate-distortion curve is a separate operation. An optimizer need not be unique, and different beta values or initializations can give different optimal channels in degenerate cases.
  • supports Source: Information Geometry and Alternating Minimization Procedures · Locator: Section5C, Eq5.9 and convergence discussion, pp233-234 / PDF29-30
Limitation #support-and-objective-direction
A zero reproduction probability is multiplicatively preserved and can exclude the best channel. In the saved symmetric binary Hamming example at beta=log(3), an interior start reaches distortion1/4 and objective log(3/2), while marginal(1,0) remains fixed with larger objective log(3)/2. The adjacent source section’s positive log(reverse/input) expression belongs to capacity maximization; using it as a minimization objective reverses that problem. The rate-distortion objective above has its own minimizing sign.
  • supports Source: Information Geometry and Alternating Minimization Procedures · Locator: Sections5B-5C, pp231-234 / PDF27-30; curator binary channel and support calculations
Limitation #historical-proof-scope
Csiszar’s1974 correspondence identifies a gap in the original Blahut convergence argument and supplies a finite-reproduction-alphabet convergence result, with weaker and conditional conclusions for countable alphabets. The record does not transfer finite-alphabet convergence unconditionally to arbitrary continuous alphabets or rely on the original proof gap as evidence that the finite algorithm fails.
  • supports Source: On the computation of rate-distortion functions (Corresp.) · Locator: Introduction, p122; SectionIII Theorems1-2 and SectionIV, pp123-124; author-uploaded article text
