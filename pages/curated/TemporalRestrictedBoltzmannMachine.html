Temporal restricted Boltzmann machine

Architectures AIMech:TemporalRestrictedBoltzmannMachine DRAFT
Aliases: TRBM
A neural sequence architecture that multiplies time-indexed conditional RBM distributions whose visible and hidden biases depend on preceding visible and hidden states through shared temporal weights.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record covers the original single-layer temporal RBM construction of Sutskever and Hinton, with its finite lag window and omitted-edge special cases. The full architecture has visible-to-visible, visible-to-hidden and hidden-to-hidden history connections. It is distinct from temporal FRBM’s factored categorical-word construction, RTRBM’s deterministic recurrent state, and the observed-history-only autoregressive CRBM. Multilayer stacking is an extension rather than part of this single-layer identity; a filtering algorithm does not define the latent probabilistic model. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For binary vectors v_t and h_t, set the visible bias a_t=a+sum_k A_k^T v_(t-k) and hidden bias b_t=b+sum_k B_k^T h_(t-k)+sum_k C_k^T v_(t-k). Define p(v_t,h_t|history)=exp(v_t^T C_0 h_t+a_t^T v_t+b_t^T h_t)/Z_t(history), and multiply these normalized factors over time using shared parameters and the source’s learned initial contributions for unavailable earlier states. Conditional on observed current and previous visibles and known previous hidden states, the current hidden posterior factorizes with probabilities sigmoid(C_0^T v_t+b_t). Unknown previous hidden states generally require marginalization; the proposed online filter instead substitutes their inferred probability vectors in b_t. Learning treats these approximate filtered states as context and uses per-step contrastive-divergence updates. The original paper distinguishes this approximation from smoothing with future observations and from an exact likelihood gradient.

  • supports Source: The Recurrent Temporal Restricted Boltzmann Machine · Locator: Section 3, Equations (4)–(5) and discussion of posterior Gibbs updates and conditional partition ratios, PDF p.3

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The conditional partition depends on history, including previous hidden states. It is not one sequence-independent global constant, and exact posterior Gibbs updates can require ratios of such partitions.
  • A factorial posterior given known latent history does not imply factorial or exact filtering after uncertain history is marginalized. Mean substitution through nonlinear normalized factors is generally approximate, and future observations can revise earlier hidden-state posteriors.
  • The paper explicitly notes that changing its parameter-dependent approximate posterior can reduce the variational bound. Finite CD, mean visible reconstructions and ignoring future evidence provide no monotonic bound or likelihood guarantee.
  • TRBM-HH denotes the variant with hidden-to-hidden links omitted, not one retaining only those links. For fully observed visible sequences its hidden posterior is factorial; missing observations and multilayer compositions change that inference problem.
  • The displayed construction has no past-hidden-to-current-visible links; a footnote mentions those as a possible extension. Gaussian visible units, deeper compositions and different lag structures require their own stated assumptions. No empirical ranking, long-memory guarantee or author-code behavior is certified.
