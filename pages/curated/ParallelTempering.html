Parallel tempering

Algorithms AIMech:ParallelTempering DRAFT
Aliases: Replica exchange Monte Carlo, Metropolis-coupled Markov chain Monte Carlo
A population Monte Carlo method that combines within-temperature target-preserving updates with corrected exchanges between replicas targeting differently tempered densities.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The record identifies fixed-temperature parallel tempering, with the source’s adjacent-swap and random-walk realization as an explicit example. The population state includes all replicas; the beta=1 replica targets the original distribution. It differs from a single-chain simulated-temperature state and from adapting the temperature ladder. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Choose a fixed L≥2 and inverse temperatures 1=beta1>...>betaL>0 for which each integral Z_beta=integral pi(x)^beta dx is finite. Initialize replicas in positive target support. The joint target is the product of pi(x_l)^beta_l/Z_beta_l. In the inspected realization, choose j uniformly from adjacent pairs and propose swapping x_j and x_(j+1), with probability min(1,[pi(x_(j+1))/pi(x_j)]^(beta_j−beta_(j+1))). Then apply a Gaussian random-walk Metropolis update at every level using acceptance min(1,[pi(y_l)/pi(x_l)]^beta_l); rejected moves retain their current replica state. Both steps preserve the joint target, so their composition does also; samples at beta1=1 have the desired stationary marginal.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • All tempered densities must be normalizable; flattening a target by taking a positive power below one need not preserve integrability.
  • Target invariance alone does not establish irreducibility, mixing speed, finite-run convergence or independence of cold-replica samples.
  • The source calls the composed chain reversible. Composition of two reversible kernels need not be reversible without additional conditions; this record asserts only their directly justified invariance.
  • The original distribution is the cold replica’s marginal, not the pooled unweighted population distribution.
  • Other valid local kernels and exchange schedules require their own conditions. The record’s explicit Gaussian and adjacent-swap realization is not a claim that all implementations use it.
