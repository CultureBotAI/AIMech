Metropolis-within-Gibbs sampling

Algorithms AIMech:MetropolisWithinGibbs DRAFT
Aliases: MWG
A componentwise Monte Carlo scheme that uses Metropolis–Hastings transitions targeting full conditional distributions for one or more coordinate blocks, optionally combined with exact Gibbs updates for other blocks.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The distinction is between an exact draw from a full conditional and a Markov transition that preserves it. The latter can be used within a cycle or fixed mixture of block updates. This record does not define every hybrid MCMC composition as Metropolis-within-Gibbs and does not require a nested chain to be run to equilibrium at each update. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Hold all coordinates outside the selected block fixed at their current values. Propose a block value from q(new|old,rest), and accept with min(1, pi(new|rest)q(old|new,rest)/(pi(old|rest)q(new|old,rest))) where defined; otherwise retain the old block. This is the substitution of the full conditional into Tierney’s Metropolis rule. The conditional normalization cancels, so an evaluable joint target ratio at the fixed rest can be used. Combine these conditional-invariant transitions, and any exact Gibbs steps, by a fixed cycle or fixed-probability mixture. A finite valid transition per selected block preserves the joint target without independently resampling that block.
  • supports Source: Markov Chains for Exploring Posterior Distributions · Locator: Sections 2.2–2.4: conditional-invariant kernels, Metropolis ratio, and the two-component Gibbs/Metropolis example; printed pp. 2–4 and 8 / PDF pp. 4–6 and 10

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The proposal-density ratio is required unless the block proposal is symmetric at the fixed values of the other coordinates.
  • A rejected proposal leaves the current block unchanged. Repeating until acceptance changes the transition unless its effect is separately accounted for.
  • A valid conditional kernel need not explore its support well. Joint convergence and mixing require additional conditions on the target, proposals and update schedule.
  • Fixed mixtures and cycles preserve the target, but Tierney explicitly warns that convergence properties of an individual component do not transfer indiscriminately to a cycle.
  • History-dependent tuning, stale parallel updates and approximate target evaluations are outside this fixed-kernel definition. No optimal number of inner steps is claimed.
