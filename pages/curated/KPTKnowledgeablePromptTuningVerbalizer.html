Knowledgeable Prompt-Tuning (KPT) Verbalizer

Algorithms AIMech:KPTKnowledgeablePromptTuningVerbalizer DRAFT
Aliases: Knowledgeable Prompt-Tuning, KPT, KPT Knowledgeable Verbalizer, Knowledge-Expanded Verbalizer Refinement for Prompt Tuning
A prompt-tuning verbalizer construction, refinement, and aggregation method that expands each class anchor label v_0 into a multi-granularity candidate label-word set V_y = N_G(v_0) union {v_0} using external knowledge bases (such as ConceptNet, WordNet, Related Words, and sentiment lexicons), denoises V_y using an unlabeled support set C_tilde via contextualized prior frequency refinement P_D(v) = (1/|C_tilde|) sum_{x in C_tilde} P_M([MASK]=v|x_p), TF-IDF-style cosine relevance refinement R(v) = r(v, f(v)) (|Y|-1) / sum_{y != f(v)} r(v, y) >= 1, contextualized prior calibration P_tilde([MASK]=v|x_p) proportional to P_M([MASK]=v|x_p)/P_D(v), and learnable per-word softmax weights alpha_v = exp(w_v) / sum_{u in V_y} exp(w_u), and predicts class labels by averaging or weighted-averaging refined word scores.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from single-word manual verbalizers in PET (AIMech:PatternExploitingTraining), from gradient- or likelihood-searched small vocabulary verbalizers in AutoPrompt (AIMech:AutoPromptGradientGuidedDiscretePromptSearch) and LM-BFF (AIMech:LMBFFBetterFewShotFineTuning), which require labeled data and find only near-synonyms, and from continuous soft verbalizers in WARP (AIMech:WARPWordLevelAdversarialReProgramming), which cannot operate in zero-shot settings.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r723-claim-bayesian-verbalizer-decomposition
Under marginalization over label words p(Y=y|x) proportional to sum_{v in V_y} [p([M]=v|Y=y) / p([M]=v)] P_M([M]=v|x_p), KPT decomposes multi-word verbalizer prediction into three factors: class-conditional word relevance p([M]=v|Y=y) (estimated by quantized Relevance Refinement R(v) >= 1 in zero-shot or continuous Learnable Refinement weights alpha_v in few-shot), contextualized word prior p([M]=v) = P_D(v) in the denominator (removed by Frequency Refinement and divided out by Contextualized Calibration), and the PLM masked token likelihood P_M([M]=v|x_p).
  • supports Source: Knowledgeable Prompt-tuning: Incorporating Knowledge into Prompt Verbalizer for Text Classification · Locator: Section 3.3 & Appendix B, Equations 2-8 and 12-17, pp. 4-5, 12-13
Mechanism #r723-claim-oov-subtoken-averaging
Because external knowledge bases contain out-of-vocabulary (OOV) multi-subword expressions not tailored to the PLM tokenizer, KPT computes the prediction score of each OOV label word by averaging the PLM prediction probabilities of its constituent subword tokens at the single [MASK] position, retaining over 100 refined label words per dataset and matching or outperforming single-token-restricted verbalizers.
  • supports Source: Knowledgeable Prompt-tuning: Incorporating Knowledge into Prompt Verbalizer for Text Classification · Locator: Section 3.3 & Appendix D.3-D.4, Table 6, Figure 4, pp. 5, 14-15
Application #r723-claim-zero-and-few-shot-error-reduction
Across five topic and sentiment classification benchmarks (AG's News, DBPedia, Yahoo, Amazon, IMDB) with RoBERTa-large, KPT reduces average classification error rate by 16%, 18%, 10%, and 7% in 0-shot, 1-shot, 5-shot, and 10-shot settings respectively compared to the best baseline while substantially lowering variance across templates and random seeds.
  • supports Source: Knowledgeable Prompt-tuning: Incorporating Knowledge into Prompt Verbalizer for Text Classification · Locator: Section 1 & Section 4.4, Tables 2-3, pp. 2, 7-8
Limitation #r723-claim-uncalibrated-kb-noise-limitation
Raw knowledge-base label word expansion without refinement introduces severe prior-probability skew and cross-class noise—dropping zero-shot AG's News accuracy from 84.8% to 55.5% when Contextualized Calibration is ablated—and KPT relies on the availability of an external knowledge graph or lexicon anchored on accurate class names plus an unlabeled support set (|C_tilde| >= 50) for zero-shot calibration.
  • supports Source: Knowledgeable Prompt-tuning: Incorporating Knowledge into Prompt Verbalizer for Text Classification · Locator: Section 4.4, Section 6, & Appendix D.1, Table 2, Figure 3, pp. 7-9, 13

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Unrefined external knowledge-base expansions suffer severe prior-probability variance and noisy words (causing zero-shot AG's News accuracy to drop from 84.8% to 55.5% without contextualized calibration), requiring an unlabeled support set of at least ~50 instances in zero-shot mode.
  • Requires an external knowledge graph or domain lexicon indexed by accurate anchor class names and is vulnerable to KB errors or malicious word injection.
