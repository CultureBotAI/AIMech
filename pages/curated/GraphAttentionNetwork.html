Graph attention network

Architectures AIMech:GraphAttentionNetwork DRAFT
Aliases: GAT
A graph neural architecture that updates node representations using learned attention weights over graph neighborhoods.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The original GAT architecture is distinct from a generic attention layer and from graph-convolution methods that do not use its learned neighborhood attention. GATv2 and other revisions are outside this identity.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
Shared transformations and a learned attention function produce neighborhood scores, softmax normalizes those scores, and their weighted feature combinations form updated node representations.
  • supports Source: Graph Attention Networks · Locator: Section 2.1, equations 1–6, PDF/printed pp. 3–4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The record uses the revised ICLR 2018 paper, including its specified attention nonlinearity.
  • Attention coefficients are computation weights; no general causal-explanation or benchmark-superiority claim is made.
