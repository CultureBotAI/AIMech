Proximal policy optimization

Algorithms AIMech:ProximalPolicyOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: PPO
A family of policy-gradient algorithms alternating collected interaction data with repeated optimization of a surrogate objective using clipping or an adaptive KL penalty.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope PPO is a policy-optimization algorithm family. It is distinct from the broad reinforcement-learning task and from DPO's direct preference-fitting objective; PPO is not synonymous with its clipped variant alone.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #mechanism
The clipped variant takes the minimum of unclipped and clipped probability-ratio advantage terms; another variant uses an adaptive KL-divergence penalty, and the algorithm performs multiple minibatch epochs on collected trajectories.
  • supports Source: Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms · Locator: Sections 3–5, equations 7–8 and Algorithm 1, PDF/printed pp. 3–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Clipping the surrogate objective is not an explicit hard bound on all policy changes.
  • The record covers the original clipped and KL-penalized variants without asserting task-independent performance.
