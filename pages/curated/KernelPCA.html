Kernel Principal Component Analysis

Algorithms AIMech:KernelPCA DRAFT
Aliases: Kernel PCA, KPCA
A spectral feature-extraction algorithm that performs principal component analysis on centered implicit feature vectors by eigendecomposing a positive-semidefinite kernel Gram matrix and evaluating new-point coordinates through kernel expansions.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The Schölkopf-Smola-Müller kernel eigenvalue construction generalizes ordinary PCA to a kernel-defined feature space. It includes linear PCA as a special case but is not the identity of ordinary input-space PCA, an SVM classifier, a dot-product KernelLayer, Isomap, LLE or Diffusion Maps. A trained embedding, chosen kernel and preimage approximation are not separate aliases for the algorithm. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #centered-kernel-eigenproblem
For M training points and a positive-semidefinite kernel k, form K_ij=k(x_i,x_j) and Kc=H K H with H=I-11^T/M. If Kc v=kappa v, ||v||=1 and kappa>0, coefficients alpha=v/sqrt(kappa) define a unit feature-space principal axis. The corresponding empirical covariance eigenvalue is kappa/M. Centered rank is at most M-1. Zero Gram eigenvalues do not define additional nonzero covariance directions.
  • supports Source: Nonlinear Component Analysis as a Kernel Eigenvalue Problem · Locator: Section 2, Equations 2.7-2.15, printed pp. 1301-1302 / PDF pp. 3-4; Appendices A-B, pp. 1315-1317 / PDF pp. 17-19; curator explicit distinction of Gram and covariance eigenvalues
Mechanism #new-point-projection
For a new x, center its similarity to each training point using the same training feature mean: kc_i(x)=k(x_i,x)-M^(-1)sum_j k(x_j,x)-M^(-1)sum_j K_ij+M^(-2)sum_jl K_jl. Its component coordinate is sum_i alpha_i kc_i(x). Centering input coordinates before a nonlinear kernel does not generally center the feature vectors; centering each test batch independently changes the learned projection.
  • supports Source: Nonlinear Component Analysis as a Kernel Eigenvalue Problem · Locator: Equation 4.1, printed p. 1305 / PDF p. 7; Appendix B, Equations B.1-B.4 and test-kernel formula, pp. 1316-1317 / PDF pp. 18-19
Scope #feature-space-optimality
With a fixed valid kernel, leading components maximize retained empirical variance and minimize rank-constrained squared reconstruction error in the centered feature space. Their sample coordinates are uncorrelated, not necessarily statistically independent. They can outnumber input dimensions. A linear kernel recovers ordinary PCA with consistent centering. Truncated feature reconstructions or principal directions need not have an exact input-space preimage, so reconstructing original inputs is a separate problem.
  • supports Source: Nonlinear Component Analysis as a Kernel Eigenvalue Problem · Locator: Sections 4.2-4.5, Figures 1-2 and Equation 4.2, printed pp. 1305-1309 / PDF pp. 7-11; Section 6.3.5, pp. 1314-1315 / PDF pp. 16-17
Limitation #kernel-validity
The feature-space covariance interpretation requires a positive-semidefinite kernel. The paper lists sigmoid similarities, but arbitrary tanh parameters do not meet that requirement: a curator two-point example k(x,y)=tanh(x*y), x in {1,2}, gives a negative Gram determinant and a negative eigenvalue even after centering. Keeping selected positive eigenpairs of an indefinite similarity does not establish that the original similarity is a Hilbert-space inner product.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Kernel and hyperparameter selection remain modeling decisions. An eigensolution for a fixed kernel does not solve all kernel-selection or preimage optimization problems.
  • The exact Gram construction requires storage quadratic in training sample count; partial eigensolvers, subsets and reduced-set projections are additional approximation choices. Implicit features avoid explicit feature-dimension costs, not all sample-size costs.
  • Eigenvector signs and bases within degenerate eigenspaces are nonunique. Variance and reconstruction guarantees concern the chosen feature space, not causal factors, independence, universal input reconstruction or classification accuracy.
  • All 21 source pages were inspected. Finite calculations verify centering, eigenvalue scaling, projection and PSD boundaries; the historical USPS classifier experiments and author implementation were not reproduced.
