Linear state-space layer

Layers & Modules AIMech:LinearStateSpaceLayer DRAFT
Aliases: LSSL
A sequence-to-sequence layer defined by discretizing a linear continuous-time state-space system, with a state recurrence and, for zero initial state, an equivalent convolutional representation plus a direct input term.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope LSSL denotes Gu and colleagues' component with state-space parameters and timescale, not the complete deep LSSL network, every recurrent network, or S4's later structured parameterization and kernel algorithm.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #sequence-mechanism
Discretize the state dynamics using a step size to obtain a recurrent state update and linear output map. With zero initial state, powers of the discretized state matrix between input and output projections form the convolution kernel. Separate feature channels use their own broadcast input/output parameters and timescales; complete networks add further layers and connections.
  • supports Source: Combining Recurrent, Convolutional, and Continuous-time Models with Linear State-Space Layers · Locator: Section 2 equations 3–5 and Sections 3.1–3.3, equations 6–7, PDF/printed pp. 4–6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The simple convolution expression assumes zero initial state; a nonzero starting state contributes an additional term.
  • The layer is linear; nonlinear expressive power of stacked networks is not a property of a single standalone LSSL. Efficient recurrent updates and kernel construction depend on the selected state-matrix structure.
