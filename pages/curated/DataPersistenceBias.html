Data Persistence Bias

Biases AIMech:DataPersistenceBias DRAFT
Aliases: Retrospective Data Persistence Bias, Social Media Data Persistence Bias, Dataset Content Persistence Bias
A systematic dataset representation and survivorship bias that arises when user-generated or web data is collected retrospectively at a time later than its creation, causing deleted, moderated, or suspended accounts and posts to be selectively omitted and thereby distorting class priors, feature distributions, and interaction graphs relative to real-time collection.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope No AIO class represents Data Persistence Bias from retrospective dataset collection; RankingBias (AIO:RankingBias) is a verified existing AIO bias class in data/aio/ representing systematic distortion in retrieved web and information system data.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #retrospective-attrition-class-prior-distortion
When data is collected retrospectively at T_collect > T_create, differential attrition from account suspensions, moderation, and user deletions alters the observed class prior P(Y=1 | persisted) relative to the true generation-time prior P(Y=1), shifting the optimal classifier decision threshold.
  • supports Source: The Impact of Data Persistence Bias on Social Media Studies · Locator: Section 1, pp. 1-2; Section 3, Figures 1-3, pp. 3-5
Mechanism #interaction-graph-and-popularity-metric-distortion
Selective removal of ephemeral or suspended nodes and edges before retrospective collection alters observed retweet/interaction network degree distributions, centrality rankings, and engagement counts.
  • supports Source: The Impact of Data Persistence Bias on Social Media Studies · Locator: Section 4-5, pp. 5-8
Application #social-media-moderation-and-bot-detection-audit-application
Auditing for data persistence bias is applied to social media machine learning benchmarks for bot detection, hate speech classification, and coordinated manipulation analysis to prevent training on survivorship-skewed datasets.
  • supports Source: The Impact of Data Persistence Bias on Social Media Studies · Locator: Section 5-6, pp. 7-10
Limitation #irreversibility-without-realtime-archival-limitation
Once non-persistent posts or accounts have been permanently deleted from platform APIs prior to collection, data persistence bias cannot be corrected purely by post-hoc reweighting unless an auxiliary real-time stream or archive recorded the missing data.
  • supports Source: The Impact of Data Persistence Bias on Social Media Studies · Locator: Section 6-7, pp. 9-11

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results
