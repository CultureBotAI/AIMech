FedU and dFedU (Laplacian-Regularized Federated Multi-Task Learning)

Algorithms AIMech:FedUAndDFedULaplacianRegularizedFederatedMultiTaskLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedU, dFedU, Laplacian-Regularized Federated Multi-Task Learning
A communication-centralized (FedU, Algorithm 1) and decentralized (dFedU, Algorithm 2) federated multi-task learning framework (Dinh, Vu, Tran, Dao, & Zhang 2022, Sections III-V, Equations 1-12, Algorithms 1-3, Theorems 1-2) that formulates client-relationship-aware learning over an undirected weighted entity graph G = (N, E, A) with graph Laplacian L = D - A (and Kronecker Laplacian L_cal = L otimes I_d) as min_{W = [w_1^T, ..., w_N^T]^T} { J(W) := sum_{k=1}^N F_k(w_k) + (eta / 2) sum_{k=1}^N sum_{l in N_k} a_{kl} ||w_k - w_l||^2 } (Equations 1-3): in each round t, active clients k in S^{(t)} perform R local stochastic gradient steps w_{k, r+1}^{(t)} = w_{k, r}^{(t)} - mu nabla F_tilde_k(w_{k, r}^{(t)}) toward their individual local optima, and the server (in FedU) or each client via direct neighbor exchange (in dFedU) applies the graph-Laplacian regularization update w_k^{(t+1)} = w_{k, R}^{(t)} - mu_tilde eta sum_{l in N_k} a_{kl} (w_{k, R}^{(t)} - w_{l, R}^{(t)}) with global step-size mu_tilde = mu R (Algorithms 1-3, Equation 45).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from pFedMe (AIMech:PFedMePersonalizedFederatedLearningWithMoreauEnvelopes, Dinh, Tran, & Nguyen 2020), which is a star-graph special case of Equation 1 with a virtual central server model w_0 and no direct inter-client relational edges a_{kl} (Section III-B, Equation 5); and distinct from MOCHA (AIMech:MochaSystemsAwarePrimalDualFederatedMultiTaskLearning, Smith et al. 2017), which is restricted to convex loss functions solved via dual coordinate ascent and alternating task-matrix updates rather than primal multi-step local SGD with Laplacian regularization for both strongly convex and nonconvex deep networks.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r486-local-sgd-and-graph-laplacian-server-or-neighbor-update-mechanism
In each communication round t, sampled clients k in S^{(t)} run R local mini-batch SGD steps w_{k, r+1}^{(t)} = w_{k, r}^{(t)} - mu nabla F_tilde_k(w_{k, r}^{(t)}) and then apply the Laplacian coupling step W^{(t+1)} = (I - mu_tilde eta S_tilde^{(t)} L_cal) W_R^{(t)}, i.e., w_k^{(t+1)} = w_{k, R}^{(t)} - mu_tilde eta sum_{l in N_k} a_{kl}(w_{k, R}^{(t)} - w_{l, R}^{(t)}) (centralized on the server in FedU Algorithm 1 or peer-to-peer among neighbors N_tilde_k in dFedU Algorithm 2), where the server-update matrix C = I - mu_tilde eta L_cal is normalized (||C||_2 = 1) if and only if mu_tilde eta rho <= 2 with rho = ||L||_2 (Dinh et al. 2022, Section IV & Appendix A/C, Equations 19-21 & 43-45, pp. 4-5 & 11-14).
  • supports Source: A New Look and Convergence Rate of Federated Multi-Task Learning with Laplacian Regularization · Locator: Section IV (Algorithms 1-2), pp. 4-5, and Appendix A & C (Equations 19-21, Algorithm 3, Equation 45), pp. 11 & 14
Scope #r486-star-graph-unification-and-linear-speedup-convergence-scope
Section III-B proves that conventional consensus FL (FedAvg, Equation 4), Moreau-envelope personalized FL (pFedMe, Equation 5), and MAML-based Per-FedAvg (Equation 6) are exact or epigraph special cases of the Laplacian FMTL objective on a star graph with virtual server vertex 0; furthermore, when eta rho > 2 beta (Lemma 1), Theorem 1 proves O_tilde(1/(alpha S) + sigma_1^2 / (alpha R S epsilon)) linear speedup for alpha-strongly convex objectives and Theorem 2 proves O(1/(S epsilon) + sigma_1^2 / (epsilon^2 R S)) sublinear speedup of order 1/2 for smooth nonconvex objectives without requiring bounded gradient dissimilarity at individual clients (Dinh et al. 2022, Section III-B & Section V, Lemma 1, Theorems 1-2, pp. 3-6).
  • supports Source: A New Look and Convergence Rate of Federated Multi-Task Learning with Laplacian Regularization · Locator: Section III-B (Equations 4-6), pp. 3-4, and Section V (Lemma 1, Theorems 1-2, Equations 7-12), pp. 5-6
Application #r486-non-iid-sensor-and-vision-multitask-classification-application
Applied to strongly convex multinomial logistic regression and nonconvex deep neural network classification across heterogeneous tasks on Human Activity Recognition, Vehicle Sensor, MNIST, and CIFAR-10 benchmarks (Dinh et al. 2022, Section VI, Table I, Figures 3-5, pp. 6-9).
  • supports Source: A New Look and Convergence Rate of Federated Multi-Task Learning with Laplacian Regularization · Locator: Section VI (Experiments, Table I, Figures 3-5), pp. 6-9
Limitation #r486-known-client-relationship-graph-requirement-limitation
Assumes the inter-client relationship weights {a_{kl}} are known a priori (and that eta satisfies eta > 2 beta / rho while mu_tilde eta rho <= 2), and in centralized FedU requires the server to maintain all N client models {w_k} and the adjacency graph (Dinh et al. 2022, Section III-B & Section IV-B, pp. 3-5).
  • supports Source: A New Look and Convergence Rate of Federated Multi-Task Learning with Laplacian Regularization · Locator: Section III-B, p. 3, and Section IV-B, pp. 4-5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires the client similarity adjacency matrix A = [a_{kl}] to be specified beforehand.
  • Centralized FedU stores all N client models and graph edges at the server, motivating decentralized dFedU when N is large.
