PHATE

Algorithms AIMech:PHATE DRAFT
Aliases: Potential of Heat Diffusion for Affinity-based Transition Embedding
A dimensionality-reduction algorithm that computes diffusion transition probabilities, measures Euclidean distances between their negative-log rows, and uses metric multidimensional scaling to embed those potential distances.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope PHATE uses potential distances between transformed diffusion distributions followed by stress fitting; AIMech:DiffusionMaps uses time-scaled Markov eigenvectors. PHATE is not generic MDS, a generative diffusion model, a branch-detection procedure or the DEMaP evaluation criterion. The core algorithm is distinct from its landmark approximation and multislice extensions. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method-scope
Build symmetric local affinities with an adaptive bandwidth and an alpha-decaying kernel, row-normalize them to a Markov operator P, and take a selected diffusion power P^t. For positive transition probabilities, form potential vectors −log(P^t_i) and their Euclidean pairwise distances. Initialize with classical MDS and optimize metric-MDS stress in the requested output dimension. The paper proposes an entropy-knee heuristic for choosing diffusion time and a separate landmark approximation for scaling.
  • supports Source: Visualizing structure and transitions in high-dimensional biological data · Locator: Results overview, printed pp. 1482–1483 / PDF pp. 1–2; Methods, local affinities through MDS, equations (1)–(9), PDF pp. 12–14

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Neighborhood affinities, bandwidth, decay exponent and diffusion time determine the geometry; excessive diffusion can remove signal as well as noise.
  • Negative logarithms require positive entries. Disconnected or numerically thresholded transitions need explicit zero-handling; the paper’s formula alone does not specify a universal remedy.
  • A two- or three-dimensional stress fit can distort potential distances and does not guarantee global optimization, preservation of every manifold property or biological trajectories.
  • The entropy discussion assumes a nonnegative spectrum for its probability normalization. This record does not certify that assumption for every adaptive kernel or treat the knee heuristic as an optimal-time theorem.
  • The inspected PDF is marked corrected; the correction notice and access limitations are tracked in the discovery report.
