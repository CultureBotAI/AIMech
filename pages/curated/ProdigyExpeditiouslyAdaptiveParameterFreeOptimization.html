Prodigy (Expeditiously Adaptive Parameter-Free Optimization)

Algorithms AIMech:ProdigyExpeditiouslyAdaptiveParameterFreeOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: Prodigy Optimizer, Prodigy Parameter-Free Learner, Weighted D-Adaptation (Prodigy)
A parameter-free adaptive optimization algorithm that estimates the initial distance to the solution D = ||x_0 - x_*|| online via a monotonically non-decreasing lower bound d_{k+1} = max(d_k, d_hat_{k+1}) and places d_i^2-weighted gradient norms inside the AdaGrad/Adam step-size denominator — setting eta_k = d_k^2 lambda_k / sqrt(d_k^2 G^2 + sum_{i=0}^k d_i^2 lambda_i^2 ||g_i||^2) in Gradient Descent (Algorithm 1), gamma_{k+1} = 1 / sqrt(lambda_{k+1} G^2 + sum_{i=0}^k lambda_i ||g_i||^2) with lambda_k = d_k^2 in Dual Averaging (Algorithm 2), and scaling moments by d_k and d_k^2 with sqrt(beta_2) EMA tracking of r_{k+1} and s_{k+1} in Adam (Algorithm 3) — improving the worst-case non-asymptotic convergence rate of D-Adaptation by a factor of O(sqrt(log(D / d_0))).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from standard D-Adaptation in that Prodigy weights each historical squared gradient norm in the step-size denominator by d_i^2 rather than unweighted ||g_i||^2, yielding a provably larger step size d_k^2 / sqrt(sum_{i=0}^k d_i^2 ||g_i||^2) >= d_k / sqrt(sum_{i=0}^k ||g_i||^2) and reducing the dependence on the initial distance underestimate D / d_0 from O(log(D / d_0)) to the optimal O(sqrt(log(D / d_0))). Distinct from DoG in that Prodigy estimates D via a dual lower bound d_hat_{k+1} rather than the maximum iterate displacement max_{i<=k} ||x_i - x_0|| and naturally supports coordinatewise Adam preconditioning.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #c-r590-d2-weighted-stepsize-and-sqrt-log-rate
By weighting historical squared gradients in the denominator by d_i^2 (and optional non-decreasing weights lambda_i), Prodigy produces step sizes eta_k = d_k^2 / sqrt(sum_{i=0}^k d_i^2 ||g_i||^2) that are provably larger than D-Adaptation's d_k / sqrt(sum_{i=0}^k ||g_i||^2) and bounds min_{t<N} d_{t+1} / sqrt(sum_{k=0}^t d_k^2) <= 4 sqrt(log_{2+}(d_N / d_0)) / sqrt(N) (Lemma 1), achieving O(G D sqrt(log_{2+}(D / d_0)) / sqrt(n)) convergence in Dual Averaging (Theorem 2) and improving upon D-Adaptation by O(sqrt(log(D / d_0))).
  • supports Source: Prodigy: An Expeditiously Adaptive Parameter-Free Learner · Locator: Section 2, pp. 2-4; Lemma 1 & Theorems 1-2, pp. 3-4; Appendix A.2-A.4, pp. 15-21
Mechanism #c-r590-adam-exponential-weighting-derivation
Setting exponential weights lambda_k = beta_2^{-k/2} in Prodigy's step-size identity yields the Adam variant (Algorithm 3), which scales first and second moment updates by d_k and d_k^2 (m_{k+1} = beta_1 m_k + (1 - beta_1) d_k g_k, v_{k+1} = beta_2 v_k + (1 - beta_2) d_k^2 g_k^2) and tracks numerator r_{k+1} and l_1-norm denominator s_{k+1} using exponential moving average factor sqrt(beta_2) to update d_hat_{k+1} = r_{k+1} / ||s_{k+1}||_1.
  • supports Source: Prodigy: An Expeditiously Adaptive Parameter-Free Learner · Locator: Algorithm 3, p. 6; Section 5, pp. 6-7; Appendix A.5, Algorithm 4 & Theorem 7, pp. 22-25
Scope #c-r590-exponentially-bounded-lower-complexity
D-Adaptation, DoG, and Prodigy all belong to the class of exponentially bounded optimization algorithms satisfying ||x_k - x_0|| <= 2^k d_0 for G-bounded gradients (Definition 1, Theorem 3), for which any algorithm without problem constants G and D suffers a worst-case lower bound min_{k<=n} f(x_k) - f_* >= D G sqrt(log_2(D / x_1)) / (2 sqrt(n + 1)) (Theorem 4), proving that Prodigy's sqrt(log(D / d_0)) dependence is optimal within this class.
  • supports Source: Prodigy: An Expeditiously Adaptive Parameter-Free Learner · Locator: Section 3, Definition 1 & Theorems 3-4, pp. 4-5; Appendix B, Theorems 8-9, pp. 25-27
Limitation #c-r590-fast-growing-weights-and-da-limitation
Prodigy's Dual Averaging convergence analysis requires monotonically decreasing step sizes gamma_{k+1} and therefore only holds for fixed weights lambda_k = d_k^2, while in the Gradient Descent variant choosing exponentially growing weights lambda_k = beta_2^{-k^p} requires p < 1/3 to guarantee worst-case convergence.
  • supports Source: Prodigy: An Expeditiously Adaptive Parameter-Free Learner · Locator: Section 2, p. 4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Dual Averaging Prodigy (Algorithm 2) requires monotonically decreasing step sizes gamma_{k+1} and therefore cannot incorporate arbitrary increasing gradient weights beyond lambda_k = d_k^2.
  • Maintains additional running state vectors (s_k in R^p alongside m_k and v_k) compared to standard Adam.
