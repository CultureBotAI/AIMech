Mean Teacher

A semi-supervised classifier-training procedure that regularizes a student prediction toward the prediction of a same-architecture teacher whose parameters are an exponential moving average of successive student parameters, alongside labeled classification loss.

  • supports Source: Mean teachers are better role models: Weight-averaged consistency targets improve semi-supervised deep learning results · Locator: Section 2, Figure 2 and the consistency/weight-EMA equations, PDF/printed pp. 2–3; Section 3.1, p. 4
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Mean Teacher averages weights, whereas temporal ensembling averages predictions and the Pi-model shares current parameters across two gradient-bearing branches. Sharing an EMA component does not make this labeled classifier-training objective identical to BYOL or DINO, which use different representation targets and objectives without the labeled cross-entropy term. This record describes a training algorithm, not a trained checkpoint or fixed network architecture. The inspected AIO SemiSupervisedLLM class specifically combines LLM pretraining with supervised fine-tuning; its related synonym self-training is not an exact identity for this method. AIO SelfsupervisedLearning excludes human-annotated labels, Distillation describes large-to-small compression, and RegularizationLayer is a layer: none supplies an equivalent class for this algorithm. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Evaluate each input with independently perturbed student and teacher networks of the same architecture. Train the student with cross-entropy on labeled examples and a weighted squared-probability consistency cost between student and teacher predictions on labeled and unlabeled examples. Treat teacher predictions as constant during the gradient update. After obtaining the updated student parameters theta_t, update teacher parameters theta_prime_t←alpha theta_prime_(t−1)+(1−alpha)theta_t. Thus the teacher changes by parameter averaging after training steps, not by backpropagation through its consistency branch. Choose the averaging coefficient and consistency-weight schedule as part of the training setup. The core description does not require a particular initialization or warm-up formula. Unlike temporal ensembling, it does not require a persistent target array indexed by the training set.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Teacher initialization, coefficient schedules, loss scaling and network state management remain implementation choices; the record does not assert a universal initialization, alpha value or handling of nonparameter buffers.
  • Consistency relies on class-preserving perturbations and can propagate wrong targets; the EMA teacher is not guaranteed to improve every prediction.
  • The original experiments include architecture and dataset-specific settings. No benchmark score, universal performance ordering, or large-to-small compression claim is asserted.
  • The read supplementary pages cover the convolutional setup and branch gradients, not all later supplement experiments or source code.
