Deep deterministic policy gradient

Algorithms AIMech:DeepDeterministicPolicyGradient DRAFT
Aliases: DDPG
An off-policy actor–critic method for continuous control using neural function approximators and deterministic policy gradients.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope DDPG combines deterministic policy gradients with deep actor/critic approximators, replay and target networks. It is distinct from stochastic maximum-entropy SAC and discrete-action DQN.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #learning-method
Replay samples train a critic toward bootstrapped targets and update a deterministic actor through the critic's action gradient; slowly updated target actor and critic networks supply the bootstrap targets.
  • supports Source: Continuous control with deep reinforcement learning · Locator: Section 3, PDF/printed pp. 3–4; Algorithm 1, PDF/printed p. 5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Exploration in the original algorithm adds noise to the actor's action; a deterministic actor does not remove the need for exploration.
  • The source's simulated-control results do not establish universal stability or performance in every continuous-action environment.
