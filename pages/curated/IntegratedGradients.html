Integrated gradients

Mathematical Methods AIMech:IntegratedGradients DRAFT
Aliases: IG
A feature-attribution method that integrates a prediction's input gradients along the straight path from a chosen baseline to the input.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Straight-path gradient integration differs from a gradient at one input, layer-wise contribution propagation and Shapley averaging across feature orderings.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #attribution-method
Each feature receives its input-minus-baseline difference multiplied by the integral of that feature's partial derivative along the path; the paper approximates the integral with a finite gradient sum.
  • supports Source: Axiomatic Attribution for Deep Networks · Locator: Section 3, equation 1, PDF p. 3; Section 5, equation 3 and baseline selection, PDF pp. 5–6

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Attributions depend on the baseline; an arbitrary zero-score baseline need not give a meaningful absence of signal.
  • Numerical integration is approximate. This record makes no universal causal-faithfulness or superiority claim.
