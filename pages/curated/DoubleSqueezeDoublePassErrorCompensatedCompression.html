DoubleSqueeze (Double-Pass Error-Compensated Gradient Compression)

Algorithms AIMech:DoubleSqueezeDoublePassErrorCompensatedCompression DRAFT
Aliases: DoubleSqueeze, Double-Pass Error-Compensated Compression for Parallel SGD, Bidirectional Error-Compensated Distributed Stochastic Gradient Descent
A bidirectional communication-compressed parallel stochastic gradient descent algorithm (Tang et al. 2019, Algorithm 1) that applies error-compensated compression C(.) in both the worker-to-server push pass (v_t^{(i)} = C(g_t^{(i)} + gamma delta_{t-1}^{(i)}), delta_t^{(i)} = g_t^{(i)} + gamma delta_{t-1}^{(i)} - v_t^{(i)}) and the server-to-worker broadcast pass (v_t = C((1/n) sum_{i=1}^n v_t^{(i)} + gamma epsilon_{t-1}), epsilon_t = (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n v_t^{(i)} + gamma epsilon_{t-1} - v_t) before updating x_{t+1} = x_t - eta v_t.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from unidirectional error-feedback compression methods such as Sparsified SGD with Error Memory (AIMech:SparsifiedSgdWithErrorMemoryForKContractiveGradientCompression, Stich, Cordonnier, & Jaggi 2018) and EF21 (AIMech:Ef21MarkovCompressorErrorFeedbackForHeterogeneousNonconvexOptimization, Richtárik, Sokolov, & Fatkhullin 2021), which compress only worker-to-server uploads and require uncompressed server-to-worker broadcasts or all-to-all aggregation.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r416-virtual-iterate-bidirectional-error-telescoping-and-nonconvex-rate
Defining the virtual sequence x_tilde_t = x_t - eta gamma (epsilon_{t-1} + (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n delta_{t-1}^{(i)}) yields the exact recurrence x_tilde_{t+1} = x_tilde_t - eta g_bar_t + eta (1 - gamma) (epsilon_t + delta_bar_t) (Supplementary Lemma 1), so setting gamma = 1 exactly cancels both worker-side and server-side compression errors in the virtual iterate and achieves the smooth non-convex convergence rate O(sigma / sqrt(n T) + xi^{2/3} / T^{2/3} + 1 / T) matching uncompressed parallel SGD's dominant O(1/sqrt(n T)) term (Theorem 1, Corollary 2).
  • supports Source: DoubleSqueeze: Parallel Stochastic Gradient Descent with Double-pass Error-Compensated Compression · Locator: Section 4 Theoretical Analysis (Theorem 1, Corollary 2) & Supplementary Material (Lemma 1, Proof of Theorem 1)
Mechanism #r416-asymmetric-worker-vs-server-compression-noise-sensitivity
Under bounded compression error E||C(u) - u|| <= xi / 2, the combined double-pass error satisfies xi^2 = O(xi_s^2 + xi_w^2 / n), showing that worker-side compression errors delta_t^{(i)} enjoy a 1/n averaging variance reduction across n workers whereas server-side broadcast compression error epsilon_t does not.
  • supports Source: DoubleSqueeze: Parallel Stochastic Gradient Descent with Double-pass Error-Compensated Compression · Locator: Section 3-4 Discussion of Asymmetric Compression & Supplementary Material (Remark on Worker vs. Server Error)
Limitation #r416-parameter-server-topology-and-bounded-error-memory-limitation
Requires a centralized parameter-server aggregation step to maintain the server error memory epsilon_t in R^d and assumes either bounded absolute compression error or contractive relative compression error to keep ||epsilon_t|| and ||delta_t^{(i)}|| bounded.
  • supports Source: DoubleSqueeze: Parallel Stochastic Gradient Descent with Double-pass Error-Compensated Compression · Locator: Section 4 Assumption 1 & Algorithm 1
Application #r416-distributed-resnet-and-cifar10-imagenet-training-application
Applied to distributed parameter-server training of ResNet-18 and ResNet-50 on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet with 1-bit quantization and Top-k sparsification in both communication directions.
  • supports Source: DoubleSqueeze: Parallel Stochastic Gradient Descent with Double-pass Error-Compensated Compression · Locator: Section 5 Experiments (Figures 2-5), pp. 6160-6163

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Server-side compression noise is not reduced by the number of workers n, unlike worker-side compression noise.
  • Requires maintaining O(d) error-compensation buffers on every worker and on the central parameter server.
