De-biased Noisy Matrix Completion and De-shrunken Low-Rank Factor Uncertainty Quantification

Aliases: De-biased Matrix Completion and De-shrunken Low-Rank Factor Estimator, Chen-Fan-Ma-Yan Noisy Matrix Completion Uncertainty Quantification, Sample-Splitting-Free De-biased Nuclear Norm and Nonconvex Matrix Completion Inference
A unified statistical inference and uncertainty quantification methodology (introduced by Yuxin Chen, Jianqing Fan, Cong Ma, and Yuling Yan in 2019) for noisy low-rank matrix completion $M_{ij} = M_{ij}^\star + E_{ij}$, $(i,j)\in\Omega$, $E_{ij}\stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim}\mathcal{N}(0,\sigma^2)$ under uniform random sampling with probability $p$. Given either the convex nuclear-norm regularized estimator $\boldsymbol{Z}^{\text{cvx}} = \arg\min_{\boldsymbol{Z}} \frac{1}{2}\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{M})\|_F^2 + \lambda\|\boldsymbol{Z}\|_*$ or the nonconvex factored gradient descent estimator $(\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{ncvx}},\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{ncvx}})$ minimizing $\frac{1}{2}\|\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{X}\boldsymbol{Y}^\top - \boldsymbol{M})\|_F^2 + \frac{\lambda}{2}(\|\boldsymbol{X}\|_F^2 + \|\boldsymbol{Y}\|_F^2)$, the method compensates for regularization shrinkage without sample splitting via two nearly equivalent estimators (Lemma 3): (1) the rank-$r$ projected *de-biased matrix estimator* $\boldsymbol{M}^{\text{d}} = \mathcal{P}_{\text{rank-}r}[\boldsymbol{Z} - \frac{1}{p}\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{M})]$ (Equation 3.7), and (2) the *de-shrunken low-rank factor estimators* $\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{d}} = \boldsymbol{X}(\boldsymbol{I}_r + \frac{\lambda}{p}(\boldsymbol{X}^\top\boldsymbol{X})^{-1})^{1/2}$ and $\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}} = \boldsymbol{Y}(\boldsymbol{I}_r + \frac{\lambda}{p}(\boldsymbol{Y}^\top\boldsymbol{Y})^{-1})^{1/2}$ satisfying $\boldsymbol{M}^{\text{d}} \approx \boldsymbol{X}^{\text{d}}\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}\top}$ (Equations 3.8–3.9). Theorem 1 proves that up to global orthogonal rotation $\boldsymbol{H}^{\text{d}}$, the rows of $\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{d}}\boldsymbol{H}^{\text{d}} - \boldsymbol{X}^\star$ and $\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}}\boldsymbol{H}^{\text{d}} - \boldsymbol{Y}^\star$ admit non-asymptotic i.i.d. $\mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{0}, \frac{\sigma^2}{p}(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}^\star)^{-1})$ decompositions matching the oracle Cramér–Rao lower bound (Lemma 1). Theorem 2 and Corollary 1 establish that $M_{ij}^{\text{d}} - M_{ij}^\star = g_{ij} + \Delta_{ij}$ with $g_{ij}\sim\mathcal{N}(0, v_{ij}^\star)$, $v_{ij}^\star = \frac{\sigma^2}{p}(\|\boldsymbol{U}_{i,\cdot}^\star\|_2^2 + \|\boldsymbol{V}_{j,\cdot}^\star\|_2^2)$, yielding valid two-sided $(1-\alpha)$ confidence intervals $[M_{ij}^{\text{d}} \pm \Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)\sqrt{v_{ij}}]$ and sharp Frobenius accuracy $\|\boldsymbol{M}^{\text{d}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_F^2 = (2+o(1))nr\sigma^2/p$ matching the oracle tangent-space lower bound including the pre-constant 2 (Theorems 3–4).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #claim-equivalence-of-debiased-matrix-and-deshrunken-factor-estimators
For regularization parameter $\lambda = C_\lambda\sigma\sqrt{np}$, Lemma 3 proves that the convex de-biased estimator $\boldsymbol{M}^{\text{cvx,d}} = \mathcal{P}_{\text{rank-}r}[\boldsymbol{Z}^{\text{cvx}} - p^{-1}\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Z}^{\text{cvx}} - \boldsymbol{M})]$, the nonconvex de-biased estimator $\boldsymbol{M}^{\text{ncvx,d}}$, the tangent-space linearized estimator $\boldsymbol{Z} - p^{-1}\mathcal{P}_T\mathcal{P}_\Omega(\boldsymbol{Z} - \boldsymbol{M})$, and the de-shrunken factor product $\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{ncvx,d}}\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{ncvx,d}\top}$ coincide within $O(n^{-4}\sigma\sqrt{n/p})$ in Frobenius norm and obey the exact Gram de-shrinking identity $\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}\top}\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}} = \boldsymbol{Y}^\top\boldsymbol{Y} + \frac{\lambda}{p}\boldsymbol{I}_r$ (Sections 3.2 and 5.1, Lemma 3, Appendix C, Equation D.4).
  • supports Source: Inference and Uncertainty Quantification for Noisy Matrix Completion · Locator: Section 3.2 (Eqs. 3.7-3.9, pp. 6-7), Section 5.1 (Lemma 3, p. 19), Appendix C & D.1 (pp. 28-34)
Mechanism #claim-nonasymptotic-gaussian-factor-and-entrywise-confidence-intervals
Under $np \gtrsim \kappa^8\mu^3 r^3\log^3 n$ and $\sigma/\sigma_{\min} \lesssim \sqrt{p/(\kappa^8\mu r n\log^2 n)}$, Theorems 1–2 and 5–6 prove that $\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{d}}\boldsymbol{H}^{\text{d}} - \boldsymbol{X}^\star = \boldsymbol{Z}_X + \boldsymbol{\Psi}_X$ with i.i.d. rows $\boldsymbol{Z}_X^\top\boldsymbol{e}_j \sim \mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{0}, \frac{\sigma^2}{p}(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}^\star)^{-1})$ and negligible residual $\|\boldsymbol{\Psi}_X\|_{2,\infty} = o(\sigma\sqrt{r/(p\sigma_{\max})})$, while each entry satisfies $M_{ij}^{\text{d}} - M_{ij}^\star = g_{ij} + \Delta_{ij}$ with $g_{ij} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, v_{ij}^\star)$, $|\Delta_{ij}| = o(\sqrt{v_{ij}^\star})$, and plug-in variance $v_{ij} = \frac{\sigma^2}{p}(\boldsymbol{X}_{i,\cdot}^{\text{d}}(\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{d}\top}\boldsymbol{X}^{\text{d}})^{-1}\boldsymbol{X}_{i,\cdot}^{\text{d}\top} + \boldsymbol{Y}_{j,\cdot}^{\text{d}}(\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}\top}\boldsymbol{Y}^{\text{d}})^{-1}\boldsymbol{Y}_{j,\cdot}^{\text{d}\top})$ achieving asymptotically exact $(1-\alpha)$ coverage matching the oracle Cramér–Rao lower bounds of Lemmas 1–2 (Sections 3.3–3.4 and 5.2–5.5).
  • supports Source: Inference and Uncertainty Quantification for Noisy Matrix Completion · Locator: Sections 3.3-3.4 (Theorems 1-2, Corollary 1, Lemmas 1-2, pp. 7-11), Sections 5.2-5.5 (Theorems 5-6, pp. 20-23)
Application #claim-exact-frobenius-estimation-optimality-including-preconstant
Summing the de-biased factor decompositions over all $n^2$ entries, Theorem 3 proves that $\|\boldsymbol{M}^{\text{d}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_F^2 = \frac{(2+o(1))nr\sigma^2}{p}$ with probability at least $1 - O(n^{-3})$, matching the oracle tangent-space lower bound $\mathbb{E}[\|\widehat{\boldsymbol{M}} - \boldsymbol{M}^\star\|_F^2 \mid \Omega] \ge \frac{(1-\varepsilon)2nr\sigma^2}{p}$ of Theorem 4 with the exact pre-constant 2 (Section 3.5, Theorems 3–4, Appendix G).
  • supports Source: Inference and Uncertainty Quantification for Noisy Matrix Completion · Locator: Section 3.5 (Theorems 3-4, p. 11), Appendix G (pp. 53-55)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • When the Leverage/row norm ||U^*_{i,.}||_2 + ||V^*_{j,.}||_2 of an entry (i,j) vanishes (so v^*_{ij} = 0), the first-order Gaussian term g_{ij} vanishes and M^d_{ij} - M^*_{ij} is distributed as the inner product u^T v of two independent Gaussian vectors rather than a normal distribution (Equation 3.24, Section 6).
  • In the symmetric matrix completion setting where M^* = (M^*)^T and P_Omega(E) is symmetric, Remark 5 shows that the diagonal entry variance v^*_{ii} doubles to (4 sigma^2 / p) ||U^*_{i,.}||_2^2 because Z_X^T e_i and Z_Y^T e_i are identical rather than independent.
