Acc-DNGD (Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent)

Algorithms AIMech:AccDngdAcceleratedDistributedNesterovGradientDescent DRAFT
Aliases: Acc-DNGD, Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent, Acc-DNGD-SC and Acc-DNGD-NSC
An accelerated decentralized first-order optimization method (Qu & Li 2020, Section II, Algorithms 1-2, Equations 3a-3d and 4a-4d) that couples three consensus primal sequences x_i(t), v_i(t), y_i(t) with a dynamic average-gradient tracker s_i(t) over a doubly stochastic mixing matrix W = [w_{ij}], updating x_i(t+1) = sum_j w_{ij} y_j(t) - eta s_i(t), v_i(t+1) = (1 - alpha_t) sum_j w_{ij} v_j(t) + alpha_t sum_j w_{ij} y_j(t) - (eta / alpha_t) s_i(t), y_i(t+1) = (x_i(t+1) + alpha_{t+1} v_i(t+1)) / (1 + alpha_{t+1}), and s_i(t+1) = sum_j w_{ij} s_j(t) + nabla f_i(y_i(t+1)) - nabla f_i(y_i(t)) with s_i(0) = nabla f_i(y_i(0)).
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from unaccelerated Distributed Gradient Tracking / DIGing (AIMech:DigingAndPushDigingDistributedInexactGradientTrackingOverTimeVaryingGraphs, Nedic, Olshevsky, & Shi 2017; Qu & Li 2018), whose iteration complexity scales linearly with the condition number O(kappa) on strongly convex problems and O(1/t) on general convex problems, and from Dual Optimal Decentralized Acceleration (Uribe et al. 2020), which requires Fenchel conjugate oracle evaluations rather than purely primal gradient-tracking Nesterov extrapolation.

Mechanism #r441-inexact-centralized-nesterov-reduction-and-accelerated-rates
Because the tracker s_i(t) preserves the exact global gradient average (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n s_i(t) = (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n nabla f_i(y_i(t)) by induction (Lemma 1), the network-averaged iterates bar_x(t), bar_v(t), bar_y(t) evolve as centralized Nesterov accelerated gradient descent perturbed by consensus errors ||y(t) - 1 bar_y(t)|| (Section III-A, Equations 5a-5c), achieving O(1 / t^{1.4 - xi}) objective decay via vanishing step sizes eta_t = eta / (t + t_0)^{0.6 + xi} in Acc-DNGD-NSC (Theorem 1) and O((1 - c sqrt(mu / L))^t) linear convergence with alpha = sqrt(mu eta) in Acc-DNGD-SC when condition number L / mu dominates the spectral gap (Theorem 2, Remark 4).
  • supports Source: Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent · Locator: Section II-C Main Results (Theorems 1-2, Remarks 1-4) & Section III Convergence Analysis (Lemma 1, Equations 5a-5c), pp. 2569-2576
Mechanism #r441-coupled-four-inequality-small-gain-consensus-stability
Bounding the coupled consensus errors ||v(t) - 1 bar_v(t)||, ||y(t) - 1 bar_y(t)||, and tracker error ||s(t) - 1 bar_s(t)|| despite the 1 / alpha_t amplification in v_i(t+1) establishes that choosing step size eta <= O((1 - sigma)^2 / L) keeps the spectral radius of the 4x4 consensus-optimality system strictly below the Nesterov contraction factor 1 - sqrt(mu eta / 2) (Section III-C, Lemmas 7-10).
  • supports Source: Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent · Locator: Section III-B Consensus Error Bounds (Lemmas 2-6) & Section III-C Proof of Theorem 2 (Lemmas 7-10), pp. 2571-2578
Limitation #r441-three-vector-communication-per-round-and-step-size-limitation
Requires communicating three d-dimensional vectors (y_j(t), v_j(t), and s_j(t)) per neighbor at each iteration, and for non-strongly convex smooth objectives Acc-DNGD-NSC achieves O(1 / t^{1.4 - xi}) rather than the centralized O(1 / t^2) bound due to consensus-error accumulation.
  • supports Source: Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent · Locator: Section II-B Algorithm Description & Section II-C Remark 2, pp. 2568-2570
Application #r441-ill-conditioned-decentralized-logistic-and-huber-regression-application
Applied to ill-conditioned decentralized l_2-regularized logistic regression and smoothed Huber loss minimization over multi-agent sensor networks.
  • supports Source: Accelerated Distributed Nesterov Gradient Descent · Locator: Section IV Numerical Simulations (Figures 1-3), pp. 2578-2580

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires exchanging three d-dimensional vectors (y_i, v_i, s_i) per neighbor per round.
  • Requires knowledge of strong convexity constant mu and smoothness L to tune Acc-DNGD-SC.
