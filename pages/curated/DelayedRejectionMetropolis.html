Delayed-rejection Metropolis

Algorithms AIMech:DelayedRejectionMetropolis DRAFT
Aliases: Delayed rejection Metropolis-Hastings
A Metropolis-Hastings sampling procedure that can propose another candidate after rejection within the same transition, correcting acceptance for the probabilities of the forward and reversed rejected paths.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the path-reversal delayed-rejection construction in Mira Sections 2–3. Rejected internal candidates are auxiliary information; the chain remains at its original state until a candidate is accepted or the transition ends. It differs from across-iteration adaptive Metropolis and from independent repeated accept-reject generation. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For current x with pi(x)>0, propose y1 from q1(x,·) and use a1(x,y1)=min(1,pi(y1)q1(y1,x)/(pi(x)q1(x,y1))). If rejected, draw y2 from q2(x,y1,·). Accept y2 with min(1, [pi(y2)q1(y2,y1)q2(y2,y1,x)(1−a1(y2,y1))]/[pi(x)q1(x,y1)q2(x,y1,y2)(1−a1(x,y1))]). The zero reverse-path case has acceptance zero. A fixed two-stage implementation retains x if both tests reject. Higher stages require the corresponding reversed-path proposal and previous-rejection products; simply applying ordinary Metropolis to each retry omits those factors.
  • supports Source: On Metropolis-Hastings algorithms with delayed rejection · Locator: Section 2, Equations (1)–(4), printed pp. 232–234 / PDF pp. 2–4; Section 4, pp. 238–240 / PDF pp. 8–10

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Proposal support and well-defined forward-path probabilities are required; formulas apply to reachable rejection paths. Irreducibility and convergence need further conditions beyond detailed balance.
  • The record gives the explicit two-stage rule and identifies the higher-stage construction, not a claim that every history-dependent retry is valid.
  • Extra stages cost proposal and target evaluations. Per-transition asymptotic-variance comparisons do not guarantee faster estimates per unit computation.
  • A finite stage cap retains the original state after all rejections. Eventual acceptance is not guaranteed for unrestricted retry schemes.
