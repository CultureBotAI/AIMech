Betting confidence sequences for bounded random variables

Mathematical Methods AIMech:BettingConfidenceSequencesForBoundedMeans DRAFT
Aliases: Hedged Capital Process Confidence Sequence (Hedged-CS), Predictable Plug-in Empirical Bernstein Confidence Sequence (PrPl-EB), Approximate GRAPA Betting Martingale (aGRAPA), Hedged Grid Kelly Confidence Sequence (hgKelly), Betting Without Replacement Confidence Sequence (Hedged-WoR)
A nonparametric, nonasymptotic sequential and fixed-sample statistical inference framework (Waudby-Smith & Ramdas 2024) for estimating the common conditional mean mu in [0, 1] of a dependent sequence of [0, 1]-bounded random variables (X_t)_{t>=1} satisfying E_P[X_t | F_{t-1}] = mu by inverting composite family of test martingales (capital processes) K_t(m) = prod_{i=1}^t (1 + lambda_i(m)(X_i - m)) driven by F_{t-1}-predictable betting fractions lambda_t(m) in (-1/(1-m), 1/m) (Equation 14), or predictable plug-in Hoeffding and empirical Bernstein test supermartingales (Proposition 1, Theorem 2), so that by Ville's inequality the sublevel set C_t = {m in [0, 1] : K_t(m) < 1/alpha} forms a time-uniform (1 - alpha)-confidence sequence satisfying sup_{P in P^mu} P(exists t >= 1 : mu not in C_t) <= alpha and P(mu not in C_tau) <= alpha at any stopping time tau (Theorem 1).

Mechanism #predictable-plugin-empirical-bernstein-supermartingale-and-asymptotic-variance-adaptivity
For any [0, 1]-valued sequence with E_P[X_t | F_{t-1}] = mu and any predictable sequence lambda_t in (0, 1), defining the predictable empirical mean mu_hat_t = (1/2 + sum_{i=1}^t X_i)/(t + 1) and variance sigma_hat_t^2 = (1/4 + sum_{i=1}^t (X_i - mu_hat_i)^2)/(t + 1) with psi_E(lambda) = (-log(1 - lambda) - lambda)/4 yields a non-negative test supermartingale prod_{i=1}^t exp(lambda_i(X_i - mu) - 4(X_i - mu_hat_{i-1})^2 psi_E(lambda_i)) via Fan et al.'s inequality exp(xi lambda - 4 xi^2 psi_E(lambda)) <= 1 + xi lambda for xi >= -1 (Proposition 2), generating the closed-form Predictable Plug-in Empirical Bernstein (PrPl-EB) confidence sequence C_t^PrPl-EB = (sum_{i=1}^t lambda_i X_i / sum_{i=1}^t lambda_i) +- (log(2/alpha) + 4 sum_{i=1}^t (X_i - mu_hat_{i-1})^2 psi_E(lambda_i)) / sum_{i=1}^t lambda_i (Theorem 2), whose fixed-n half-width W_n under i.i.d. variance sigma^2 scales asymptotically as sigma sqrt(2 log(2/alpha) / n) almost surely when lambda_i = sqrt(2 log(2/alpha) / (n sigma_hat_{i-1}^2)) wedge c (Proposition 9).

Mechanism #hedged-capital-process-quasiconvexity-and-kelly-betting-hierarchies
For m-independent predictable pre-truncation bets lambda_dot_t^+, lambda_dot_t^- >= 0 truncated to lambda_t^+(m) = min(lambda_dot_t^+, c/m) and lambda_t^-(m) = min(lambda_dot_t^-, c/(1 - m)) with c in [0, 1) and allocation weight theta in [0, 1], the positive capital K_t^+(m) = prod_{i=1}^t (1 + lambda_i^+(m)(X_i - m)) is nonincreasing and log-convex on the active region, the negative capital K_t^-(m) = prod_{i=1}^t (1 - lambda_i^-(m)(X_i - m)) is nondecreasing, and both the hedged maximum K_t^pm(m) = max(theta K_t^+(m), (1 - theta) K_t^-(m)) and mixture martingale M_t^pm(m) = theta K_t^+(m) + (1 - theta) K_t^-(m) are quasiconvex in m so that their (1 - alpha)-sublevel sets B_t^pm = {m in [0, 1] : K_t^pm(m) < 1/alpha} and C_t^pm = cap_{i=1}^t B_i^pm are guaranteed connected intervals (Theorem 3, Lemma 2, and Section A.2); whereas the Hedged Grid Kelly process K_t^hgKelly(m) (Table 1, Proposition 5) is globally convex in m, unhedged m-dependent aGRAPA capital K_t^aGRAPA(m) can exhibit disconnected non-interval sublevel sets (Section E.4 counterexample).

Mechanism #universal-law-of-the-iterated-logarithm-rate-and-owens-empirical-likelihood-duality
Any Hoeffding factor exp(lambda(x - m) - lambda^2/8) with lambda in [-4/5, 4/5] is pointwise dominated on x, m in [0, 1] by a linear betting factor 1 + gamma^m(lambda)(x - m) with gamma^m(lambda) = exp(lambda(1 - m) - lambda^2/8) - exp(-lambda m - lambda^2/8) in [-1, 1] (Proposition 6), which implies via stitched geometric-grid mixtures that a discrete mixture of linear betting martingales achieves the universal law-of-the-iterated-logarithm shrinkage rate nu(C_t^L) = O(sqrt(log log t / t)) uniformly over all P in P^mu (Proposition 7) and O(1/sqrt(n)) fixed-sample width (Proposition 8); moreover, the optimal hindsight capital K_t^HS(m) = sup_{lambda in [-1/(1-m), 1/m]} prod_{i=1}^t (1 + lambda(x_i - m)) equals the exact reciprocal 1 / R(x_1^t; m) of Owen's (2001) nonparametric empirical likelihood ratio at lambda^EL = lambda^HS (Section E.6).

Application #finite-population-sampling-without-replacement-hedged-betting-confidence-sequences
For sequential sampling without replacement (WoR) from any finite population {x_1, ..., x_N} subset [0, 1]^N with mean mu = (1/N) sum_{i=1}^N x_i, replacing the static null mean m at step t by the predictable remaining population mean m_t^WoR = (N m - sum_{i=1}^{t-1} X_i)/(N - t + 1) in the hedged capital factors K_t^+(m) = prod_{i=1}^t (1 + lambda_i^+(m)(X_i - m_i^WoR)) and K_t^-(m) = prod_{i=1}^t (1 - lambda_i^-(m)(X_i - m_i^WoR)) with truncation lambda_t^+(m) = min(lambda_dot_t^+, c / m_t^WoR) and lambda_t^-(m) = min(lambda_dot_t^-, c / (1 - m_t^WoR)) yields an exact test martingale under H_0^WoR : mu = m (Proposition 4, Section A.3) whose (1 - alpha)-level set C_t^WoR forms a valid finite-population confidence sequence that collapses to the exact singleton {mu} at t = N (Theorem 4).

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Constructing valid betting martingales K_t(m) = prod_{i=1}^t (1 + lambda_i(m)(X_i - m)) requires known deterministic a priori support bounds [a, b] (rescaled to [0, 1]) so that predictable bets satisfy lambda_t(m) in (-1/(1-m), 1/m) and keep capital strictly non-negative; unbounded or heavy-tailed observations without known support bounds cannot use linear betting factors without breaking non-negativity (Section 1.2 and Section 4.1).
  • Unlike the closed-form Predictable Plug-in Hoeffding (PrPl-H) and Empirical Bernstein (PrPl-EB) intervals, the Hedged Capital confidence sequence C_t^pm has no closed-form algebraic endpoints and requires root-finding or grid evaluation over m in [0, 1] (with per-step cost O(G) for grid size G, though monotonicity of K_t^+(m) and K_t^-(m) enables binary search; Section 4.3 and Section B.4), while unhedged m-dependent aGRAPA bets can violate quasiconvexity and produce disconnected non-interval sublevel sets in pathological low-prior-variance regimes (Section E.4).
  • In finite-population sampling without replacement (Hedged-WoR), whenever the candidate remaining mean m_t^WoR = (N m - sum_{i=1}^{t-1} X_i)/(N - t + 1) falls outside [0, 1] at step t, the null value m is deterministically falsified (K_t^WoR(m) = infinity), and the method requires knowing the exact finite population size N in advance (Section 5.1).
