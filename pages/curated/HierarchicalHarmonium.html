Aliases: HH
A labeled harmonium model family coupling binary words, Gaussian image features and binary category indicators to shared Gaussian latent variables, with a proper joint Gaussian precision and classification through posterior label probabilities.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: Harmonium Models for Semantic Video Representation and Classification · Locator: Section 3.3, PDF pp.5-6, Figure 3 and Equations 3.8-3.12; Section 6, p.11
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Scoped to Yang and colleagues' shared hierarchical harmonium for semantic video. The label layer couples to the same hidden topics as text and images, unlike FoH's independently parameterized class models. Although its diagram has three tiers, the graph is bipartite between the latent variables and the combined text/image/label layer. The source uses one-hot labeled training shots and a single-label decision rule, while its Bernoulli label conditionals define a joint over the full binary label cube; the conclusion explicitly permits multiple active categories. This is not an ontology class hierarchy or an asserted one-hot generative model. Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #shared-label-coupling
The model shares word/image parameters across classes and adds label biases tau_t and label-topic couplings V_tk. Given h, words and labels have independent Bernoulli logits alpha+Wh and tau+Vh, while image features have Gaussian mean beta+Uh and identity covariance. Given all x,z,y, hidden variables are independent unit Gaussians with mean W^T x+U^T z+V^T y. As in FoH, the unbounded image/hidden joint requires I-UU^T positive definite; finite binary words and labels do not remove this requirement.
  • supports Source: Harmonium Models for Semantic Video Representation and Classification · Locator: Section 3.3, PDF pp.5-6, Equations 3.8-3.11; inherited Gaussian specification in Section 3.2, p.5
Mechanism #label-support-and-posterior
For observed x,z, write a=W^T x+U^T z. Integrating hidden variables gives p(y|x,z) proportional to exp(tau dot y+||a+V^T y||^2/2) over all binary label vectors. Labels generally become dependent after hidden marginalization; exact summation over 2^T configurations is possible in principle. Restricting support to exactly one active label would define a different model with categorical, rather than independent Bernoulli, h-conditioned labels. The reported single-label classifier chooses the largest posterior marginal P(Y_t=1|x,z); this decision does not enforce one-hot support in the joint.
  • supports Source: Harmonium Models for Semantic Video Representation and Classification · Locator: Section 3.3, PDF p.6, Equations 3.8-3.12; Section 6, p.11
Mechanism #missing-label-inference
When labels are unknown, the simple all-observed hidden Gaussian is replaced by a mixture over label configurations. Substituting the posterior mean hidden vector into a logistic label conditional is not generally the exact label marginal. The source explores fully factorized mean field and finite-chain approximations for prediction and learning, and compares CD with a noisy parameter-gradient procedure. These approximations are distinguished from exact finite label enumeration and from an independently verified posterior sampler over model parameters.
  • supports Source: Harmonium Models for Semantic Video Representation and Classification · Locator: Sections 4.1-4.4, PDF pp.7-8; Section 5.2, pp.9-11

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • One-hot training observations, independent Bernoulli label conditionals and a single-label argmax decision are different statements. The binary-cube law can assign positive probability to zero or multiple labels; posterior marginals need not sum to one. The graph's tiers do not establish hierarchical class subsumption.
  • Normalizability requires constraints on Gaussian couplings, which the inspected manuscript does not describe enforcing. The Appendix log-partition display is qualified as for FoH. The source's claimed general convergence of CD to maximum likelihood is not adopted.
  • The Gibbs description lists label and hidden updates appropriate to clamped-feature prediction, without fully specifying every free-phase update used during learning. The Langevin display perturbs parameters with likelihood-gradient information and short-chain approximations; its target, prior and discretization controls are not sufficient to certify exact Bayesian parameter inference.
  • The selected TRECVID experiments, latent-topic visualizations and class-similarity heatmap do not establish causal relationships, uniquely identified topics or universal classification gains. Finite calculations check binary versus one-hot support and a plug-in inference discrepancy; no author code or benchmark was run.
