DeepLIFT

Algorithms AIMech:DeepLIFT DRAFT
Aliases: Deep Learning Important FeaTures
A neural-network attribution method that distributes an output's difference from a reference activation to input differences using backward contribution propagation.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record covers the 2017 DeepLIFT method including distinct local rules; it is not identical to gradients, integrated gradients, Deep SHAP or the earlier Rescale-only formulation.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #attribution-method
Reference inputs determine reference activations. Local contribution multipliers compose backward subject to summation-to-delta; Linear, Rescale and RevealCancel rules determine local allocation, with RevealCancel treating positive and negative differences separately.
  • supports Source: Learning Important Features Through Propagating Activation Differences · Locator: Sections 3.1–3.5, equations 1–4 and Linear/Rescale/RevealCancel rules, PDF pp. 2–5

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Reference and local-rule choices affect attribution. The paper discusses cases favoring Rescale or RevealCancel rather than one universally superior rule.
  • Contribution conservation is not a proof of causal feature importance or exact Shapley values for an arbitrary network.
