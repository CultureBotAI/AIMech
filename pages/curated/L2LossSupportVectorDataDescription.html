L2-loss support vector data description

Algorithms AIMech:L2LossSupportVectorDataDescription DRAFT
Aliases: L2-loss SVDD, Squared-hinge-loss support vector data description
A support-vector data-description algorithm that learns a feature-space sphere using squared-hinge penalties on squared-distance violations, yielding a unique optimal center, squared radius and slack vector for a fixed positive penalty and feature map.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the target-only L2-loss extension in Chang, Lee and Lin Section 4.1. L2 denotes squared slack loss, not merely the Euclidean geometry already used by standard SVDD. It changes the dual penalty and zero-radius threshold and is distinct from linear-slack SVDD, negative-example SVDD, origin-separating one-class SVM and support-vector clustering. Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #squared-slack-objective
For C>0, minimize t+C*sum_i xi_i² subject to t>=0 and ||Phi(x_i)-a||²<=t+xi_i. Explicit nonnegativity constraints on xi are redundant at an optimum: a negative slack can be replaced by zero to improve the objective. This convex formulation has strong duality and a unique optimal center, squared radius and slack vector. Its squared violation penalty changes the method, even though both SVDD variants use squared Euclidean distances.
  • supports Source: A Revisit to Support Vector Data Description · Locator: Section 4.1, PDF p.12, Equation 27 and Theorem 7; Appendix E, pp.17-18
Mechanism #zero-radius-threshold
First consider the zero-radius auxiliary problem minimizing sum_i ||Phi(x_i)-a||^4, with optimal squared distances xi_i*. When their sum is positive, define C*=1/(2*sum_i xi_i*). For 0<C<=C*, the optimal squared radius is zero and the auxiliary center is optimal; for C>C* the radius is positive. This threshold depends on the data and feature map and is not the standard SVDD threshold 1/N. The auxiliary center need not be the sample mean. If all training features coincide, the optimal radius and slacks are zero at their common feature and the denominator formula is inapplicable.
  • supports Source: A Revisit to Support Vector Data Description · Locator: Section 4.1, PDF pp.12-14, Theorem 8 and Equations 28-29, 37; Appendix F, pp.18-20
Mechanism #positive-radius-dual
For C>C*, maximize sum_i alpha_i*K_ii-alpha^T*K*alpha-sum_i alpha_i²/(4*C) subject to alpha_i>=0 and sum_i alpha_i=1. The additional quadratic term makes the maximization objective strictly concave; there is no standard SVDD upper bound alpha_i<=C, though the simplex implies alpha_i<=1. Recover a=sum_i alpha_i*Phi(x_i), xi_i=alpha_i/(2*C), and t=||Phi(x_i)-a||²-alpha_i/(2*C) using any positive coefficient. This equality-simplex recovery is scoped to the positive-radius regime and must not be applied indiscriminately below C*.

Limitation #prediction-and-evidence-limits
Prediction compares feature-space squared distance to the learned squared radius, using the kernel expansion when appropriate. A zero-radius fit accepts only queries mapped exactly to the center. The reassessment supplies mathematical derivations rather than a benchmark comparison establishing superiority of squared-hinge SVDD over linear-slack SVDD. Standard SVDD support-count and box-constraint error arguments do not transfer unchanged to this loss. For the symmetric linear-kernel sample {-2,-1,1,2}, C*=1/20; at C=1/10 the solution has center 0, t=3/2 and objective 11/4, as verified by exact KKT and primal-dual calculations.
