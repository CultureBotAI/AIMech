Locally Linear Embedding

Algorithms AIMech:LocallyLinearEmbedding DRAFT
Aliases: LLE
A nonlinear dimensionality-reduction algorithm that learns sparse, sum-to-one linear reconstructions of each point from nearby points and computes embedding coordinates by minimizing reconstruction error with those same weights.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope LLE preserves local reconstruction relations rather than explicitly fitting all-pairs geodesic distances. It is distinct from Isomap, a linear PCA projection, Laplacian Eigenmaps, and a learned neural embedding layer. The expanded 2003 paper elaborates the same LLE identity; optional extensions are not automatically separate aliases. Mechanism #method-scope
Select neighbors, solve a constrained least-squares reconstruction for each input with weights zero outside its neighborhood and summing to one, then keep the weights fixed while minimizing embedded reconstruction error under centering and covariance constraints. The final step uses the smallest eigenmodes of (I − W)^T(I − W), excluding the constant translation mode. Weight regularization is needed for singular or poorly conditioned local fits.
  • supports Source: Nonlinear Dimensionality Reduction by Locally Linear Embedding · Locator: Equations (1)–(3), Figure 2 and notes 5–9, printed pp. 2323–2326 / PDF pp. 1–4
Limitation #local-fit-and-extension-limits
The expanded LLE paper explicitly distinguishes similarity-transform invariance from shear invariance, discusses neighborhood and connectivity failures, conditions singular Gram matrices with a weight penalty, and separates the fixed-data embedding from optional mappings to unseen points.
  • supports Source: Think Globally, Fit Locally: Unsupervised Learning of Low Dimensional Manifolds · Locator: Section 2 and Figure 4, pp. 122–125 / PDF pp. 4–7; Sections 4.1–4.3, pp. 131–135 / PDF pp. 13–17; Section 6, p. 141 / PDF p. 23

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Neighborhoods must adequately represent local structure; excessive neighborhood size can violate local linearity and sparse or disconnected neighborhoods require separate treatment.
  • The optimized weights are invariant to translation, rotation and homogeneous scaling of a neighborhood, not to arbitrary affine shears.
  • Global minimization of the stated quadratic embedding objective does not establish faithful recovery of an arbitrary manifold or a unique coordinate system when eigenspaces are degenerate.
  • The core algorithm embeds its supplied point set; applying it to new points requires an additional mapping or recomputation.
