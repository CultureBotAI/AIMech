Wiener model

Model Families AIMech:WienerModel DRAFT
A real-valued, single-input single-output, discrete-time deterministic nonlinear dynamical model family formed by a causal linear time-invariant system followed by a memoryless static nonlinearity.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #linear-then-static
Write z_t=sum_{j=0}^{t-1} h_j*u_(t-j) for the zero-state linear response and y_t=g(z_t). The linear block first combines inputs across time; the memoryless nonlinearity then transforms that scalar. This differs in general from applying g before filtering: with h=(1,2), inputs (1,2,-1) and g(v)=v², the first three Wiener outputs are (1,16,9), whereas the corresponding Hammerstein outputs are (1,6,9). This is a finite curator example of block-order dependence, not an empirical comparison.
  • supports Source: Input-Output Parametric Models for Nonlinear Systems. Part II Stochastic Nonlinear Systems · Locator: Section 3, Figure 3.1 and Equations 3.1–3.4, PDF pp. 13–14 / printed pp. 10–11; curator exact calculations in research/discovery/cascade-model-boundaries.py
Mechanism #conditional-output-recurrence
For a finite-dimensional linear block satisfying z_(t+n)=sum_{i=1}^n a_i*z_(t+n-i)+sum_{j=0}^n b_j*u_(t+n-j), an inverse of g on the relevant internal-signal range gives y_(t+n)=g(sum_i a_i*g^(-1)(y_(t+n-i))+sum_j b_j*u_(t+n-j)). If the inverse is only valid on a selected interval, the required internal history must stay on that branch. The convolutional cascade itself does not require an inverse. Finite state order also does not imply a finite impulse-response memory.
  • supports Source: Input-Output Parametric Models for Nonlinear Systems. Part II Stochastic Nonlinear Systems · Locator: Section 3, Equations 3.2–3.8, PDF pp. 14–15 / printed pp. 11–12
Limitation #noninjective-output-boundary
For the source example x_next=x+u and y=x+x², two reachable internal states x=0 and x=-1 have the same output 0, but with the same current input u=1/4 the next outputs are 5/16 and -3/16. Thus a single-valued update from only current y and u cannot cover both branches. Restricting x>-1/2 gives the inverse branch used in the source. This does not rule out other histories, state tracking or a narrower operating domain.
  • supports Source: Input-Output Parametric Models for Nonlinear Systems. Part II Stochastic Nonlinear Systems · Locator: Section 2, Example 2.1, Equations 2.6 and 2.22–2.25, PDF pp. 9,11–12 / printed pp. 6,8–9; curator exact calculations in research/discovery/cascade-model-boundaries.py
Limitation #internal-scale-ambiguity
Without a normalization, internal gain can be exchanged between the two blocks. For nonzero c, replacing the zero-state linear operator L by c*L and g(v) by g(v/c) preserves g(Lu) for every admissible input. A c=3 finite example reproduces the same outputs. This algebraic ambiguity means identical external responses alone do not uniquely determine both internal block gains; it does not establish the full set of identification ambiguities.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The scoped evidence covers real-valued, single-input single-output, discrete-time deterministic cascades with a causal time-invariant linear block and a memoryless single-valued nonlinearity. Direct feedthrough is permitted. Continuous-time, multivariable, parallel and stochastic variants need explicit additional assumptions.
  • A model family is distinct from its parameter estimator, internal-state realization, trained instance and physical mechanism. Noise placement, initial state, stability, excitation, identifiability and generalization are separate choices; no universal identification or forecasting guarantee is asserted.
  • Invertibility assumptions used to derive a particular output-history recurrence are not definition-level requirements for a cascade model. Nonlinear and linear special cases can overlap across model families without making their ordered structures equivalent.
  • Evidence is the fully read 1984 technical report. The final 1985 journal version and earlier correlation-based identification reports remain follow-up sources. Several displayed equations in the report have scope or transcription problems; the cited cascade definitions and directly checked algebra are used here, not its unchecked global claims.
  • No model was trained and no empirical result was replicated. Exact finite examples are curator derivations, not author-reported experiments. Both records remain DRAFT; author self-review does not constitute independent scientific approval.
