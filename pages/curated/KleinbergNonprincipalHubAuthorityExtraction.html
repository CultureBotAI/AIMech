Kleinberg nonprincipal hub-authority extraction

Algorithms AIMech:KleinbergNonprincipalHubAuthorityExtraction DRAFT
Aliases: Nonprincipal eigenvector hub-authority extraction
A link-analysis procedure that uses paired nonprincipal eigenvectors of the authority and hub Gram matrices of a query-focused directed page graph, selecting extreme coordinates to obtain additional hub and authority lists that implicitly summarize different linked topics.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

5 Claims
Mechanism #paired-modes
For directed adjacency A, consider nonprincipal eigenvectors x of A^T A and their corresponding hub directions in A A^T. For positive eigenvalue lambda and unit x, the unit paired vector is y=A x/sqrt(lambda). Applying the authority or hub propagation keeps these paired directions invariant up to scalar multiplication. The source uses modes beyond the principal pair to obtain additional strongly linked collections.
  • supports Source: Authoritative Sources in a Hyperlinked Environment · Locator: Section 6 and Proposition 6.1, printed p. 21 / PDF p. 22; unit-eigenvector convention in Section 3, printed p. 10 / PDF p. 11; curator normalization qualification
Mechanism #coordinate-selection
For each selected pair, the source takes the c most positive and c most negative coordinates as hub and authority summaries. These are extrema-based representatives, not a full geometric embedding followed by a required partition. Sign reversal of both paired vectors exchanges the two ends without changing the represented pair of collections. When a mode has only one nonzero sign, the paper does not provide a meaningful second signed group or a zero-coordinate tie policy.
  • supports Source: Authoritative Sources in a Hyperlinked Environment · Locator: Section 6, printed pp. 21–22 / PDF pp. 22–23; sign qualification illustrated in research/discovery/hits-boundaries.py
Limitation #normalization-and-sign-boundaries
Proposition 6.1 prints y=A x although the earlier notation specifies unit eigenvectors. Positive modes require division by sqrt(lambda) for a unit pair; a zero mode maps to zero and cannot supply a nonzero paired eigenvector through this formula. The statement that every nonprincipal vector has both signs also needs qualification for reducible Gram matrices: an eleven-vertex example with nonzero eigenvalues 9 and 4 has a nonnegative secondary eigenvector. These are explicit curator algebraic qualifications, not claims about an inspected author implementation.
  • supports Source: Authoritative Sources in a Hyperlinked Environment · Locator: Section 3 unit-basis convention and Section 6 Proposition 6.1/sign assertion, printed pp. 10,21 / PDF pp. 11,22; exact arithmetic in research/discovery/hits-boundaries.py
Scope #implicit-cluster-scope
The extracted lists are intended as compact topic summaries of large clusters that are not explicitly represented. Different modes can repeat the same highly ranked pages, and the illustrated distinct collections were selected by hand from several modes. There is no guarantee of a disjoint partition, exhaustive query-sense coverage, unique semantic labeling or a prescribed number of distinct clusters. Bases within repeated eigenspaces are not uniquely determined by the eigenproblem.
  • supports Source: Authoritative Sources in a Hyperlinked Environment · Locator: Sections 5–6, printed pp. 18–23 / PDF pp. 19–24; eigenbasis qualification is curator linear-algebra context
Limitation #historical-evaluation
The manuscript illustrates multiple senses and related communities using ranked URL lists and describes a narrow-query example selected by keyword counts among early nonprincipal-mode lists. These demonstrations do not establish universal semantic separation or measured clustering accuracy. The Section 8 human studies use enhanced systems and are not direct evidence for this extension alone; the sign of an eigenvector carries no inherent social or normative meaning.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Mode selection, coordinate cutoffs, degeneracies and repeated leading pages affect summaries. Zero eigenvalues and one-sided modes do not provide the unqualified two-sided construction described in the source prose.
  • Historical hand-selected examples and finite arithmetic do not certify recovery of real communities, exhaustive retrieval or current-web effectiveness. Enhanced-system user-study results remain separate.
