FedCorr (Multi-Stage Federated Learning for Heterogeneous Label Noise Correction)

Algorithms AIMech:FedCorrMultiStageLabelNoiseCorrectionForFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedCorr, Multi-Stage Federated Learning for Label Noise Correction, LID Prediction Subspace and Adaptive Proximal Federated Label Noise Correction
A three-stage federated learning framework for heterogeneous client label noise (Xu et al. 2022, Section 3, Equations 1-7, Algorithm 1) that (1) in a federated pre-processing stage (T_1 iterations with small無-replacement sampling fraction gamma = 1/N), trains local models using Mixup cross-entropy plus an adaptive local proximal regularizer L(X_b) = L_CE(f_k^{(t)}(X_tilde_b), Y_tilde_b) + beta * mu_hat_k^{(t-1)} ||w_k^{(t)} - w^{(t-1)}||^2 proportional to each client's estimated noise rate mu_hat_k (Equation 5), partitions noisy versus clean clients via a two-component Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) over cumulative Local Intrinsic Dimensionality (LID) scores of local prediction subspaces LID(D_k, f_k^{(t)}) = (1 / |D_k|) sum_{x in D_k} (-(1 / k) sum_{i=1}^k log(r_i(f(x)) / r_max(f(x))))^{-1} (Equations 1-2), separates noisy local samples D_k^n via a second per-client loss GMM, and partially relabels the top-pi fraction of high-loss samples exceeding global prediction confidence theta (Equations 6-7); (2) finetunes the global model on relatively clean clients (mu_hat_k <= kappa) for T_2 rounds followed by a second relabeling pass; and (3) performs standard FedAvg training across all corrected client datasets for T_3 rounds.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from centralized noisy-label learning methods such as DivideMix (AIMech:DivideMixLearningWithNoisyLabelsAsSemiSupervisedLearning, Li et al. 2020) and Co-teaching (AIMech:CoTeachingRobustTrainingOfDeepNeuralNetworksWithExtremelyNoisyLabels, Han et al. 2018), which assume a single centralized dataset with homogeneous noise and cannot distinguish clean versus noisy clients or bound non-IID weight divergence across distributed clients; and distinct from Byzantine-robust gradient aggregators (such as Krum or Trimmed Mean), which discard client updates rather than correcting corrupted local sample labels.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r462-cumulative-lid-gmm-client-and-sample-noise-identification-mechanism
Computes each client's Local Intrinsic Dimensionality score LID(D_k, f_k^{(t)}) over its M-dimensional prediction vectors via k-NN distance ratios (Equations 1-2), exploits the higher cumulative LID of noisy prediction subspaces to separate noisy clients S_n from clean clients S_c via a server-side 2-component GMM, and then fits a client-side 2-component GMM on per-sample cross-entropy losses on k in S_n to isolate corrupted samples D_k^n and estimate the local noise level mu_hat_k^{(t)} = |D_k^n| / |D_k|.
  • supports Source: FedCorr: Multi-Stage Federated Learning for Label Noise Correction · Locator: Section 3.1 Dimensionality of Prediction Subspace (Equations 1-2, Figure 2) & Section 3.2 Stage 1: Pre-processing (Algorithm 1 lines 1-13), pp. 3-5
Scope #r462-adaptive-proximal-regularization-and-partial-relabeling-scope
Stabilizes local training under joint data heterogeneity and heterogeneous label noise without sharing raw client data by scaling each client's proximal penalty beta * mu_hat_k^{(t-1)} ||w_k^{(t)} - w^{(t-1)}||^2 proportionally to its estimated noise rate (Equation 5) and relabeling only the top-pi highest-loss noisy samples whose global-model confidence max(f_G^{(t)}(x)) >= theta (Equations 6-7).
  • supports Source: FedCorr: Multi-Stage Federated Learning for Label Noise Correction · Locator: Section 3.2 Adaptive Local Proximal Regularization & Label Correction (Equations 5-7), pp. 5-6
Limitation #r462-knn-lid-computation-and-clean-client-subset-limitation
Requires O(|D_k|^2 M) pairwise distance computations on each client's local prediction vectors per pre-processing iteration to estimate k-NN LID scores, and assumes the existence of a non-empty subset of relatively clean clients (mu_hat_k <= kappa) for Stage 2 finetuning.
  • supports Source: FedCorr: Multi-Stage Federated Learning for Label Noise Correction · Locator: Section 3.1-3.3 (Equations 1-2, Algorithm 1 lines 14-18) & Section 4.3 Ablation Study, pp. 3-8
Application #r462-federated-classification-under-heterogeneous-label-noise-application
Applied to federated image classification under heterogeneous synthetic and real-world label noise on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and Clothing1M.
  • supports Source: FedCorr: Multi-Stage Federated Learning for Label Noise Correction · Locator: Section 4 Experiments (Tables 1-5, Figures 3-5), pp. 6-9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires pairwise k-NN distance computation over local prediction vectors on every client during Stage 1 pre-processing.
  • Relies on a bimodal GMM separation assumption for both client-level cumulative LID scores and sample-level cross-entropy losses.
