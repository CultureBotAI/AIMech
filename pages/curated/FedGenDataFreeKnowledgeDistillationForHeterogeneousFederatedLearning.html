FedGen (Data-Free Knowledge Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning)

Algorithms AIMech:FedGenDataFreeKnowledgeDistillationForHeterogeneousFederatedLearning DRAFT
Aliases: FedGen, Data-Free Knowledge Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning, Federated Latent-Space Generative Knowledge Distillation
A data-free knowledge distillation algorithm for heterogeneous federated learning (Zhu, Hong, & Zhou 2021, Section 3, Algorithm 1) in which the server trains a lightweight conditional latent-space generator G_w(z | y, epsilon ~ N(0, I)) solely from uploaded client predictor heads {theta_k^p}_{k=1}^K and empirical label counts p_hat(y) by minimizing the label-count-weighted ensemble cross-entropy min_w E_{y ~ p_hat(y), z ~ G_w(.|y)}[l(sigma(sum_{k=1}^K lambda_k^y g(z; theta_k^p)), y)] plus a diversity loss (Eq. 4, Section 9), and broadcasts G_w to clients to regularize local training on synthetic latent representations z ~ G_w(.|y) without requiring any external proxy dataset.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from proxy-data ensemble distillation FedDF (AIMech:FedDfEnsembleDistillationForRobustModelFusionInFederatedLearning, Lin et al. 2020), which requires an external unlabeled public dataset on the server to query client ensembles in raw input space X, whereas FedGen is strictly data-free and learns a compact conditional generator in the intermediate feature space Z.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r423-conditional-kl-divergence-minimization-and-domain-discrepancy-contraction
Training client k's predictor on latent representations z ~ G_w(.|y) minimizes the conditional KL divergence D_KL(r(z | y) || p(z | y; theta_k)) between the global ensemble induced latent distribution r(z | y) and the local model (Remark 1), and augmenting m local samples from D_k~ with m synthetic samples from D~ contracts the H Delta H domain discrepancy to d_{H Delta H}(D_k'~, D~) <= (1/2) d_{H Delta H}(D_k~, D~) + (1/2) epsilon while doubling effective sample size m' = 2m, strictly tightening the federated generalization bound whenever epsilon < d_{H Delta H}(D_k~, D~) (Section 4.3, Theorem 1, Corollary 1, Section 8).
  • supports Source: Data-Free Knowledge Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 Theoretical Analysis (Remark 1, Theorem 1, Corollary 1) & Supplementary Section 8 (Proofs of Remark 1 and Corollary 1)
Mechanism #r423-modular-latent-space-distillation-when-sharing-feature-extractors-is-infeasible
Because G_w generates representations in the penultimate feature space Z rather than raw high-dimensional inputs X, FedGen remains functional even when clients only upload lightweight prediction heads theta_k^p and keep heterogeneous or privacy-sensitive feature extractors theta_k^f strictly local (Section 3.2, Table 3).
  • supports Source: Data-Free Knowledge Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.2 Local Model Training & Section 5.2 Sharing Only Prediction Layer (Table 3)
Limitation #r423-client-label-count-reporting-and-generator-broadcast-overhead-limitation
Requires active clients to report empirical class counts n_k^y to construct the label-weighted ensemble logits lambda_k^y (or else fall back to uniform label priors with reduced accuracy under severe label skew) and adds the communication and local forward-pass cost of the generator G_w.
  • supports Source: Data-Free Knowledge Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.1-3.2 & Supplementary Section 9-10 Privacy and Label Count Discussion
Application #r423-mnist-emnist-cifar10-and-celeba-non-iid-federated-application
Applied to heterogeneous federated classification on MNIST, EMNIST, CelebA, and Dirichlet-partitioned CIFAR-10, outperforming FedAvg, FedProx, FedEnsemble, and FedDF without using any external proxy dataset.
  • supports Source: Data-Free Knowledge Distillation for Heterogeneous Federated Learning · Locator: Section 5 Experiments (Tables 1-3, Figures 3-6), pp. 12884-12887

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires server-side optimization of the conditional generator G_w in every communication round.
  • Broadcasts G_w to participating clients alongside the global model parameters.
