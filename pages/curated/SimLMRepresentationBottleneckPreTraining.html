SimLM Representation-Bottleneck Replaced Language Model Pre-Training

Algorithms AIMech:SimLMRepresentationBottleneckPreTraining DRAFT

Aliases: SimLM, Similarity Matching with Language Model Pre-Training, SimLM Bottleneck Replaced Language Modeling for Dense Retrieval
A self-supervised pre-training algorithm and bottleneck architecture for dense passage retrieval that corrupts an input passage x twice using a frozen ELECTRA-style generator g with nested replacement probabilities p_enc = 30% and p_dec = 50% (x_enc = Sample(g, Mask(x, p_enc)), x_dec = Sample(g, Mask(x, p_dec)) such that every token replaced in x_enc is also replaced in x_dec), encodes x_enc with a deep Transformer encoder into a single last-layer [CLS] representation bottleneck vector h_cls without skip connections from earlier layers, feeds h_cls and x_dec into a shallow 2-layer bidirectional Transformer decoder, and optimizes the sum of all-position token-level cross-entropy replaced language modeling losses L_pt = L_enc + L_dec where L_enc = -(1/|x|) sum_{i=1}^{|x|} log p(x[i] | x_enc) and L_dec = -(1/|x|) sum_{i=1}^{|x|} log p(x[i] | h_cls, x_dec) before discarding the decoder for multi-stage bi-encoder fine-tuning and KL cross-encoder distillation.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from Condenser (AIMech:CondenserPreTrainingArchitectureForDenseRetrieval) and coCondenser (AIMech:CoCondenserUnsupervisedCorpusAwareLanguageModelPreTraining), which include a short-circuit skip connection from early encoder layers to the head and use [MASK]-based MLM; distinct from RetroMAE (AIMech:RetroMAERetrievalOrientedMaskedAutoEncoder), which uses [MASK] tokens and specialized position-specific attention masks in a 1-layer decoder; and distinct from ELECTRA (AIMech:ELECTRAReplacedTokenDetection), which trains a binary Replaced Token Detection (RTD) discriminator without a [CLS] bottleneck decoder (and performs poorly on dense retrieval: 31.9 vs. 33.7 MRR@10 in Table 1 and 36.2 vs. 38.0 in Table 7).

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r756-claim-skip-free-bottleneck-replaced-lm
To eliminate the input distribution mismatch of [MASK] tokens and prevent the decoder from bypassing the [CLS] representation via skip connections, SimLM samples replaced tokens from a frozen ELECTRA_base generator at rate p_enc = 30% for the 12-layer encoder and nested rate p_dec = 50% for the 2-layer bidirectional decoder, optimizing original-token cross-entropy L_pt = L_enc + L_dec across all sequence positions 1..|x| rather than binary replaced-token detection (outperforming Enc-Dec RTD at 36.2, Condenser at 36.9, and Enc-Dec MLM at 37.7 with 38.0 MRR@10 under BM25 negatives).
  • supports Source: SimLM: Pre-training with Representation Bottleneck for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section 1, Section 3.1, & Section 5.1-5.2, Equations 1-2, Figure 1, Tables 7-8, pp. 1-3, 6-7
Mechanism #r756-claim-three-stage-finetuning-and-distillation
After discarding the shallow decoder, SimLM initializes bi-encoder retrievers in a standalone three-stage pipeline (Retriever_1 on BM25 negatives, Retriever_2 on Retriever_1-mined hard negatives, and Retriever_distill distilled from an ELECTRA_base cross-encoder reranker) using temperature-scaled cosine similarity phi(q, d) = exp((1/tau) cos(h_q, h_d)) (tau = 0.02) with both query-negative and passage-negative contrastive denominators sum_{n_i in N} (phi(q^+, n_i) + phi(d^+, n_i)) and KL divergence interpolation L = L_kl + alpha L_cont (alpha = 0.2).
  • supports Source: SimLM: Pre-training with Representation Bottleneck for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section 3.2 & Appendix B, Equations 3-5, Figure 2, Table 13, pp. 3-4, 12-13
Application #r756-claim-msmarco-trec-nq-application
On MS-MARCO Passage Ranking Dev, SimLM progresses from 38.0 MRR@10 / 98.3 R@1k with BM25 negatives (Retriever_1) to 39.1 MRR@10 / 98.6 R@1k with mined hard negatives (Retriever_2) and 41.1 MRR@10 / 87.8 R@50 / 98.7 R@1k with distillation (Retriever_distill), outperforming multi-vector ColBERTv2 (39.7 MRR@10, >150GB two-stage index vs. SimLM's 27GB single-stage index) while achieving 71.4 nDCG@10 on TREC DL 2019, 69.7 nDCG@10 on TREC DL 2020, and 85.2 R@20 / 89.7 R@100 on Natural Questions test.
  • supports Source: SimLM: Pre-training with Representation Bottleneck for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section 4.2, Tables 2-4 and 6, pp. 5-6
Limitation #r756-claim-no-zero-shot-and-extreme-masking-limitation
Because SimLM pre-training optimizes token-level replaced language modeling without any sequence-level contrastive objective, the pre-trained checkpoint cannot be used directly as a zero-shot dense retriever without supervised fine-tuning; furthermore, setting the decoder replacement rate to 100% removes all local context and degrades MS-MARCO Dev MRR@10 from 38.0 to 36.6, and SimLM underperforms ELECTRA_base when used to initialize a cross-encoder reranker (42.9 vs. 43.7 MRR@10).
  • supports Source: SimLM: Pre-training with Representation Bottleneck for Dense Passage Retrieval · Locator: Section 4.2 (Table 5), Section 5.2 (Table 8), & Limitations, pp. 6-7, 9

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Cannot be used as an unsupervised zero-shot dense retriever directly after pre-training because its pre-training objective contains no sequence-level contrastive loss.
  • Less effective than ELECTRA_base as an initialization checkpoint for cross-encoder rerankers (42.9 vs. 43.7 MRR@10), and degrades when the decoder replacement rate reaches 100% (dropping from 38.0 to 36.6 MRR@10).
