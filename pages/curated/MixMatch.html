MixMatch

Algorithms AIMech:MixMatch DRAFT
A semi-supervised classifier-training procedure that averages predictions over augmented unlabeled inputs, sharpens the resulting soft targets, mixes augmented labeled and unlabeled input-target pairs, and optimizes labeled cross-entropy plus an unlabeled squared-probability loss.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope MixMatch combines averaged augmented-view label guessing, probability sharpening and the modified MixUp pairing rule. These components define a distinct training algorithm rather than an alias for MixUp, Mean Teacher or a hard pseudo-label confidence filter. Its classification soft targets differ from representation matching in BYOL, DINO and SimSiam. This record describes a training algorithm, not a trained checkpoint or fixed network architecture. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Given B labeled examples with one-hot labels and B unlabeled examples, augment each labeled input once and each unlabeled input K times. Average the current classifier probabilities across the K views of each unlabeled input, then sharpen the average q_bar using q_c=q_bar_c^(1/T)/sum_j q_bar_j^(1/T), for T>0; treat these guessed labels as constants during differentiation. Create augmented labeled pairs X_hat of size B and unlabeled pairs U_hat of size KB, then shuffle their concatenation into W. Mix each X_hat pair with the corresponding first B pairs in W, and each U_hat pair with the corresponding remaining KB pairs. For each MixUp operation draw lambda from Beta(alpha,alpha), alpha>0, set lambda_prime=max(lambda,1−lambda), and combine both inputs and targets with weights lambda_prime and 1−lambda_prime, keeping at least half of the first pair. For the resulting X_prime and U_prime, minimize average soft-target cross-entropy over X_prime plus lambda_u/(C|U_prime|) times sum over U_prime of squared target/prediction differences, where C is the number of classes. The reported implementation ramps lambda_u linearly. Guessed labels use current-model predictions; the evaluation-only weight EMA in Section 4.1 is not a Mean Teacher target generator.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The original setup pairs B labeled examples with B unlabeled examples and K augmentations per unlabeled input. Alternative batching or interpolation rules require an explicit variant scope.
  • For 0<T<1 sharpening concentrates probability. As a curator consequence of the formula, the T→0 limit is one-hot only with a unique maximum; tied maxima retain equal limiting mass. T>1 smooths rather than sharpens.
  • Correct label-preserving augmentation and useful interpolation are assumptions; guessed labels can be wrong and the algorithm has no defining hard confidence threshold.
  • The record does not assert published error rates or universal superiority. Reported T, K, alpha, lambda_u and evaluation-EMA settings are experimental choices, not fixed constants of every implementation.
