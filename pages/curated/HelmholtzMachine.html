Helmholtz machine

Architectures AIMech:HelmholtzMachine DRAFT
A hierarchical probabilistic neural model that pairs a top-down generative model with a separately parameterized bottom-up recognition model approximating the distribution of hidden causes given observed input.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record covers the original paired generative/recognition model family in Dayan and colleagues’ paper. Its original layered binary construction admits both a deterministic mean-field-inspired training approximation and a stochastic wake-sleep procedure; the architecture is not synonymous with either training rule. The generative and recognition parameters are separate, rather than tied symmetric connections of a Boltzmann machine. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
A top-down directed model p_theta(x,h) assigns probabilities to observed inputs and hidden configurations; a separately parameterized bottom-up model q_phi(h|x) produces a tractable approximate posterior. In the original layered binary construction, unit states in a layer are conditionally independent given the adjacent driving layer, with independent top-layer generative priors. Recognition proceeds through an acyclic bottom-up pass. The variational free energy is E_q[log q(h|x)-log p(x,h)], and log p(x) equals its negative plus KL(q(h|x)||p(h|x)); the negative free energy is therefore a lower bound under the normalized-model and support conditions. The paper describes stochastic recognition samples and, separately, a deterministic approximation that substitutes recognition means for sampled binary activities and neglects associated correlations. Its stochastic training alternative uses wake-sleep. The generative conditional need not be sigmoid: Appendix A considers noisy-or and other imaging rules. These choices do not change the defining presence of distinct generative and recognition models.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The tractable recognition family need not contain the true posterior, even when the directed generative model factors locally. Explaining-away dependencies can obstruct exact posterior matching.
  • The deterministic substitution of mean activations is an additional approximation, not exact evaluation of every nonlinear expectation in the stochastic model. The exact free-energy identity does not validate all approximation formulas or practical training updates.
  • Wake-sleep is a training option, and its sleep recognition step is not generally the data-ELBO gradient. A shared variational motivation does not establish a single objective decreased by all model variants.
  • The inspected author manuscript’s Equation (16) prints negative free energy as a positive sum of Bernoulli KL terms, conflicting with the preceding sign convention. The curated identity uses the explicit Section 2 decomposition; Appendix B derivative implementation was not audited and that printed approximate formula is not adopted.
  • The record describes a reusable architecture family, not a trained density estimator or a verified biological explanation. No exact posterior, universal compression gain or empirical ranking is asserted.
