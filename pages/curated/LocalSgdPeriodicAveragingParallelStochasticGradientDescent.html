Local SGD Periodic Model Averaging Parallel Stochastic Gradient Descent

Algorithms AIMech:LocalSgdPeriodicAveragingParallelStochasticGradientDescent DRAFT
Aliases: Local SGD, Parallel Restarted SGD, Periodic Model Averaging SGD, K-Step Averaging Stochastic Gradient Descent
A communication-efficient parallel stochastic optimization algorithm (Local SGD) in which K workers independently advance local parameter vectors via x_{t+1}^k = x_t^k - eta_t * grad f_{i_t^k}(x_t^k) and synchronize by global averaging x_{t+1}^k = (1/K) sum_{j=1}^K (x_t^j - eta_t * grad f_{i_t^j}(x_t^j)) only at communication steps t + 1 in I_T with maximum synchronization gap gap(I_T) <= H, bounding the mean squared worker drift from the virtual average x_bar_t = (1/K) sum_{k=1}^K x_t^k by 4 * eta_t^2 * G^2 * H^2 and achieving the optimal O(sigma^2 / (mu * K * T)) linear K-worker speedup with up to H = O(sqrt(T / K)) fewer communication rounds.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from control-variate client-drift correction in heterogeneous federated learning (AIMech:ScaffoldStochasticControlledAveragingForFederatedLearning), from proximal heterogeneous federated optimization with variable inexact local solvers (AIMech:FedProxProximalHeterogeneousFederatedOptimization), and from compressed every-step SGD with error memory (AIMech:SparsifiedSgdWithErrorMemoryForKContractiveGradientCompression). No pinned AIO class covers Local SGD periodic averaging or its virtual-average drift analysis.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r383-virtual-average-variance-reduction-and-drift-bound
For the virtual average sequence x_bar_t = (1/K) sum_{k=1}^K x_t^k, Lemma 3.2 establishes that the variance of the averaged stochastic gradient is reduced by a factor of K (E ||g_t - g_bar_t||^2 <= sigma^2 / K), and Lemma 3.3 proves that for stepsizes satisfying eta_t <= 2 * eta_{t+H} and synchronization gap at most H, the expected squared worker deviation from x_bar_t is uniformly bounded by (1/K) sum_{k=1}^K E ||x_bar_t - x_t^k||^2 <= 4 * eta_t^2 * G^2 * H^2.
  • supports Source: Local SGD Converges Fast and Communicates Little · Locator: Section 3 Proof of Theorem 2.2, Lemma 3.1, Lemma 3.2, and Lemma 3.3, pp. 4-5
Mechanism #r383-linear-parallel-speedup-and-communication-reduction
Under L-smoothness and mu-strong convexity with stepsizes eta_t = 4 / (mu * (a + t)), shift a > max(16 * kappa, H), and quadratic iterate weights w_t = (a + t)^2, Theorem 2.2 proves E[f(x_hat_T)] - f(x*) <= O(sigma^2 / (mu * K * T)) + O(kappa * H^2 * G^2 / (mu * T^2)) + O(G^2 * (kappa + H)^3 / (mu * T^3)), achieving linear K-fold parallel speedup whenever H = O(sqrt(T / (K * b))).
  • supports Source: Local SGD Converges Fast and Communicates Little · Locator: Section 2 Theorem 2.2, Corollary 2.3, and Remark 2.4, pp. 3-4
Limitation #r383-bounded-second-moment-and-heterogeneous-drift-limitation
The drift bound in Lemma 3.3 assumes a uniform second-moment bound E ||grad f_i(x)||^2 <= G^2 and shared objective f across workers; when workers optimize heterogeneous local objectives f_k != f with large H, local iterates drift toward distinct local optima unless corrected.
  • supports Source: Local SGD Converges Fast and Communicates Little · Locator: Section 2 Equations (3)-(4), Lemma 3.3, and Section 6 Conclusion, pp. 2, 5, 8
Application #r383-communication-reduced-parallel-training-application
Applied to parallel mini-batch and asynchronous stochastic optimization of regularized logistic regression and convex empirical risk minimization with periodic worker averaging.
  • supports Source: Local SGD Converges Fast and Communicates Little · Locator: Section 4 Empirical Observations and Section 5 Asynchronous Local SGD, Figures 1-2, pp. 6-8

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires synchronization period H <= O(sqrt(T / (K * b))) to prevent the O(kappa * H^2 * G^2 / (mu * T^2)) drift term from dominating the leading O(sigma^2 / (mu * K * T)) statistical term.
  • Suffers from client drift when local worker data distributions are non-IID unless augmented with control variates or proximal regularization.
