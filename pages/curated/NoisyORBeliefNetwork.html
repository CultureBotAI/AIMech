Noisy-OR belief network

Architectures AIMech:NoisyORBeliefNetwork DRAFT
A directed acyclic binary probabilistic network that models a node’s inactivity as the product of a leak-failure factor and failure factors for its active parents.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope The core record denotes the noisy-OR conditional model described by Neal, with zero/one variables and failure factors between zero and one. It is a probabilistic conditional family, not additive input noise, a deterministic OR gate or a sigmoid function. Neal also studies a signed-weight exponential extension; its distinct validity condition and loss of an individual failure-probability interpretation are stated explicitly rather than silently treating negative weights as ordinary noisy-OR failures. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
For node i, let q_i0 be the failure probability for an always-on bias or leak source and q_ij the failure probability associated with active parent j. Set P(s_i=0|parents)=q_i0 times the product of q_ij over parents with state1, and set P(s_i=1|parents) to its complement. This corresponds to independently failing activation contributions; it does not require the parent variables themselves to be independent. Multiply normalized node factors in a topological order to obtain the joint model and sample it ancestrally. For strictly positive failure factors, define w_ij=-log(q_ij), including the bias; then P(s_i=1|parents)=1-exp(-a_i), with a_i=w_i0+sum_j w_ij s_j and nonnegative standard weights. Neal’s separate signed-weight extension retains this exponential formula if a_i is nonnegative for every binary parent configuration, equivalently w_i0+sum_(j:w_ij<0) w_ij>=0. Negative weights in that extension need not correspond to failure probabilities in[0,1]. Parameter updates must preserve validity; the architecture itself does not specify a unique learning algorithm.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • A negative weighted input gives an invalid negative activation probability. The signed extension therefore needs explicit constraints and is not an unconstrained sigmoid parameterization.
  • Zero failure probabilities yield deterministic activation contributions and infinite log-failure weights; the probability-product form still defines the boundary, but the finite-weight reparameterization excludes those endpoints.
  • The independent-failure interpretation concerns contributions conditional on parent states, not a proof that the parent variables are independent or that the model’s edges identify interventions in real data.
  • Local normalization and ancestral generation do not remove hidden-variable marginalization or guarantee Gibbs mixing. Boundary zero probabilities can also obstruct positivity conditions used by sampling arguments.
  • Neal’s constraint-projection and learning discussion was inspected, but its appendix proofs and executable implementations were not audited. No projection-optimality theorem, general convergence or empirical advantage is certified.
