FedAUX (Certainty-Weighted Unsupervised Auxiliary Ensemble Distillation for Federated Learning)

Aliases: FedAUX, Federated Auxiliary Data Distillation, Certainty-Weighted Federated Ensemble Distillation with Unlabeled Auxiliary Data
An unlabeled-auxiliary-data federated learning and ensemble distillation framework (Sattler, Korjakow, Rischke, & Samek 2021, Sections 3.2-3.5, Equations 1-10, Algorithm 1, Theorems 1-2) that leverages an unlabeled auxiliary dataset D_aux = D_train U D_distill U D^- in two stages: (1) self-supervised contrastive feature extractor pretraining of h_0 on D_train to initialize client feature extractors in a common representation basin, and (2) certainty-weighted server ensemble distillation on D_distill where each client i solves a strongly convex L2-regularized logistic regression problem J(w, h_0, D_i, D^-) = (1/(|D_i| + |D^-|)) sum_{x in D_i U D^-} log(1 + exp(-t_x <w, h_tilde_0(x)>)) + (lambda/2) ||w||_2^2 separating its private data D_i (t_x = +1) from negative auxiliary data D^- (t_x = -1) (Equation 4), perturbs w_i^* with calibrated Gaussian noise N(0, sigma^2 I) for (epsilon, delta)-differential privacy (Equations 5 & 10, Theorem 2), and weights each client's teacher logits f_i(x) on x in D_distill by its certainty score s_i(x) = (1 + exp(-<w_i^* + rho, h_tilde_0(x)>))^{-1} + xi via A_s(x) = softmax(sum_{i in S_t} s_i(x) f_i(x) / sum_{i in S_t} s_i(x)) (Equations 3 & 6).

Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from FedDF (AIMech:FedDfEnsembleDistillationForRobustModelFusionInFederatedLearning, Lin et al. 2020), which averages client teacher logits with uniform weights A_mean(x) = softmax((1/|S_t|) sum_{i in S_t} f_i(x)) (Equation 2) and degrades under extreme non-IID domain/label skew where most clients are out-of-distribution on x; and distinct from FedMD (AIMech:FedMdHeterogeneousFederatedLearningViaPublicDatasetModelDistillation, Li & Wang 2019), which requires a labeled public dataset for transfer pretraining and uses unweighted logit averaging without differentially private out-of-distribution certainty scoring.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

4 Claims
Mechanism #r484-certainty-weighted-ensemble-distillation-mechanism
Instead of weighting all active client teachers equally on unlabeled distillation sample x in D_distill, FedAUX trains a one-vs-auxiliary logistic certainty scorer w_i^* on normalized frozen-extractor features h_tilde_0(x) separating private client samples D_i from negative auxiliary samples D^- (Equation 4), evaluates certainty s_i(x) = (1 + exp(-<w_i^* + rho, h_tilde_0(x)>))^{-1} + xi (Equation 6), and distills the certainty-weighted soft label A_s(x) = softmax(sum_{i in S_t} s_i(x) f_i(x) / sum_{i in S_t} s_i(x)) into the server prototype model (Sattler et al. 2021, Section 3.4, Equations 3-6, Algorithm 1, p. 4).
  • supports Source: FEDAUX: Leveraging Unlabeled Auxiliary Data in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.4 (Equations 3-6, Algorithm 1), p. 4
Scope #r484-l2-sensitivity-and-differentially-private-scoring-scope
Theorem 1 proves that the L2 sensitivity of the lambda-regularized logistic certainty classifier w_i^* over unit-norm features ||h_tilde_0(x)||_2 <= 1 is bounded by 2 / (lambda (|D_i| + |D^-|)) (Equation 8), and Theorem 2 establishes that adding Gaussian noise rho ~ N(0, sigma^2 I) with variance sigma^2 >= 8 ln(1.25 / delta) / (epsilon^2 lambda^2 (|D_i| + |D^-|)^2) (Equation 10) guarantees (epsilon, delta)-differential privacy for the published certainty scoring mechanism (Sattler et al. 2021, Section 3.5 & Appendix J, Theorems 1-2, pp. 5 & 18-21).
  • supports Source: FEDAUX: Leveraging Unlabeled Auxiliary Data in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.5 (Theorems 1-2, Equations 8-10), p. 5, and Appendix J, pp. 18-21
Application #r484-heterogeneous-vision-and-chest-xray-federated-distillation-application
Applied to homogeneous and heterogeneous neural network ensembles under severe Dirichlet label skew on CIFAR-10, STL-10, Office-Home domain shift, and ChestX-ray14 medical imaging benchmarks using unlabeled ImageNet / CIFAR-100 auxiliary data (Sattler et al. 2021, Section 4, Tables 1-6, Figures 2-6).
  • supports Source: FEDAUX: Leveraging Unlabeled Auxiliary Data in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 4 (Experimental Evaluation, Tables 1-6, Figures 2-6), pp. 6-10
Limitation #r484-unlabeled-auxiliary-dataset-availability-limitation
Requires the server and clients to have access to an unlabeled auxiliary dataset D_aux whose input modality is compatible with the target task for contrastive pretraining, negative scoring, and ensemble distillation (Sattler et al. 2021, Section 3.1-3.4, pp. 3-4).
  • supports Source: FEDAUX: Leveraging Unlabeled Auxiliary Data in Federated Learning · Locator: Section 3.1-3.4, pp. 3-4

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Requires an unlabeled auxiliary dataset for contrastive pretraining and ensemble distillation.
  • Strong differential privacy budgets (small epsilon) on small local datasets |D_i| require larger regularization lambda or higher Gaussian noise variance sigma^2, attenuating certainty-score discrimination.
