Aliases: N-pair-ovo loss
A supervised metric-learning objective that sums separate positive-versus-negative softplus comparisons for each anchor in a batch of positive pairs from distinct classes.
Definition Evidence
  • supports Source: Improved Deep Metric Learning with Multi-class N-pair Loss Objective · Locator: Section 3.2, PDF p. 4, Equations (7)–(8); Section 3.2.2, p. 5, regularization discussion
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is the named N-pair-ovo objective in Sohn’s Equation (8). It shares the N-pair batch construction with N-pair-mc but uses a sum of binary softplus comparisons, not one joint log-sum-exp over negatives. They coincide for one negative per anchor (N=2), but generally differ for N>2. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Given N>=2 positive pairs (x_i,x_i+) from N distinct classes and embeddings f_i,f_i+, define L_ovo=(1/N) sum_i sum_(j!=i) log[1+exp(f_i^T f_j+−f_i^T f_i+)]. Other positive-side examples supply N−1 different-class negatives to each anchor. The printed divisor is N, not N(N−1), so it averages across anchors while summing each anchor’s pairwise comparisons. In contrast, N-pair-mc places the sum over negatives inside a single logarithm. The expression uses dot products without an explicit temperature or cosine normalization.
  • supports Source: Improved Deep Metric Learning with Multi-class N-pair Loss Objective · Locator: Section 3.2, PDF p. 4, Equations (7)–(8); Section 3.2.2, p. 5, regularization discussion

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Use N positive pairs from N distinct classes; other positive-side examples must represent different classes for the stated objective. Pair sampling and class selection are part of this configuration, not interchangeable with arbitrary augmented-view negatives.
  • The main paper uses embedding-norm regularization instead of a hard unit-length constraint. This record specifies the loss term; it does not prescribe a regularization coefficient, encoder, optimizer or full training schedule.
  • No unconditional equivalence to a margin triplet hinge or an information lower bound is asserted. Objective shape and label-based batching do not establish downstream accuracy. No training or benchmark was reproduced.
  • The proceedings Supplemental.zip inspected during retrieval contains a different paper by Steinhardt and Liang. It is not supporting evidence; this record relies on the inspected nine-page N-pair main paper.
