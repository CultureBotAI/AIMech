A constructive combinatorial optimization method in which artificial ants sample solution components using shared pheromone trails and problem-specific heuristic information, then modify those trails through reinforcement and evaporation.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
In the inspected positive-distance traveling-salesman formulation, place m ants at starting cities and initialize pheromone trails. An ant at city i samples an unvisited city j with probability proportional to tau_ij^alpha * eta_ij^beta, where eta_ij=1/d_ij; normalize over the currently unvisited cities. Each ant stores its visited cities and returns to its start after visiting every city. After all tours are complete, the ant-cycle rule updates every trail as tau_ij_new=rho*tau_ij+sum_k delta_tau_ij_k, with delta_tau_ij_k=Q/L_k if ant k used that edge and zero otherwise. Here rho is retention, 1−rho is evaporation, and L_k is the completed tour length. Reset tour-specific visited lists and deposit accumulators for the next iteration while retaining the best tour found. Ant-density instead deposits a constant Q per traversed edge at each step; ant-quantity deposits Q/d_ij per step. Those variants do not use the ant-cycle complete-tour deposit.
  • supports Source: The Ant System: Optimization by a colony of cooperating agents · Locator: Sections II–III, PDF pp.5–8, Equations 1–4 and ant-cycle pseudocode; discussion and conclusions, PDF pp.22–24

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The displayed inverse-distance and inverse-tour-length expressions require positive finite distances and tour lengths. Probability normalization needs finite nonnegative weights with a positive total. Zero pheromone, underflow or infeasible candidates require explicit handling.
  • Setting alpha=0 removes pheromone dependence; it leaves heuristic-weighted random selection, not a deterministic nearest-neighbor rule. Equal initial pheromone likewise does not imply uniform selection when heuristic values differ.
  • All-ant reinforcement can concentrate search prematurely. A best-so-far archive is not an optimality certificate; the source’s empirical convergence discussion does not establish a general finite-budget optimum. Evaporation alone does not ensure every feasible solution retains positive sampling probability.
  • Representation, heuristic, parameters, start placement, constraints, tie conventions, stopping and update order define a concrete implementation. The natural-ant analogy and the source’s application claims are not independent validation or a universal performance guarantee.
