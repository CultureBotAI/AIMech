Multiple-try Metropolis

Algorithms AIMech:MultipleTryMetropolis DRAFT
Aliases: MTM
A Metropolis sampling algorithm that selects a candidate from several weighted forward proposals, draws a backward reference set containing the original state, and corrects acceptance using the ratio of their total weights.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This record covers Liu–Liang–Wong Section 2 finite-k MTM with one proposal kernel and a symmetric weight factor. It is not simple selection of the highest-density candidate, importance resampling without a correction, or the general auxiliary-variable extension in Section 3.1. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Choose k≥1, a proposal T with T(a,b)>0 iff T(b,a)>0, and nonnegative symmetric lambda positive wherever T is positive. Define w(a,b)=pi(a)T(a,b)lambda(a,b). From current x, draw k independent yj from T(x,·), and select y with probabilities proportional to w(yj,x). Draw k−1 independent xj* from T(y,·), and append xk*=x. Accept y with min(1,sum_j w(yj,x)/sum_j w(xj*,y)); otherwise retain x. For k=1 the symmetric lambda cancels, recovering Metropolis-Hastings. Selection and the backward reference set are essential to this correction.
  • supports Source: The Multiple-Try Method and Local Optimization in Metropolis Sampling · Locator: Section 2, Equations (2)–(5), numbered algorithm and Theorem 1, printed p. 122 / PDF p. 2; Section 7 discussion, p. 133 / PDF p. 13; Section 3.1 weight-order discrepancy, p. 123 / PDF p. 3

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Weight sums must be finite and usable. If all forward candidates have zero target weight, the transition can be completed by retaining x; weighted selection is not performed with a zero denominator.
  • The number of proposals and target evaluations grows with k. Larger k does not imply universal improvement per unit computation or independent retained draws.
  • The auxiliary-variable extension in Section 3.1 prints selection weights with an argument order inconsistent with Section 2 and its own acceptance expression. That extension is not asserted by this record.
  • Target invariance under the stated construction does not alone establish irreducibility or a mixing rate.
