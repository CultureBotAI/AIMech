Strategic oscillation

A search-control strategy that alternates search directions or regimes around selected boundaries, controlling excursions and returns to explore solutions beyond the region of a single one-direction search.

Identity Notes & Concept Scope The concept is deliberate boundary-relative control, not incidental oscillation of objective values and not every tabu-search method. Feasibility/infeasibility is an important application, while the primary source also describes cardinality boundaries, opposing constructive/destructive searches and rebounds without crossing. The rudimentary Glover–Hao rule is one instance, not the universal definition. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Choose a boundary or paired search regimes, move-selection rules for the relevant sides or directions, and a policy controlling reversal or excursion depth. Drive search toward and beyond a boundary, or rebound from it at specified depths, then reverse or change regimes so subsequent trajectories explore alternative approaches. Glover describes tabu conditions that compel crossings/returns or graduated penalties whose weights change with position and direction; a fixed sine wave or single depth schedule is not required. For the later illustrated feasibility instance, while feasible choose an objectively best improving feasible move when one exists, otherwise an objectively best available move, which may enter infeasibility. While infeasible, restrict to moves that improve the infeasibility measure and select the objectively best among them; if none improves infeasibility, first restrict to the least deterioration of that measure and then select by objective quality. These two ranking levels are not interchangeable. Keep feasible solution quality distinct from temporary infeasible search states.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The rudimentary rule does not ensure that every local step crosses a boundary, and generic strategic oscillation is not restricted to feasibility crossing. The boundary, quality/infeasibility measures, depth control, memory, ties and stopping must be specified for an executable instance.
  • Finding a better objective in infeasible space does not make it a valid answer to the constrained problem. The strategy alone provides no general feasibility, cycle-freedom or global-optimality guarantee.
  • The later paper analyzes a constructed hard example and explicitly disclaims a universally prevailing strategy. Its complete example derivation, broader appendix and empirical performance were not audited here; no generalized theorem or benchmark claim is curated.
