Escape-Through-a-Mesh Null-Space Phase Transition Thresholds for Nuclear Norm and PSD Trace Minimization

Aliases: Oymak-Hassibi Nuclear Norm Recovery Thresholds, Escape-Through-a-Mesh Matrix Rank Minimization Thresholds, Strong Sectional and Weak Null-Space Thresholds for Nuclear Norm Minimization, PSD Trace Minimization vs PSD Uniqueness Phase Transitions
A high-dimensional random-matrix and Grassmannian escape-through-a-mesh phase-transition methodology (introduced by Samet Oymak and Babak Hassibi in 2010) that derives explicit asymptotic sampling thresholds $\mu = m / n^2$ (and $\mu = m / (n(n+1)/2)$ for symmetric positive semidefinite matrices) as a function of normalized rank $\beta = r / n \in (0, 1)$ for exact recovery of rank-$r$ matrices $X_0 \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ from i.i.d. Gaussian linear measurements $y_0 = \mathcal{A}(X_0) \in \mathbb{R}^m$ via convex nuclear norm minimization $\min_X \|X\|_\star \text{ s.t. } \mathcal{A}(X) = \mathcal{A}(X_0)$ (Equation 2) and positive semidefinite (PSD) trace minimization $\min_X \operatorname{trace}(X) \text{ s.t. } \mathcal{A}(X) = \mathcal{A}(X_0),\, X \succeq 0$ (Equation 113). Combining exact Strong ($2\|W\|_r < \|W\|_\star$, Lemma 6), Sectional ($\|(I - P)W(I - Q^\top)\|_\star > \|PWQ^\top\|_\star$, Lemma 10), and Weak ($\operatorname{trace}(U^\top WV) + \|\bar{U}^\top W\bar{V}\|_\star > 0$, Lemma 13) null-space characterizations with Gordon's Escape-Through-a-Mesh theorem (Theorem 1), Stojnic's tail-thresholding variational bound (Lemmas 7, 11, 14), and the incomplete moments $\gamma(\beta) = \int_{F^{-1}(1 - \beta)}^2 x\phi(x)\,dx$ and $\gamma_2(\beta) = \int_{F^{-1}(1 - \beta)}^2 x^2\phi(x)\,dx$ of the Marchenko–Pastur quarter-circle singular-value law $\phi(x) = \frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{\pi}$ (and Wigner semicircle law $\phi_s(x) = \frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{2\pi}$ for the Gaussian Orthogonal/Unitary Ensemble $\mathcal{D}(n)$), Theorems 2–6 yield closed-form fixed-point curves $\mu_{\mathrm{strong}}(\beta)$, $\mu_{\mathrm{sec}}(\beta)$, $\mu_{\mathrm{weak}}(\beta)$, $\mu_{\mathrm{PSD,weak}}(\beta)$, and $\mu_{\mathrm{PSD,strong}}(\beta)$ that overcome the $\beta \to 0$ floor $1 - \frac{64}{9\pi^2} \approx 0.2795$ of earlier null-space bounds and demonstrate that weak recovery requires at most $3\times$ the degrees-of-freedom complexity $\theta(\beta) = \beta(2 - \beta)$.

Claims & Grounded Evidence

3 Claims
Mechanism #strong-sectional-weak-null-space-conditions-and-quarter-circle-threshold-equations
For an $n \times n$ matrix $X$ of rank $r = \beta n$ and a linear operator $\mathcal{A} : \mathbb{R}^{n \times n} \to \mathbb{R}^m$ ($m = \mu n^2$), exact recovery via nuclear norm minimization $\min \|X\|_\star$ is characterized by three progressively weaker null-space conditions on $W \in \mathcal{N}(\mathcal{A}) \setminus \{0\}$: (1) Strong recovery of all rank-$r$ matrices iff $2\|W\|_r < \|W\|_\star$ (Lemma 6, Eq. 20); (2) Sectional recovery of all matrices sharing support $\{P, Q\}$ iff $\|(I - P)W(I - Q^\top)\|_\star > \|PWQ^\top\|_\star$ (Lemma 10, Eq. 46); and (3) Weak recovery of a fixed $X = U\Sigma V^\top$ iff $\operatorname{trace}(U^\top WV) + \|\bar{U}^\top W\bar{V}\|_\star > 0$ (Lemma 13, Eq. 78). Combining Gordon's Escape-Through-a-Mesh theorem (Theorem 1) with quarter-circle tail moments $\gamma(\beta) = \int_{F^{-1}(1 - \beta)}^2 x\frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{\pi}\,dx$ and $\gamma_2(\beta) = \int_{F^{-1}(1 - \beta)}^2 x^2\frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{\pi}\,dx$ yields the sufficient sampling thresholds: (i) Theorem 2 (Eqs. 44-45): $\mu_{\mathrm{strong}}(\beta) = \gamma_2(1 - \delta_s) - \frac{(\gamma(1 - \delta_s) - 2\gamma(\beta))^2}{1 - \delta_s}$ where $\frac{\gamma(1 - \delta_s) - 2\gamma(\beta)}{1 - \delta_s} = F^{-1}(\delta_s)$; (ii) Theorem 3 (Eqs. 76-77): $\mu_{\mathrm{sec}}(\beta) = 1 - (1 - \beta)^2(1 - \gamma_2(1 - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}})) - \frac{((1 - \beta)^{3/2}\gamma(1 - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}) - \beta^{3/2}\gamma(1))^2}{1 - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}(1 - \beta)}$ where $\frac{(1 - \beta)^{3/2}\gamma(1 - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}) - \beta^{3/2}\gamma(1)}{\sqrt{1 - \beta}(1 - \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}(1 - \beta))} = F^{-1}(\delta_{\mathrm{sec}})$; and (iii) Theorem 4 (Eqs. 95-96): $\mu_{\mathrm{weak}}(\beta) = 1 - (1 - \beta)^2(1 - \gamma_2(1 - \delta_w)) - \frac{(1 - \beta)^3\gamma(1 - \delta_w)^2}{1 - \delta_w(1 - \beta)}$ where $\frac{(1 - \beta)\gamma(1 - \delta_w)}{1 - \delta_w(1 - \beta)} = F^{-1}(\delta_w)$.

Mechanism #elimination-of-suboptimal-low-rank-sampling-floor-and-three-times-weak-oversampling
As noted in Section 1 (p. 2) and Section 6 (p. 26), setting the tail-thresholding fractions $\delta_s, \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}, \delta_w$ to $0$ in Lemmas 8, 11, and 15 reproduces the suboptimal thresholds of Recht, Xu, & Hassibi [4] and Oymak, Khajehnejad, & Hassibi [12], which require at least $\mu > 1 - \gamma(1)^2 = 1 - \frac{64}{9\pi^2} \approx 0.2795$ even as $\beta \to 0$. Optimizing $\delta_s, \delta_{\mathrm{sec}}, \delta_w \in (0, 1)$ via Equations (45), (77), and (96) drives all three thresholds to $0$ as $\beta \to 0$ and ensures that the weak threshold $\mu_{\mathrm{weak}}(\beta)$ requires at most $3\times$ the normalized model complexity $\theta(\beta) = \beta(2 - \beta)$ across all $\beta \in (0, 1)$.

Mechanism #psd-inertia-null-space-conditions-trace-minimization-thresholds-and-uniqueness-gap
For symmetric positive semidefinite matrices $X \in \mathbb{S}_+^n$ of rank $r = \beta n$ measured by $m = \mu n(n+1)/2$ Gaussian observations, Lemmas 20 and 21 (pp. 19-20) prove that both weak and sectional recovery via trace minimization (Eq. 113) hold if every Hermitian $W \in \mathcal{N}_s(\mathcal{A}) \setminus \{0\}$ satisfies $\operatorname{trace}(W) > 0$ or $\eta_-(\bar{U}^\top W\bar{U}) > 0$, yielding the Lemma 25 / Theorem 5 threshold (Eqs. 137-138) $\mu_{\mathrm{PSD,weak}}(\beta) = 1 - (1 - \beta)^2\left(1 - \frac{\gamma_2(1 - \delta_{wp})}{2}\right) - \frac{(1 - \beta)^3\gamma(1 - \delta_{wp})^2}{2(1 + \beta - (1 - \beta)\delta_{wp})}$ where $\frac{(1 - \beta)\gamma(1 - \delta_{wp})}{1 + \beta - (1 - \beta)\delta_{wp}} = F^{-1}(\delta_{wp})$. Strong PSD recovery holds iff $\operatorname{trace}(W) > 0$ or $\eta_-(W) > r$ (Lemma 26, p. 24), yielding Theorem 6 (Eqs. 150-151) $\mu_{\mathrm{PSD,strong}}(\beta) = \frac{1}{2}\left[\gamma_2(1 - \delta_{sp}) + \gamma_2(2\beta) - \frac{(\gamma(1 - \delta_{sp}) - \gamma(2\beta))^2}{2\beta + 1 - \delta_{sp}}\right]$ for $\beta < 1/2$ with $\frac{\gamma(1 - \delta_{sp}) - \gamma(2\beta)}{2\beta + 1 - \delta_{sp}} = F^{-1}(\delta_{sp})$. By contrast, uniqueness of a PSD solution without trace minimization (Eq. 115) requires $\eta_-(\bar{U}^\top W\bar{U}) > 0$ (weak, Lemma 28) or $\eta_-(W) \ge r + 1$ (strong, Lemma 30), yielding $\mu_{\mathrm{uniq,weak}}(\beta) = 1 - \frac{(1 - \beta)^2}{2} > \frac{1}{2}$ (Lemma 29, Eq. 152) and $\mu_{\mathrm{uniq,strong}}(\beta) = \frac{1 + \gamma_2(2\beta)}{2}$ (Lemma 31, Eq. 154), proving that trace minimization dramatically reduces required sampling below the $50\%$ uniqueness barrier.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The phase-transition thresholds in Theorems 2–6 are derived in the proportional asymptotic regime $r = \beta n$ and $m = \mu n^2$ (or $m = \mu n(n+1)/2$) as $n \to \infty$ under i.i.d. Gaussian measurement operators $\mathcal{A}$ whose null spaces are Haar-uniform on the Grassmannian; structured non-Gaussian measurements (such as entrywise matrix completion) do not possess Haar-uniform null spaces and require incoherence conditions.
  • Theorems 2–6 provide sufficient recovery thresholds via upper bounds on the Gaussian width $\omega(S)$ in Gordon's Escape-Through-a-Mesh inequality; while empirical $40 \times 40$ SDP simulations match the weak thresholds $\mu_{\mathrm{weak}}(\beta)$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{PSD,weak}}(\beta)$ closely (Figures 1 and 2), formal converse tightness proofs require lower-bounding the Gaussian width on the mesh.
  • Primary-source typographical audit of `arXiv:1011.6326v1`: (1) Section 4.1 Proof of Lemma 6 line 4 (p. 5) writes '$\|X + W\| = \sum_{i=r+1}^n \sigma_i(W)$' omitting the nuclear-norm subscript '$\star$'; (2) Section 4.2 Eq. (57) (p. 11) writes '$\sum_{i=1}^c h_{2i}$' without the comma subscript used in '$h_{2,i}$' on the same line; (3) Section 4.2.1 Eqs. (72) and (74) (p. 13) write '$\|H\|_F - \sum_{i=1}^{c_{\mathrm{sec}}} h_{2,i}^2$' inside the square root, omitting the exponent '$2$' on '$\|H\|_F^2$' (compare Eq. 59 and Eq. 93); (4) Figure 1 legend (p. 17) spells 'Sectional threhold'; and (5) Section 5.5.1 Lemma 28 line 1 (p. 26) writes '$\Lambda \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times t}$' instead of '$\Lambda \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times r}$'.
