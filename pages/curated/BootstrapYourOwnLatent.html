Bootstrap Your Own Latent

Algorithms AIMech:BootstrapYourOwnLatent DRAFT
Aliases: BYOL
A self-supervised image representation-learning procedure that trains an online predictor to match a stopped-gradient target projection from another augmented view, while updating the target encoder and projector by exponential moving averages.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope BYOL is the 2020 online/target learning procedure, not statistical bootstrap resampling or a particular trained network. Only the online branch has the prediction head. Its normalized regression objective has no explicit negative-pair term or historical key queue. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Generate two augmented views v and v′ of each image. The online branch computes p(v)=q_theta(g_theta(f_theta(v))), and the target branch computes z_xi(v′)=g_xi(f_xi(v′)). Normalize both nonzero vectors and minimize their squared distance, plus the reversed-view term p(v′) versus z_xi(v), averaged over the batch. Stop gradients at target projections and optimize only the online encoder, projector and predictor. After the optimizer step update the corresponding target encoder/projector parameters by xi←tau xi+(1−tau)theta with tau in [0,1] and a chosen decay schedule. The target branch has no predictor to update. After training retain the online encoder f_theta and discard the training heads and target branch for the stated downstream representation.

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • The paper’s general same-architecture wording is qualified by its explicit online-only predictor in Figure 2 and the algorithm. Matching encoder/projector weights are averaged; a nonexistent target predictor is not required.
  • Absence of an explicit negative-pair term does not prove collapse impossible. The source’s conditional-variance explanation assumes an optimal predictor and simplified unnormalized, unsymmetrized dynamics; it is an intuition, not a general convergence theorem.
  • Batch normalization still couples examples. The batch-size ablation also accumulates gradients to control effective update size; it does not establish unconditional batch-size independence.
  • Augmentation, normalization, predictor design and target-decay schedule affect training. tau=1 freezes the target and tau=0 copies the online parameters; these endpoints do not by themselves reproduce the usual slow-target regime.
  • Vector normalization needs a defined nonzero-norm numerical treatment. No universal transfer benefit, benchmark reproduction or model checkpoint is asserted.
