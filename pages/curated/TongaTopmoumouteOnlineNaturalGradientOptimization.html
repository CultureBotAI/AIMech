TONGA Topmoumoute Online Natural Gradient Optimization

Algorithms AIMech:TongaTopmoumouteOnlineNaturalGradientOptimization DRAFT
Aliases: TONGA, Topmoumoute Online Natural Gradient Algorithm, Block-Diagonal TONGA
TONGA (Topmoumoute Online Natural Gradient Algorithm) is an online natural gradient optimization algorithm that approximates the empirical gradient second-moment (uncentered covariance) matrix C_t in R^{p x p} using a low-rank factor X_t in R^{p x r} (r = k + b << p) updated via C_t = gamma * C_hat_{t-1} + g_t g_t^T = X_t X_t^T with X_t = [sqrt(gamma) X_{t-1}, g_t]. Using the Woodbury matrix identity and an incrementally updated r x r Gram matrix G_t = X_t^T X_t, TONGA computes the regularized natural gradient direction v_t = (X_t X_t^T + lambda I_p)^{-1} g_t = X_t (G_t + lambda I_r)^{-1} y_t (where y_t = [0, ..., 0, 1]^T) in O(p r + r^3) time, and every b steps compresses X_{t+b} back to rank k via the eigendecomposition G_t = V D V^T. In its neural network block-diagonal variant, TONGA applies this rank-k online update independently to each per-neuron weight block, achieving rank k * (number of blocks) at O(p b) per-update complexity.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope Distinct from exact Natural Gradient Descent (AIMech:NaturalGradientDescent), which requires O(p^2) covariance storage and O(n p^2 + p^3) inversion; distinct from Kronecker-factored natural gradient methods (AIMech:KFAC, AIMech:EKFACEigenvalueCorrectedKroneckerFactorizationNaturalGradient, AIMech:TensorNormalTrainingKroneckerNaturalGradientOptimization), which factorize layer curvature as Kronecker products of activations and pre-activation derivatives; and distinct from Frequent Directions sketching of AdaGrad/Shampoo preconditioners (AIMech:SketchyFrequentDirectionsAdaptiveRegularizationOptimization), which subtracts the l-th singular value squared sigma_l^2 I_r during streaming SVD updates.

Mechanism #r648-bayesian-frequentist-generalization-optimality
Modeling the empirical training gradient g = (1/n) sum_{i=1}^n g_i as Gaussian around the true generalization gradient g_tilde ~ N(g_tilde, C_tilde / n), both the Bayesian formulation (with isotropic Gaussian prior g_tilde ~ N(0, sigma^2 I)) and the frequentist linear-preconditioner formulation (v = M^T g) show that the descent direction minimizing the probability P(v^T g_tilde > 0) of increasing generalization error is the natural gradient direction v proportional to -C_tilde^{-1} g (Section 2.1-2.2, Eqs. 1-7).
  • supports Source: Topmoumoute online natural gradient algorithm · Locator: Section 2, Section 2.1-2.2, Eqs. 1-7, pp. 2-4
Mechanism #r648-uncentered-covariance-collinearity-and-woodbury-gram
Replacing the centered gradient covariance C with the uncentered second moment C + g g^T yields (C + g g^T)^{-1} g = v / (1 + g^T v) where v = C^{-1} g, preserving the exact natural gradient direction while bounding step norm by 1 / (||g|| cos(g, v)) (Eq. 8); moreover, using the Woodbury identity and incremental r x r Gram matrix G_t = X_t^T X_t (Eqs. 10-14), TONGA computes the regularized natural direction and rank-k eigenvector truncation in O(p b) time per step when k <= b and k + b << sqrt(p).
  • supports Source: Topmoumoute online natural gradient algorithm · Locator: Section 3.2.1-3.2.3, Eqs. 8-14, pp. 4-5
Application #r648-block-diagonal-neural-network-approximation
By partitioning multilayer perceptron parameters into per-neuron weight blocks (weights entering each hidden unit and weights entering each output unit) and retaining the top k eigenvectors per block, Block-Diagonal TONGA increases the effective covariance approximation rank from k to k * (number of blocks) at the same O(p b) cost and achieves faster CPU-time convergence and lower test negative log-likelihood and classification error than stochastic gradient descent on MNIST and the 900,000-example Rectangles-images benchmark.
  • supports Source: Topmoumoute online natural gradient algorithm · Locator: Section 4, Figures 1-2, pp. 6-7; Section 5.1-5.2, Figures 3-4, pp. 7-8
Limitation #r648-full-matrix-low-rank-sensitivity-limitation
When TONGA is applied as a single low-rank approximation to the full p x p covariance matrix with small rank k to keep computational overhead low, it captures a much smaller fraction of the Frobenius norm than the block-diagonal variant, performs only comparably to stochastic gradient descent, and is sensitive to hyperparameter choices including the damping schedule lambda.
  • supports Source: Topmoumoute online natural gradient algorithm · Locator: Section 4, Figure 2, p. 6; Section 5, p. 7

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Full-matrix low-rank TONGA with small rank k captures a much smaller fraction of gradient covariance Frobenius norm than block-diagonal TONGA, performs only on par with SGD, and is sensitive to hyperparameter selection (Section 4-5, pp. 6-7).
  • TONGA introduces additional hyperparameters (retained rank k, eigendecomposition interval b, discount factor gamma, and damping parameter lambda) and lacks an automatic tuning procedure for lambda (Section 5 & Conclusion, pp. 7-8).
