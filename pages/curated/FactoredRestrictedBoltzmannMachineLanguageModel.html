Factored restricted Boltzmann machine language model

Architectures AIMech:FactoredRestrictedBoltzmannMachineLanguageModel DRAFT
Aliases: FRBM
A conditional neural word model that factorizes position-specific word-to-hidden weights through a shared word-embedding matrix and couples the next categorical word to binary hidden units in a context-conditioned restricted Boltzmann machine.
Identity Notes & Concept Scope This is Mnih and Hinton’s static FRBM language architecture. Factored refers to matrices G_i=R W_i with shared learned word features, not explicit part-of-speech or morphological factors. Its context words are conditioned on; their marginal distribution is not learned by this single conditional. Temporal FRBM adds directed hidden-state history, while LBL removes stochastic hidden variables and Replicated Softmax models an undirected document of a given length. Claims & Grounded Evidence

1 Claim
Mechanism #method
Write r_w for the shared column embedding of word w. For context positions 1 through n-1 and candidate word w at position n, define t=b_h+sum_(i<n) W_i^T r_context_i+W_n^T r_w and candidate bias a_w=b_r^T r_w+b_w. The conditional joint energy is E(w,h;context)=-t^T h-a_w. Summing binary h gives unnormalized word mass exp(a_w) times product_j(1+exp(t_j)); normalize these masses over candidate words. Given all words, hidden units are independent Bernoulli with probabilities sigmoid(t_j). Given h, the next word has softmax scores r_w^T W_n h+a_w. Exact conditional likelihood gradients use data-posterior statistics minus model expectations, whose vocabulary sum is explicit. The paper instead trains latent-variable models using finite contrastive divergence: start at the observed word, update hidden, word and hidden states, and contrast resulting statistics. Some runs replace the sampled one-hot word with its probability vector, a mean-field reconstruction variant.
  • supports Source: Three New Graphical Models for Statistical Language Modelling · Locator: Sections 2–2.2, Equations (1)–(10), Figure 1a and context-conditioning footnote, printed pp.642–644 / PDF pp.2–4; Equation (5) bias signs qualified against Equations (2)–(4) and (9)

⚠️ Curated Limitations & Negative Results

  • Equation (5) prints negative candidate-bias terms, whereas exp(-E) from Equations (2)–(4) and the word conditional in Equation (9) require positive a_w. The curated marginal follows those defining equations and discloses the sign conflict; it does not infer author-code behavior.
  • The conditional partition is tractable by summing the vocabulary after binary-hidden marginalization. Generic RBM partition-function intractability must not be copied unchanged into this clamped-context construction; the exact calculation can still be expensive for a large vocabulary.
  • Finite-step contrastive divergence is not the exact conditional maximum-likelihood gradient. A deterministic probability-vector reconstruction is not a categorical Gibbs sample.
  • Sharing word features across positions does not make sequence context permutation invariant because W_i depends on position. Low-rank factorization constrains the effective interaction matrices.
  • No predictive ranking, semantic quality guarantee, global training convergence or implementation certification is asserted.
