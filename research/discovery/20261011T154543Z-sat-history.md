| [AIMech:HSAT](../../data/curated/HSAT.yaml) | A GSAT variant that breaks ties among best-scoring variable flips by choosing the variable flipped least recently in the current attempt, using a fixed arbitrary order for ties among never-flipped variables. |
| [AIMech:Novelty](../../data/curated/Novelty.yaml) | A clause-based SAT local-search heuristic that ranks candidate flips by total violation count with oldest-variable tie breaks, normally selects the best candidate, and sometimes selects the second-best when the best was the most recently flipped variable in that clause. |
| [AIMech:RNovelty](../../data/curated/RNovelty.yaml) | A Novelty-derived SAT heuristic whose choice between the best and second-best flips depends on their objective-score gap when the best variable was most recently flipped; its original 1997 search strategy also includes a periodic random clause-variable move. |

All three records have bounded AIO no-match assessments. HSAT chooses the oldest among global best-score candidates; Novelty conditions a best/second-best choice on recency; R-Novelty also uses the score gap. The original periodic random override remains distinct from the1999 per-step random-walk extension and the differently named2004 configuration. Finite failure does not certify UNSAT.
