# Basis pursuit

Added [basis pursuit](../../data/curated/BasisPursuit.yaml) as a DRAFT mathematical method after visually reading all 31 pages of the 2001 primary reprint. The corpus contains 525 native concepts and 442 unchanged AIO records.

[Exact certificates](basis-pursuit-boundaries.py) distinguish minimum L1 from minimum support, verify nonunique optima and dictionary-scaling effects, and separate exact reconstruction from penalized denoising. No solver or benchmark was run.

The penalized denoising objective overlaps Lasso under matched conventions, so no duplicate native record was created. Constrained noisy forms and primary Lasso evidence remain follow-up work. Seven adjacent source cohorts were added; 461 pending and 45 partly addressed cohorts remain. Discovery continues.
